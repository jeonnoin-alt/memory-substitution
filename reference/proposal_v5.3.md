## Name

memory_or_instruction

## Title

Is Your Agent's Memory Just an Un-Optimized Prompt? An Absorbed Share **and** a Residual-Gain Test in ALFWorld

*(The title names both halves of the confirmatory family, because the previous round's decisive finding was that an absorbed share alone cannot answer the title's question. Which of six pre-registered titles the paper is submitted under is fixed by the truth table in Experiments, before any test cell runs; the one above is the title of the branch in which the full conjunction passes.)*

## Short Hypothesis

In ALFWorld, an instruction that a reflective optimizer wrote from a set of training episodes already holds the value that a procedural-memory bank distilled from those same episodes would add: at a fixed instruction and on the same held-out games, at least half of the bank's task-type-specific advantage is absorbed (A ≥ 0.5 with a 90% lower bound ≥ 0.2, co-primarily against the off-type denominator A = 1 − Inner(I_own)/Inner(I_oth) and against a provenance-neutral one A0 = 1 − Inner(I_own)/Inner(I0\*), which must agree) **and** what the bank still adds over no memory at that optimized instruction is equivalent to zero within ±3pt. The conjunction is the claim: an absorbed share on its own measures only the bank's task-type discriminability and can pass while the bank is still worth several points, so the residual gain g(I_own, B_own) is part of the confirmatory family and not a footnote. Everything else in this proposal is either an alternative explanation for that pair of numbers (headroom compression across each denominator's own level gap, mis-specification repair, bank staleness, distractor-robustness, task-type identification without episode content, retrieval format, compute) or a consequence of it (a block compiled once from the bank replaces retrieval on top of the optimized instruction).

## Related Work

**(0) Three concessions, stated first, and a ceiling.** The qualitative answer to "does an optimizer given the same episodes get roughly what a memory bank gets" is already published. EvoAgentBench (arXiv 2607.05202) puts ReasoningBank (+3.6) and GEPA (+1.2) on the same 528/267 training trajectories on Qwen3.5-27B, and MIPROv2 (arXiv 2406.11695) established the instruction-versus-demonstration ablation on a single training set with the finding that the split is task-dependent. Second, "memory as an instruction" is not a framing we invented: ExpeL (arXiv 2308.10144) distills training episodes into a natural-language *insights* block and prepends it to the prompt, so its learned artifact is an instruction in all but name, and GEPA (arXiv 2507.19457) and ExpeL run the same learning principle — reflect on trajectories, write down the lesson — differing only in where the lesson is stored and how it is read. **Third, and this is the concession three rounds of reviewers were right to press: ExpeL's own ablation is a measurement of this kind, not merely a design pattern.** It removes the prepended insight block and the retrieved-trajectory block **separately**, both built from one undifferentiated training pool, and reports each single-container condition against the full system in ALFWorld among other environments. That is a redundancy measurement between two containers filled from the same corpus, in this environment, in print since 2023. **The sentence "nobody has reported the number" is therefore withdrawn as written.** What is still unreported is the number *under provenance control*: ExpeL's two containers draw on one pool, so its ablation measures redundancy between two **representations of a corpus** rather than absorption of **specific episodes**; it is unpaired, not token-matched, single-run per condition, and its instruction container is written by a fixed extraction routine rather than by a reflective optimizer with a search budget. What this proposal adds is therefore narrow and precisely stated: labelling both containers by the provenance of the episodes that filled them, holding the test games and the instruction fixed while swapping only the injected block, separating "absorbed these episodes" from "learned this task type from any episodes" with an episode-disjoint instruction and from "was told which task types are coming" with a hand-written type-naming instruction, checking at item granularity whether the content the bank encodes is the content the instruction contains, and reporting both the absorbed share and the residual gain with confidence intervals. **The ceiling that follows is stated once and not argued with:** this is identification and a number on top of an accepted qualitative result, judges have converged on a novelty score of 4–5 for exactly that reason, that caps the venue expectation, and the value of the work is the pre-registration, the interval and the falsification routes rather than the surprise. We do not quote ExpeL's ablation numbers here, because arXiv is unreachable from this node and we will not cite a table we have not read; reading and quoting it exactly is the first item of pre-freeze obligation (5).

**(1) Procedural experience memory — the targets.** ExpeL (arXiv 2308.10144) collects training-task experiences, extracts insights and recalls them at inference; ReasoningBank (arXiv 2509.25140) distills strategies from self-judged successes and failures; Memp (arXiv 2508.06433) builds, retrieves and updates procedural memory and reports on ALFWorld; A-MEM (arXiv 2502.12110) and retrieval-augmented agents trained to use retrieved trajectories (arXiv 2603.18272) share one evaluation template: the memory-versus-no-memory delta is measured at a fixed, hand-written scaffold, or memory is compared against fine-tuning. The template assumes memory and instruction are non-substitutable containers. ALFWorld is where several of these effects were measured, which is why the confirmatory test lives there: MemHarness (arXiv 2607.28272) reports raw memory injection at 70.1% against 76.4% for no-memory RL and 85.2% for reconstructed memory; a procedural-memory retrieval study on ALFWorld (arXiv 2511.21730) finds embedding retrieval degrades under vocabulary shift while LLM abstractions transfer; "When Continual Learning Moves to Memory" (arXiv 2604.27003) documents retrieval pollution, context competition and dilution with negative transfer concentrated on hard cases; "Harness the Memory" (arXiv 2608.15008) finds excessive retrieval harms sequential decision-making specifically. These are effects of 5–15 points, an order of magnitude above the 0.6–3.6pt this program measured for the same bank family in tool-augmented multi-hop QA, and that gap is the whole reason for the environment.

**(2) Reflective prompt optimizers.** GEPA (arXiv 2507.19457, ICLR 2026 oral) samples trajectories, reflects in natural language and proposes prompt updates, outperforming MIPROv2 by >10% and GRPO by ~10% with up to 35× fewer rollouts. MIPROv2 (arXiv 2406.11695) is the closer framing precedent: it factorizes a prompt into instructions and bootstrapped demonstrations and optimizes both on one training set, and its instruction-only versus demonstration-only ablations are the nearest published prior on how much of a training set's value each container can carry. A retrieved experience bank is a demonstration container with a query-conditioned read; the present study is that ablation with retrieval, distillation, provenance labelling, an episode-disjoint control and token matching added. "Prompt Optimization Is a Coin Flip" (arXiv 2604.14585) reports that 49% of optimizer runs in compound systems land below zero-shot and that gains need exploitable output structure; with a local 32B reflector rather than a frontier one this is a live risk, so I_own > I0\* is a gated prerequisite (G2), not an assumption.

**(3) Three-way comparisons on shared trajectories — the closest work.** EvoAgentBench (arXiv 2607.05202) evaluates Vanilla, Memento, ReasoningBank, GEPA and a curated reference on a shared 528/267 split across four domains and three backbones, reporting (Table 3, mean ± SE over three runs) ReasoningBank +3.6, GEPA +1.2 and Memento −2.4 over a 37.5 baseline on Qwen3.5-27B, and GEPA +5.7 on Gemma-4-31B. It is the first published table with a retriever and GEPA on the same training trajectories, and we cite its headline as motivation rather than claiming it. Verified from its PDF: no combined optimizer-plus-memory condition (GEPA is "the only no-retrieval evolution condition"), no token matching, no compiled-bank arm, no paired statistics, and **no provenance manipulation** — every method sees the same undifferentiated training pool, so the table cannot say whether an optimizer's gain is specific to the episodes it saw, or whether a bank's gain survives once an instruction built from those episodes is in place. EvoClinician (arXiv 2601.22964) lists prompt-optimization and memory agents as baseline categories in a clinical application (excluded domain). TERMS-Bench (arXiv 2605.13909) uses GEPA as a prompt ablation to test whether prompt engineering saturates a benchmark — the same logical move, in another domain, without memory.

**(4) The confound-audit literature.** MemDelta (arXiv 2606.29914) shows headline memory gains flip under one-variable changes; "Diagnosing Retrieval vs. Utilization Bottlenecks" (arXiv 2603.02473) finds retrieval method spans 20 points while write strategy spans 3–8 and raw chunks match lossy writes; Memory-R2 (arXiv 2605.21768) fixes credit-assignment unfairness in RL-trained memory; EvoMemBench (arXiv 2605.18421) finds long-context baselines remain highly competitive against 15 memory methods; AutoMEM (arXiv 2606.04315) reports a self-managed flat-file harness beating eight memory systems. All audit confounds inside the memory pipeline or against context dumping; none posits an optimized instruction as a rival container, and none manipulates the provenance of what each container was built from.

**(5) The provenance primitive, and what we do not claim.** Asking whether a learned artifact's benefit is specific to the data it was built from is a standard move in the contamination and data-attribution literatures, and we have **not** been able to search that literature from this node. A targeted search is pre-freeze obligation (2) and is now **freeze-blocking**. **We claim no novelty for the crossover primitive itself.** We claim its use as the identification of container substitution — an instruction and a bank each labelled by the task types whose episodes produced them, absorption read off the instruction-provenance × bank-provenance interaction at matched test type, with an episode-disjoint same-type instruction separating episode-level from type-level absorption — and that claim is explicitly conditional on the scan. If the scan finds the primitive already applied to prompt optimizers and memory, the contribution reduces to the ALFWorld measurement, the on-policy control, the item-level content check and the compiled-block comparison, and the paper says so.

**(6) Containers other than the prompt.** Experience Distillation (arXiv 2607.21051) measures how much of an in-context gain survives when the experience moves into weights by context distillation (≥64.8%) versus SFT (3.8%) — the same retention-ratio shape as ours, with weights as the second container; it is the nearest measurement object and the reason our endpoint is a ratio rather than a difference. TMEM (arXiv 2606.04536) stores experience in fast LoRA weights; experience internalization (arXiv 2606.04703) finds principle-level experience more durable than instance-level. None uses an optimized instruction as the container, and none needs gradient access to the served model, which ours does not.

**(7) Compiled procedures.** **Two pre-2026 ALFWorld precedents belong at the front of this paragraph, and a scan weighted to the last six months missed them.** AutoManual (arXiv 2405.16247, NeurIPS 2024) builds rules from ALFWorld interaction and compiles them into a single markdown manual that replaces per-episode experience lookup; AutoGuide (arXiv 2403.08978) extracts state-aware guidelines from offline experience and retrieves them. Between them they already establish, **in this exact environment**, that episodic experience can be rewritten as instruction text and that a compiled manual competes with retrieval — so our prediction 6 is closer to a replication than a surprise, and we claim no novelty for the compile-once arm. Neither labels either container by the provenance of the episodes that filled it and neither runs the crossover, so the identification instrument survives; the compiled-block framing does not. Distilled skills beat Workflow Memory by 6.06pt in matched comparison, with 65.7% of the benefit attributable to procedural anchoring and retrieval precision collapsing from 29.6% to 3.3% as the pool grows (arXiv 2608.14036); model-generated skills help on average but hurt 25% of cases (arXiv 2605.23899); a single rewrite given the full training signal matches ten rounds of refinement within 0.2% (arXiv 2606.30775); procedural-memory refinement rounds are worth +3.7 to +6.7 points on enterprise tasks (arXiv 2606.23127). These make compile-once plausible rather than surprising; none compares a compiled artifact against live per-episode retrieval from the same bank at matched tokens on top of an optimized instruction, which is our compiled arm. MemAPO (arXiv 2603.21520) runs the coupling in the opposite direction (memory inside the optimizer); MAS-PromptBench (arXiv 2606.23664) studies prompt optimization without memory.

**Our position.** A memory bank and an optimized instruction are two containers for the same training episodes. MIPROv2 established the two-container ablation for static demonstrations; ExpeL already stores its memory as a prepended instruction **and already ablates the two containers against each other on one training pool**; AutoManual already compiles ALFWorld experience into a manual that replaces lookup; EvoAgentBench put a retriever and GEPA in one table. What none of them does is label the containers by *episode provenance*, and that is the whole of the identification: absorption read off the instruction-provenance × bank-provenance interaction at matched test type, with an episode-disjoint same-type instruction, a hand-written type-naming instruction and a token-matched raw-trajectory container separating the three rival explanations, and with the absorbed share **and** the residual gain each reported with a confidence interval — in an environment where procedural memory demonstrably carries 5–15 points, at matched tokens, with an on-policy bank control and a paired design. That is a narrower contribution than the title's question, and the paper's own novelty ceiling follows from saying so.

## Abstract

Agentic memory systems are evaluated by comparing an agent with a retrieved-experience bank against the same agent without one, holding a hand-written scaffold prompt fixed. We ask what fraction of that value is already held by an instruction that a prompt optimizer wrote from the same training episodes, and we report it as a scale-free ratio rather than a difference, so that the answer does not depend on how large the memory effect happens to be. The instrument is a **task-type provenance square** in ALFWorld. The six task types are partitioned by procedural similarity into a fetch-and-place set and an appliance-state-change set; from each set's training games we build one memory bank and one GEPA-optimized instruction from the *same* episodes. On held-out games of a given set we measure the **relevance advantage** Inner(I) = acc(I×B_own) − acc(I×B_oth) at a fixed instruction on the same games — a contrast in which headroom, policy and the residual unsolved set are identical by construction, because only the injected text changes. The **absorbed share A = 1 − Inner(I_own)/Inner(I_oth)** carries a threshold of 0.5 and a 90% lower bound of 0.2; the square is run in both directions, so bank relevance is a main effect and is not confounded with provenance. **An absorbed share alone cannot answer the title's question, and this version says so in its confirmatory family rather than in a limitation.** Inner compares two banks, so a bank that is worth the same to every task type cancels out of it: A = 1.0 is compatible with the bank still adding five points over no memory at the optimized instruction. The **residual gain g(I_own, B_own) = acc(I_own×B_own) − acc(I_own×M0)** and the **retention ratio R = g(I_own,B_own)/g(I0\*,B_own)** are therefore computed at n = 1,200 from cells already in the grid, and the headline is licensed only by the conjunction — **A and A0 pass and g is equivalent to zero within ±3pt**, the smallest margin this sample supports, which makes the test declarable only when the observed residual is within 0.7pt of zero. The outcome in which the shares pass while the residual survives is pre-registered as a different finding with a different title ("the type-specific content is absorbed, the generic content is not"), and every combination of {A, A0, residual equivalence, the mis-specification verdict, the mechanism bucket} is mapped to a claim and a title by one truth table fixed before any test cell runs. Because the off-type instruction is not merely lower-level but **mis-specified** for own-type games — it pushes wrong procedure, an on-type bank repairs the damage, and the repair would be scored as relevance in the denominator — the endpoint is **co-primary against a provenance-neutral denominator**: A0 = 1 − Inner(I_own)/Inner(I0\*) at the hand-written scaffold, under the same threshold, with the absorption claim requiring A and A0 to agree; a third denominator at the pooled instruction a practitioner would deploy (A_all, from a new Tier-0 cell) is reported alongside, and a pre-registered inflation check plus an item-level repair diagnostic decide in advance when A is not interpretable as absorption at all. Because each Inner pair is measured at instructions of different accuracy, every ratio retains one bias — the ratio of headroom compression factors across its own level gap — and it is computed **per denominator** rather than once: ≈0.06–0.12 for A across a 3–6 point gap, **≈0.16–0.22 for A0 across the 8–11 point gap to the hand-written scaffold**, ≈0.04–0.08 for A_all. A0's floor is therefore at or above the flat 0.2 bound, so A0's lower bound is read against a **matched-gap null replicate** — the same ratio computed between two ladder instructions of identical episode provenance separated by exactly that gap — which puts its operating requirement at 0.25–0.30 and makes A0 the harder of the two co-primaries to pass. The correction is bracketed from both sides: best-of-w sampling under-corrects, and a **level ladder of instructions optimized on a task-type-matched but episode-disjoint pool** over-corrects; the claim is read at the conservative end. That same episode-disjoint instruction decomposes A multiplicatively into an episode-specific share and a type-level share, so "same episodes, two containers" is tested rather than asserted, and the pre-registered reading of a null episode-specific share is a title change, not a failure. Around the endpoint: an **on-policy bank** distilled under the optimized instruction (the staleness rival); an **episode-disjoint bank** distilled from a different pool of the same task types, so episode provenance is manipulated on the bank side as well as on the instruction side; a **distractor-robustness** control run at both instructions with a pre-registered rule that routes the shrinkage to the help component or to the harm component; an **item-level content check** in which the bank is split by textual overlap with the instruction and the high- and low-overlap halves are run at both instructions; a planted control calibrating the instrument at the scale being measured; a treatment-free null replicate of the ratio itself; **a hand-written instruction that names the three upcoming task types and nothing else, and a token-matched container that pastes the same retrieved episodes raw instead of distilling them** — the two rival explanations three reviewers named, both now at Tier 0; the pooled practitioner configuration with its compiled-once block; and a jointly optimized instruction-plus-demonstration prompt as the prompt container's ceiling. Scope: n = 1,200 held-out ALFWorld training games, two optimizer seeds, 18 Tier-0 rows, ≈56,850 episodes and ≈990,000 model calls — 60–84 hours on the two A100-80GB this node actually has — every model role local (Qwen3-32B reflector, distiller and compiler), success determined by the environment rather than a judge, and every "no difference" statement reported as a bounded loss with a CI.

## Experiments

**HELD FIXED THROUGHOUT.** ReAct scaffold over ALFWorld's text interface; admissible-action list presented identically in every cell; **step limit L0 = 30 identical in every cell** except the two cells where the budget is the manipulated variable; one re-prompt on an unparseable action, then a no-op charged to the budget; **episodes end at success or step limit** — the ALFWorld analogue of proposal ①'s answerless-episode problem is the timeout, so **timeout rate, mean step count and invalid-action rate are logged per cell** and every accuracy delta is decomposed into success / timeout / invalid components; retrieval is per episode (goal text plus first observation as the key), MiniLM dense, k fixed by a dev tuning run with a budget equal to one optimizer run; decoding temperature and seed schedule {11, 23, 37}; one training pool feeds both containers; the held-out test split is touched once. Only the instruction slot and the memory-injection variant differ.

**MODEL ROLES (no frontier API).** Agent: the screened one of Qwen3.5-27B and Qwen3-32B, thinking disabled, one vLLM replica per A100. Reflector (GEPA), distiller (bank items), compiler (the static block) and demonstration selector: **Qwen3-32B**, switched to Qwen3.5-27B if the agent is Qwen3-32B so that no model reflects on its own traces. medgemma-27b-it is a cross-family check (Tier 1) and gpt-oss-120b an explicitly out-of-scope scale check (Tier 2). **No LLM judge is used for any quantity**: ALFWorld returns success directly. Consequence of a local reflector, stated up front: GEPA's published gains used frontier reflectors, so our I_own is a lower bound on what the instruction container can hold; a weaker instruction absorbs less, so the refusal **lowers A** and is conservative for the claim; and the optimizer-failure branch (G2) is live rather than decorative.

**TYPE SETS AND SPLITS (pre-registered, seeded, IDs committed).** The partition is fixed by a **zero-episode criterion computed before any episode runs**: for each of the ten 3–3 splits of the six task types that leaves both sets with ≥ 1,500 games, compute the mean share of a game's top-k retrieved items (over a 200-game sample, retriever only) that come from the other set, and take the split that minimizes it. The expected and pre-named winner is **T_A = {pick_and_place, pick_two, look_in_light}** — *fetch-and-place-or-illuminate*: locate an object, optionally a second instance of it, and carry it to a receptacle or to a desklamp that is switched on — against **T_B = {clean, cool, heat}** — *appliance-operate*: locate an object, carry it to one specific large appliance (sink, fridge, microwave), operate that appliance on it, then place it. v5.1 described T_A as involving "no object state change", which is **wrong**: look_at_obj_in_light finds and toggles a desklamp, and that is a device operation. The corrected distinction is that T_B's three types share a single skeleton whose only free slot is which kitchen appliance *transforms* the carried object, while T_A's three share the fetch-and-carry skeleton whose terminal act is placement or illumination and never transforms the object. **The criterion, not the story, fixes the split**: the retrieval-overlap number decides, the procedural reading is only the pre-named expectation with its number committed in advance, and if the criterion selects another split that split is used and the expectation is published as wrong. The number is committed with the split. This replaces v5's count-balanced partition, which split the near-isomorphic pairs across sets and therefore minimized exactly the relevance advantage G3 needs.

Game counts are estimated from task-directory proportions (train: pick_and_place 322, pick_two 354, clean 268, cool 212, heat 186, look_in_light 123 directories over 3,553 games), giving T_A ≈ 1,937 and T_B ≈ 1,616 games; **T_B binds every per-set allocation**. Per type set: **P_train 300** (the shared pool from which both the bank and the instruction are built), **P′_train 200** (disjoint, same three types, for Ladder T and I′), **S_screen 150** (agent × bank-format selection; its own line in this allocation, drawn from neither the reserve nor any gate or test split), **S_dev2 250** (every gate), **S_test 600** (touched once, by the final grid), reserve ≈116 in T_B and ≈437 in T_A. Pooled across directions: P_train 600, P′_train 400, S_screen 300, S_dev2 500, **S_test 1,200**. The GEPA rollout budget (350) is set to exceed P_train (300) so that episode coverage is ≈ one full sweep, which is how the lineage's original fairness constraint — both containers see the same episodes — is enforced rather than corrected after the fact; measured coverage is reported and, if it falls below 90%, the coverage-matched bank B_touched (Tier 1) is built. The official `valid_seen` (140) and `valid_unseen` (134) sets are never used for the primary and appear only as a Tier-1 external check. The exact recount happens at freeze: surplus goes first to S_dev2 (gate power), then to S_test; a shortfall reduces S_test equally in both sets and the MDE is recomputed and published **before** any test cell runs.

**The reserve, in priority order, with the games each use consumes.** The balanced reserve is **116 games per set, 232 pooled** (T_A's ≈437 cannot substitute, because every use must be balanced across the two directions). It is spent in this order and no other: **(1) an S_test shortfall at the exact freeze recount**, up to 116 per set — this pre-empts everything else, and a shortfall beyond it reduces S_test symmetrically with a republished MDE; **(2) the single pre-declared G3 re-measurement** in the [6, 9) window, which consumes ≈232 pooled, i.e. the whole balanced reserve; **(3) null-floor remediation on row 10**, which would need ≈200 pooled to move that row from n = 600 to n = 800. **At most one of (2) and (3) can be funded**, and if (1) consumes the reserve, neither is. The pre-registered fallback when a use is unfunded replaces the purchase rather than deferring it: G3 is then decided on S_dev2 alone, and the null-floor remedy becomes raising the threshold rather than raising n, decided and logged before unblinding. No game is promised twice.

**ARTIFACTS (built once, frozen, hashed).**
- **I0^bare** — the hand-written ReAct scaffold, no demonstrations, fixed and hashed before anything runs.
- **I0^demo** — the same scaffold plus **two bundled-expert trajectories** from the test game's own type set (the handcoded expert is verified working on this node, so the demonstrations are reproducible and cost no LLM calls). The published 5–15pt ALFWorld memory effects were obtained under scaffolds like this, so it is the harder baseline. **I0\*** denotes whichever variant the pre-registered rule selects.
- **B_A, B_B** — banks distilled by Qwen3-32B from all P_train episodes of the set's three task types, generated under I0^bare. Format fixed by screening (ExpeL-style INSIGHT/PROCEDURE/OUTCOME items, or ReasoningBank-style strategy items from successes and failures). ALFWorld returns ground-truth success, so the ReasoningBank-style variant uses environment-verified labels instead of self-judgement — a deviation from the published recipe that makes the bank *stronger*, which is conservative for our claim, and is logged as such. Item counts, k and dedup reported.
- **B_A^on, B_B^on** — the same distillation applied to P_train episodes **generated under that set's own optimized instruction I(T, seed 1)**: the on-policy bank.
- **B′_A, B′_B** — the bank-side mirror of I′: the same distillation applied to **P′_train** episodes (disjoint games, the same three task types, generated under I0^bare), k- and token-matched to B_own at read time. Its source pool is 200 games against B_own's 300, which can only weaken it, so the reading "Inner(B′_own) ≈ Inner(B_own) ⇒ the bank carries type-level content only" is conservative.
- **M_shuf** — token-matched random items drawn from the *own-type* bank with retrieval bypassed: the **within-domain** distractor control, promoted to Tier 0 because X_dist is out-of-domain text that a competent agent may simply ignore, while B_oth is topically plausible ALFWorld procedure.
- **B^hi, B^lo** — B_own split at the median textual overlap with the instruction into k- and token-matched halves; the overlap is computed against the **seed-2** instruction while the cells are run at the **seed-1** instruction, so the split is not selected on the arm it is evaluated in.
- **B_oth+own50** (and **B_oth+own25** at Tier 1) — the planted control: the off-type bank with 50% of its items replaced by on-type items from the same pool, engineered to reduce the relevance advantage by a known fraction (calibration at 2–6pt, the scale being measured). **The 25% arm moves to Tier 1 in v5.3** to help fund rows 19 and 20, so the control is single-point and its own dose-response is no longer testable; that cost is stated in prediction 2 and in Risk Factors.
- **X_dist** — a token-matched, plausibly worded, task-irrelevant block: procedural items distilled by the same distiller from this program's E1 multi-hop-QA episodes, reformatted into the ALFWorld item format and truncated to the median per-episode injection length of B_own. Fluent, structurally identical, zero ALFWorld relevance.
- **I_type** — a hand-written instruction given the three task-type names of the test set and their canonical skeleton and **nothing else**: no episodes, no trajectories, no distilled items. Written and SHA256-hashed before S_dev2 runs. It is the rival explanation for the entire headline — an instruction that absorbed nothing episode-specific but merely encodes which task family is coming would produce the predicted shrinkage in Inner — and three reviewers named it. **Tier 0 in v5.3 (row 19).**
- **R_raw** — the **token-matched raw-trajectory container**: the same per-episode retrieval, the same k, the same injected token budget as B_own, but the retrieved training episodes are pasted verbatim (observation/action/reward lines, truncated to the token budget) instead of distilled into items. It is the cheapest rival container — "just paste the episodes" — and it isolates the distillation step from the container question. **Tier 0 in v5.3 (row 20).** Its un-token-matched cousin **M_all** (the whole bank dumped into context) stays Tier 1: its prompt is ≈5× longer, costs ≈2× per call in throughput, and breaks the token match every other cell in the grid holds.
- **I(T_A, s), I(T_B, s)** — GEPA-style reflective optimization of the instruction slot on that set's P_train episodes only, seeded with I0\* verbatim, 350 rollouts, memory absent from context, s ∈ {1, 2}; episode coverage instrumented. **"Same episodes" is enforced by identity, not by the rollout cap:** I(T, s) and B_T are built from the *identical* set of P_train episode ids, the sorted id list is SHA256-hashed, and that hash is stored in both artifacts and checked at load time. The 350-rollout budget is a **consequence** of that identity (350 rollouts ≥ 300 episodes, i.e. ≈ one full sweep), not a fairness knob applied to the optimizer; Ladder T's larger budgets run on P′_train only, whose episodes enter neither container.
- **I′(T, r)** — the same procedure on **P′_train** (disjoint games, same three task types), one run per set carried to 500 rollouts with the incumbent snapshotted at r ∈ {50, 100, 200, 350, 500}. This is **Ladder T**: a single monotone knob (rollout budget), five levels, each level an instruction of the same kind as the treatment, produced by the same optimizer, that has never seen a P_train episode. **Three of its rungs go to S_test in v5.3, not two:** r_lo and r_hi, the pair that brackets ℓ_own, and **r_0**, the rung whose dev level sits within 2pt of ℓ_0 (the hand-written scaffold's level). r_0 exists to supply the **matched-gap null replicate** that A0 needs — the pair (r_0, r_hi) spans exactly A0's I0\*-to-I_own level gap with **no** difference in episode provenance between its two members. If r = 50 already overshoots ℓ_0 + 2pt on dev, one further snapshot at r = 25 is titrated (M0 only, on dev) and used as r_0.
- **I_all(s)** — GEPA on the union of both sets' P_train, 350 rollouts, s ∈ {1, 2}: the practitioner's instruction. **B_all** is the pooled bank; **C_all** the block compiled once from B_all.
- **I2(T, s)** — as I(T) but with the on-type bank retrieved into context during optimization; one seed per set (the coupling arm). **Tier 1 in v5.2**: the arm and its optimizer run move out of Tier 0 to fund the neutral denominator and the bank-side mirror.
- **J(T, s)** — MIPROv2-style **joint** optimization of instruction plus bootstrapped demonstrations on the same episodes, token-matched to the memory injection; two seeds per set. The prompt container's ceiling and a named refutation route.
- **C_all, C_own** — static blocks written offline by the compiler from the whole bank, length fixed at the median per-episode injection length measured on S_dev2; compiler prompt fixed on dev and reported verbatim.
- **Compute controls (not level controls):** best-of-w action sampling at w\*, where w\* is the value that matches memory's per-episode token overhead as measured on dev (expected w\* = 2); and step budget L = 1.5·L0.

**SCREENING (pre-registered, split-sample, published either way).** The environment is fixed by decision; the screening axis is agent × bank format, run on **S_screen (150 per set, 300 pooled — its own line in the allocation above, drawn from neither the reserve nor any gate or test split, and never used for gates or test)**. For each of the two agents, generate a 250-game bank-source pool under I0^bare, distil both formats, and measure the memory gain at the hand-written instruction for the four agent × format pairings. The pairing with the largest gain that also clears 5pt proceeds; every pairing's number is published and the losing pairings are reported as co-equal results, including the memory-weak regime. All gates are then measured afresh on **S_dev2**, which selection never saw, so the winner's curse is removed by construction.

**GATES (all on S_dev2, n = 500 pooled; each needs a point estimate and a lower 80% bootstrap CI; a failure triggers its pre-registered branch, never a silent continuation).**
- **G0 (dynamic range):** no-memory success on dev in [20%, 80%]; L0 set so the no-memory timeout rate lies in [20%, 60%]. Out of range → L0 is re-set once on dev and logged; the calls/episode budget is recomputed with it.
- **G1 (memory has something to give), measured under both I0 variants:** g(I0, B_own) ≥ 5pt, lower CI ≥ 2pt, with ≥ 20 fixes. **The primary scaffold I0\* is I0^demo if G1 passes under it; otherwise bare I0, and the dependence of the published effect on an empty demonstration slot becomes a headline caveat.** If G1 fails under both variants for every screened pairing → the memory-null branch.
- **G2 (the optimizer works):** acc(I(T)×M0) − acc(I0\*×M0) ≥ 3pt on both seeds and both type sets.
- **G3 (relevance — the gate the ratio's denominator needs):** Inner(I0\*) = acc(I0\*×B_own) − acc(I0\*×B_oth) ≥ **9pt** with lower 80% CI ≥ 5pt. If the point estimate lands in [6, 9) with lower CI ≥ 3pt, G3 is re-measured **once** on the full balanced reserve (≈230 pooled games) and the pooled estimate decides; this single extension is declared here, its multiplicity is noted in the analysis, and no further extension is permitted.
- **G3b (instruction provenance — restated in v5.2 so that it no longer gates on the mis-specification that inflates its own denominator):** acc(I_own×M0) − acc(I_oth×M0) on own-type games with a **lower 80% CI > 0** — direction only; the 3pt point floor is **removed**, because an instruction that learned nothing type-specific cannot have absorbed anything type-specific, but a *large* deficit is damage to I_oth rather than evidence for absorption, and v5.1 required exactly that damage. In its place comes a **routing rule, not a stop**: if acc(I_oth×M0) falls more than 3pt below acc(I0\*×M0) on own-type games, I_oth is logged as **mis-specified** rather than merely lower-level, the square still runs, and **A0 becomes the sole confirmatory number** with A reported as the repair-inclusive upper bound.
- **G4 (bracketing):** Ladder T's levels must bracket ℓ_own = acc(I_own×M0) on dev. The titration measures ℓ(r) at all five budgets (M0 only); the two bracketing budgets r_lo, r_hi go to S_test. If even r = 500 lands below ℓ_own, one more run is extended to 750 rollouts on dev; if it still cannot bracket, the targeted correction is an extrapolation, is declared so **before S_test is touched**, and the confirmatory reading falls back to the arithmetic headroom floor A_h with the best-of-w bracket as its upper bound.
- **G5 (instrument hygiene and throughput):** retriever relevance ≥ 60% on a 100-episode audit; stored-item truthfulness ≥ 70% on a 100-item audit; **aggregate throughput ≥ 11,000 model calls/hour**; and the dev-measured timeout rate is used to recompute calls/episode and re-publish the compute plan.
- **G6 (denominator sufficiency *and neutrality* — moved in v5.3 from S_test to S_dev2, because a ratio conditioned on its own denominator clearing a floor in the same sample is biased upward in the hypothesis's direction and its one-sided bootstrap bound loses nominal coverage).** It is funded by one new dev cell, **I_oth × {B_own, B_oth} at n = 500, 1 seed** (1,000 episodes ≈ 17,000 calls); Inner(I0\*) on dev is already G3's own statistic, so it needs no cell of its own. **Requirement:** Inner(I_oth) ≥ 5pt on S_dev2 with a lower 80% CI ≥ 2pt (SE(Inner) ≈ 2.15pt at n = 500 with one optimizer seed), and Inner(I0\*) ≥ 5pt, which G3's 9pt requirement already subsumes and which is reported separately. Below the floor, SE(A) at test would exceed 0.25 and the 0.5-against-0.2 test cannot conclude: the *ratio* half of the confirmatory family is declared inconclusive **before S_test is touched**, rows 2 and 3 still run because the residual-gain endpoint g needs no denominator, and the paper's primary becomes the residual-gain result with bounded-loss CIs. **Neutrality is decided here too, on dev:** if Inner(I_oth) − Inner(I0\*) exceeds max(2pt, 1.5 × the bootstrap SE of that paired difference), or if Rule R's R_repair − p0 ≥ 0.15 with a 90% lower bound above 0, then A is declared **not interpretable as absorption** for the whole study, **A0 becomes the confirmatory ratio**, and A is published as the repair-inclusive upper bound with the gap between them reported as the size of the mis-specification channel. **Nothing about which endpoint is primary is decided on S_test.** On S_test all three denominators, the inflation difference and R_repair − p0 are recomputed and published as **diagnostics that gate nothing**; the study proceeds whatever they show, and if the dev and test routings disagree both are reported and the conservative routing (A0 carries) stands, which is a deterministic rule and not a reading. **The coverage claim that survives, stated exactly:** conditional on the dev gates, the S_test estimates of A, A0 and g are unbiased and their bootstrap intervals have nominal coverage over games and decoding seeds; coverage across replications of the *whole* study does not survive, because dev gating selects which studies report — so the dev estimates are published next to the test estimates and the selection is visible. A simulation at the planning parameters is published with them: conditioning on a 5pt floor in the analysis sample when the true Inner is 6–8pt inflates Â by ≈0.03–0.06 and costs the nominal 90% bound ≈2–4 points of coverage, which is the size of the error v5.2 would have made.
- **Branches, all publishable and all pre-registered here.** *G1 fails* → a memory-null report **in the environment where the literature says memory works**, with the Tier-1 official-split cells putting it on the same axis as the published numbers, and the I0^demo/I0^bare contrast saying whether the published effect needs an empty demonstration slot. *G2 fails* → the optimizer-failure paper: I_own versus I0\* reported as the prerequisite result alongside the coin-flip literature (arXiv 2604.14585), with the bank-side and content-check results carrying the rest. *G3 fails* → **the type-generic finding**: what ALFWorld memory carries is not task-type-specific, which contradicts the retrieval-relevance premise of the ALFWorld memory literature (arXiv 2511.21730, arXiv 2604.27003) and is reported as such, together with the practitioner cells (I_all × {M0, B_all, C_all}) as bounded-loss results and the zero-episode content check. **It does not fall back to a level-ladder endpoint; four rounds of reviewers rejected that instrument and it is not resurrected here.** *G3b fails* → the square is uninterpretable and is not run on S_test; the study reports the bank-side and practitioner results and the finding that this optimizer learns type-generic scaffolding only. *G6 fails on dev* → the ratio half of the family is inconclusive before any test cell runs, rows 2 and 3 still run for the residual-gain endpoint, and the paper is the residual-gain paper with bounded-loss CIs. **THE CONFIRMATORY FAMILY — an absorbed share *and* a residual gain.** For an instruction I and test games of type set T, the **relevance advantage** is the paired quantity

  **Inner(I) = acc(I × B_own) − acc(I × B_oth)** on the same games.

Only the injected text differs between its two terms, so no-memory accuracy, policy, headroom and the residual unsolved set are identical by construction. The confirmatory endpoint is the scale-free ratio

  **A = 1 − Inner(I_own) / Inner(I_oth)**,

computed from direction-pooled numerator and denominator (per-direction ratios reported separately with the direction × type-set interaction fitted), averaged over optimizer seeds. **Test:** one-sided, α = .05, cluster bootstrap over games *and* optimizer runs (10k resamples), with the delta-method CI as a cross-check. **Claim threshold for the share:** A ≥ 0.5 with the 90% one-sided lower bound ≥ max(0.2, the 95th percentile of A's pooled null), and the same form on the co-primary A0 defined below, whose bound is read against **its own matched-gap null** rather than against a flat 0.2. Because A is a ratio, a genuine 60–70% absorption of a 6pt advantage is a pass rather than the sub-MDE inconclusive that v5's absolute threshold would have produced — this is the arithmetic incompatibility the fifth round found, and it is the reason the endpoint became a ratio. **The sentence that used to stand here — "both must pass; nothing else gates the confirmatory claim" — is deleted**, because it contradicted Rule M and because the shares alone cannot license the title; what is claimed under every combination of outcomes is fixed by the truth table below and nowhere else.

**THE RESIDUAL GAIN, AND WHY IT IS IN THE FAMILY.** Inner is the bank's task-type **discriminability**, not its value: it compares two banks, so any component of the bank's worth that is the same for every task type cancels out of both its terms. A = 1.0 is therefore fully compatible with the bank still adding five points over no memory at the optimized instruction — the share would say the type-specific content moved, and the reader's question, *is the memory just an un-optimized prompt*, would still be answered **no**. Three reviewers made that point and it is correct. The quantity that answers it is free from cells already in the grid:

  **g(I, B_own) = acc(I × B_own) − acc(I × M0)** at n = 1,200 for I ∈ {I0\*, I_own} (rows 1, 2 and 3)  ·  **R = g(I_own, B_own) / g(I0\*, B_own)**, the retention ratio.

**Endpoint E** is the pre-registered **±3pt TOST of g(I_own, B_own) against zero**: both bounds of its 90% CI must lie inside ±3pt. **R** is reported with its 90% CI as the scale-free statement of the same contrast (SE(R) ≈ 0.13 / 0.16 / 0.22 at g(I0\*, B_own) = 10 / 8 / 6pt) and is **not** separately thresholded — a threshold on R and a threshold on g would double-count one comparison — but it is what the branch titles are stated in, because "the instruction retains 1 − R of the bank's total value" is the sentence a practitioner needs. **The margin is the one the sample supports, stated rather than chosen:** SE(g) ≈ 1.41pt at n = 1,200 with two optimizer seeds, so the 90% CI half-width is 2.32pt, the smallest arithmetically declarable margin is ±2.4pt, and at ±3pt equivalence is declarable only when |ĝ| ≤ 0.68pt. **We accept the consequence and write it into the pre-registration: only a near-zero residual gain licenses the strong claim.** A 2pt residual gain fails E and routes the paper to the partial-absorption title; a ±2pt margin would need n ≈ 1,750 on rows 2 and 3, which the 84-hour hardware ceiling does not hold.

**The six titles, fixed here.** **T1** — *"Your Agent's Memory Is Just an Un-Optimized Prompt: an Absorbed Share and a Null Residual Gain in ALFWorld"*. **T2** — *"Optimized Instructions Absorb Memory's Task-Specific Content, Not Its Generic Value"*. **T3** — *"Two Denominators, Two Answers: What an Absorbed-Share Estimate Depends On"*. **T4** — *"Optimized Instructions Resist Injected Text Rather Than Contain It"*. **T5** — *"Nothing Left to Add: Memory's Gain Vanishes at an Optimized Instruction Without Type-Specific Absorption"*. **T6** — *"Memory Still Pays on Top of an Optimized Instruction: a Null Result for the Substitution Hypothesis"*. **T7** is the gate branch's own title, already fixed in the Branches paragraph above.

**Two things the table does not do, said explicitly.** (i) **Rule F (fix/break) and Rule C (content) do not select rows.** They are reported diagnostics that must be published beside whichever row fires; if Rule F's bucket disagrees with Rule M's, the row still fires as written and the disagreement is reported as a result about the two rules. (ii) **Rows 19 and 20 qualify the claim rather than change which row fires.** If Inner(I_type) is not distinguishable from Inner(I_own) (row 19), every row's claim gains the pre-registered qualifier *"by an instruction that names the task types"*, and the abstract's mechanism sentence changes accordingly; if R_raw at I_own matches B_own at I_own within ±3pt (row 20), the claim gains the qualifier *"and the distillation step is not what is being absorbed"*. Both qualifiers are fixed here so that neither is available as a post-hoc reading.

**The denominator is not neutral either, so there are three of them.** I_oth is not merely a lower-level instruction: it was optimized on the *other* type set and is then run on own-type games, so it can actively push wrong procedure that B_own then repairs — and that repair is scored as relevance advantage in the denominator, inflating A with no content transfer whatever. Neither existing correction reaches the channel: A_h is a scalar level correction, while mis-specification is a *shape* effect concentrated on exactly the games the on-type bank fixes, and Ladder T is same-type by construction, so it titrates level without ever instantiating wrong-type procedure. Three denominators are therefore pre-registered, two of them free from cells already in the grid:

  **A = 1 − Inner(I_own)/Inner(I_oth)** (row 2)  ·  **A0 = 1 − Inner(I_own)/Inner(I0\*)** (rows 1 and 2)  ·  **A_all = 1 − Inner(I_own)/Inner(I_all)** (rows 2 and 16).

**A and A0 are co-primary.** Both must clear their thresholds for either to be read as absorption, and if they disagree, **the disagreement is the reported finding** (truth-table row 6) and neither is presented as the answer. A_all, measured at the pooled instruction a practitioner would deploy (optimizer-exposed, but labelled toward neither type set), is reported with its CI alongside them and gates nothing. **What the intersection–union rule does and does not control, stated plainly, because v5.2 overclaimed it.** It controls the type-I error at α for the composite null "at least one share is below threshold", it needs no multiplicity correction, and — the property that actually matters here — it removes the freedom to pick whichever denominator flatters the result after seeing both. **It does not remove a bias that both marginals share**, and here they do not even have independent biases: A is biased upward by mis-specification repair and A0 by headroom and context competition, and because the two ratios share a numerator their sampling errors are strongly positively correlated, so requiring both to pass behaves closer to one test than to two independent ones. Each bias is therefore attacked separately and by construction — **A's by Rule R and the inflation check, A0's by the matched-gap null replicate** — and it is those constructions, not the conjunction, that the claim's coverage rests on. Two pre-registered rules protect A's reading, **both decided on S_dev2 in v5.3 so that no routing decision is made on the analysis sample**. **The inflation check (at G6, on dev):** if Inner(I_oth) − Inner(I0\*) exceeds max(2pt, 1.5 × the bootstrap SE of that paired difference — ≈2.2pt at the dev n = 500, so ≈3.3pt) then A is not interpretable as absorption and A0 is the reported number; the same difference is recomputed on S_test (where its SE is ≈1.4pt) and published as a diagnostic that changes nothing. **Rule R, the item-level repair diagnostic:** let R_repair be the share of the games B_own fixes at I_oth (failed at I_oth×B_oth, solved at I_oth×B_own) that I0\*×M0 already solves, and p0 the same share among *all* games I_oth×B_oth fails. Type-specific relevance draws its fixes from the hard residue, giving R_repair ≤ p0; repair of mis-specification concentrates them on games a neutral instruction handles unaided, giving R_repair > p0. If R_repair − p0 ≥ 0.15 with a 90% bootstrap lower bound above 0, the denominator is declared repair-dominated and A0 carries the claim. On dev both quantities come free from the G3 cells and the new G6 cell; on test they come free from the row-1 and row-2 logs and are published as diagnostics.

**The residual bias, computed once per denominator, and bounded from both sides.** Each ratio's two Inner terms are measured at instructions of different accuracy, and headroom compresses Inner multiplicatively. With no absorption at all, a ratio with denominator instruction x would equal

  **A_h(x) = 1 − (1 − ℓ_own)/(1 − ℓ_x) = (ℓ_own − ℓ_x)/(1 − ℓ_x)**.

v5.2 computed this once, for A, and then let a single null replicate stand for all three ratios. That was wrong, and the correction is arithmetic rather than rhetorical:

- **A_h(A)**, denominator I_oth: the expected level gap is 3–6pt at ≈50% accuracy → **0.06–0.12**, one fifth of the threshold and below the 0.2 floor.
- **A_h(A0)**, denominator I0\*: G2 fixes ℓ_own − ℓ_0 ≥ 3pt, and the design expects ≈5pt from I0\* to I_oth and roughly the same again to I_own, so the gap A0 spans is **8–11pt** → **A_h(A0) ≈ 0.16–0.22**, i.e. **at or above the flat 0.2 lower bound v5.2 asked A0 to clear.** The co-primary that was introduced to remove one bias carries the largest one of the three, and it is the reviewers' finding, not ours.
- **A_h(A_all)**, denominator I_all: the pooled instruction sits between I_oth and I_own on own-type games, expected gap 2–4pt → **0.04–0.08**.

All three levels (ℓ_own, ℓ_oth, ℓ_0, ℓ_all) are measured directly from rows 3, 1 and 16, so all three A_h values are reported numbers rather than assumptions. **A0's lower bound is read against a matched-gap null, not against 0.2.** Row 4's new bottom rung r_0 is titrated on dev to sit within 2pt of ℓ_0, and the pair (r_0, r_hi) gives the null replicate A0 needs: the same ratio computed between two instructions with **identical episode provenance** — both are I′ snapshots from P′_train — separated by exactly the I0\*-to-I_own level gap. Its true value is A_h(A0) *plus* whatever type-level absorption the ladder itself performs across that gap, so it over-states the floor and is **conservative by construction**; a reader who wants the pure headroom number reads A_h(A0) directly. The operating requirement becomes LB(A0) ≥ max(0.2, the matched-gap null's 95th percentile), which the planning arithmetic puts at **0.25–0.30**. We expect A0 to be the harder of the two co-primaries to pass and say so in advance rather than discovering it in the results. Around that arithmetic the correction is bracketed:
- **Under-correcting end (best-of-w, row 14).** Best-of-w raises accuracy by resolving games lost to sampling noise and attenuates prompt sensitivity, so at matched level it leaves the type-specific-fixable games unsolved: it corrects too little and flatters the hypothesis. It gives A_gen.
- **Over-correcting end (Ladder T, row 4).** I′(r) raises accuracy by learning type-specific procedure from episodes of the same types, exactly the channel the treatment uses — but from a **disjoint** pool. It therefore also absorbs, and corrects too much. It gives **A_epi = 1 − Inner(I_own)/Înner′(ℓ_own)**, the episode-specific share, where Înner′ is interpolated between the two bracketing ladder levels.
- **The bracket and the sensitivity table are robustness, not the bar.** v5.1's "A − A_h ≥ 0.30" wording is **withdrawn** and stays withdrawn: the bar is the truth table's conjunction, and nothing else. A_h remains a reported diagnostic per denominator and enters the decision only through the nulls — each null replicate's true value *is* the corresponding A_h, so requiring each point estimate above its own null's 95th percentile is what prices the headroom floor into the decision, and **v5.3's correction is that A0 now has a null that actually spans A0's gap** instead of borrowing A's adjacent-rung one. The sensitivity table (correction term scaled by 0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.25, 1.5) is published with the primary so a reader can see exactly how much of A survives a correction that is 25% or 50% wrong in either direction, and the conservative (Ladder T) end is reported first.

**The decomposition that tests the title (secondary, reported with CIs, not thresholded).** (1 − A) = (1 − A_epi)(1 − A_type) with A_type = 1 − Înner′(ℓ_own)/Inner(I_oth). Row 4 supplies it at n = 500, one optimizer seed, with Înner′ interpolated between two ladder levels: the instance SE is √(0.20/500) = 2.0pt, ≈2.15pt with the optimizer component, and propagating two such levels through the ratio gives **SE(A_epi) ≈ 0.30**, so a 90% lower bound of 0.2 would require Â_epi ≈ 0.6. **A_epi and A_type are therefore reported with their CIs and are not thresholded**; funding row 4 at row-2 scale was costed at ≈100,000 extra calls and refused as breaking the compute envelope. The pre-registered *reading* survives as a description, published in either direction: a large A_epi means absorption is episode-specific and "the same training episodes in two containers" stands; A_epi near zero with a large A_type means absorption is at task-type granularity — an optimizer that has seen *any* episodes of the type already holds the bank's type-specific value — the title changes to say so, and the practical conclusion (a bank distilled from the same task types is redundant with an optimized instruction) is unchanged and arguably stronger. **The episode-versus-type question is carried at Tier 0 on the bank side instead**, by row 17: B′_own is distilled from P′_train — different episodes, the same three task types — and run at both instructions. If Inner(B′_own) − Inner(B_own) has a 90% CI inside ±3pt, the bank itself carries only type-level content, and "the same episodes in two containers" is dead on the container this paper is about, whatever A_epi says. Because B′_own comes from a 200-game pool against B_own's 300 (k- and token-matched at read time), that equality reading is conservative. Neither branch is a failure and neither is chosen after seeing the data. **On the instruction side, v5.3 adds row 19, the arm three reviewers demanded:** I_type is a hand-written instruction that names the three task types and their canonical skeleton and has seen no episodes at all. Inner(I_type) − Inner(I_own) is published with a 90% CI; a lower bound above zero says the optimizer absorbed something the type *label* does not supply, and a CI covering zero is reported as **not excluding** the type-label explanation, which then qualifies every row of the truth table. Its limits are stated with it: at n = 500 against row 2's n = 1,200 the paired difference has SE ≈ 2.6pt, so only a difference of ≈4.3pt or more is distinguishable from zero, and this is a directional reading rather than an equivalence test. That is the arm the 84-hour ceiling can afford, and it is more than v5.2 had.

**The mechanism rules (pre-registered, three buckets each).** Inner decomposes against row 3 into help(I) = acc(I×B_own) − acc(I×M0) and off(I) = acc(I×B_oth) − acc(I×M0), so ΔInner = [help(I_oth) − help(I_own)] + [off(I_own) − off(I_oth)]. **Rule M** assigns a bucket and **nothing else**: help ≥ 2/3 of ΔInner → *help-dominant*; off ≥ 2/3 → *off-dominant*, the distractor-robustness reading, in which the optimized instruction is simply more resistant to irrelevant injected text and row 8 confirms or refutes it independently through harm(I) = acc(I×M0) − acc(I×X_dist); otherwise *mixed*, with both components reported. **The bucket is a column of the truth table, not a second bar.** v5.2's wording — "absorption is claimed only if the help term carries ≥ 2/3 of ΔInner" — contradicted its own "both must pass; nothing else gates" sentence, and both sentences are deleted: the table above says what is claimed when the shares pass and the bucket is off-dominant (row 3 or 5: no absorption claim, title T4), and there is no second place to look. **Rule F (fix/break)** and **Rule C (content)** are **reported diagnostics that select no row**: Rule F's buckets are fix deficit at equal breaks → absorption, break excess → interference, break deficit → distractor-robustness, at 2/3 shares and otherwise mixed; Rule C's signature is the item-level dose-response of row 7 — [help_hi(I_oth) − help_hi(I_own)] > [help_lo(I_oth) − help_lo(I_own)] — the only signature that content transfer predicts and that neither headroom nor distractor-robustness does, since both of those are blind to which items the bank holds. Both are published beside whichever row fires, and a Rule F bucket that disagrees with Rule M's is reported as a disagreement rather than resolved in the paper's favour.

**Why G3 and Rule M do not collide.** G1 fixes help(I0\*) ≥ 5pt and G3 fixes Inner(I0\*) ≥ 9pt, which together imply off(I0\*) ≤ −4pt: the off-type bank is expected to *harm* at the reference scaffold, exactly as the retrieval-pollution literature predicts (arXiv 2604.27003, arXiv 2608.15008). That is a **level**, and Rule M is applied to **ΔInner between instructions** — a difference of differences, in which an off term that is large but instruction-invariant contributes exactly zero. Only a *change* in off-type harm across the two instructions can route the reading to distractor-robustness, and that change is measured directly by row 8 (X_dist, out-of-domain) and by row 18 (**M_shuf**: token-matched random items from the own-type bank with retrieval bypassed — within-domain and topically plausible, which X_dist is not, and therefore the harder control). The power requirement and the mechanism rule are thus orthogonal, and G3 is not relaxed.

## Baselines and Ablations

**Baselines that could plausibly win and would refute us.**
(i) **A's 90% lower bound falls below 0.2 while the raw retention is large** — memory's value at the optimized instruction shrank through headroom and generic compression, and no episode-specific content moved between containers. The "un-optimized prompt" framing is withdrawn and the paper reports the relevance advantage as a function of instruction provenance, in an environment where memory works, as its result.
(ii) **The on-policy bank recovers the gain (row 6): g(I_own, B_own^on) − g(I_own, B_own) ≥ 3pt** — the shrinkage was bank staleness. The headline becomes "memory banks must be re-distilled under the deployed policy", which is useful and different, and it is a genuine risk rather than a formality: this program's own proposal ① carried the same off-policy confound.
(iii) **G3 fails, or Inner(I_oth) on S_test is under 5pt (G6)** — the bank's value in ALFWorld is task-type-generic, so there is no type-specific content to absorb. The paper reports the type-generic finding, the practitioner cells as bounded losses, and the zero-episode content check. This is a real, publishable contradiction of the retrieval-relevance premise in the ALFWorld memory literature.
(iv) **A_epi ≈ 0 with A_type large** — absorption is at task-type granularity, not episode granularity; the title changes, "same episodes, two containers" is reported as *not* the mechanism, and the practical conclusion survives. Pre-registered, not a save.
(v) **The off component carries ≥ 2/3 of ΔInner and harm(I_own) < harm(I_oth) (rows 3, 8)** — the optimized instruction is more robust to irrelevant injected text, and what looked like absorption is distractor-robustness. This is R1's and R3's alternative and it now has its own Tier-0 arm and its own rule.
(vi) **No item-level dose-response (row 7)** — the high-overlap and low-overlap halves of the bank lose the same amount at I_own. Whatever shrank Inner is not the content the overlap statistic identifies, and the content claim is downgraded to a behavioural one in print.
(vii) **g(I_all, B_all) has a 90% CI above 3pt (row 5)** — the bank still pays on top of the instruction a practitioner would deploy; build both, and the paper says so in the deployment configuration rather than in the square's half-space one.
(viii) **J_own×M0 ≥ I_own×B_own + MDE (row 12)** — the demonstration slot, not memory-versus-instruction, is the story: a jointly optimized static prompt reaches where the bank reaches. This is why J is Tier 0, and the demonstration-bearing I0^demo baseline attacks the same question from the baseline side.
(ix) **I_all×C_all − I_all×B_all has a 90% CI below −3pt (row 11)** — per-episode retrieval carries value no static compilation holds; the compile-once recommendation dies.
(x) **I_own(w\*)×M0 ≥ I_own×B_own, or I_own(1.5·L0)×M0 ≥ I_own×B_own (row 14; the step control is Tier 1)** — spending memory's token or step budget on sampling or exploration buys what memory buys; the recommendation becomes "sample more", not "compile".
(xi) **I2_own×B_own exceeds max(I_own×B_own, I_own×M0) by more than the MDE (row 13, Tier 1 in v5.2)** — co-adaptation synergy: the coupling the track is named for is real and the substitution framing is incomplete.
(xii) **The planted control's single 50% dilution does not reduce the relevance advantage in the engineered direction and approximate size (row 9)** — the instrument cannot see reductions of the size it is meant to measure, and A is not interpreted in either direction. The 25% arm was sold to fund rows 19 and 20, so this is now a one-point calibration and the dose-response of the instrument itself is no longer testable; that is a real cost of the promotion and it is stated rather than absorbed.
(xiii) **The null replicate's ratio (row 10) exceeds 0.25** — the endpoint's floor is at the claim's scale; the study reports the floor, raises n from the reserve or raises the threshold before unblinding, and declares the primary inconclusive if neither is available.
(xiv) **G1 passes only under bare I0 and fails under I0^demo** — the published ALFWorld memory effect depends on an empty demonstration slot. That is a finding about the memory literature's evaluation template and is reported as the headline if it happens.
(xv) **A and A0 disagree, or the inflation check or Rule R trips (rows 1, 2, 16)** — the off-type instruction's mis-specification, not absorption, produced the ratio: an on-type bank was repairing wrong-type procedure and the repair was being scored as relevance. A0 becomes the reported number, A is published as the repair-inclusive upper bound, and the gap between them is reported as the size of that channel. This is the reviewers' strongest objection to v5.1 and it now has three denominators, a gate-level check and an item-level diagnostic aimed at it.
(xvi) **Inner(B′_own) − Inner(B_own) lies inside ±3pt (row 17)** — a bank distilled from *different* episodes of the same task types is worth as much as the bank distilled from the instruction's own episodes, so the bank carries type-level content only and "the same episodes, two containers" is a type-level statement on the container the paper is named for. Published as the finding, not as a failure.
(xvii) **The residual gain survives: g(I_own, B_own) fails the ±3pt TOST while A and A0 pass (rows 1–3)** — this is the outcome the previous round showed v5.2 could not distinguish from a win, and it is now a distinct, pre-registered result with its own title (truth-table row 4, title T2): the bank's *type-specific* content is absorbed, its *generic* content is not, the retention ratio R quantifies what is left, and the practical recommendation becomes "optimize the instruction and keep a smaller bank" rather than "the bank is redundant". A reviewer who believes this is the true state of the world should expect the paper to say so, in the title.
(xviii) **Inner(I_type) is not distinguishable from Inner(I_own) (row 19)** — a hand-written instruction that merely names the three upcoming task types absorbs as much as the optimizer did, and the result is about task identification rather than about episodes moving between containers. Every truth-table row's claim then carries that qualifier, and the contribution shrinks to "telling the agent which task family is coming does what a memory bank does". This is the cheapest arm in the design that can kill the paper's own interpretation, which is why it is at Tier 0 and on the never-cut list.
(xix) **R_raw at I_own matches B_own at I_own within ±3pt (row 20)** — pasting the same retrieved episodes raw, at the same token budget, does what the distilled bank does, so the "container" question is about retrieval and token budget rather than about distillation, and the absorbed share is a statement about retrieved *text*, not about distilled *procedure*. EvoMemBench (arXiv 2605.18421) and AutoMEM (arXiv 2606.04315) make this a live possibility rather than a formality.

**Ablations that isolate the mechanism.**
- **The square (row 2)** — the identification itself: Inner at two instructions produced by the same optimizer at the same rollout budget on equally sized pools, differing only in the provenance of the episodes. No "different intervention kinds" objection applies.
- **Both directions** — bank relevance is estimated as a main effect at I0\* (row 1) and absorption as the interaction, which is what makes a type-based split legitimate where v4.4 refused it.
- **Ladder T (row 4)** — a targeted, single-knob, five-level control built from episode-disjoint but type-matched episodes: simultaneously the conservative headroom correction, the A_epi/A_type decomposition and the answer to "did the instruction absorb *these* episodes or just learn this task type".
- **Best-of-w (row 14; the step budget moves to Tier 1)** — compute-matched controls, and the optimistic end of the correction bracket; explicitly *not* the level control any more.
- **Three denominators (rows 1, 2, 16)** — Inner read at the off-type instruction, at the hand-written scaffold and at the pooled deployed instruction, with a pre-registered inflation check and an item-level repair diagnostic; the co-primary A0 is what keeps a mis-specified I_oth from being scored as absorption.
- **The episode-disjoint bank B′_own (row 17)** — the bank-side mirror of Ladder T's instruction: episode provenance manipulated on both containers rather than only on the instruction.
- **M_shuf (row 18)** — the within-domain, token-matched, retrieval-bypassed distractor, which is the control Rule M needs because X_dist is out-of-domain and may be trivially ignorable.
- **On-policy bank (row 6)** — the only arm that separates absorption from staleness; named as the missing baseline by two of three reviewers in the previous round.
- **X_dist at three instructions (row 8)** — separates content absorption from distractor-robustness, with a pre-registered decomposition rule instead of a post-hoc reading.
- **Item overlap and the hi/lo sub-banks (row 7 and the zero-episode statistic)** — the direct content check: is the content the bank encodes the content the instruction contains, and does the bank's advantage shrink *in proportion to that overlap*?
- **Planted control (row 9)** — instrument calibration at 2–6pt, the scale being measured, not a 15–30pt sledgehammer; single-point in v5.3.
- **Null replicates (row 10, the adjacent-rung ladder null, and the new matched-gap null)** — the endpoints' own floors, measured rather than assumed, on the ratios themselves, with **one null per denominator gap** so that A0 is no longer judged against a floor computed for A.
- **I_type (row 19)** — the instruction that names the task types and has seen no episodes: the rival explanation for the headline, and the instruction-side counterpart to row 17's bank-side B′_own.
- **R_raw (row 20)** — the same episodes at the same token budget, pasted instead of distilled: the cheapest rival container, and the arm that says whether distillation is doing any of the work.
- **The residual gain and the retention ratio (rows 1–3)** — free from the grid, confirmatory, and the quantity every memory paper we cite actually reports; without it the absorbed share could pass while the title's question is answered no.
- **I_all, C_all (rows 5, 11)** — the practitioner's configuration, at power, so no deployment claim is made in a condition never measured.
- **J (row 12, one seed in v5.3) and D_opt (Tier 1)** — the prompt container's optimized ceiling and the static-demonstration container. J loses its second seed to pay for rows 19 and 20; its between-seed spread is therefore not reported and it is read as a bound.
- **I2 (row 13, Tier 1 in v5.2)** — the track's named coupling: an instruction optimized in the presence of the bank, and its dependence when the bank is removed. It is a consequence of the claim rather than part of its identification, which is why it was the arm that paid for the neutral denominator.
- **Screening over agent × bank format, published either way** — the claim's relativity is measured and the memory-weak pairing is a co-equal result.

## Falsifiable Predictions

1. **The confirmatory conjunction (this is the whole bar; the truth table says what every other outcome licenses).** **(a)** A = 1 − Inner(I_own)/Inner(I_oth) ≥ 0.5 with a one-sided 90% cluster-bootstrap lower bound ≥ max(0.2, the 95th percentile of A's pooled null). **(b)** A0 = 1 − Inner(I_own)/Inner(I0\*) ≥ 0.5 with a one-sided 90% lower bound ≥ max(0.2, the 95th percentile of **its own matched-gap null**, planned at 0.25–0.30 — A0's headroom floor is 0.16–0.22 and a flat 0.2 bound would have been no bound at all). **(c)** The residual gain g(I_own, B_own) = acc(I_own×B_own) − acc(I_own×M0) **passes a ±3pt TOST against zero at n = 1,200**, which at SE(g) ≈ 1.41pt is declarable only when |ĝ| ≤ 0.68pt. All three are required for the title's claim; A_all and the retention ratio R = g(I_own,B_own)/g(I0\*,B_own) are reported with their CIs alongside. **If (a) and (b) pass and (c) fails, the pre-registered finding is partial absorption under title T2 and not a pass.** If A and A0 disagree, the disagreement is the finding (T3); if the dev-stage inflation check or Rule R declared the off-type denominator repair-dominated, A0 alone carries the ratio half and A is published as the repair-inclusive upper bound. Achieved SE(A), SE(A0), SE(g) and the realized power are reported; if SE(A0) exceeds 0.25 the ratio half is downgraded to directional before unblinding.
2. **Relevance and calibration.** On **S_dev2**, Inner(I0\*) ≥ 9pt (G3) and Inner(I_oth) ≥ 5pt (G6) — both gates now live on dev, and their S_test values are published as diagnostics that gate nothing. The single-point planted control (50% of the off-type bank's items replaced by on-type items) reduces the relevance advantage in the engineered direction, by 40–60% of its clean value, within its CI. **The monotonicity clause of v5.2 is withdrawn**: the 25% arm was sold to fund rows 19 and 20, so the instrument is calibrated at one point and its own dose-response is no longer testable. That is a cost, and it is stated rather than hidden.
3. **Headroom is not the explanation — for either denominator.** The measured floors are A_h(A) = (ℓ_own − ℓ_oth)/(1 − ℓ_oth) ≤ 0.15 and **A_h(A0) = (ℓ_own − ℓ_0)/(1 − ℓ_0) ≤ 0.25** (planned at 0.16–0.22), with A_h(A_all) ≈ 0.04–0.08 reported alongside; A exceeds the 95th percentile of its pooled null and **A0 exceeds the 95th percentile of the matched-gap null**, whose true value is A_h(A0) plus the ladder's own type-level absorption and which is therefore a conservative floor. The claim survives the conservative (Ladder T) end of the correction bracket and every row of the ±25%/±50% sensitivity table.
4. **Episode versus type, now tested on both containers.** Row 17 (bank side): **Inner(B′_own) − Inner(B_own) fails a ±3pt TOST**, i.e. a bank built from *these* episodes is worth measurably more than a bank built from other episodes of the same types. Row 19 (instruction side, new at Tier 0): **Inner(I_type) − Inner(I_own) has a 90% lower bound above zero**, i.e. an instruction that merely names the three task types does *not* absorb what the optimizer absorbed. Its limits are pre-registered with it: at n = 500 against row 2's n = 1,200 the paired difference has SE ≈ 2.6pt, so only a difference of ≈4.3pt or more is distinguishable from zero and a CI covering zero is reported as **not excluding** the type-label explanation, which then qualifies every row of the truth table. Row 4's A_epi and A_type are reported with 90% CIs and **not thresholded**, because at n = 500 with one optimizer seed and an interpolated denominator SE(A_epi) ≈ 0.30. If A_epi's CI covers zero while A_type is large, the pre-registered reading is task-type-granular absorption and the title changes; that reading is a published description, not a decision rule and not an escape hatch.
5. **Staleness is not the explanation.** g(I_own, B_own^on) − g(I_own, B_own) has a 90% one-sided upper bound below 3pt: re-distilling the bank under the optimized instruction does not restore what the instruction took. At row 6's n = 800 with one optimizer seed (the second seed was sold to fund rows 19 and 20; the bank is built under a single instruction seed in any case) SE ≈ 1.77pt, so the bound is declarable only when the observed recovery is below ≈0.7pt; a recovery of 3pt or more kills the claim outright and replaces it with the staleness finding, and an intermediate value is reported as a bound with its CI.
6. **The practitioner's numbers, in the practitioner's configuration (equivalence, not a one-sided bound).** g(I_all, B_all) passes a **TOST equivalence test against a ±3pt margin** — both bounds of its 90% CI inside ±3pt — against a gated ≥ 5pt gain for the same bank family at the hand-written scaffold. Row 5 keeps n = 800 and two optimizer seeds for exactly this reason: at SE ≈ 1.68pt the 90% half-width is ≈2.8pt, so equivalence is declarable only when the observed effect is within ≈0.2pt of zero and **a true 2.5pt gain fails this test and is reported as a gain**. The compile-once contrast I_all×C_all − I_all×B_all is tested against the same ±3pt margin, but **row 11 now runs at one optimizer seed** (SE ≈ 1.77pt, half-width ≈2.9pt), so its equivalence verdict is declarable only within ≈0.1pt of zero and it is reported primarily as a bounded loss with its CI; the second seed is the first Tier-1 buy-back. 3pt is the smallest margin these n support; 2pt would need n ≈ 1,750–1,800, which the ceiling does not hold.
7. **Mechanism (this selects a truth-table row; it is not a second bar).** The help component carries ≥ 2/3 of ΔInner (Rule M's help-dominant bucket, which selects row 1 or 4 rather than licensing anything on its own); harm(I_own) − harm(I_oth) ≥ −2pt, so distractor-robustness cannot account for the shrinkage, with M_shuf (row 18) as the within-domain check that X_dist cannot provide; and the high-overlap sub-bank loses more of its advantage between I_oth and I_own than the low-overlap sub-bank does (Rule C). **v5.2's "absorption is claimed only if the help term carries ≥ 2/3 of ΔInner" is deleted here as well as in Experiments**: if the off component dominates while the shares pass, the truth table sends the paper to title T4 and no absorption is claimed, and that is the only place that decision is made.
8. **Rival containers.** J_own×M0 lies within ±3pt of I_own×B_own — the jointly optimized prompt reaches where the bank reaches (one seed in v5.3, so this is read as a bound). **R_raw at I_own (row 20) does *not* match B_own at I_own within ±3pt**: distillation buys something a raw token-matched dump of the same episodes does not, and if it does match, the container question is about retrieval and tokens rather than about distilled procedure, and every claim carries that qualifier. **Inner(I_type) is materially below Inner(I_own) (prediction 4).** At Tier 1, I2_own×B_own ≤ max(I_own×B_own, I_own×M0) + MDE and I2_own×M0 < I_own×M0 by ≥ 2pt: an instruction optimized with memory present depends on it. The I2 clauses are reported only if Tier 1 runs; unrun means unrun.

**Refutation.** Outcome (xvii) — the shares passing while the residual gain survives — is now the single most informative way the *title* dies while the *design* still delivers a number, and it is pre-registered with its own title rather than argued about afterwards. Outcome (i) — A's lower bound below 0.2 with a large raw retention — is the most likely way the hypothesis itself dies and is reported as the primary result. Outcome (ii) — the on-policy bank recovering the gain — kills the claim and replaces it with a staleness finding. Outcome (v) — the off component and X_dist carrying the shrinkage — replaces it with a distractor-robustness finding, which is a real and publishable statement about what optimized instructions do. Outcome (xviii) — I_type absorbing as much as I_own — makes the result about task identification rather than about episodes. Outcome (viii) — J matching the memory cell — moves the headline from memory to the demonstration slot. Outcome (iii) — no type-specific content to absorb — contradicts the relevance premise of the ALFWorld memory literature and is reported as that. Predictions 6 and 8's Tier-1 clauses changing the recommendation do not touch the claim.

## Measurement and Noise Control

Three variance sources are handled separately, with components taken from this program's own measurements where they exist and measured on dev where they do not.

**(a) Evaluation variance.** Every S_test game runs under every cell with the same seed schedule; ALFWorld games are deterministic given the game file, so the only stochasticity is decoding, and all comparisons are within-game. Pairwise superiority tests are exact McNemar on fix/break counts, Holm-corrected within pre-declared families: **F-A** {A, A0 and the residual-gain equivalence E — the confirmatory family, evaluated as an intersection–union rule in which all three must pass for the headline claim, which controls the type-I error at α without any correction, and which is **not** presented as a fix for either marginal's bias (see the paragraph on what the intersection–union rule does not control); the retention ratio R is reported inside this family with its CI but is not thresholded}; **F-B** {Inner(I0\*); the planted control}; **F-C** {on-policy; practitioner gain; compile-once}; **F-D** {Ladder T, the matched-gap null, the compute controls, I_type, R_raw, the coupling arm, J, the item-ablation contrast, the distractor contrasts and every Tier-1 control}. The primary A, a ratio of contrasts, is tested by cluster bootstrap over games **and** optimizer runs (10k resamples), never by a normal approximation, with the delta method reported alongside for transparency. Every delta carries its fix/break decomposition and, because this is ALFWorld, its **timeout and mean-step decomposition**: proposal ①'s 2026-09-04 finding was that a memory arm can look different only because it changes how often the agent fails to commit, and the ALFWorld analogue is a change in timeout rate. Any cell whose accuracy delta is more than half accounted for by a timeout-rate change is flagged and reported as such. Bounded-loss statements use 90% game-level bootstrap CIs; absence of significance is never reported as equivalence.

**(b) Optimizer variance.** Two GEPA seeds per type set on the square (rows 2 and 3), on the practitioner cells (rows 5 and 16) and nowhere else at Tier 0: **rows 6, 11 and 12 run at one seed in v5.3**, sold to fund the two rival-container arms and the matched-gap rung, and their between-seed spreads are consequently not reported and their contrasts are read as bounds. Ladder T, I_type, R_raw and I2 are single-run by construction or by cost. The program measured a between-seed spread of optimized-instruction accuracy of 1.0–1.6pt (n = 500, two GEPA and two MIPROv2 runs) and a treatment-free decoding-seed spread of 2.8–3.4pt (three seeds, n = 500) in E1; these are the planning priors, and the corresponding ALFWorld quantities are measured on S_dev2 before S_test is touched and replace them if they differ. Because Inner is a *difference of two conditions at the same instruction*, the optimizer run affects both terms alike and its contribution to SE(Inner) is ≈0.8pt per run, not 1.6pt. Cell comparisons use a mixed-effects logistic model with game and optimizer run as crossed random effects; A is reported per seed and per direction, never as one number only, and the direction × type-set interaction is fitted rather than assumed away. **With two seeds the crossed optimizer-run effect is not separately estimable** — a cluster bootstrap over runs would resample two clusters — so the pre-registered analysis treats **seeds as fixed strata and bootstraps over games**, reports the between-seed spread per direction as a descriptive quantity, and flags the 0.8pt-per-run component as transferred from E1 rather than estimated here. The third seed is the first purchase if the G5 throughput gate clears with margin.

**(c) Null replicates and the null floor.** **Four in v5.3, all pre-registered, and — the correction the last round forced — one null per denominator gap rather than one null for all three ratios.** (1) Row 1 re-run at seed 37 gives the treatment-free accuracy floor. (2) **Row 10 recomputes Inner at one instruction across two decoding seeds and forms A from it** — a treatment-free replicate of the *ratio*, whose true value is zero. (3) An **adjacent-rung ladder null**: A computed between two adjacent Ladder-T levels with those points excluded from the fit, whose true value is A_h across a small level gap — this exercises the whole estimation pipeline including the correction, and it is the null **A** is read against. (4) **The matched-gap null**, new: A computed between Ladder-T rungs **r_0 and r_hi**, two instructions of identical episode provenance whose level gap is titrated on dev to equal the I0\*-to-I_own gap that **A0** actually spans. Its true value is A_h(A0) ≈ 0.16–0.22 plus whatever type-level absorption the ladder performs across that gap, so it is a conservative floor; **A0's lower bound is read against its 95th percentile (planned 0.25–0.30), not against the flat 0.2 that v5.2 used and that A0's own headroom floor already reaches.** No effect is claimed for a ratio unless its point estimate exceeds the 95th percentile of *its own* null.

**Minimum detectable effect (stated before running).** Inner is a paired difference between two memory conditions at one instruction; with a planning discordance rate of 0.20 (the fraction of games whose outcome differs between on-type and off-type banks; the achieved value is reported) its instance SE at n = 1,200 is √(0.20/1200) = 1.29pt, and with the optimizer component (0.8pt per run, two runs) SE(Inner) ≈ **1.41pt**. For A = 1 − X/Y the delta method with a game-level correlation of 0.3 between X and Y gives, at A = 0.5:

**This table is the consistency argument the last round demanded, and it is what sets the gates.** G3 requires ≥ 9pt on dev; applying the measured compression factor (1 − ℓ_oth)/(1 − ℓ0) ≈ 0.91 for a 5pt level gain from I0\* to I_oth projects ≈8pt at test, i.e. the ≈70% row; **G6 stops the ratio half of the family at a dev denominator below 5pt**, where the test provably cannot conclude — and it now does so on S_dev2 rather than on the analysis sample, because conditioning a ratio on its own denominator clearing a floor in the sample that computes it biases the ratio upward and costs the one-sided bound its nominal coverage. **The simulation that quantifies what v5.2 would have cost is published with the results:** at the planning parameters, conditioning on a 5pt floor when the true Inner is 6–8pt inflates Â by ≈0.03–0.06 and costs the nominal 90% bound ≈2–4 points of coverage. **The coverage claim that survives is stated exactly:** conditional on the dev gates, the S_test estimates of A, A0 and g are unbiased and their bootstrap intervals have nominal coverage over games and decoding seeds; coverage across replications of the whole study does not survive, because dev gating decides which studies report, so the dev estimates are published beside the test estimates and the selection is visible to the reader. A gate whose point requirement equals the true effect passes half the time by construction, so G3 at 9pt against a literature prior of 5–15pt total memory effect — and a partition chosen to maximize between-set procedural distance — is a deliberate coin-flip with a publishable branch on the other side, plus the single pre-declared reserve extension in the [6, 9) window.

**The other three ratios' precision, and the equivalence margins.** A0 shares its numerator with A and takes its denominator Inner(I0\*) from row 1 at n = 1,200 on the same games, so **SE(A0) ≈ SE(A)** and the two are strongly positively correlated — their agreement test is a paired comparison and one bootstrap resamples games for both, which is also why requiring both to pass costs little power. **SE(A_all) is ≈1.5× larger** because row 16 drops to n = 500 in v5.3 to help fund rows 19 and 20 (instance SE √(0.20/500) = 2.0pt, ≈2.08pt with the optimizer component, against 1.41pt at n = 1,200), i.e. ≈0.21 / 0.25 / 0.34 at Inner(I_all) = 10 / 8 / 6pt — which is acceptable precisely because A_all is a reported diagnostic and gates nothing. **The residual-gain endpoint's precision, which is new in v5.3 and is what makes the conjunction a real bar rather than a slogan:** g is a paired difference between a memory condition and the no-memory condition at one instruction, so at the same planning discordance of 0.20 its instance SE at n = 1,200 is 1.29pt and **SE(g) ≈ 1.41pt** with the optimizer component; at a discordance of 0.25 it is 1.55pt. The 90% CI half-width is therefore 2.32pt, the smallest arithmetically declarable TOST margin is ±2.4pt, and at the pre-registered ±3pt margin equivalence is declarable only when |ĝ| ≤ 0.68pt. **SE(R) ≈ 0.13 / 0.16 / 0.22** at g(I0\*, B_own) = 10 / 8 / 6pt by the delta method with a game-level correlation of 0.5 between numerator and denominator, which is why R is reported with a CI rather than thresholded. **The three headroom floors are reported side by side** — A_h(A) ≈ 0.06–0.12, **A_h(A0) ≈ 0.16–0.22**, A_h(A_all) ≈ 0.04–0.08 — and each ratio's null replicate spans that ratio's own gap. **SE(A_epi) ≈ 0.30** from row 4 (n = 500, one seed, interpolated denominator: 2.0pt instance, ≈2.15pt with the optimizer component, propagated through the ratio against a 6–8pt quantity), which is why A_epi is reported with a CI and not thresholded. **The SE of the denominator difference** Inner(I_oth) − Inner(I0\*) is ≈1.4pt by bootstrap at n = 1,200 (the two contrasts share their B_oth arm and are positively correlated), which is what sets the inflation check's 1.5 × SE ≈ 2.1pt against its flat 2pt floor: the check fires on a difference the design can actually see. **Equivalence tests:** at n = 800 with two seeds, SE ≈ 1.68pt gives a 90% half-width of ≈2.8pt; at n = 800 with one seed (rows 6 and 11) SE ≈ 1.77pt gives ≈2.9pt; at n = 1,200 with two seeds (prediction 1c) SE ≈ 1.41pt gives ≈2.32pt. The ±3pt TOST margins in predictions 1, 4 and 6 are therefore the smallest this design supports at each of those n, they are stated as such rather than chosen to be passable, and the consequence is written into the claim: **the strong headline is licensable only by a residual gain within ≈0.7pt of zero**, and a 2pt residual routes the paper to the partial-absorption title. *Secondary quantities:* Inner at a single instruction has SE ≈ 1.41pt (MDE ≈ 3.5pt); bounded-loss statements at n = 800–1,200 have 90% CI half-widths of ≈1.7–2.3pt, which is why predictions 5 and 6 use 3pt bounds. *Null floor on the ratio:* the treatment-free replicate's A_null has SD ≈ (1 − ρ_dec)-scaled and is expected at 0.12–0.21 at n = 600 with Inner ≈ 8pt; it is dominated by the *denominator's* noise, which is the same reason G3/G6 protect the endpoint. If the measured null floor's 95th percentile exceeds 0.25, n is raised from the balanced reserve or the threshold is raised — decided and logged **before** unblinding, per the house rules. Sub-MDE differences are declared inconclusive in advance, in either direction.

**Two honest caveats about the numbers above.** First, every variance component is transferred from E1; ALFWorld's discordance rate, run-to-run spread and timeout behaviour are unmeasured on this node. The dev stage measures all three, the MDE and the compute plan are recomputed and republished before S_test is touched. Second, the 0.20 discordance assumption drives SE(Inner); at 0.30 it rises to 1.66pt and every row of the table above shifts one line down, which is exactly why the endpoint is a ratio (whose precision depends mostly on the denominator the gate controls) rather than an absolute difference (whose threshold the last round showed to be unreachable).

**Instrumentation per episode.** Task type, game ID, type set, instruction hash with its provenance tag and rollout budget, bank ID with its provenance tag, injected item IDs and token count, seed, steps taken, success, timeout, invalid-action count, and the full action trace. Optimizer runs log per-rollout game IDs (coverage), candidate lineage and reflector inputs. One script produces every table in the paper from the JSONL logs.

## Preprint Collision Check

**Method.** Fresh scan on 2026-09-03 (prior scan 2026-08-21): 15 HuggingFace Papers queries (244 unique papers, 44 dated ≥ 2026-06), by-id abstract verification for 54 arXiv IDs, and Claude server-side web search for targeted collision queries and for IDs not indexed on HuggingFace. Every ID cited in this proposal appears in `reference/litscan_2026-09-03.md` with title, date, verification channel and role; **no ID has been added in v5 or v5.1 that is not in that table, v5.2 added exactly two (AutoManual and AutoGuide, both pre-cutoff and reviewer-supplied), and v5.3 adds none.** Semantic Scholar was reachable but rate-limited without a key; arXiv is unreachable from this node, so arXiv pages were confirmed through web-search results only.

**What is conceded, plainly.** Two things that reviewers have named three rounds running are true and are stated in Related Work's first paragraph rather than buried. (a) **EvoAgentBench (arXiv 2607.05202) publishes the qualitative headline.** Its Table 3 puts ReasoningBank (+3.6) and GEPA (+1.2) on the same 528/267 trajectories on Qwen3.5-27B; "an optimizer given the same episodes gets roughly what the bank gets" is therefore in the literature and we cite it as motivation. (b) **ExpeL (arXiv 2308.10144) already makes "memory as an instruction" a design pattern**: its insights are distilled from training episodes and prepended to the prompt as a natural-language block, so the two containers this proposal compares are not architecturally distinct in the closest target system. (c) **ExpeL is also a measurement collision and not only a framing one, and v5.2 did not say so.** Its published ablation removes the insight container and the retrieved-trajectory container **separately**, both built from one undifferentiated training pool, and reports each single-container condition against the full system in ALFWorld. That is a redundancy measurement between two containers filled from the same corpus, in this environment. **"Nobody has reported the number" is withdrawn.** What is unreported is the number *under provenance control*: ExpeL's containers draw on one pool, so its ablation compares two **representations of a corpus** rather than absorption of **specific episodes**; it is unpaired, not token-matched, single-run per condition, and its instruction container is written by a fixed extraction routine rather than by an optimizer with a search budget. We do not quote its numbers — arXiv is unreachable from this node and we will not cite a table we have not read — and reading it is the first item of obligation (5). **The contribution is therefore the identification and the number under provenance control, not the idea**: provenance labelling of both containers, the episode-disjoint same-type instruction and the type-naming instruction that separate episode-level from type-level from label-level absorption, the item-level overlap dose-response, an absorbed share *and* a residual gain each with a confidence interval, and a null replicate per denominator gap. **The ceiling that follows is accepted rather than argued with: judges have converged on novelty 4–5 for exactly these reasons, that caps the venue expectation, and the value of the work is the pre-registration, the interval and the falsification routes rather than the surprise.** A reviewer who thinks identification-and-quantification on top of a published qualitative result is not worth a main-track slot has a defensible position, and this proposal does not argue otherwise.

**Closest work: EvoAgentBench — read from the PDF.** Vanilla, +Memento, +ReasoningBank, +GEPA and a curated Anchor† reference on the same trajectories, two scaffolds, three backbones, four domains, results as mean ± SE over three runs. Confirmed absent from the paper: any combined optimizer-plus-memory condition (GEPA is "the only no-retrieval evolution condition"), any token matching (the word does not occur), any paired test or bootstrap, any compiled-bank arm, and any provenance manipulation — all methods draw on one undifferentiated training pool. What remains ours: the provenance square and the interaction it identifies, the episode-disjoint control, the on-policy bank, the item-level content check, the combined cells, token-matched compiled and jointly optimized arms, paired statistics with a null replicate of the endpoint, and the ALFWorld setting.

**Older framing collision: MIPROv2 (arXiv 2406.11695).** Instruction-only versus demonstration-only versus joint optimization on one training set is the two-container question for static bootstrapped demonstrations, and its finding that the split is task-dependent pre-empts the qualitative answer. It does not retrieve, distill, manipulate provenance, token-match, or report a residual. It is cited as the prior on the retention quantity, and its joint variant is run here as row 12 precisely so the headline cannot be quietly about the demonstration slot.

**Nearest measurement object: Experience Distillation (arXiv 2607.21051).** "Retains ≥ 64.8% of the in-context gain" is our absorbed share with weights as the second container; it is also the precedent for reporting this quantity as a ratio rather than a difference, which is the change v5.1 makes to its endpoint.

**Compile-once is partially pre-empted in direction** by arXiv 2608.14036 (skills beat Workflow Memory by 6.06pt matched; 65.7% procedural anchoring), arXiv 2606.30775 (a single rewrite matches ten rounds) and arXiv 2605.23899 (skills help on average, hurt 25%). None compares a compiled artifact against live per-episode retrieval from the same bank at matched tokens on top of an optimized instruction. Prediction 6 is therefore a quantified bound, not a surprise.

**Looking back, not only forward.** The scan was weighted toward the last six months and structurally could not catch two **pre-2026 ALFWorld precedents** that a reviewer supplied: **AutoManual (arXiv 2405.16247, NeurIPS 2024)**, which builds rules from ALFWorld interaction and compiles them into a single markdown manual that replaces per-episode experience lookup, and **AutoGuide (arXiv 2403.08978)**, which extracts state-aware guidelines from offline experience and retrieves them. Stated plainly: **in this exact environment they already show that episodic experience can be rewritten as instruction text, and that a compiled manual competes with retrieval.** Prediction 6 is therefore closer to a replication than a surprise, no novelty is claimed for the compile-once arm, and the "memory as an un-optimized prompt" framing is older than this proposal and older than the 2026 papers it cites. Neither paper labels either container by the provenance of the episodes that filled it, and neither runs the crossover, so the identification instrument survives. These two IDs are pre-cutoff and independently verifiable; they are the **only** citations added in v5.2, and they are entered into `reference/litscan_2026-09-03.md` at freeze with their verification channel recorded. The lesson for the scan's calibration is recorded with them.

**ALFWorld-specific priors are cited, not competed with:** MemHarness (arXiv 2607.28272), Memp (arXiv 2508.06433), the ALFWorld procedural-retrieval study (arXiv 2511.21730), "When Continual Learning Moves to Memory" (arXiv 2604.27003) and "Harness the Memory" (arXiv 2608.15008) establish that memory effects there are 5–15 points and that excessive retrieval harms sequential decision-making. None manipulates what the instruction was optimized on, because none optimizes an instruction.

## Risk Factors and Limitations

1. **The gates are the study's main risk, and each has a published branch.** G1 (memory must gain ≥ 5pt at the hand-written scaffold, now tested under a demonstration-bearing variant too) restates the ALFWorld literature's own claim with other agents and scaffolds. G3 (on-type content must beat off-type content by ≥ 9pt) is the gate the ratio's denominator needs and is deliberately a coin flip at a true 9pt effect; a failure yields the type-generic finding, **not** a fallback to the level-ladder endpoint that four rounds of reviewers rejected. G3b (the optimizer must learn something type-specific) is **restated in v5.2 as a direction-only requirement plus a routing rule**, because its v5.1 form required a 3pt deficit at I_oth — i.e. it gated on exactly the mis-specification that inflates its own denominator; it can still stop the square before S_test. G2 (the optimizer must beat I0\*) is at real risk with a local 32B reflector. **G6 moved from S_test to S_dev2 in v5.3** — a ratio conditioned on its own denominator clearing a floor in the sample that computes it is biased upward and its one-sided bound loses coverage — so it stops the ratio half of the family *before* the test set is touched, and the residual-gain endpoint still runs if it fires.
2. **Weaker reflector, no frontier API (refusal by hardware fact, not by preference).** The track brief describes four A100s and an API budget; **this node has two A100-80GB and no API key**. Every model role is therefore local and the optimized instruction is a lower bound on what the instruction container can hold. This biases **against** the hypothesis — a stronger reflector would absorb more, raising A — and it also shrinks the level gap and hence the headroom bias. The coin-flip result (arXiv 2604.14585) makes G2's branch live. Pre-registered amendment if a key appears before the freeze: only the reflection calls change (≈4,000 calls); the grid does not.
3. **The headroom correction is an arithmetic bound plus a bracket, computed per denominator, and it is not eliminated.** Inside each Inner, headroom is identical by construction. Across the two instructions the residual is the compression ratio over that denominator's own level gap: ≈0.06–0.12 for A over 3–6pt, **≈0.16–0.22 for A0 over the 8–11pt gap to the hand-written scaffold — at or above the flat 0.2 bound v5.2 asked A0 to clear, which is why A0 is now read against a matched-gap null whose 95th percentile is planned at 0.25–0.30** — and ≈0.04–0.08 for A_all. All under the multiplicative model, which is a model, not a law. It is bounded from below by best-of-w (which under-corrects) and from above by Ladder T (which over-corrects because a same-type instruction also absorbs), the claim is read at the conservative end, and a ±25%/±50% sensitivity table is published. If Ladder T cannot bracket ℓ_own even at 750 rollouts, this is declared before S_test and the confirmatory reading falls back to A_h with the best-of-w bracket as its upper bound.
4. **Ladder T is conservative by construction, and that is a real cost.** I′ learns the same task types from different episodes, so A_epi understates episode-specific absorption by however much I′ itself absorbs. The consequence is stated in advance: a null A_epi does not prove that absorption is type-level, it bounds the episode-specific component from below, and the pre-registered title change reflects that bound rather than a proof.
5. **Type sets are not exchangeable, by design, and that has a cost.** T_A and T_B differ in difficulty and in how much procedure they require, so the two directions are not statistical replicates; they are reported separately with the direction interaction fitted, and a second partition is a Tier-2 robustness check rather than a promise. The partition rule (minimum cross-set retrieval overlap over the ten admissible splits) is committed before any episode, so the choice is not a forking path — but it is a retriever-based proxy for procedural similarity, not a measurement of transfer.
6. **Approximate type counts.** Game counts per task type were inferred from task-directory proportions in the feasibility report; T_B binds every allocation and its reserve is thin (≈116 games per set). The exact recount happens at freeze, surplus goes to S_dev2 first, and a shortfall reduces S_test symmetrically with a republished MDE.
7. **Statistical reach.** SE(A) ranges from 0.14 to 0.27 as Inner(I_oth) falls from 10pt to 5pt; power for the 0.5-against-0.2 test is 80/70/50% at 10/8/6pt. **A0 is harder still**, because its floor is its own matched-gap null's 95th percentile (0.25–0.30) rather than 0.2, and we say so in advance. **The residual-gain half of the conjunction is tighter than it looks generous:** SE(g) ≈ 1.41pt at n = 1,200, so the ±3pt TOST is declarable only when |ĝ| ≤ 0.68pt — the smallest margin the design supports is ±2.4pt and ±2pt would need n ≈ 1,750. The honest consequence is that **the strong headline requires a residual that is nearly exactly zero**, so the most likely outcome of a real absorption effect of realistic size is the *partial*-absorption title, not the strong one. These all rest on a planning discordance rate of 0.20 and on E1-measured run-to-run spread transferring to ALFWorld. Both are measured on dev first, the downgrade rule in prediction 1 applies, and an inconclusive primary is reported as inconclusive.
8. **The null floor of a ratio is wider than the null floor of a difference.** A_null is dominated by the denominator's noise; at Inner ≈ 8pt and n = 600 its 95th percentile is expected at ≈0.21, uncomfortably close to the 0.2 lower-bound requirement. This is measured (row 10), not assumed, and the pre-registered response — raise n from the reserve or raise the threshold, before unblinding — is the honest one; the alternative, quietly comparing against a nominal CI only, is what the house rules forbid.
9. **Off-policy residue.** The on-policy bank is built under one instruction seed only, so it controls the first-order staleness confound but not seed-level variation in it; the arm is descriptive of magnitude, not of variance.
10. **The content check is a proxy at both ends.** Textual overlap between a bank item and an instruction is not proof of content transfer, and the hi/lo split is computed from a held-out optimizer seed's instruction, which decouples selection from evaluation but assumes the two seeds' instructions overlap the bank similarly. A null dose-response downgrades the content claim to a behavioural one; a positive one is evidence, not proof.
11. **Templated goals (no human annotations available on this node).** Goal text is ALFWorld's default template, which makes the environment more repetitive than the human-annotated variant. This helps both containers, so it is not obviously biased for or against absorption, but it limits external validity to human-phrased goals.
12. **Compilation is lossy and compiler-dependent.** C_all depends on a prompt fixed on dev and reported verbatim; it is treated as a lower bound, with the Tier-1 whole-bank arm bounding from the other side.
13. **Agent and bank specificity.** Two dense 27–32B agents of one family carry the confirmatory result, with a cross-family (medgemma-27b-it) check at Tier 1 and an out-of-scope (gpt-oss-120b) check at Tier 2. Nothing is pooled across pairings; the memory-weak pairing is a co-equal result.
14. **Descoped: the E1 replication leaves Tier 0.** With a total memory effect of ≈2.4pt there, the ratio's denominator would be ≈1–2pt and SE(A) > 0.6, so E1 cannot bear the endpoint even as a bound. It is Tier 1, reported as a direct-difference bound with CIs, never as the ratio and never as replication failure; unrun means unrun.
15. **Descoped: the third optimizer seed.** SE(Inner) moves from 1.41 to 1.37pt for a 50% cost increase on the four largest cells; the seed is bought back first if G5 clears with margin.
16. **Refused: making a level-matched information-free instruction the primary identifying instrument.** Four rounds rejected it, and the fifth round showed the best-of-w version is not information-free with respect to the correction it makes. It survives only as one end of a bracket, over an interval whose bracketing is verified on dev.
17. **Refused: dropping the sharp claim for a measurement study.** The reviewers' aggregate advice would be satisfied by reporting the relevance advantage as a function of provenance and declining to name a mechanism. That would remove the only sentence a reviewer could disagree with. The claim, the ratio threshold and the pre-registered refutation routes stay.
18. **Refused: test-time self-editing memory.** Absorption requires that both containers see identical episodes and that test games stay paired across cells; a bank that rewrites itself during evaluation breaks both. ReasoningBank's item format is adopted with its update step frozen after training; named as future work.
19. **Refused: full reproduction of a published memory result, and refused: promoting the official splits to Tier 0.** The targets' scaffolds, agents and training are not reproducible here. The official `valid_seen` (140) and `valid_unseen` (134) splits run at Tier 1 with pooled artifacts, so our raw-memory-versus-no-memory numbers land on the same axis as the published ALFWorld numbers; the comparison is labelled qualitative and no claim is made to have re-measured anyone's system. They stay at Tier 1 for one reason: 274 games cannot carry an endpoint whose SE(A) is 0.14–0.27 at n = 1,200. The cost is a **bridging assumption**, stated in risk 24.
20. **Refused: a three-set Latin square, and refused: re-partitioning after a gate failure.** A finer partition would cut n per cell below 400 and push SE(A) past 0.3. Re-partitioning after seeing G3 would be a forking path; the partition is fixed by a zero-episode criterion committed in advance, and a G3 failure is reported as a finding.
21. **Novelty of the design primitive is unverified, and the obligation is now freeze-blocking.** The provenance-crossover primitive has never been searched for from this node; obligation (1) is run by the PI before DESIGN.md is hashed, and the contribution is reduced in print if it lands. The unopened appendix of arXiv 2608.14036 remains a live risk to prediction 6.
22. **Infrastructure.** Two A100-80GB; non-persistent root filesystem with everything on NFS; ALFWorld game data materialised from the HuggingFace datasets-server with SHA256 recorded (the release-asset host is firewalled, and the fetcher pins the one reachable CloudFront IP); runs resumable and append-only; per-game Inform7 compilation at reset adds environment overhead that batching absorbs but that is charged to the G5 throughput gate, now stated in calls/hour.
23. **Scope.** Procedural experience memory in an environment with recurring task templates, on 27–32B open-weight agents, with every model role local. Nothing is claimed about episodic personal memory, long-conversation memory, frontier-scale agents, or agents whose tasks do not recur.
24. **Bridging assumption: held-out training games are not `valid_seen`/`valid_unseen`.** The 5–15pt ALFWorld memory prior that G1 is benchmarked against was measured on the official evaluation splits, while this study measures on held-out *training* games because 274 games cannot carry the endpoint's n. The two sit on the same axis only qualitatively; the Tier-1 official-split cells are what test the bridge, and if they show a materially different memory effect the Tier-0 numbers are reported as being about held-out training games and nothing more.
25. **The denominator was the largest remaining hole, and v5.2 answers it with a co-primary rather than a repair.** Inner(I_oth) is measured at an instruction that is mis-specified for own-type games, so an on-type bank can be repairing damage rather than adding relevance; A0 (at the hand-written scaffold) and A_all (at the deployed pooled instruction) are the neutral references, the inflation check and Rule R decide in advance when A is uninterpretable, and G3b no longer requires the damage. What remains unfixed is that all three denominators share the same numerator, so a numerator artifact would move all three together; rows 7, 8, 17, 18 and now 19 and 20 attack the numerator, and they are behavioural rather than causal. **v5.3 adds the thing v5.2 got wrong about this pair:** A0's own headroom floor (0.16–0.22) is the largest of the three, so the "neutral" denominator carried the biggest uncomputed bias, and requiring both marginals to pass does **not** fix a bias they both have — the intersection–union rule controls the composite type-I error and removes denominator-shopping, nothing more. A0's floor is now read against a matched-gap null and A's against Rule R and the inflation check, both decided on dev.
26. **A_epi is under-powered and is no longer a threshold.** At row 4's n = 500 with one optimizer seed and an interpolated denominator, SE(A_epi) ≈ 0.30, so the v5.1 threshold of a 0.2 lower bound would have needed Â_epi ≈ 0.6. It is reported with a CI instead, and the episode-versus-type question is carried at Tier 0 on the bank side by row 17 — which is a genuinely different test, not a substitute of equal strength: B′_own answers whether the *bank* is type-generic, not whether the *instruction* absorbed these particular episodes. The instruction-side question is therefore answered only descriptively in this study, and we say so rather than thresholding a number the design cannot support.
27. **Two optimizer seeds, not three — and one seed on three more cells in v5.3.** The crossed optimizer-run effect is not separately estimable at two seeds; seeds are treated as fixed strata, the bootstrap is over games, the between-seed spread is descriptive, and the 0.8pt-per-run variance component remains transferred from E1 multi-hop QA rather than measured in ALFWorld. The third seed was refused because rows 16, 17 and 18 put Tier 0 at the 84-hour ceiling, and v5.3 goes further: **rows 6 (on-policy), 11 (compile-once) and 12 (J) drop to one seed** to fund rows 19 and 20 and the matched-gap rung. Their contrasts are consequently read as bounds, their between-seed spreads are not reported, and the compile-once equivalence verdict is declarable only within ≈0.1pt of zero. Buy-back order if G5 clears with margin: the second seed on row 11, then row 12, then row 6, then the third seed on the square.
28. **The reserve funds at most one contingency.** 116 games per set (232 pooled balanced) cannot serve an S_test shortfall, the G3 re-measurement and null-floor remediation at once. The priority order is fixed in Experiments, and the fallback for an unfunded use is a *changed rule* — G3 decided on S_dev2 alone, or the null threshold raised instead of n — decided before unblinding. The honest consequence is that if the freeze recount is unfavourable, the single G3 extension disappears.
29. **Two of v5.2's three Tier-0 refusals are reversed in v5.3, and the reversal is the point.** **I_type is now Tier 0 (row 19)** — v5.2 refused it on the reasoning that its decisive role was interpreting a branch that is no longer a decision rule, and that reasoning was wrong once A_epi stopped being thresholded: without I_type, a large absorbed share is equally consistent with "the optimizer absorbed these 300 episodes" and "any text naming the three upcoming task types kills the on-type bank's edge". **R_raw is now Tier 0 (row 20)** at matched tokens — v5.2 refused it as testing the distillation step rather than the container question, but if pasting the same episodes raw reproduces the bank's effect then the container question is about retrieval and tokens, which is a different paper. **Still refused for Tier 0: {C_own, C_oth} × {B_own, B_oth}** (whether *any* prose summary absorbs the bank, rather than a reflective optimizer's, is a second paper's question and cannot be bought without cutting the endpoint's own controls) and **M_all**, the un-token-matched whole-bank dump (≈5× longer prompts, ≈2× the per-call cost, and it breaks the token match the rest of the grid holds). The cost of the first refusal is real and unchanged: without the compiled-provenance square, a positive result says "a GEPA instruction built from these episodes absorbs the bank", not "any prose written from these episodes does".
30. **Conceded: AutoManual (arXiv 2405.16247) and AutoGuide (arXiv 2403.08978) already rewrite ALFWorld experience as instruction text**, and AutoManual already compiles it into one manual that replaces lookup. Prediction 6 is a replication with token matching and an optimized-instruction base, not a discovery, and the compile-once arm carries no novelty claim. This also shows the scan's calibration was tilted toward the last six months; the two IDs are added to the scan table at freeze and the pre-2026 window is re-swept before submission under obligation (5).
31. **Conceded: ExpeL's ablation is a measurement collision, not only a framing one.** It separates a distilled-insight container from a retrieved-trajectory container built from the same training pool, in ALFWorld, and v5.2 cited ExpeL five times without engaging it. "Nobody has reported the number" is withdrawn; what survives is the number *under provenance control*, and the difference between the two claims is exactly the difference between one training pool and two labelled ones. We do not quote ExpeL's table because we have not read it from the source — arXiv is unreachable here — and reading it is the first item of obligation (5). If that table turns out to report a paired, token-matched retention ratio, the contribution reduces further and the paper says so.
32. **The novelty ceiling, stated once and not argued with.** Three independent judges have converged on novelty 4–5 because of collisions this proposal itself concedes: EvoAgentBench publishes the qualitative headline, ExpeL already ablates the two containers, AutoManual already compiles ALFWorld experience into instruction text, and the provenance-crossover primitive is not ours. **The contribution is identification and a number on top of an accepted qualitative result, that caps the venue expectation, and no further design round moves it.** The design's value is the pre-registration, the intervals and the fifteen named refutation routes; nothing in this version is strengthened rhetorically to compensate, and the conjunction added in v5.3 makes the headline harder to reach, not easier.
33. **The resource record, corrected, because every refusal in this document leans on it.** The track brief describes four A100s and an API budget. **This node has two A100-80GB and no API key** (PI decision, direction A). The 84-hour ceiling is therefore a hardware fact about the machine the study runs on, not a self-imposed limit, and the refusals it justifies — the third optimizer seed, row 4 at row-2 scale, rows 13 and 15, the second seeds on rows 6, 11 and 12 — are consequences of that fact. On four GPUs the same grid runs in ≈30–42 hours and the purchase order is pre-registered. **The throughput band itself (11,800–16,600 calls/hour per node) is transferred from a 6-call multi-hop-QA setting with much shorter prompts**, is labelled as transferred everywhere it appears, and is measured by G5 before anything depends on it; if the real ALFWorld number is materially lower, the pre-registered Tier-0 cut order runs and the study ships smaller rather than late.
34. **The strong headline is nearly unreachable by design, and that is deliberate.** Endpoint E declares equivalence only when the residual gain is within ≈0.7pt of zero, so a real absorption effect of realistic size — an absorbed share of 0.6 with a 2pt residual — routes to the partial-absorption title T2 rather than to T1. We prefer that to the alternative the last round exposed, in which a passing share sat next to an unrefuted 4pt residual gain and the paper's own question was answered "no" while the bar read *pass*. The cost is that the most likely publishable outcome is the narrower title.
35. **The instrument's calibration is now single-point.** Dropping the 25% planted arm to fund I_type and R_raw means the graded control becomes one dilution level: the direction and approximate size of the engineered reduction are still checked, but the instrument's own dose-response is not. If the single point comes back inconsistent with its engineered size, A is not interpreted in either direction and the 25% arm is the first Tier-1 buy-back after the seeds.
36. **I_type is at Tier 0 but at n = 500, not at the endpoint's n.** The paired difference Inner(I_type) − Inner(I_own) has SE ≈ 2.6pt, so only a gap of ≈4.3pt or more is distinguishable from zero. The arm can therefore *raise* the type-label explanation but can only weakly *exclude* it, and a CI covering zero is reported as not excluding it rather than as evidence against it. That is what the ceiling affords, and it is more than v5.2 had.
37. **R_raw answers the token-matched version of the long-context question, not the long-context question.** The Tier-0 arm pastes the same retrieved episodes at the same token budget; the un-matched whole-bank dump (M_all), which is what EvoMemBench and AutoMEM actually test, stays at Tier 1 because its ≈5× longer prompts cost ≈2× per call and break the grid's token match. If R_raw matches the distilled bank, the natural next question — whether a bigger context beats retrieval outright — is explicitly out of this study's scope and is named as such.
