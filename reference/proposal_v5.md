## Name

## Title

Is Your Agent's Memory Just an Un-Optimized Prompt? A Task-Type Provenance Square in ALFWorld That Separates Absorption from Headroom

## Short Hypothesis

In ALFWorld, where task templates recur and procedural experience has real content to carry, the task-type-specific value of a retrieved procedural-experience bank is mostly absorbed by an instruction that a reflective optimizer wrote from episodes of the same task types: on the same held-out games and at a fixed instruction, the bank's advantage of on-type content over off-type content is at least 5 points smaller when the instruction was optimized on-type than when it was optimized on the other task types, after subtracting the decline that the same contrast shows along an information-free level ladder across the same accuracy interval. Everything else in this proposal is either an alternative explanation for that number (headroom, bank staleness, retrieval format, compute) or a consequence of it (a block compiled once from the bank replaces retrieval on top of the optimized instruction).

## Related Work

**(1) Procedural experience memory — the targets.** ExpeL (arXiv 2308.10144) collects training-task experiences, extracts natural-language insights and recalls them at inference; ReasoningBank (arXiv 2509.25140) distills strategies from self-judged successes and failures; Memp (arXiv 2508.06433) builds, retrieves and updates procedural memory and reports on ALFWorld; A-MEM (arXiv 2502.12110) and retrieval-augmented agents trained to use retrieved trajectories (arXiv 2603.18272) share the evaluation template: the memory-versus-no-memory delta is measured at a fixed, hand-written scaffold, or memory is compared against fine-tuning. The template assumes memory and instruction are non-substitutable containers. ALFWorld is the environment where several of these effects were measured, which is why the confirmatory test moves there: MemHarness (arXiv 2607.28272) reports raw memory injection at 70.1% against 76.4% for no-memory RL and 85.2% for reconstructed memory on ALFWorld; a procedural-memory retrieval study on ALFWorld (arXiv 2511.21730) finds embedding retrieval degrades under vocabulary shift while LLM abstractions transfer; "When Continual Learning Moves to Memory" (arXiv 2604.27003) documents retrieval pollution, context competition and dilution with negative transfer concentrated on hard cases; "Harness the Memory" (arXiv 2608.15008) finds excessive retrieval harms sequential decision-making specifically. These are effects of 5–15 points, an order of magnitude above the 0.6–3.6pt this program measured for the same bank family in tool-augmented multi-hop QA, and that gap is the whole reason for the environment move.

**(2) Reflective prompt optimizers.** GEPA (arXiv 2507.19457, ICLR 2026 oral) samples trajectories, reflects in natural language and proposes prompt updates, outperforming MIPROv2 by >10% and GRPO by ~10% with up to 35× fewer rollouts. GEPA and ExpeL run the same learning principle — reflect on trajectories, distill lessons — and differ only in where the lesson is stored and how it is read. The older and closer framing precedent is MIPROv2 (arXiv 2406.11695), which factorizes a prompt into instructions and bootstrapped demonstrations and optimizes both on one training set; its instruction-only versus demonstration-only ablations are the nearest published prior on how much of a training set's value each container can carry, with the recurring finding that the split is task-dependent. A retrieved experience bank is a demonstration container with a query-conditioned read; the present study is that ablation with retrieval, distillation, a provenance manipulation and token matching added. "Prompt Optimization Is a Coin Flip" (arXiv 2604.14585) reports that 49% of optimizer runs in compound systems land below zero-shot and that gains require exploitable output structure; with a local 32B reflector rather than a frontier one, this is a live risk here and I1 > I0 is a gated prerequisite, not an assumption.

**(3) Three-way comparisons on shared trajectories — the closest work.** EvoAgentBench (arXiv 2607.05202) evaluates Vanilla, Memento, ReasoningBank, GEPA and a curated reference on a shared 528/267 train/test split across four domains and three backbones, and reports (Table 3, mean ± SE over three runs) that on Qwen3.5-27B ReasoningBank gains +3.6, GEPA +1.2 and Memento −2.4 over a 37.5 baseline, and that GEPA is the strongest automatic method on Gemma-4-31B (+5.7). It is the first published table with a retriever and GEPA on the same training trajectories, and its qualitative headline — an optimizer on the same episodes gets roughly what the bank gets — is already in the literature; we cite it as motivation rather than claiming it. Verified from its PDF: no combined optimizer-plus-memory condition (GEPA is "the only no-retrieval evolution condition"), no token matching, no compiled-bank arm, no paired statistics, and above all **no provenance manipulation** — every method sees the same undifferentiated training pool, so the table cannot say whether an optimizer's gain is specific to the episodes it saw or whether a bank's gain survives once an instruction built from those same episodes is in place. That is the object here. EvoClinician (arXiv 2601.22964) lists prompt-optimization and memory agents as baseline categories in a clinical application (excluded domain). TERMS-Bench (arXiv 2605.13909) uses GEPA as a prompt ablation to test whether prompt engineering saturates a benchmark — the same logical move, in another domain, without memory.

**(4) The confound-audit literature.** MemDelta (arXiv 2606.29914) shows headline memory gains flip under one-variable changes; "Diagnosing Retrieval vs. Utilization Bottlenecks" (arXiv 2603.02473) finds retrieval method spans 20 points while write strategy spans 3–8 and raw chunks match lossy writes; Memory-R2 (arXiv 2605.21768) fixes credit-assignment unfairness in RL-trained memory; EvoMemBench (arXiv 2605.18421) finds long-context baselines remain highly competitive against 15 memory methods; AutoMEM (arXiv 2606.04315) reports a self-managed flat-file harness beating eight memory systems. All audit confounds inside the memory pipeline or against context dumping; none posits an optimized instruction as a rival container, and none manipulates the provenance of what each container was built from.

**(5) The provenance primitive, and what we do not claim.** Asking whether a learned artifact's benefit is specific to the data it was built from is a standard move in the contamination and data-attribution literatures, and we have **not** been able to search that literature from this node: a targeted search for held-out-episode and data-provenance crossovers in prompt optimization and contamination work is item (2) of the pre-freeze re-scan list. **We therefore claim no novelty for the crossover primitive itself.** What we claim is its use as the identification of container substitution — an instruction and a bank each labelled by the task types whose episodes produced them, with absorption read off the instruction-provenance × bank-provenance interaction at matched test type — and that claim is explicitly conditional on the pre-freeze scan. If the scan finds the primitive already applied to prompt optimizers and memory, the contribution reduces to the ALFWorld measurement, the on-policy control and the compiled-block comparison, and the paper says so.

**(6) Containers other than the prompt.** Experience Distillation (arXiv 2607.21051) measures how much of an in-context gain survives when the experience moves into weights by context distillation (≥64.8%) versus SFT (3.8%) — the same retention-ratio shape as ours, with weights as the second container. TMEM (arXiv 2606.04536) stores experience in fast LoRA weights; experience internalization (arXiv 2606.04703) finds principle-level experience more durable than instance-level. The container question is live; none of these uses an optimized instruction as the container, and none needs gradient access to the served model, which ours does not.

**(7) Compiled procedures.** Distilled skills beat Workflow Memory by 6.06pt in matched comparison, with 65.7% of the benefit attributable to procedural anchoring rather than fact injection and retrieval precision collapsing from 29.6% to 3.3% as the pool grows (arXiv 2608.14036); model-generated skills help on average but hurt 25% of cases (arXiv 2605.23899); a single rewrite given the full training signal matches ten rounds of refinement within 0.2% (arXiv 2606.30775); procedural-memory refinement rounds are worth +3.7 to +6.7 points on enterprise tasks (arXiv 2606.23127). These make compile-once plausible rather than surprising; none compares a compiled artifact against live per-instance retrieval from the same bank at matched tokens on top of an optimized instruction, which is our compiled arm. MemAPO (arXiv 2603.21520) runs the coupling in the opposite direction (memory inside the optimizer); MAS-PromptBench (arXiv 2606.23664) studies prompt optimization without memory.

**Our position.** A memory bank and an optimized instruction are two containers for the same training episodes. MIPROv2 established the two-container ablation for static demonstrations; EvoAgentBench put a retriever and GEPA in one table; the memory literature measures its deltas with the instruction container empty. Nobody has labelled the two containers by the provenance of the episodes that filled them and read absorption off the interaction, in an environment where procedural memory demonstrably carries 5–15 points, at matched tokens, with an on-policy bank control and a paired design.

## Abstract

Agentic memory systems are evaluated by comparing an agent with a retrieved-experience bank against the same agent without one, holding a hand-written scaffold prompt fixed. We ask how much of that gain survives once the instruction slot is filled by a prompt optimizer given the same training episodes, and we separate the answer from the ceiling effect that any better prompt produces. The instrument is a **task-type provenance square** in ALFWorld. The six ALFWorld task types are split into two sets; from each set's training games we build one memory bank and one GEPA-optimized instruction, using the same episodes for both containers. On held-out games of a given type set we then measure the **relevance advantage** — accuracy with the on-type bank minus accuracy with the off-type bank, at a fixed instruction, on the same games — which holds headroom, policy and the residual unsolved set exactly constant because only the injected text changes. Absorption is the drop in that advantage when the instruction is the on-type one rather than the other set's, corrected for the decline the same advantage shows along an information-free level ladder (best-of-w action sampling, w ∈ {1,2,4,8}, verified on dev to bracket both instructions' accuracy) over the identical accuracy interval. The square is run in both directions, so bank relevance is estimated as its own main effect and is not confounded with provenance. Around the endpoint: an **on-policy bank** distilled from episodes generated under the optimized instruction, which is the mundane rival explanation (staleness) for the same drop; a graded planted control that calibrates the instrument at 2–6 points rather than 15–30; two null replicates of the endpoint itself, one treatment-free and one built from ladder levels where the true value is zero by construction; a second, mechanistically different level knob (step budget) that tests the one-dimensional headroom assumption; compute-matched sampling and step controls at the optimized instruction; a token-matched block compiled once from the bank; and a jointly optimized instruction-plus-demonstration prompt as the prompt container's ceiling. Scope: n = 1,500 held-out ALFWorld training games, three optimizer seeds on the four square cells, ≈60,000 must-ship ALFWorld episodes plus a ≈23,000-episode replication in tool-augmented multi-hop QA, all model roles local (Qwen3-32B reflector, distiller and compiler), success determined by the environment rather than a judge, a claim threshold of 5 points set above a derived minimum detectable effect of ≈4.8 points, and every "no difference" statement reported as a bounded loss with a CI.

## Experiments

**HELD FIXED THROUGHOUT.** ReAct scaffold over ALFWorld's text interface; admissible-action list presented identically in every cell; **step limit L0 identical in every cell** except the two cells where the budget is the manipulated variable; one re-prompt on an unparseable action, then a no-op charged to the budget; **episodes end at success or step limit** — ALFWorld's analogue of proposal ①'s answerless-episode problem is the timeout, so **timeout rate, mean step count and invalid-action rate are logged per cell** and every accuracy delta is decomposed into success/timeout/invalid components; retrieval is per episode (goal text plus first observation as the key), MiniLM dense, k fixed by a dev tuning run with a budget equal to one optimizer run; decoding temperature and seed schedule {11, 23, 37}; one training pool feeds both containers; the held-out test split. Only the instruction slot and the memory-injection variant differ.

**MODEL ROLES (no frontier API).** Agent: the screened one of Qwen3.5-27B and Qwen3-32B, thinking disabled, one vLLM replica per A100. Reflector (GEPA), distiller (bank items), compiler (the static block) and demonstration selector: **Qwen3-32B**, switched to Qwen3.5-27B if the agent is Qwen3-32B so that no model reflects on its own traces. medgemma-27b-it is a cross-family check and gpt-oss-120b an explicitly out-of-scope scale check, both Tier 2. **No LLM judge is used for any primary quantity**: ALFWorld returns success directly. Consequence of a local reflector, stated up front: GEPA's published gains used frontier reflectors, so our I1 is a lower bound on what the instruction container can hold, absorption is measured against a weaker optimizer than a practitioner with an API would run, and the optimizer-failure branch is live rather than decorative.

**TYPE SETS AND SPLITS (pre-registered, seeded, IDs committed).** The six ALFWorld task types are partitioned into **T_A = {pick_two, clean, look_in_light}** and **T_B = {pick_and_place, cool, heat}**, chosen so that both sets hold enough games (train-split game counts are ≈1,810 and ≈1,749 by task-directory proportions; exact counts are recomputed at freeze). From the 3,553 training games: **P_train** 1,000 (500 per set) — the shared pool that both containers see; **S_dev** 600 (300 per set) — screening and gates, split into S_dev1 300 for selection and S_dev2 300 for gating; **S_test** 1,500 (750 per set) — touched once, by the final grid; the remainder is a reserve, untouched except for logged, gate-triggered rebuilds. The official `valid_seen` (140) and `valid_unseen` (134) sets are never used for the primary and appear only as a Tier-1 external check. If the exact recount leaves a set short, n is reduced equally in both sets and the MDE is recomputed and published **before** any test cell runs.

**ARTIFACTS (built once, frozen, hashed).**
- **B_A, B_B** — banks distilled by Qwen3-32B from all P_train episodes of the set's three task types, run under I0. Format fixed by screening (ExpeL-style INSIGHT/PROCEDURE/OUTCOME items, or ReasoningBank-style strategy items from successes and failures). ALFWorld returns ground-truth success, so the ReasoningBank-style variant uses environment-verified labels instead of self-judgement: a deviation from the published recipe that makes the bank stronger, which is conservative for our claim, and is logged as such. Item counts, k and dedup reported.
- **B_A^on, B_B^on** — the same distillation applied to P_train episodes of the set's own types **generated under that set's optimized instruction I(T, seed 1)**: the on-policy bank.
- **B_oth+own25, B_oth+own50** — the graded planted control: the off-type bank with 25% or 50% of its items replaced by on-type items drawn from the same pool, engineered so the relevance advantage is reduced by a known fraction.
- **I0** — the hand-written scaffold instruction, fixed and hashed before anything runs.
- **I(T_A, s), I(T_B, s)** — GEPA-style reflective optimization of the instruction slot on that set's P_train episodes only, seeded with I0 verbatim, 350 rollouts, memory absent from context, s ∈ {1,2,3}; episode coverage instrumented.
- **I2(T, s)** — as I(T) but with the on-type bank retrieved into context during optimization; one seed per set (the coupling arm).
- **J(T, s)** — MIPROv2-style **joint** optimization of instruction plus bootstrapped demonstrations on the same episodes, token-matched to the memory injection; two seeds per set. This is the prompt container's ceiling and a named refutation route.
- **C_T** — one static block written offline by the compiler from all of B_T, length fixed at the median per-episode injection length of B_T measured on S_dev2; compiler prompt fixed on dev and reported verbatim.
- **Level ladder A (information-free, single knob, primary):** best-of-w action sampling, w ∈ {1, 2, 4, 8} — at each step the agent samples w candidate actions and commits to the modal admissible one. w = 1 is the base configuration, so the ladder passes through every I0 cell for free. It carries no episode content by construction and is simultaneously the compute-matched sampling control the reviewers asked for.
- **Level ladder B (second knob, for the one-dimensionality test):** step limit L ∈ {0.6·L0, 1.8·L0}.

**SCREENING (pre-registered, split-sample, published either way).** The environment is fixed by decision; the screening axis is agent × bank format. *Stage S1 (selection, S_dev1, n=300):* for each of the two agents, generate a bank-source pool of 400 games under I0, distil both formats, and measure the memory gain at the hand-written instruction, g(I0, B) = acc(I0×M_B) − acc(I0×M0), for the four agent × format pairings. The pairing with the largest gain that also clears 5pt proceeds. *Stage S2 (gating, S_dev2, n=300, never used in selection):* the gates below are measured afresh on the proceeding pairing, which removes the winner's curse. Everything measured in S1 and S2 is published, and the losing pairings are reported as co-equal results.

**GATES (S_dev2; each needs a point estimate and a lower 80% bootstrap CI; a failure triggers its pre-registered branch, never a silent continuation).**
- **G0 (dynamic range):** no-memory success on dev in [20%, 80%]; L0 is set on dev so the no-memory timeout rate lies in [20%, 60%]. Out of range → L0 and the agent's step limit are re-set once on dev, logged.
- **G1 (memory has something to give):** g(I0, B_own) ≥ 5pt, lower CI ≥ 2pt, with ≥ 20 fixes.
- **G2 (the optimizer works):** acc(I(T)×M0) − acc(I0×M0) ≥ 3pt on both dev seeds and both type sets.
- **G3 (relevance — the gate the endpoint needs):** the relevance advantage at the hand-written instruction, g(I0,B_own) − g(I0,B_oth), is ≥ 4pt with lower CI ≥ 1pt.
- **G4 (bracketing):** the ladder-A levels must bracket both acc(I_own×M0) and acc(I_oth×M0) on dev. If they do not, the ladder is extended once (w = 12, or ladder B at 3·L0) on dev; if it still cannot bracket, the correction is an extrapolation and the primary is downgraded, before S_test is touched, to the uncorrected difference-in-differences with its headroom bias bounded and reported.
- **G5 (instrument hygiene):** retriever relevance ≥ 60% on a 100-episode audit; stored-item truthfulness ≥ 70% on a 100-item audit; throughput ≥ 700 ALFWorld episodes/hour aggregate.
- **Branches.** G1 fails for every pairing → the paper is the memory-null report **in the environment where the literature says memory works**, which is a stronger negative result than v4.4's would have been, and the Tier-1 official-split cells put it on the same axis as the published numbers. G3 fails → the bank's value is type-generic; the primary becomes the level-ladder endpoint (below) and the paper reports that what memory carries in ALFWorld is not task-type-specific. G2 fails → the optimizer-failure branch: I1 vs I0 is reported as the prerequisite result with the coin-flip literature, and the bank-side results plus the ladder carry the paper. Every branch is a publishable outcome and all of them are pre-registered here.

**PRIMARY (CONFIRMATORY) ENDPOINT — Δ_abs.** For an instruction I and test games of type set T, the **relevance advantage** is the paired quantity Inner(I) = acc(I × B_own) − acc(I × B_oth) on the same games. Only the injected text differs between its two terms, so no-memory accuracy, policy, headroom and the residual unsolved set are identical by construction — the objection that sank v4.4's endpoint ("two prompts at equal accuracy leave different residual unsolved sets") cannot be raised against it. The raw absorption signal is the difference-in-differences D = Inner(I_oth) − Inner(I_own). D still carries one bias: I_own is more accurate than I_oth on its own type set, and headroom compresses both terms of Inner multiplicatively, so a purely mechanical D arises from the level gap ℓ_own − ℓ_oth alone. That gap is a few points, not the ten-plus points between I0 and I1, and it is bracketed by the ladder. The confirmatory statistic subtracts exactly that much:

  **Δ_abs = [Inner(I_oth) − Inner(I_own)] − [Înner_H(ℓ_oth) − Înner_H(ℓ_own)]**

where Înner_H(·) is the relevance advantage along the information-free ladder, fitted over ladder A's four levels (plus ladder B's two as a robustness fit) and evaluated at the two measured no-memory levels. The ladder is used **only across the interval between the two optimized instructions**, never extrapolated (G4 enforces bracketing on dev), and its residual scatter enters the null distribution. Δ_abs is computed per optimizer-seed pair and per direction, then averaged over the two directions and three seeds. Test: one-sided Δ_abs > 0 at α = .05 by cluster bootstrap over games and optimizer runs; **claim threshold 5pt**, above the derived MDE of ≈4.8pt and above the null floor. **Mechanism (1b):** the fix/break decomposition of Inner(I_own) against Inner(I_oth) — absorption predicts a fix deficit at equal breaks; interference predicts a break excess (rule: fix-deficit share ≥ 2/3 → absorption; break-excess share ≥ 2/3 → interference; otherwise mixed). **Descriptive:** the absorbed share, 1 − Inner(I_own)/Inner(I_oth), with a delta-method CI, and the raw retention 1 − g(I_own,B_own)/g(I0,B_own).

**SECONDARY ENDPOINTS.** (a) *Level-based absorption* (v4.4's endpoint, repaired and demoted): ĝ_H(ℓ_own) − g(I_own, B_own), where ĝ_H is memory's gain along ladder A — the generic-absorption component, reported with the ladder's residual scatter as its null and the one-dimensionality check attached. (b) *On-policy* (cell 12): g(I_own, B_own^on) − g(I_own, B_own) with a 90% CI — if the shrinkage at I_own is staleness rather than absorption, this recovers it. (c) *Practitioner* (cells 4, 8): g(I_own, B_own) with a 90% CI — does the bank still pay at the optimized instruction? (d) *Compile once* (cell 15): I_own×C_own − I_own×B_own with a 90% CI. (e) *Instruction provenance* (cells 8, 9): acc(I_own×M0) − acc(I_oth×M0) — is the optimizer's own gain type-specific? This also moderates the primary: an instruction that learned nothing type-specific cannot have absorbed anything type-specific, and the two quantities are reported together. (f) *Prompt-container ceiling* (cell 16): J_own×M0 versus I_own×B_own. (g) *Coupling* (cell 17): I2_own×B_own versus max(I_own×B_own, I_own×M0), and I2_own×M0 versus I_own×M0. (h) *Compute* (cell 18): does sampling or step budget at the optimized instruction buy what memory buys? (i) Per-task-type breakdown of every square cell, free from the existing runs.

## Baselines and Ablations

**Baselines that could plausibly win and would refute us.**
(i) **Δ_abs's CI covers zero while the raw retention is large** — the whole drop in memory's value at the optimized instruction is headroom plus level-matched compression, and no episode content moved between containers. The "un-optimized prompt" framing is withdrawn and the paper reports the relevance advantage as a function of level, in an environment where memory works, as its result.
(ii) **The on-policy bank recovers the gain (cell 12): g(I_own, B_own^on) − g(I_own, B_own) ≥ 3pt** — the shrinkage was bank staleness, not absorption. The headline becomes "memory banks must be re-distilled under the deployed policy", which is a useful and different finding, and it is a genuine risk rather than a formality: this program's own proposal ① carried the same off-policy confound.
(iii) **G3 fails, or the relevance advantage on S_test is under 2pt** — the bank's value in ALFWorld is task-type-generic, so there is no type-specific content to absorb; the level-based endpoint carries the paper and the claim narrows to the generic component, stated as such.
(iv) **g(I_own, B_own) has a 90% CI above 3pt** — the bank still pays on top of an instruction optimized on the same episodes; the practitioner should build both, and the paper says so.
(v) **J_own×M0 ≥ I_own×B_own + MDE** — the demonstration container, not memory-versus-instruction, is the story: a jointly optimized static prompt reaches where the bank reaches, and the headline changes from "memory is an un-optimized prompt" to "the demonstration slot is the whole of it". This is why J is Tier 0.
(vi) **I_own×C_own − I_own×B_own has a 90% CI below −3pt** — per-episode retrieval carries value no static compilation holds; the compile-once recommendation dies.
(vii) **I_own(w=3)×M0 ≥ I_own×B_own, or I_own(L=1.5·L0)×M0 ≥ I_own×B_own** — spending memory's token or step budget on sampling or exploration buys what memory buys; the recommendation becomes "sample more", not "compile".
(viii) **I2_own×B_own exceeds max(I_own×B_own, I_own×M0) by more than the MDE** — co-adaptation synergy: the coupling the track is named for is real and the substitution framing is incomplete.
(ix) **Break-excess signature in the fix/break decomposition** — interference, not absorption; the headline becomes context competition, consistent with the memory-harm literature.
(x) **The graded planted control is not recovered monotonically** — the instrument cannot see reductions of the size it is meant to measure, and Δ_abs is not interpreted in either direction.
(xi) **The null replicates produce a |Δ_abs| above 3pt** — the endpoint's floor is at the claim's scale; the study reports the floor and declares the primary inconclusive, exactly as this program's house rules require.

**Ablations that isolate the mechanism.**
- **The square's four cells (4–7)** — the identification itself: the relevance advantage at two instructions that differ only in the provenance of the episodes they were optimized on. Two instructions of the same kind, produced by the same optimizer at the same rollout budget on equally sized pools, so no "different intervention kinds" objection applies.
- **Both directions** — separates bank relevance (a main effect, measured at I0 in cells 2–3) from bank provenance (the interaction). This is what makes a type-based split legitimate where v4.4 refused it.
- **On-policy bank (12)** — the only arm that separates absorption from staleness; named as the missing baseline by two of three reviewers.
- **Ladder A (10)** — one monotone knob, four levels, information-free by construction, bracketing verified on dev; supplies the headroom correction, the null distribution, and the compute-matched sampling control in one object.
- **Ladder B (11)** — a mechanistically different knob at matched levels: the direct test of the one-dimensionality assumption that the headroom correction rests on. If the two ladders disagree at matched level by more than their scatter, the correction is not interpretable and the paper says so before unblinding.
- **Null replicates (13, and the leave-two-out ladder null)** — the endpoint's own floor, measured rather than assumed.
- **Graded planted control (14)** — instrument calibration at 2–6pt, replacing v4.4's 15–30pt sledgehammer.
- **C_own on the optimized instruction (15)** — the deployment the paper would recommend, measured where it would be deployed and at matched tokens.
- **J (16) and D_opt (Tier 1)** — the prompt container's optimized ceiling, so that the headline cannot silently be about the demonstration slot.
- **I2 (17)** — the track's named coupling: an instruction optimized in the presence of the bank, and its dependence when the bank is removed.
- **M_shuf, M_all, R_raw, B^re, B_touched, oracle, length control (Tier 1)** — format versus content, the retrieval → compile → dump ladder, wording redundancy under a different distiller, optimizer coverage matching, the retrieval-quality ceiling and the length artifact, each bounded rather than assumed.
- **Screening over agent × bank format, published either way** — the claim's relativity is measured, and the memory-weak pairing is a co-equal result.

## Falsifiable Predictions

1. **Absorption (confirmatory).** Δ_abs ≥ 5pt, one-sided α = .05, by cluster bootstrap over games and optimizer runs, exceeding the 95th percentile of the pooled null distribution (treatment-free replicate plus leave-two-out ladder replicate). The achieved MDE is reported; if it exceeds 5.5pt the prediction is downgraded to directional before unblinding.
1b. **Mechanism.** The deficit is a fix deficit at equal breaks (absorption), not a break excess (interference), by the pre-registered 2/3 rule.
2. **Relevance and calibration.** On S_test, g(I0,B_own) − g(I0,B_oth) ≥ 4pt (G3 re-confirmed), and the graded planted control reduces the relevance advantage monotonically, with the 50% condition producing roughly twice the reduction of the 25% condition (within its CI). Both must hold for Δ_abs to be interpreted.
3. **Headroom is not the explanation.** The ladder-corrected Δ_abs retains at least 60% of the uncorrected difference-in-differences, and the two ladders agree at matched no-memory level within the fitted residual scatter.
4. **Staleness is not the explanation.** g(I_own, B_own^on) − g(I_own, B_own) has a 90% CI whose upper bound is below 3pt: re-distilling the bank under the optimized instruction does not restore what the instruction took.
5. **The practitioner's number.** The 90% CI of g(I_own, B_own) lies below 3pt, against a gated ≥5pt gain for the same bank at the hand-written instruction: at an instruction optimized on the same episodes, the bank stops paying.
6. **Compile once.** The 90% CI of I_own×C_own − I_own×B_own lies above −3pt: a block compiled once from the bank replaces per-episode retrieval on top of the optimized instruction, at matched tokens.
7. **The container ceiling and the coupling.** J_own×M0 lies within ±3pt of I_own×B_own (the jointly optimized prompt reaches where the bank reaches), and I2_own×B_own ≤ max(I_own×B_own, I_own×M0) + MDE while I2_own×M0 < I_own×M0 by ≥ 2pt (an instruction optimized with memory present depends on it).
8. **Reach.** In the E1 replication the relevance advantage at I0 is positive but small, and the 90% CI of Δ_abs there lies below 4pt — reported as a **bound**, pre-declared as uninformative about ALFWorld, and not counted as replication either way.

**Refutation.** Outcome (i) — Δ_abs's CI covering zero with a large raw retention — is the most likely way the hypothesis dies and is reported as the primary result: what disappears at the optimized instruction is headroom, not absorbed content. Outcome (ii) — the on-policy bank recovering the gain — kills the claim and replaces it with a staleness finding. Outcome (v) — J matching the memory cell — moves the headline from memory to the demonstration slot. Outcome (iii) — no type-specific content to absorb — narrows the claim to the generic component and is reported as the finding that ALFWorld memory carries generic procedure. A break-excess signature changes the headline to interference. Predictions 5, 6 and 7 changing the recommendation do not touch the claim.

## Measurement and Noise Control

Three variance sources are handled separately, with components taken from this program's own measurements where they exist and measured on dev where they do not.

**(a) Evaluation variance.** Every S_test game runs under every cell with the same seed schedule; ALFWorld games are deterministic given the game file, so the only stochasticity is decoding, and all comparisons are within-game. Pairwise superiority tests are exact McNemar on fix/break counts, Holm-corrected within pre-declared families: **F-A** {Δ_abs — the confirmatory family, one test}; **F-B** {relevance advantage at I0; the graded control's two levels}; **F-C** {on-policy, practitioner gain, compile-once}; **F-D** {ladders, compute controls, coupling, J, and every Tier-1 control}. The primary Δ_abs, a contrast of contrasts, is tested by cluster bootstrap over games **and** optimizer runs (10k resamples), not by a normal approximation. Every delta carries its fix/break decomposition and, because this is ALFWorld, its **timeout and mean-step decomposition**: proposal ①'s 2026-09-04 finding was that a memory arm can look different only because it changes how often the agent fails to commit, and the ALFWorld analogue is a change in timeout rate. Any cell whose accuracy delta is more than half accounted for by a timeout-rate change is flagged and reported as such. Bounded-loss statements use 90% game-level bootstrap CIs; absence of significance is never reported as equivalence.

**(b) Optimizer variance.** Three GEPA seeds per type set on the four square cells, two on the M0 and derived cells, two for J, one for I2. The program measured a between-seed spread of optimized-instruction accuracy of 1.0–1.6pt (n=500, two GEPA and two MIPROv2 runs) and a treatment-free decoding-seed spread of 2.8–3.4pt (three seeds, n=500) in E1; these are the priors used below, and the corresponding ALFWorld quantities are measured on S_dev2 before S_test is touched and replace them if they differ. Cell comparisons use a mixed-effects logistic model with game and optimizer run as crossed random effects; Δ_abs is reported per seed and per direction, never as one number only, and the direction × type-set interaction is fitted and reported rather than assumed away.

**(c) Null replicates and the null floor.** Three, all pre-registered. (1) Cell 1 re-run at seed 37 gives the treatment-free accuracy floor. (2) Cell 13 recomputes the **relevance advantage** at one instruction across two decoding seeds and forms Δ_abs from it: a treatment-free replicate of the endpoint itself, whose true value is zero. (3) A **leave-two-out ladder replicate**: Δ_abs computed between two information-free ladder levels with those two points excluded from the fit — true value zero by construction, and it exercises the entire estimation pipeline including the correction. No effect is claimed unless it exceeds both its threshold and the 95th percentile of the pooled null.

**Minimum detectable effect (stated before running).** The relevance advantage is a paired difference between two memory conditions at one instruction; with a planning discordance rate of 0.20 (the fraction of games whose outcome differs between on-type and off-type banks; the achieved value is reported) its SE at n = 1,500 is √(0.20/1500) = 1.15pt. Δ_abs differences two such contrasts measured on the same games under different instructions; at a game-level correlation of 0.3 the instance component is 1.15·√(2·0.7) = 1.36pt. The optimizer-run component is bounded by the measured between-run accuracy SD of 1.6pt; averaged over three seeds in each of two directions it contributes 1.6·√2/√6 = 0.92pt. The ladder correction is a fitted difference over the interval between two levels a few points apart: with six ladder points at n = 400–500 (per-point SE ≈ 2.0pt) and a level spread of ≈15pt, the slope SE is ≈0.16 per accuracy point, so a 5pt gap contributes ≈0.8pt. **SE(Δ_abs) ≈ √(1.36² + 0.92² + 0.8²) = 1.85pt, one-sided 80%-power MDE ≈ 4.6–4.8pt**, and the claim threshold is 5pt — above the MDE and above the expected null floor, which is the arithmetic v4.4 failed. *Secondary quantities:* the relevance advantage at a single instruction has SE ≈ 1.15pt (MDE ≈ 2.9pt); bounded-loss statements at n = 1,000–1,500 have 90% CI half-widths of ≈1.5–1.9pt, which is why predictions 4, 5 and 6 use 3pt bounds rather than 2pt ones. *Gates:* at n = 300 a paired gain has SE ≈ 2.6pt, so G1 at 5pt with a lower-CI requirement of 2pt passes a true 5pt effect roughly 60% of the time and a true 2pt effect under 10%; the split-sample design removes selection inflation and every gate failure has a published branch. Sub-MDE differences are declared inconclusive in advance, in either direction.

**Two honest caveats about the numbers above.** First, every variance component is transferred from E1; ALFWorld's discordance rate, run-to-run spread and timeout behaviour are unmeasured on this node. The dev stage measures all three, the MDE is recomputed and republished before S_test is touched, and if the measured treatment-free floor for the relevance advantage exceeds 3pt, n is raised from the reserve or the claim threshold is raised — decided and logged before unblinding. Second, the 0.20 discordance assumption drives the MDE; if the achieved value is 0.30 the MDE rises to ≈5.6pt and the pre-registered downgrade in prediction 1 applies.

**Instrumentation per episode.** Task type, game ID, type set, instruction hash with its provenance tag, bank ID with its provenance tag, injected item IDs and token count, seed, steps taken, success, timeout, invalid-action count, and the full action trace. Optimizer runs log per-rollout game IDs (coverage), candidate lineage and reflector inputs. One script produces every table in the paper from the JSONL logs.

## Risk Factors and Limitations

1. **The gates are the study's main risk, and each has a published branch.** G1 (memory must gain ≥5pt at the hand-written instruction) is the ALFWorld literature's own claim, but that literature used other agents and scaffolds; if it fails for every screened pairing, the paper is a memory-null report in the environment where memory is supposed to work, with the official-split cells putting it on the same axis as the published numbers. G3 (on-type bank content must beat off-type content) is the new gate the endpoint needs; if it fails, memory's value in ALFWorld is type-generic and the level-based endpoint carries the paper. G2 (the optimizer must beat I0) is at real risk with a local 32B reflector.
2. **Weaker reflector, no frontier API (refused alternative: none available).** Every model role is local, so the optimized instruction is a lower bound on what the instruction container can hold. This biases **against** the hypothesis — a stronger reflector would absorb more — which is the direction a limitation should point, and it is stated rather than hidden. The coin-flip result (arXiv 2604.14585) makes G2's branch live.
3. **The headroom correction remains an assumption, but a much smaller one.** The relevance advantage is headroom-controlled by construction at each instruction; what the ladder corrects is only the compression difference across the few-point level gap between two optimized instructions. The one-dimensionality assumption is tested directly by ladder B at matched levels, bracketing is enforced on dev by G4, extrapolation is forbidden, and the residual scatter enters the null. If ladder A and ladder B disagree at matched level, the correction is declared uninterpretable before unblinding and the uncorrected difference-in-differences is reported with its headroom bias bounded.
4. **Type sets are not exchangeable, by design, and that has a cost.** T_A and T_B differ in difficulty and in how much procedure they require, so the two directions are not statistical replicates of each other; they are reported separately with the direction interaction fitted, and a second partition is a Tier-2 robustness check rather than a promise.
5. **Approximate type counts.** Game counts per task type were inferred from task-directory proportions in the feasibility report; the exact recount happens at freeze and a shortfall reduces n symmetrically with a republished MDE.
6. **Statistical reach.** SE(Δ_abs) ≈ 1.85pt and MDE ≈ 4.6–4.8pt rest on a planning discordance rate of 0.20 and on E1-measured run-to-run spread transferring to ALFWorld. Both are measured on dev first; the downgrade rule in prediction 1 applies; an inconclusive primary is reported as inconclusive.
7. **Off-policy residue.** The on-policy bank is built under one instruction seed only, so it controls the first-order staleness confound but not seed-level variation in it; the limitation is stated and the arm is descriptive of magnitude, not of variance.
8. **Templated goals (no human annotations available on this node).** Goal text is ALFWorld's default template, which makes the environment more repetitive than the human-annotated variant. This helps both containers, so it is not obviously biased for or against absorption, but it limits external validity to human-phrased goals.
9. **Compilation is lossy and compiler-dependent.** C_own depends on a prompt fixed on dev and reported verbatim; it is treated as a lower bound, with the whole-bank arm (Tier 1) bounding from the other side.
10. **Agent and bank specificity.** Two dense 27–32B agents of one family carry the confirmatory result, with a cross-family (medgemma-27b-it) check at Tier 1 and an out-of-scope (gpt-oss-120b) check at Tier 2. Nothing is pooled across pairings; the memory-weak pairing is a co-equal result.
11. **Refused: making the level-ladder endpoint primary.** Four rounds of reviewers rejected a level-matched hand-written control as the identifying instrument, and the repaired ladder is still a curve fitted across interventions of one kind evaluated at a point produced by another. It is kept as a secondary endpoint and as a correction over a narrow bracketed interval, which is the most weight it can carry.
12. **Refused: dropping the sharp claim for a measurement study.** The reviewers' aggregate advice would be satisfied by reporting the relevance advantage as a function of level and declining to name a mechanism. That would remove the only sentence in the proposal a reviewer could disagree with. The claim, the 5pt threshold and the pre-registered refutation routes stay.
13. **Refused: test-time self-editing memory.** Absorption requires that both containers see identical episodes and that test games stay paired across cells; a bank that rewrites itself during evaluation breaks both. ReasoningBank's item format is adopted with its update step frozen after training; named as future work.
14. **Refused: full reproduction of a published memory result.** The targets' scaffolds, agents and training are not reproducible here. What v5 does instead is run the official `valid_seen` and `valid_unseen` splits at Tier 1 with pooled artifacts, so our raw-memory-versus-no-memory numbers are on the same axis as the published ALFWorld numbers; the comparison is labelled qualitative and no claim is made to have re-measured anyone's system.
15. **Refused: a three-set Latin square.** A finer partition would estimate more provenance contrasts but would cut n per cell to ≈500 and push the MDE well above any plausible effect. Two sets run in both directions is the largest design the envelope supports at a threshold above the MDE.
16. **Refused: adding environments or agents beyond those listed.** The envelope is ≈2.7–3.7 days of two-GPU time for Tier 0; anything more would be cut in the field, and a proposal whose modal outcome is an unrun grid is worse than a smaller one that runs.
17. **Novelty of the design primitive is unverified.** The provenance-crossover primitive has never been searched for from this node; the claim is scoped accordingly in Related Work and the search is a blocking pre-freeze obligation, along with the unopened appendix of arXiv 2608.14036.
18. **Infrastructure.** Two A100-80GB; non-persistent root filesystem with everything on NFS; ALFWorld game data materialised from the HuggingFace datasets-server with SHA256 recorded (the release-asset host is firewalled, and the fetcher pins the one reachable CloudFront IP); runs resumable and append-only; per-game Inform7 compilation at reset adds environment overhead that batching absorbs but that is included in the G5 throughput gate.
19. **Scope.** Procedural experience memory in an environment with recurring task templates, plus a bounded replication in tool-augmented multi-hop QA. Nothing is claimed about episodic personal memory, long-conversation memory, or agents whose tasks do not recur.
