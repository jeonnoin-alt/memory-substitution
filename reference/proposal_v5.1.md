## Name

memory_or_instruction

## Title

Is Your Agent's Memory Just an Un-Optimized Prompt? Measuring the Absorbed Share with a Task-Type Provenance Square in ALFWorld

## Short Hypothesis

In ALFWorld, at a fixed instruction and on the same held-out games, at least half of a procedural-memory bank's task-type-specific value — the accuracy advantage of an on-type bank over an off-type one — is already held by an instruction that a reflective optimizer wrote from episodes of the same task types: the absorbed share A = 1 − Inner(I_own)/Inner(I_oth) is ≥ 0.5 with a 90% lower bound ≥ 0.2. Everything else in this proposal is either an alternative explanation for that ratio (headroom compression across the level gap, bank staleness, distractor-robustness, retrieval format, compute) or a consequence of it (a block compiled once from the bank replaces retrieval on top of the optimized instruction).

## Related Work

**(0) Two concessions, stated first.** The qualitative answer to "does an optimizer given the same episodes get roughly what a memory bank gets" is already published. EvoAgentBench (arXiv 2607.05202) puts ReasoningBank (+3.6) and GEPA (+1.2) on the same 528/267 training trajectories on Qwen3.5-27B, and MIPROv2 (arXiv 2406.11695) established the instruction-versus-demonstration ablation on a single training set with the finding that the split is task-dependent. Second, "memory as an instruction" is not a framing we invented: ExpeL (arXiv 2308.10144) distills training episodes into a natural-language *insights* block and prepends it to the prompt, so its learned artifact is an instruction in all but name, and GEPA (arXiv 2507.19457) and ExpeL run the same learning principle — reflect on trajectories, write down the lesson — differing only in where the lesson is stored and how it is read. **What is not published is the identification and the number:** nobody has labelled the two containers by the provenance of the episodes that filled them, held the test games and the instruction fixed while swapping only the injected block, separated "absorbed these episodes" from "learned this task type from any episodes" with an episode-disjoint instruction, checked at item granularity whether the content the bank encodes is the content the instruction contains, and reported the absorbed share with a confidence interval. That is this proposal, and its worth is capped accordingly: it is identification-and-quantification on top of an accepted qualitative claim, and we say so rather than dressing it as a new phenomenon.

**(1) Procedural experience memory — the targets.** ExpeL (arXiv 2308.10144) collects training-task experiences, extracts insights and recalls them at inference; ReasoningBank (arXiv 2509.25140) distills strategies from self-judged successes and failures; Memp (arXiv 2508.06433) builds, retrieves and updates procedural memory and reports on ALFWorld; A-MEM (arXiv 2502.12110) and retrieval-augmented agents trained to use retrieved trajectories (arXiv 2603.18272) share one evaluation template: the memory-versus-no-memory delta is measured at a fixed, hand-written scaffold, or memory is compared against fine-tuning. The template assumes memory and instruction are non-substitutable containers. ALFWorld is where several of these effects were measured, which is why the confirmatory test lives there: MemHarness (arXiv 2607.28272) reports raw memory injection at 70.1% against 76.4% for no-memory RL and 85.2% for reconstructed memory; a procedural-memory retrieval study on ALFWorld (arXiv 2511.21730) finds embedding retrieval degrades under vocabulary shift while LLM abstractions transfer; "When Continual Learning Moves to Memory" (arXiv 2604.27003) documents retrieval pollution, context competition and dilution with negative transfer concentrated on hard cases; "Harness the Memory" (arXiv 2608.15008) finds excessive retrieval harms sequential decision-making specifically. These are effects of 5–15 points, an order of magnitude above the 0.6–3.6pt this program measured for the same bank family in tool-augmented multi-hop QA, and that gap is the whole reason for the environment.

**(2) Reflective prompt optimizers.** GEPA (arXiv 2507.19457, ICLR 2026 oral) samples trajectories, reflects in natural language and proposes prompt updates, outperforming MIPROv2 by >10% and GRPO by ~10% with up to 35× fewer rollouts. MIPROv2 (arXiv 2406.11695) is the closer framing precedent: it factorizes a prompt into instructions and bootstrapped demonstrations and optimizes both on one training set, and its instruction-only versus demonstration-only ablations are the nearest published prior on how much of a training set's value each container can carry. A retrieved experience bank is a demonstration container with a query-conditioned read; the present study is that ablation with retrieval, distillation, provenance labelling, an episode-disjoint control and token matching added. "Prompt Optimization Is a Coin Flip" (arXiv 2604.14585) reports that 49% of optimizer runs in compound systems land below zero-shot and that gains need exploitable output structure; with a local 32B reflector rather than a frontier one this is a live risk, so I_own > I0\* is a gated prerequisite (G2), not an assumption.

**(3) Three-way comparisons on shared trajectories — the closest work.** EvoAgentBench (arXiv 2607.05202) evaluates Vanilla, Memento, ReasoningBank, GEPA and a curated reference on a shared 528/267 split across four domains and three backbones, reporting (Table 3, mean ± SE over three runs) ReasoningBank +3.6, GEPA +1.2 and Memento −2.4 over a 37.5 baseline on Qwen3.5-27B, and GEPA +5.7 on Gemma-4-31B. It is the first published table with a retriever and GEPA on the same training trajectories, and we cite its headline as motivation rather than claiming it. Verified from its PDF: no combined optimizer-plus-memory condition (GEPA is "the only no-retrieval evolution condition"), no token matching, no compiled-bank arm, no paired statistics, and **no provenance manipulation** — every method sees the same undifferentiated training pool, so the table cannot say whether an optimizer's gain is specific to the episodes it saw, or whether a bank's gain survives once an instruction built from those episodes is in place. EvoClinician (arXiv 2601.22964) lists prompt-optimization and memory agents as baseline categories in a clinical application (excluded domain). TERMS-Bench (arXiv 2605.13909) uses GEPA as a prompt ablation to test whether prompt engineering saturates a benchmark — the same logical move, in another domain, without memory.

**(4) The confound-audit literature.** MemDelta (arXiv 2606.29914) shows headline memory gains flip under one-variable changes; "Diagnosing Retrieval vs. Utilization Bottlenecks" (arXiv 2603.02473) finds retrieval method spans 20 points while write strategy spans 3–8 and raw chunks match lossy writes; Memory-R2 (arXiv 2605.21768) fixes credit-assignment unfairness in RL-trained memory; EvoMemBench (arXiv 2605.18421) finds long-context baselines remain highly competitive against 15 memory methods; AutoMEM (arXiv 2606.04315) reports a self-managed flat-file harness beating eight memory systems. All audit confounds inside the memory pipeline or against context dumping; none posits an optimized instruction as a rival container, and none manipulates the provenance of what each container was built from.

**(5) The provenance primitive, and what we do not claim.** Asking whether a learned artifact's benefit is specific to the data it was built from is a standard move in the contamination and data-attribution literatures, and we have **not** been able to search that literature from this node. A targeted search is pre-freeze obligation (2) and is now **freeze-blocking**. **We claim no novelty for the crossover primitive itself.** We claim its use as the identification of container substitution — an instruction and a bank each labelled by the task types whose episodes produced them, absorption read off the instruction-provenance × bank-provenance interaction at matched test type, with an episode-disjoint same-type instruction separating episode-level from type-level absorption — and that claim is explicitly conditional on the scan. If the scan finds the primitive already applied to prompt optimizers and memory, the contribution reduces to the ALFWorld measurement, the on-policy control, the item-level content check and the compiled-block comparison, and the paper says so.

**(6) Containers other than the prompt.** Experience Distillation (arXiv 2607.21051) measures how much of an in-context gain survives when the experience moves into weights by context distillation (≥64.8%) versus SFT (3.8%) — the same retention-ratio shape as ours, with weights as the second container; it is the nearest measurement object and the reason our endpoint is a ratio rather than a difference. TMEM (arXiv 2606.04536) stores experience in fast LoRA weights; experience internalization (arXiv 2606.04703) finds principle-level experience more durable than instance-level. None uses an optimized instruction as the container, and none needs gradient access to the served model, which ours does not.

**(7) Compiled procedures.** Distilled skills beat Workflow Memory by 6.06pt in matched comparison, with 65.7% of the benefit attributable to procedural anchoring and retrieval precision collapsing from 29.6% to 3.3% as the pool grows (arXiv 2608.14036); model-generated skills help on average but hurt 25% of cases (arXiv 2605.23899); a single rewrite given the full training signal matches ten rounds of refinement within 0.2% (arXiv 2606.30775); procedural-memory refinement rounds are worth +3.7 to +6.7 points on enterprise tasks (arXiv 2606.23127). These make compile-once plausible rather than surprising; none compares a compiled artifact against live per-episode retrieval from the same bank at matched tokens on top of an optimized instruction, which is our compiled arm. MemAPO (arXiv 2603.21520) runs the coupling in the opposite direction (memory inside the optimizer); MAS-PromptBench (arXiv 2606.23664) studies prompt optimization without memory.

**Our position.** A memory bank and an optimized instruction are two containers for the same training episodes. MIPROv2 established the two-container ablation for static demonstrations; ExpeL already stores its memory as a prepended instruction; EvoAgentBench put a retriever and GEPA in one table. Nobody has labelled the containers by episode provenance, measured the absorbed share as a scale-free ratio at a fixed instruction, separated episode-level from type-level absorption with an episode-disjoint instruction, and checked at item granularity that the content the bank encodes is the content the instruction contains — in an environment where procedural memory demonstrably carries 5–15 points, at matched tokens, with an on-policy bank control and a paired design.

## Abstract

Agentic memory systems are evaluated by comparing an agent with a retrieved-experience bank against the same agent without one, holding a hand-written scaffold prompt fixed. We ask what fraction of that value is already held by an instruction that a prompt optimizer wrote from the same training episodes, and we report it as a scale-free ratio rather than a difference, so that the answer does not depend on how large the memory effect happens to be. The instrument is a **task-type provenance square** in ALFWorld. The six task types are partitioned by procedural similarity into a fetch-and-place set and an appliance-state-change set; from each set's training games we build one memory bank and one GEPA-optimized instruction from the *same* episodes. On held-out games of a given set we measure the **relevance advantage** Inner(I) = acc(I×B_own) − acc(I×B_oth) at a fixed instruction on the same games — a contrast in which headroom, policy and the residual unsolved set are identical by construction, because only the injected text changes. The confirmatory endpoint is the **absorbed share A = 1 − Inner(I_own)/Inner(I_oth)**, with a threshold of 0.5 and a 90% lower bound of 0.2; the square is run in both directions, so bank relevance is a main effect and is not confounded with provenance. Because the two Inner terms are measured at instructions of slightly different accuracy, the ratio retains one bias — the ratio of headroom compression factors across a 3–6 point level gap, arithmetically ≈0.10 and measured, not assumed. It is bracketed from both sides: best-of-w sampling under-corrects, and a **level ladder of instructions optimized on a task-type-matched but episode-disjoint pool** over-corrects; the claim is read at the conservative end. That same episode-disjoint instruction decomposes A multiplicatively into an episode-specific share and a type-level share, so "same episodes, two containers" is tested rather than asserted, and the pre-registered reading of a null episode-specific share is a title change, not a failure. Around the endpoint: an **on-policy bank** distilled under the optimized instruction (the staleness rival); a **distractor-robustness** control run at both instructions with a pre-registered rule that routes the shrinkage to the help component or to the harm component; an **item-level content check** in which the bank is split by textual overlap with the instruction and the high- and low-overlap halves are run at both instructions; a graded planted control calibrating the instrument at 2–6 points; a treatment-free null replicate of the ratio itself; the pooled practitioner configuration with its compiled-once block; and a jointly optimized instruction-plus-demonstration prompt as the prompt container's ceiling. Scope: n = 1,200 held-out ALFWorld training games, two optimizer seeds, 15 Tier-0 rows, ≈53,000 episodes and ≈928,000 model calls — 56–79 hours on two A100s — every model role local (Qwen3-32B reflector, distiller and compiler), success determined by the environment rather than a judge, and every "no difference" statement reported as a bounded loss with a CI.

## Experiments

**HELD FIXED THROUGHOUT.** ReAct scaffold over ALFWorld's text interface; admissible-action list presented identically in every cell; **step limit L0 = 30 identical in every cell** except the two cells where the budget is the manipulated variable; one re-prompt on an unparseable action, then a no-op charged to the budget; **episodes end at success or step limit** — the ALFWorld analogue of proposal ①'s answerless-episode problem is the timeout, so **timeout rate, mean step count and invalid-action rate are logged per cell** and every accuracy delta is decomposed into success / timeout / invalid components; retrieval is per episode (goal text plus first observation as the key), MiniLM dense, k fixed by a dev tuning run with a budget equal to one optimizer run; decoding temperature and seed schedule {11, 23, 37}; one training pool feeds both containers; the held-out test split is touched once. Only the instruction slot and the memory-injection variant differ.

**MODEL ROLES (no frontier API).** Agent: the screened one of Qwen3.5-27B and Qwen3-32B, thinking disabled, one vLLM replica per A100. Reflector (GEPA), distiller (bank items), compiler (the static block) and demonstration selector: **Qwen3-32B**, switched to Qwen3.5-27B if the agent is Qwen3-32B so that no model reflects on its own traces. medgemma-27b-it is a cross-family check (Tier 1) and gpt-oss-120b an explicitly out-of-scope scale check (Tier 2). **No LLM judge is used for any quantity**: ALFWorld returns success directly. Consequence of a local reflector, stated up front: GEPA's published gains used frontier reflectors, so our I_own is a lower bound on what the instruction container can hold; a weaker instruction absorbs less, so the refusal **lowers A** and is conservative for the claim; and the optimizer-failure branch (G2) is live rather than decorative.

**TYPE SETS AND SPLITS (pre-registered, seeded, IDs committed).** The partition is fixed by a **zero-episode criterion computed before any episode runs**: for each of the ten 3–3 splits of the six task types that leaves both sets with ≥ 1,500 games, compute the mean share of a game's top-k retrieved items (over a 200-game sample, retriever only) that come from the other set, and take the split that minimizes it. The expected and pre-named winner is **T_A = {pick_and_place, pick_two, look_in_light}** — fetch-and-place, no object state change — against **T_B = {clean, cool, heat}** — locate, carry to a specific appliance, operate it, place — because the three T_B types share a single skeleton that differs only in which appliance is used, and the three T_A types share the fetch-and-place skeleton. The number is committed with the split. This replaces v5's count-balanced partition, which split the near-isomorphic pairs across sets and therefore minimized exactly the relevance advantage G3 needs.

Game counts are estimated from task-directory proportions (train: pick_and_place 322, pick_two 354, clean 268, cool 212, heat 186, look_in_light 123 directories over 3,553 games), giving T_A ≈ 1,937 and T_B ≈ 1,616 games; **T_B binds every per-set allocation**. Per type set: **P_train 300** (the shared pool from which both the bank and the instruction are built), **P′_train 200** (disjoint, same three types, for Ladder T and I′), **S_screen 150** (agent × bank-format selection), **S_dev2 250** (every gate), **S_test 600** (touched once, by the final grid), reserve ≈116 in T_B and ≈437 in T_A. Pooled across directions: P_train 600, P′_train 400, S_screen 300, S_dev2 500, **S_test 1,200**. The GEPA rollout budget (350) is set to exceed P_train (300) so that episode coverage is ≈ one full sweep, which is how the lineage's original fairness constraint — both containers see the same episodes — is enforced rather than corrected after the fact; measured coverage is reported and, if it falls below 90%, the coverage-matched bank B_touched (Tier 1) is built. The official `valid_seen` (140) and `valid_unseen` (134) sets are never used for the primary and appear only as a Tier-1 external check. The exact recount happens at freeze: surplus goes first to S_dev2 (gate power), then to S_test; a shortfall reduces S_test equally in both sets and the MDE is recomputed and published **before** any test cell runs.

**ARTIFACTS (built once, frozen, hashed).**
- **I0^bare** — the hand-written ReAct scaffold, no demonstrations, fixed and hashed before anything runs.
- **I0^demo** — the same scaffold plus **two bundled-expert trajectories** from the test game's own type set (the handcoded expert is verified working on this node, so the demonstrations are reproducible and cost no LLM calls). The published 5–15pt ALFWorld memory effects were obtained under scaffolds like this, so it is the harder baseline. **I0\*** denotes whichever variant the pre-registered rule selects.
- **B_A, B_B** — banks distilled by Qwen3-32B from all P_train episodes of the set's three task types, generated under I0^bare. Format fixed by screening (ExpeL-style INSIGHT/PROCEDURE/OUTCOME items, or ReasoningBank-style strategy items from successes and failures). ALFWorld returns ground-truth success, so the ReasoningBank-style variant uses environment-verified labels instead of self-judgement — a deviation from the published recipe that makes the bank *stronger*, which is conservative for our claim, and is logged as such. Item counts, k and dedup reported.
- **B_A^on, B_B^on** — the same distillation applied to P_train episodes **generated under that set's own optimized instruction I(T, seed 1)**: the on-policy bank.
- **B^hi, B^lo** — B_own split at the median textual overlap with the instruction into k- and token-matched halves; the overlap is computed against the **seed-2** instruction while the cells are run at the **seed-1** instruction, so the split is not selected on the arm it is evaluated in.
- **B_oth+own25, B_oth+own50** — the graded planted control: the off-type bank with 25% or 50% of its items replaced by on-type items from the same pool, engineered to reduce the relevance advantage by a known fraction (calibration at 2–6pt, the scale being measured).
- **X_dist** — a token-matched, plausibly worded, task-irrelevant block: procedural items distilled by the same distiller from this program's E1 multi-hop-QA episodes, reformatted into the ALFWorld item format and truncated to the median per-episode injection length of B_own. Fluent, structurally identical, zero ALFWorld relevance.
- **I(T_A, s), I(T_B, s)** — GEPA-style reflective optimization of the instruction slot on that set's P_train episodes only, seeded with I0\* verbatim, 350 rollouts, memory absent from context, s ∈ {1, 2}; episode coverage instrumented.
- **I′(T, r)** — the same procedure on **P′_train** (disjoint games, same three task types), one run per set carried to 500 rollouts with the incumbent snapshotted at r ∈ {50, 100, 200, 350, 500}. This is **Ladder T**: a single monotone knob (rollout budget), five levels, each level an instruction of the same kind as the treatment, produced by the same optimizer, that has never seen a P_train episode.
- **I_all(s)** — GEPA on the union of both sets' P_train, 350 rollouts, s ∈ {1, 2}: the practitioner's instruction. **B_all** is the pooled bank; **C_all** the block compiled once from B_all.
- **I2(T, s)** — as I(T) but with the on-type bank retrieved into context during optimization; one seed per set (the coupling arm).
- **J(T, s)** — MIPROv2-style **joint** optimization of instruction plus bootstrapped demonstrations on the same episodes, token-matched to the memory injection; two seeds per set. The prompt container's ceiling and a named refutation route.
- **C_all, C_own** — static blocks written offline by the compiler from the whole bank, length fixed at the median per-episode injection length measured on S_dev2; compiler prompt fixed on dev and reported verbatim.
- **Compute controls (not level controls):** best-of-w action sampling at w\*, where w\* is the value that matches memory's per-episode token overhead as measured on dev (expected w\* = 2); and step budget L = 1.5·L0.

**SCREENING (pre-registered, split-sample, published either way).** The environment is fixed by decision; the screening axis is agent × bank format, run on **S_screen (300 pooled games drawn from the reserve, never used for gates or test)**. For each of the two agents, generate a 250-game bank-source pool under I0^bare, distil both formats, and measure the memory gain at the hand-written instruction for the four agent × format pairings. The pairing with the largest gain that also clears 5pt proceeds; every pairing's number is published and the losing pairings are reported as co-equal results, including the memory-weak regime. All gates are then measured afresh on **S_dev2**, which selection never saw, so the winner's curse is removed by construction.

**GATES (all on S_dev2, n = 500 pooled; each needs a point estimate and a lower 80% bootstrap CI; a failure triggers its pre-registered branch, never a silent continuation).**

**PRIMARY (CONFIRMATORY) ENDPOINT — the absorbed share A.** For an instruction I and test games of type set T, the **relevance advantage** is the paired quantity

  **Inner(I) = acc(I × B_own) − acc(I × B_oth)** on the same games.

Only the injected text differs between its two terms, so no-memory accuracy, policy, headroom and the residual unsolved set are identical by construction. The confirmatory endpoint is the scale-free ratio

  **A = 1 − Inner(I_own) / Inner(I_oth)**,

computed from direction-pooled numerator and denominator (per-direction ratios reported separately with the direction × type-set interaction fitted), averaged over optimizer seeds. **Test:** one-sided, α = .05, cluster bootstrap over games *and* optimizer runs (10k resamples), with the delta-method CI as a cross-check. **Claim threshold: A ≥ 0.5 with the 90% one-sided lower bound ≥ 0.2, and the point estimate above the 95th percentile of the pooled null distribution.** Because A is a ratio, a genuine 60–70% absorption of a 6pt advantage is a pass rather than the sub-MDE inconclusive that v5's absolute threshold would have produced — this is the arithmetic incompatibility the last round found, and it is the reason the endpoint changed.

**The one residual bias, and how it is bounded from both sides.** Inner(I_own) and Inner(I_oth) are measured at instructions of different accuracy, and headroom compresses Inner multiplicatively. With no absorption at all, A would equal

  **A_h = 1 − (1 − ℓ_own)/(1 − ℓ_oth) = (ℓ_own − ℓ_oth)/(1 − ℓ_oth)**,

which for the expected 3–6pt level gap at ≈50% accuracy is **0.06–0.12** — one fifth of the threshold and below the 0.2 lower-bound floor. ℓ_own and ℓ_oth are measured directly (row 3), so A_h is a reported number, not an assumption. Around that arithmetic the correction is bracketed:
- **Under-correcting end (best-of-w, row 14).** Best-of-w raises accuracy by resolving games lost to sampling noise and attenuates prompt sensitivity, so at matched level it leaves the type-specific-fixable games unsolved: it corrects too little and flatters the hypothesis. It gives A_gen.
- **Over-correcting end (Ladder T, row 4).** I′(r) raises accuracy by learning type-specific procedure from episodes of the same types, exactly the channel the treatment uses — but from a **disjoint** pool. It therefore also absorbs, and corrects too much. It gives **A_epi = 1 − Inner(I_own)/Înner′(ℓ_own)**, the episode-specific share, where Înner′ is interpolated between the two bracketing ladder levels.
- **The confirmatory reading is taken at the conservative end**: the claim requires A ≥ 0.5 with lower bound ≥ 0.2 *and* A − A_h ≥ 0.30, and the sensitivity table (correction term scaled by 0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.25, 1.5) is published with the primary so a reader can see exactly how much of A survives a correction that is 25% or 50% wrong in either direction.

**The decomposition that tests the title.** (1 − A) = (1 − A_epi)(1 − A_type) with A_type = 1 − Înner′(ℓ_own)/Inner(I_oth). Pre-registered reading, both branches published: **A_epi ≥ 0.2** → absorption is episode-specific and "the same training episodes in two containers" stands. **A_epi ≈ 0 with A_type ≥ 0.4** → absorption is at task-type granularity: an optimizer that has seen *any* episodes of the type already holds the bank's type-specific value, the title changes to say so, and the practical conclusion (a bank distilled from the same task types is redundant with an optimized instruction) is unchanged and arguably stronger. Neither branch is a failure and neither is chosen after seeing the data.

**The mechanism rules (pre-registered, three buckets each).** Inner decomposes against row 3 into help(I) = acc(I×B_own) − acc(I×M0) and off(I) = acc(I×B_oth) − acc(I×M0), so ΔInner = [help(I_oth) − help(I_own)] + [off(I_own) − off(I_oth)]. **Rule M:** absorption is claimed only if the help term carries ≥ 2/3 of ΔInner; if the off term carries ≥ 2/3 the reading is **distractor-robustness** — the optimized instruction is simply more resistant to irrelevant injected text — and row 8 confirms or refutes it independently through harm(I) = acc(I×M0) − acc(I×X_dist); otherwise mixed, and both components are reported. **Rule F (fix/break):** fix deficit at equal breaks → absorption; break excess → interference; **break deficit → distractor-robustness**; 2/3 shares decide, otherwise mixed. **Rule C (content):** the item-level dose-response of row 7 — [help_hi(I_oth) − help_hi(I_own)] > [help_lo(I_oth) − help_lo(I_own)] — is the only signature that content transfer predicts and that neither headroom nor distractor-robustness does, since both of those are blind to which items the bank holds.

## Baselines and Ablations

**Baselines that could plausibly win and would refute us.**
(i) **A's 90% lower bound falls below 0.2 while the raw retention is large** — memory's value at the optimized instruction shrank through headroom and generic compression, and no episode-specific content moved between containers. The "un-optimized prompt" framing is withdrawn and the paper reports the relevance advantage as a function of instruction provenance, in an environment where memory works, as its result.
(ii) **The on-policy bank recovers the gain (row 6): g(I_own, B_own^on) − g(I_own, B_own) ≥ 3pt** — the shrinkage was bank staleness. The headline becomes "memory banks must be re-distilled under the deployed policy", which is useful and different, and it is a genuine risk rather than a formality: this program's own proposal ① carried the same off-policy confound.
(iii) **G3 fails, or Inner(I_oth) on S_test is under 5pt (G6)** — the bank's value in ALFWorld is task-type-generic, so there is no type-specific content to absorb. The paper reports the type-generic finding, the practitioner cells as bounded losses, and the zero-episode content check. This is a real, publishable contradiction of the retrieval-relevance premise in the ALFWorld memory literature.
(iv) **A_epi ≈ 0 with A_type large** — absorption is at task-type granularity, not episode granularity; the title changes, "same episodes, two containers" is reported as *not* the mechanism, and the practical conclusion survives. Pre-registered, not a save.
(v) **The off component carries ≥ 2/3 of ΔInner and harm(I_own) < harm(I_oth) (rows 3, 8)** — the optimized instruction is more robust to irrelevant injected text, and what looked like absorption is distractor-robustness. This is R1's and R3's alternative and it now has its own Tier-0 arm and its own rule.
(vi) **No item-level dose-response (row 7)** — the high-overlap and low-overlap halves of the bank lose the same amount at I_own. Whatever shrank Inner is not the content the overlap statistic identifies, and the content claim is downgraded to a behavioural one in print.
(vii) **g(I_all, B_all) has a 90% CI above 3pt (row 5)** — the bank still pays on top of the instruction a practitioner would deploy; build both, and the paper says so in the deployment configuration rather than in the square's half-space one.
(viii) **J_own×M0 ≥ I_own×B_own + MDE (row 12)** — the demonstration slot, not memory-versus-instruction, is the story: a jointly optimized static prompt reaches where the bank reaches. This is why J is Tier 0, and the demonstration-bearing I0^demo baseline attacks the same question from the baseline side.
(ix) **I_all×C_all − I_all×B_all has a 90% CI below −3pt (row 11)** — per-episode retrieval carries value no static compilation holds; the compile-once recommendation dies.
(x) **I_own(w\*)×M0 ≥ I_own×B_own, or I_own(1.5·L0)×M0 ≥ I_own×B_own (rows 14–15)** — spending memory's token or step budget on sampling or exploration buys what memory buys; the recommendation becomes "sample more", not "compile".
(xi) **I2_own×B_own exceeds max(I_own×B_own, I_own×M0) by more than the MDE (row 13)** — co-adaptation synergy: the coupling the track is named for is real and the substitution framing is incomplete.
(xii) **The graded planted control is not recovered monotonically (row 9)** — the instrument cannot see reductions of the size it is meant to measure, and A is not interpreted in either direction.
(xiii) **The null replicate's ratio (row 10) exceeds 0.25** — the endpoint's floor is at the claim's scale; the study reports the floor, raises n from the reserve or raises the threshold before unblinding, and declares the primary inconclusive if neither is available.
(xiv) **G1 passes only under bare I0 and fails under I0^demo** — the published ALFWorld memory effect depends on an empty demonstration slot. That is a finding about the memory literature's evaluation template and is reported as the headline if it happens.

**Ablations that isolate the mechanism.**
- **The square (row 2)** — the identification itself: Inner at two instructions produced by the same optimizer at the same rollout budget on equally sized pools, differing only in the provenance of the episodes. No "different intervention kinds" objection applies.
- **Both directions** — bank relevance is estimated as a main effect at I0\* (row 1) and absorption as the interaction, which is what makes a type-based split legitimate where v4.4 refused it.
- **Ladder T (row 4)** — a targeted, single-knob, five-level control built from episode-disjoint but type-matched episodes: simultaneously the conservative headroom correction, the A_epi/A_type decomposition and the answer to "did the instruction absorb *these* episodes or just learn this task type".
- **Best-of-w and the step budget (rows 14–15)** — compute-matched controls, and the optimistic end of the correction bracket; explicitly *not* the level control any more.
- **On-policy bank (row 6)** — the only arm that separates absorption from staleness; named as the missing baseline by two of three reviewers in the previous round.
- **X_dist at three instructions (row 8)** — separates content absorption from distractor-robustness, with a pre-registered decomposition rule instead of a post-hoc reading.
- **Item overlap and the hi/lo sub-banks (row 7 and the zero-episode statistic)** — the direct content check: is the content the bank encodes the content the instruction contains, and does the bank's advantage shrink *in proportion to that overlap*?
- **Graded planted control (row 9)** — instrument calibration at 2–6pt, the scale being measured, not a 15–30pt sledgehammer.
- **Null replicates (row 10, plus the ladder-level null)** — the endpoint's own floor, measured rather than assumed, on the ratio itself.
- **I_all, C_all (rows 5, 11)** — the practitioner's configuration, at power, so no deployment claim is made in a condition never measured.
- **J (row 12) and D_opt, I_type (Tier 1)** — the prompt container's optimized ceiling, the static-demonstration container, and what the task-type *label* alone buys without any episodes.
- **I2 (row 13)** — the track's named coupling: an instruction optimized in the presence of the bank, and its dependence when the bank is removed.
- **Screening over agent × bank format, published either way** — the claim's relativity is measured and the memory-weak pairing is a co-equal result.

## Falsifiable Predictions

1. **Absorption (confirmatory).** A = 1 − Inner(I_own)/Inner(I_oth) ≥ 0.5, with a one-sided 90% cluster-bootstrap lower bound ≥ 0.2 and a point estimate above the 95th percentile of the pooled null. The achieved SE(A) and the realized power are reported; if SE(A) exceeds 0.25 the prediction is downgraded to directional before unblinding.
2. **Relevance and calibration.** On S_test, Inner(I0\*) ≥ 8pt (G3 re-confirmed at test) and Inner(I_oth) ≥ 5pt (G6); the graded planted control reduces the relevance advantage monotonically, with the 50% condition producing roughly twice the reduction of the 25% condition within its CI. Both must hold for A to be interpreted.
3. **Headroom is not the explanation.** The measured headroom floor A_h = (ℓ_own − ℓ_oth)/(1 − ℓ_oth) is ≤ 0.15, and A − A_h ≥ 0.30; the claim survives the conservative (Ladder T) end of the correction bracket and every row of the ±25%/±50% sensitivity table.
4. **Episode versus type (the title's claim, tested).** A_epi = 1 − Inner(I_own)/Înner′(ℓ_own) ≥ 0.2 — the instruction built from *these* episodes holds more of the bank's advantage than a same-type instruction built from other episodes and titrated to the same accuracy. If instead A_epi's CI covers zero while A_type ≥ 0.4, the pre-registered reading is task-type-granular absorption and the title changes; this is a decision rule, not an escape hatch.
5. **Staleness is not the explanation.** g(I_own, B_own^on) − g(I_own, B_own) has a 90% CI whose upper bound is below 3pt: re-distilling the bank under the optimized instruction does not restore what the instruction took.
6. **The practitioner's numbers, in the practitioner's configuration.** The 90% CI of g(I_all, B_all) lies below 3pt, against a gated ≥ 5pt gain for the same bank family at the hand-written scaffold; and the 90% CI of I_all×C_all − I_all×B_all lies above −3pt, so a block compiled once replaces per-episode retrieval at matched tokens.
7. **Mechanism.** The help component carries ≥ 2/3 of ΔInner (Rule M); harm(I_own) − harm(I_oth) ≥ −2pt, so distractor-robustness cannot account for the shrinkage; and the high-overlap sub-bank loses more of its advantage between I_oth and I_own than the low-overlap sub-bank does (Rule C).
8. **The container ceiling and the coupling.** J_own×M0 lies within ±3pt of I_own×B_own — the jointly optimized prompt reaches where the bank reaches — while I2_own×B_own ≤ max(I_own×B_own, I_own×M0) + MDE and I2_own×M0 < I_own×M0 by ≥ 2pt: an instruction optimized with memory present depends on it.

**Refutation.** Outcome (i) — A's lower bound below 0.2 with a large raw retention — is the most likely way the hypothesis dies and is reported as the primary result. Outcome (ii) — the on-policy bank recovering the gain — kills the claim and replaces it with a staleness finding. Outcome (v) — the off component and X_dist carrying the shrinkage — replaces it with a distractor-robustness finding, which is a real and publishable statement about what optimized instructions do. Outcome (viii) — J matching the memory cell — moves the headline from memory to the demonstration slot. Outcome (iii) — no type-specific content to absorb — contradicts the relevance premise of the ALFWorld memory literature and is reported as that. Predictions 6 and 8 changing the recommendation do not touch the claim.

## Measurement and Noise Control

Three variance sources are handled separately, with components taken from this program's own measurements where they exist and measured on dev where they do not.

**(a) Evaluation variance.** Every S_test game runs under every cell with the same seed schedule; ALFWorld games are deterministic given the game file, so the only stochasticity is decoding, and all comparisons are within-game. Pairwise superiority tests are exact McNemar on fix/break counts, Holm-corrected within pre-declared families: **F-A** {A — the confirmatory family, one test}; **F-B** {Inner(I0\*); the graded control's two levels}; **F-C** {on-policy; practitioner gain; compile-once}; **F-D** {Ladder T, the compute controls, the coupling arm, J, the item-ablation contrast, the distractor contrasts and every Tier-1 control}. The primary A, a ratio of contrasts, is tested by cluster bootstrap over games **and** optimizer runs (10k resamples), never by a normal approximation, with the delta method reported alongside for transparency. Every delta carries its fix/break decomposition and, because this is ALFWorld, its **timeout and mean-step decomposition**: proposal ①'s 2026-09-04 finding was that a memory arm can look different only because it changes how often the agent fails to commit, and the ALFWorld analogue is a change in timeout rate. Any cell whose accuracy delta is more than half accounted for by a timeout-rate change is flagged and reported as such. Bounded-loss statements use 90% game-level bootstrap CIs; absence of significance is never reported as equivalence.

**(b) Optimizer variance.** Two GEPA seeds per type set on the square and on the derived M0/practitioner/compile/J cells, one on Ladder T and I2. The program measured a between-seed spread of optimized-instruction accuracy of 1.0–1.6pt (n = 500, two GEPA and two MIPROv2 runs) and a treatment-free decoding-seed spread of 2.8–3.4pt (three seeds, n = 500) in E1; these are the planning priors, and the corresponding ALFWorld quantities are measured on S_dev2 before S_test is touched and replace them if they differ. Because Inner is a *difference of two conditions at the same instruction*, the optimizer run affects both terms alike and its contribution to SE(Inner) is ≈0.8pt per run, not 1.6pt. Cell comparisons use a mixed-effects logistic model with game and optimizer run as crossed random effects; A is reported per seed and per direction, never as one number only, and the direction × type-set interaction is fitted rather than assumed away.

**(c) Null replicates and the null floor.** Three, all pre-registered. (1) Row 1 re-run at seed 37 gives the treatment-free accuracy floor. (2) **Row 10 recomputes Inner at one instruction across two decoding seeds and forms A from it** — a treatment-free replicate of the *ratio*, whose true value is zero. (3) A **ladder-level null**: A computed between two adjacent Ladder-T levels with those points excluded from the fit, whose true value is the computed headroom floor A_h and nothing more — this exercises the whole estimation pipeline including the correction. No effect is claimed unless the point estimate exceeds the 95th percentile of the pooled null.

**Minimum detectable effect (stated before running).** Inner is a paired difference between two memory conditions at one instruction; with a planning discordance rate of 0.20 (the fraction of games whose outcome differs between on-type and off-type banks; the achieved value is reported) its instance SE at n = 1,200 is √(0.20/1200) = 1.29pt, and with the optimizer component (0.8pt per run, two runs) SE(Inner) ≈ **1.41pt**. For A = 1 − X/Y the delta method with a game-level correlation of 0.3 between X and Y gives, at A = 0.5:

| Inner(I_oth) | SE(A) | 90% LB at Â = 0.5 | power for LB ≥ 0.2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10pt | 0.14 | 0.32 | ≈80% |
| 8pt | 0.17 | 0.28 | ≈70% |
| 6pt | 0.23 | 0.21 | ≈50% |

**This table is the consistency argument the last round demanded, and it is what sets the gates.** G3 requires ≥ 9pt on dev; applying the measured compression factor (1 − ℓ_oth)/(1 − ℓ0) ≈ 0.91 for a 5pt level gain from I0\* to I_oth projects ≈8pt at test, i.e. the ≈70% row; G6 stops the study at 5pt, where the test provably cannot conclude. A gate whose point requirement equals the true effect passes half the time by construction, so G3 at 9pt against a literature prior of 5–15pt total memory effect — and a partition chosen to maximize between-set procedural distance — is a deliberate coin-flip with a publishable branch on the other side, plus the single pre-declared reserve extension in the [6, 9) window. *Secondary quantities:* Inner at a single instruction has SE ≈ 1.41pt (MDE ≈ 3.5pt); bounded-loss statements at n = 800–1,200 have 90% CI half-widths of ≈1.7–2.3pt, which is why predictions 5 and 6 use 3pt bounds. *Null floor on the ratio:* the treatment-free replicate's A_null has SD ≈ (1 − ρ_dec)-scaled and is expected at 0.12–0.21 at n = 600 with Inner ≈ 8pt; it is dominated by the *denominator's* noise, which is the same reason G3/G6 protect the endpoint. If the measured null floor's 95th percentile exceeds 0.25, n is raised from the balanced reserve or the threshold is raised — decided and logged **before** unblinding, per the house rules. Sub-MDE differences are declared inconclusive in advance, in either direction.

**Two honest caveats about the numbers above.** First, every variance component is transferred from E1; ALFWorld's discordance rate, run-to-run spread and timeout behaviour are unmeasured on this node. The dev stage measures all three, the MDE and the compute plan are recomputed and republished before S_test is touched. Second, the 0.20 discordance assumption drives SE(Inner); at 0.30 it rises to 1.66pt and every row of the table above shifts one line down, which is exactly why the endpoint is a ratio (whose precision depends mostly on the denominator the gate controls) rather than an absolute difference (whose threshold the last round showed to be unreachable).

**Instrumentation per episode.** Task type, game ID, type set, instruction hash with its provenance tag and rollout budget, bank ID with its provenance tag, injected item IDs and token count, seed, steps taken, success, timeout, invalid-action count, and the full action trace. Optimizer runs log per-rollout game IDs (coverage), candidate lineage and reflector inputs. One script produces every table in the paper from the JSONL logs.

## Preprint Collision Check

**Method.** Fresh scan on 2026-09-03 (prior scan 2026-08-21): 15 HuggingFace Papers queries (244 unique papers, 44 dated ≥ 2026-06), by-id abstract verification for 54 arXiv IDs, and Claude server-side web search for targeted collision queries and for IDs not indexed on HuggingFace. Every ID cited in this proposal appears in `reference/litscan_2026-09-03.md` with title, date, verification channel and role; **no ID has been added in v5 or v5.1 that is not in that table**. Semantic Scholar was reachable but rate-limited without a key; arXiv is unreachable from this node, so arXiv pages were confirmed through web-search results only.

**What is conceded, plainly.** Two things that reviewers have named three rounds running are true and are stated in Related Work's first paragraph rather than buried. (a) **EvoAgentBench (arXiv 2607.05202) publishes the qualitative headline.** Its Table 3 puts ReasoningBank (+3.6) and GEPA (+1.2) on the same 528/267 trajectories on Qwen3.5-27B; "an optimizer given the same episodes gets roughly what the bank gets" is therefore in the literature and we cite it as motivation. (b) **ExpeL (arXiv 2308.10144) already makes "memory as an instruction" a design pattern**: its insights are distilled from training episodes and prepended to the prompt as a natural-language block, so the two containers this proposal compares are not architecturally distinct in the closest target system. **The contribution is therefore the identification and the number, not the idea**: provenance labelling of both containers, the episode-disjoint same-type instruction that separates episode-level from type-level absorption, the item-level overlap dose-response, an absorbed share with a confidence interval, and a null replicate of that ratio. A reviewer who thinks identification-and-quantification on top of a published qualitative result is not worth a main-track slot has a defensible position, and this proposal does not argue otherwise.

**Closest work: EvoAgentBench — read from the PDF.** Vanilla, +Memento, +ReasoningBank, +GEPA and a curated Anchor† reference on the same trajectories, two scaffolds, three backbones, four domains, results as mean ± SE over three runs. Confirmed absent from the paper: any combined optimizer-plus-memory condition (GEPA is "the only no-retrieval evolution condition"), any token matching (the word does not occur), any paired test or bootstrap, any compiled-bank arm, and any provenance manipulation — all methods draw on one undifferentiated training pool. What remains ours: the provenance square and the interaction it identifies, the episode-disjoint control, the on-policy bank, the item-level content check, the combined cells, token-matched compiled and jointly optimized arms, paired statistics with a null replicate of the endpoint, and the ALFWorld setting.

**Older framing collision: MIPROv2 (arXiv 2406.11695).** Instruction-only versus demonstration-only versus joint optimization on one training set is the two-container question for static bootstrapped demonstrations, and its finding that the split is task-dependent pre-empts the qualitative answer. It does not retrieve, distill, manipulate provenance, token-match, or report a residual. It is cited as the prior on the retention quantity, and its joint variant is run here as row 12 precisely so the headline cannot be quietly about the demonstration slot.

**Nearest measurement object: Experience Distillation (arXiv 2607.21051).** "Retains ≥ 64.8% of the in-context gain" is our absorbed share with weights as the second container; it is also the precedent for reporting this quantity as a ratio rather than a difference, which is the change v5.1 makes to its endpoint.

**Compile-once is partially pre-empted in direction** by arXiv 2608.14036 (skills beat Workflow Memory by 6.06pt matched; 65.7% procedural anchoring), arXiv 2606.30775 (a single rewrite matches ten rounds) and arXiv 2605.23899 (skills help on average, hurt 25%). None compares a compiled artifact against live per-episode retrieval from the same bank at matched tokens on top of an optimized instruction. Prediction 6 is therefore a quantified bound, not a surprise.

**ALFWorld-specific priors are cited, not competed with:** MemHarness (arXiv 2607.28272), Memp (arXiv 2508.06433), the ALFWorld procedural-retrieval study (arXiv 2511.21730), "When Continual Learning Moves to Memory" (arXiv 2604.27003) and "Harness the Memory" (arXiv 2608.15008) establish that memory effects there are 5–15 points and that excessive retrieval harms sequential decision-making. None manipulates what the instruction was optimized on, because none optimizes an instruction.

**Correction to v2's check.** ActMem (2603.00026), MAS-PromptBench (2606.23664), TERMS-Bench (2605.13909), MemAPO (2603.21520) and StructMemEval (2602.11243) were wrongly retracted in v2; all five exist and are reinstated with their actual roles. We claim no novelty for that primitive; only for its use here as the identification of container substitution, and if the search finds it already applied to optimizers and memory banks the contribution is reduced in print to the ALFWorld measurement, the on-policy control, the item-level content check and the compiled-block comparison, and the paper says so in its introduction. ## Risk Factors and Limitations

1. **The gates are the study's main risk, and each has a published branch.** G1 (memory must gain ≥ 5pt at the hand-written scaffold, now tested under a demonstration-bearing variant too) restates the ALFWorld literature's own claim with other agents and scaffolds. G3 (on-type content must beat off-type content by ≥ 9pt) is the gate the ratio's denominator needs and is deliberately a coin flip at a true 9pt effect; a failure yields the type-generic finding, **not** a fallback to the level-ladder endpoint that four rounds of reviewers rejected. G3b (the optimizer must learn something type-specific) is new and can stop the square before S_test. G2 (the optimizer must beat I0\*) is at real risk with a local 32B reflector. G6 stops the primary at test if the denominator is too small to estimate a ratio.
2. **Weaker reflector, no frontier API (refusal by constraint).** There is no API key on this node, so every model role is local and the optimized instruction is a lower bound on what the instruction container can hold. This biases **against** the hypothesis — a stronger reflector would absorb more, raising A — and it also shrinks the level gap and hence the headroom bias. The coin-flip result (arXiv 2604.14585) makes G2's branch live. Pre-registered amendment if a key appears before the freeze: only the reflection calls change (≈4,000 calls); the grid does not.
3. **The headroom correction is now an arithmetic bound plus a bracket, but it is not eliminated.** Inside each Inner, headroom is identical by construction. Across the two instructions the residual is the compression ratio over a 3–6pt level gap, ≈0.06–0.12 in A units under the multiplicative model — a model, not a law. It is bounded from below by best-of-w (which under-corrects) and from above by Ladder T (which over-corrects because a same-type instruction also absorbs), the claim is read at the conservative end, and a ±25%/±50% sensitivity table is published. If Ladder T cannot bracket ℓ_own even at 750 rollouts, this is declared before S_test and the confirmatory reading falls back to A_h with the best-of-w bracket as its upper bound.
4. **Ladder T is conservative by construction, and that is a real cost.** I′ learns the same task types from different episodes, so A_epi understates episode-specific absorption by however much I′ itself absorbs. The consequence is stated in advance: a null A_epi does not prove that absorption is type-level, it bounds the episode-specific component from below, and the pre-registered title change reflects that bound rather than a proof.
5. **Type sets are not exchangeable, by design, and that has a cost.** T_A and T_B differ in difficulty and in how much procedure they require, so the two directions are not statistical replicates; they are reported separately with the direction interaction fitted, and a second partition is a Tier-2 robustness check rather than a promise. The partition rule (minimum cross-set retrieval overlap over the ten admissible splits) is committed before any episode, so the choice is not a forking path — but it is a retriever-based proxy for procedural similarity, not a measurement of transfer.
6. **Approximate type counts.** Game counts per task type were inferred from task-directory proportions in the feasibility report; T_B binds every allocation and its reserve is thin (≈116 games per set). The exact recount happens at freeze, surplus goes to S_dev2 first, and a shortfall reduces S_test symmetrically with a republished MDE.
7. **Statistical reach.** SE(A) ranges from 0.14 to 0.27 as Inner(I_oth) falls from 10pt to 5pt; power for the 0.5-against-0.2 test is 80/70/50% at 10/8/6pt. These rest on a planning discordance rate of 0.20 and on E1-measured run-to-run spread transferring to ALFWorld. Both are measured on dev first, the downgrade rule in prediction 1 applies, and an inconclusive primary is reported as inconclusive.
8. **The null floor of a ratio is wider than the null floor of a difference.** A_null is dominated by the denominator's noise; at Inner ≈ 8pt and n = 600 its 95th percentile is expected at ≈0.21, uncomfortably close to the 0.2 lower-bound requirement. This is measured (row 10), not assumed, and the pre-registered response — raise n from the reserve or raise the threshold, before unblinding — is the honest one; the alternative, quietly comparing against a nominal CI only, is what the house rules forbid.
9. **Off-policy residue.** The on-policy bank is built under one instruction seed only, so it controls the first-order staleness confound but not seed-level variation in it; the arm is descriptive of magnitude, not of variance.
10. **The content check is a proxy at both ends.** Textual overlap between a bank item and an instruction is not proof of content transfer, and the hi/lo split is computed from a held-out optimizer seed's instruction, which decouples selection from evaluation but assumes the two seeds' instructions overlap the bank similarly. A null dose-response downgrades the content claim to a behavioural one; a positive one is evidence, not proof.
11. **Templated goals (no human annotations available on this node).** Goal text is ALFWorld's default template, which makes the environment more repetitive than the human-annotated variant. This helps both containers, so it is not obviously biased for or against absorption, but it limits external validity to human-phrased goals.
12. **Compilation is lossy and compiler-dependent.** C_all depends on a prompt fixed on dev and reported verbatim; it is treated as a lower bound, with the Tier-1 whole-bank arm bounding from the other side.
13. **Agent and bank specificity.** Two dense 27–32B agents of one family carry the confirmatory result, with a cross-family (medgemma-27b-it) check at Tier 1 and an out-of-scope (gpt-oss-120b) check at Tier 2. Nothing is pooled across pairings; the memory-weak pairing is a co-equal result.
14. **Descoped: the E1 replication leaves Tier 0.** With a total memory effect of ≈2.4pt there, the ratio's denominator would be ≈1–2pt and SE(A) > 0.6, so E1 cannot bear the endpoint even as a bound. It is Tier 1, reported as a direct-difference bound with CIs, never as the ratio and never as replication failure; unrun means unrun.
15. **Descoped: the third optimizer seed.** SE(Inner) moves from 1.41 to 1.37pt for a 50% cost increase on the four largest cells; the seed is bought back first if G5 clears with margin.
16. **Refused: making a level-matched information-free instruction the primary identifying instrument.** Four rounds rejected it, and the fifth round showed the best-of-w version is not information-free with respect to the correction it makes. It survives only as one end of a bracket, over an interval whose bracketing is verified on dev.
17. **Refused: dropping the sharp claim for a measurement study.** The reviewers' aggregate advice would be satisfied by reporting the relevance advantage as a function of provenance and declining to name a mechanism. That would remove the only sentence a reviewer could disagree with. The claim, the ratio threshold and the pre-registered refutation routes stay.
18. **Refused: test-time self-editing memory.** Absorption requires that both containers see identical episodes and that test games stay paired across cells; a bank that rewrites itself during evaluation breaks both. ReasoningBank's item format is adopted with its update step frozen after training; named as future work.
19. **Refused: full reproduction of a published memory result.** The targets' scaffolds, agents and training are not reproducible here. Instead the official `valid_seen` and `valid_unseen` splits run at Tier 1 with pooled artifacts, so our raw-memory-versus-no-memory numbers land on the same axis as the published ALFWorld numbers; the comparison is labelled qualitative and no claim is made to have re-measured anyone's system.
20. **Refused: a three-set Latin square, and refused: re-partitioning after a gate failure.** A finer partition would cut n per cell below 400 and push SE(A) past 0.3. Re-partitioning after seeing G3 would be a forking path; the partition is fixed by a zero-episode criterion committed in advance, and a G3 failure is reported as a finding.
21. **Novelty of the design primitive is unverified, and the obligation is now freeze-blocking.** The provenance-crossover primitive has never been searched for from this node; obligation (1) is run by the PI before DESIGN.md is hashed, and the contribution is reduced in print if it lands. The unopened appendix of arXiv 2608.14036 remains a live risk to prediction 6.
22. **Infrastructure.** Two A100-80GB; non-persistent root filesystem with everything on NFS; ALFWorld game data materialised from the HuggingFace datasets-server with SHA256 recorded (the release-asset host is firewalled, and the fetcher pins the one reachable CloudFront IP); runs resumable and append-only; per-game Inform7 compilation at reset adds environment overhead that batching absorbs but that is charged to the G5 throughput gate, now stated in calls/hour.
23. **Scope.** Procedural experience memory in an environment with recurring task templates, on 27–32B open-weight agents, with every model role local. Nothing is claimed about episodic personal memory, long-conversation memory, frontier-scale agents, or agents whose tasks do not recur.
