## Name

memory_or_instruction

## Title

Is Your Agent's Memory Just an Un-Optimized Prompt? Measuring How Much of Retrieved-Experience Value an Instruction Optimized on the Same Episodes Absorbs Beyond Headroom

## Short Hypothesis

Published agentic-memory gains are measured at a hand-written instruction. For procedural experience memory (ExpeL/ReasoningBank-style banks retrieved per query), we hypothesize that most of that gain is *absorbed* by an instruction optimized on the same training episodes — absorbed beyond headroom, i.e. beyond what an accuracy-matched instruction that contains no training-episode content also takes away. Everything else is mechanism or scope: whether the absorbed part is episode-specific or generic (a provenance crossover), whether it is absorption or interference (the fix/break signature), and whether a block compiled once from the bank replaces retrieval on top of the optimized instruction. The claim is agent-, bank- and environment-relative and is tested inside the track's model class after a pre-registered, split-sample screening over two open-weight 27–32B agents, two bank formats and two environments; the pairings where memory has nothing to absorb are reported as co-equal results.

## Related Work

**(1) Procedural experience memory — the targets.** ExpeL (arXiv 2308.10144) gathers training-task experiences, extracts natural-language insights, and recalls them at inference. ReasoningBank (arXiv 2509.25140) distills strategies from self-judged successes and failures. Memp (arXiv 2508.06433), A-MEM (arXiv 2502.12110) and retrieval-augmented agents trained to use retrieved trajectories (arXiv 2603.18272) share the evaluation template: the memory-vs-no-memory delta is measured at a fixed, hand-written scaffold, or memory is compared against fine-tuning. The template assumes memory and instruction are non-substitutable containers.

**(2) Reflective prompt optimizers.** GEPA (arXiv 2507.19457, ICLR 2026 oral) samples trajectories, reflects in natural language, and proposes prompt updates, outperforming MIPROv2 by >10% and GRPO by ~10% with up to 35× fewer rollouts. GEPA and ExpeL run the same learning principle — reflect on trajectories, distill lessons — and differ in where the lesson is stored. The older and closer framing precedent is MIPROv2 (arXiv 2406.11695), which factorizes a prompt into instructions and bootstrapped demonstrations and optimizes both on the same training set; its instruction-only versus demonstration-only ablations are the nearest published prior on how much of a training set's value each container can carry, with the recurring finding that the split is task-dependent. A retrieved experience bank is a demonstration container with a query-conditioned read; our study is that ablation with retrieval, distillation and a held-out-episode crossover added. "Prompt Optimization Is a Coin Flip" (arXiv 2604.14585) shows that in compound systems 49% of optimizer runs land below zero-shot and that gains require exploitable output structure; this is the prerequisite risk for any optimizer-as-control design and we treat I1 > I0 as a gated result, not an assumption.

**(3) Three-way comparisons on shared trajectories — the closest work.** EvoAgentBench (arXiv 2607.05202) evaluates Vanilla, Memento (retrieve nearest retained case), ReasoningBank (retrieve distilled memory), GEPA (evolve one prompt) and a curated reference on a shared 528/267 train/test split across four domains and three backbones, and reports (Table 3, mean ± SE over three runs) that on Qwen3.5-27B ReasoningBank gains +3.6, GEPA +1.2 and Memento −2.4 over a 37.5 baseline, that GEPA is the strongest automatic method on Gemma-4-31B (+5.7), and that no automatic method is positive in every cell. GEPA is described there as 'the only no-retrieval evolution condition'. It is the first published table with a retriever and GEPA on the same training trajectories. Verified from its PDF: it contains no combined optimizer-plus-memory condition, no token matching, no compiled-bank arm and no paired statistics, so it cannot say how much of memory's value survives once the instruction container is filled — which is our object. EvoClinician (arXiv 2601.22964) lists "prompt optimization agent" and "memory agent" as baseline categories in a clinical application (excluded domain here). TERMS-Bench (arXiv 2605.13909) uses GEPA as a prompt-ablation to test whether prompt engineering saturates a negotiation benchmark — the logical move "run an optimizer as the control for a non-prompt claim", in a different domain and without memory.

**(4) The confound-audit literature.** MemDelta (arXiv 2606.29914) shows headline memory gains flip under one-variable changes (embedding swap +6.2pp; Mem0 vs RAG reverses). "Diagnosing Retrieval vs. Utilization Bottlenecks" (arXiv 2603.02473) finds retrieval method spans 20 points while write strategy spans 3–8, and raw chunks match lossy writes. Memory-R2 (arXiv 2605.21768) fixes credit-assignment unfairness in RL-trained memory. EvoMemBench (arXiv 2605.18421) finds long-context baselines "remain highly competitive" against 15 memory methods. All audit confounds inside the memory pipeline or against context-dumping; none posits an optimized instruction as a rival container.

**(5) Memory harm.** "When Continual Learning Moves to Memory" (arXiv 2604.27003) documents retrieval pollution, context competition and dilution, with negative transfer concentrated on hard cases; MemHarness (arXiv 2607.28272) reports raw memory injection at 70.1% versus 76.4% for no-memory RL on ALFWorld. A pre-registered study in this program (proposal ①, 33 arms, ~29,500 episodes, 2026-08) found an ExpeL bank's paired effect TOST-equivalent to zero on Qwen3.5-27B (+0.6pt, 90% CI [−1.2, +2.5]; +2.4pt, p=.155, once completion mechanics were applied to both arms in a later exploratory re-test, because the bare memory arm had 27.6% answerless episodes against 16.2% without memory) and +7.7pt (p=.0018) on gpt-oss-120b, and that a one-line instruction dominated every memory manipulation on the strong agent. That result, together with EvoAgentBench's +3.6/+1.2 on the same 27B model, is why v4.4 screens agent × bank × environment pairings on a split dev sample before committing the primary grid, gates on the quantity the test needs, and reports the memory-null pairings as co-equal results; the pilot's design, gate log and results are an appendix of this proposal.

**(6) Containers other than the prompt.** Experience Distillation (arXiv 2607.21051) measures how much of an in-context-learning gain survives when the experience is moved into weights by context distillation (≥64.8%) versus SFT (3.8%) — the same "retention ratio" shape as S, with weights as the second container. TMEM (arXiv 2606.04536) stores experience in fast LoRA weights; experience-internalization work (arXiv 2606.04703) finds principle-level experience more durable than instance-level. These establish that the container question is live; none uses an optimized instruction as the container, and none needs gradient access to the served model, which S does not.

**(7) Compiled procedures.** Skills (distilled SKILL.md) beat Workflow Memory by 6.06pt in matched comparison, with 65.7% of the benefit attributable to procedural anchoring rather than fact injection, and retrieval precision collapsing from 29.6% to 3.3% as the pool grows (arXiv 2608.14036); model-generated skills help on average but hurt 25% of cases (arXiv 2605.23899); a single rewrite given the full training signal matches 10 rounds of refinement within 0.2% (arXiv 2606.30775); embedding retrieval of procedures degrades under vocabulary shift while LLM abstractions transfer (arXiv 2511.21730). These make "compile once" a plausible direction; none tests a compiled artifact against live per-instance retrieval at matched tokens with the same bank, which is what our compiled arm C does. MemAPO (arXiv 2603.21520) runs the coupling in the opposite direction (memory inside the optimizer) and MAS-PromptBench (arXiv 2606.23664) studies prompt optimization without memory.

**Our position.** A memory bank and an optimized instruction are two containers for the same training-episode information. MIPROv2 established the two-container ablation for static demonstrations; EvoAgentBench put a retriever and GEPA in one table; nobody has measured how much of the first container's value survives when the second is filled first, at matched information exposure and matched tokens, with the combined cell, a compiled-bank arm, a whole-bank arm, and a positive control that certifies the instrument can detect non-substitutable value.

**(8) Headroom and level-matched controls.** The observation that a treatment's marginal gain shrinks as the baseline improves is a ceiling effect, and the standard remedy is a level-matched control that raises the baseline without carrying the treatment's information. We build one from information-free instructions (an evidence-citation line, a decompose-and-verify line, extra search budget) and treat memory's gain along that family as the headroom curve; the confirmatory quantity is memory's gain at the optimized instruction relative to that curve at the same level.

## Abstract

Agentic memory systems are evaluated by comparing an agent with a retrieved-experience bank against the same agent without one, holding a hand-written scaffold prompt fixed. We ask how much of that gain survives once the instruction slot is filled by a prompt optimizer given the same training episodes — and whether what disappears is *information the instruction absorbed* or merely *headroom that any better prompt consumes*. The design separates the two. A family of information-free instructions (an evidence-citation line, a decompose-and-verify line, and extra search budget) traces the headroom curve: memory's gain as a function of no-memory accuracy when the instruction carries no episode content. The confirmatory endpoint, Δ_abs, is the gap between memory's gain on that curve at the optimized instruction's accuracy level and memory's actual gain at the optimized instruction, paired on the same test instances at full data. A provenance crossover — two disjoint training halves, each yielding a bank and an optimized instruction, compared own-bank against other-bank at a fixed instruction — then says whether the absorbed content is episode-specific or generic, with a planted half-specific control certifying the probe. Around the core: a token-matched compiled block on top of the optimized instruction, a shuffled-content bank, a compute-matched search-budget control, an instruction co-optimized with the bank, and null replicates. Agent, bank format and environment (tool-augmented multi-hop QA over fixed corpora, or ALFWorld with held-out training games as the test pool) are chosen by a split-sample screening whose gate is the quantity the test needs: memory must still add at the level-matched information-free instruction. Scope: n=1,500 paired test instances, 4 optimizer seeds at full data, ≈67,000 must-ship episodes, cluster bootstrap over instances and runs, and a minimum detectable effect derived from this program's measured seed variance.

## Experiments

**HELD FIXED THROUGHOUT.** ReAct scaffold; tool stack (BM25 search over each dataset's fixed corpus in E1; the ALFWorld action interface in E2); step limit (8 calls, +3 only in the budget-control arms); **completion mechanics** (one budget extension when no Finish was attempted, and a terminal forced-Finish — proposal ①'s A6/A8 — applied to every arm without exception, so no arm can end answerless and no instruction can gain from mechanics alone; the answerless rate is logged per cell); retriever (MiniLM dense, CPU); decoding temperature and seed schedule {11, 23, 37}; the training pool from which all learned artifacts derive; the held-out test split. Only the instruction slot and the memory-injection variant differ.

**SCREENING (pre-registered, split-sample, published either way).** Candidates: agents Qwen3.5-27B and Qwen3-32B (thinking disabled; one vLLM replica per A100); bank formats ExpeL-style (INSIGHT / PROCEDURE / OUTCOME from solved episodes) and ReasoningBank-style (self-judged success and failure distilled into strategy items); environments E1 and E2. *Stage S1 (selection, S_dev1 n=300):* for each agent×environment, run bare I0 on the 800-episode training pool (bank-gen), distill both formats into full-data banks B_ab, and measure g(I0,B_ab) = acc(I0×M_ab) − acc(I0×M0) for the eight agent×format×environment pairings. The two pairings with the largest g(I0,B_ab) that are ≥3pt (one per environment if both have one) proceed. *Stage S2 (confirmation, S_dev2 n=300, never used in S1):* for each proceeding pairing, run two GEPA seeds of I1_ab and the information-free family, and measure the gates below. The confirmatory pairing is the one that passes G1, G2 and G7′ with the larger G7′ margin. Everything measured in S1 and S2 is published; the losing pairings are reported as co-equal results in the paper (memory-null or memory-weak regimes). gpt-oss-120b (out of scope) and medgemma-27b-it (cross-family) are Tier-2 checks on the chosen cells only.

**ENVIRONMENTS.** *E1:* tool-augmented multi-hop QA over fixed corpora, pooled HotpotQA-distractor / MuSiQue / 2WikiMultihopQA (HippoRAG subsets; provenance and SHA256 committed); pool 3,000 questions. *E2:* ALFWorld; the test pool is 1,500 held-out *training* games (stratified by the six task types), the training halves are drawn from the remaining training games, and the official seen/unseen eval sets are reported as an external check only. E2 has ~25 calls per episode, so its grid runs the pre-registered reduced budget (crossover at 2 seeds, headroom family of two members). E2's feasibility on this node — the `alfworld` package and its game data — is a precondition checked before screening; if it cannot be installed, that is logged and E1 is the only candidate. Public data only.

**SPLITS (seeded, stratified, IDs pre-registered).** S_train_a 400, S_train_b 400 (disjoint halves of the 800-episode training pool; every learned artifact is tagged with its half or with "ab"), S_dev1 300, S_dev2 300, S_test 1,500 (touched once, by the final grid; the crossover cells use its first 1,000 IDs). Gold-document title overlap between the training pool and S_test removed and counted (E1). Reserve untouched except for gate-triggered rebuilds, logged.

**ARTIFACTS (built once, frozen, hashed).**
- **B_ab, B_a, B_b:** banks of the screened format from the full pool and from each half; same distiller prompt, dedup and retriever, k=7. Item counts reported.
- **B_a+proc10:** B_a plus, for a random pre-registered 10% of the crossover instances, one instance-specific *procedural* item written from that instance's gold documents (the disambiguation or decomposition step it needs) with the answer withheld and leakage-audited. Planted in B_a **only**, so it is half-specific by construction: the crossover's positive control.
- **B_a^re (Tier 1):** half-a episodes re-distilled by a different model (local Qwen3-32B), same template — provenance without wording redundancy.
- **B_touched(s) (Tier 1):** the subset of B_a whose source episodes GEPA run I1a(s) touched.
- **M_shuf:** the memory slot filled with k=7 items drawn at random from B_ab (retrieval bypassed), token-matched to M_ab.
- **I0:** the hand-written scaffold instruction. **Information-free family (no training-episode content, fixed before any optimizer runs, hashed):** **H1** = the one-line evidence-citation instruction from this program's prior study (verbatim); **H2** = "decompose the question into sub-questions, search each, and verify before finishing"; **I0+3** = I0 with the search budget raised from 8 to 11 calls, spending roughly memory's injected-token budget on search instead of context.
- **I1_ab(s):** GEPA-style reflective optimization of the instruction slot on the full 800-episode pool, seeded with I0 verbatim, 350 rollouts, memory absent from context, 4 seeds; coverage instrumented. **I1a(s), I1b(s):** the same on each half, 3 seeds. **I1_ab+3:** I1_ab with the 11-call budget (compute control at the optimized level).
- **I2_ab(s):** as I1_ab but with B_ab retrieved into context during optimization, 2 seeds.
- **C_ab:** the compiler reads all of B_ab offline and writes one static block whose length is fixed at the median per-instance injection length of M_ab on S_dev2; its no-memory comparator is padded to the same prompt length; compiler prompt fixed on S_dev2, reported verbatim.
- **Oracle (Tier 1):** for each test instance, the single bank item that most improves the outcome, chosen post hoc — a descriptive upper bound on retrieval quality.
- **D_opt / J (Tier 1):** an optimized static demonstration set and a MIPROv2-style joint instruction+demonstration prompt on the full pool, token-matched.

**GATES (S_dev2, n=300; each requires a point estimate and a lower 80% bootstrap CI; any fail → the pre-registered branch, never a silent continuation).** **G1** bank effect at I0: g(I0,B_ab) ≥ 4pt, lower CI ≥ 2pt, fixes ≥ 12. **G2** optimizer headroom: acc(I1_ab×M0) − acc(I0×M0) ≥ 2pt on ≥ 2 of 2 dev seeds. **G7′** memory survives a level-matched information-free instruction: with I_H* the family member whose acc(×M0) is closest to acc(I1_ab×M0) on S_dev2, g(I_H*,B_ab) ≥ 3pt, lower CI ≥ 1pt. **G3** retriever relevance ≥ 60% (100-item audit); **G4** stored-record truthfulness ≥ 70%; **G5** throughput ≥ 400 episodes/hour aggregate. Branches: G1 fails everywhere → memory-null paper (six TOST results, co-equal). G7′ fails but G1 passes → **outcome B**: memory's gain is what generic prompting gives; the paper's primary is the headroom curve and the ladder, the crossover runs at 2 seeds as a descriptive probe. G2 fails → optimizer-failure branch (I1 vs I0 reported as the prerequisite; the ladder and co-adaptation cells become the body).

**PRIMARY (CONFIRMATORY) ENDPOINT — Δ_abs, absorption beyond headroom.** For an instruction I and bank B, g(I,B) is the paired gain acc(I×M_B) − acc(I×M0) on S_test. The headroom curve ĝ_H(ℓ) is memory's gain as a function of no-memory accuracy ℓ across the information-free points {I0, H1, H2, I0+3} (linear interpolation between the two nearest points; if I1_ab's level lies outside the family's range, the nearest point is used and the extrapolation is reported as a limitation). Δ_abs = ĝ_H(ℓ₁) − g(I1_ab,B_ab), where ℓ₁ = acc(I1_ab×M0), computed per optimizer seed at the instance level and averaged; the pre-registered primary comparator is the single family member I_H* nearest ℓ₁, with the interpolated value as the secondary estimate. Test: one-sided Δ_abs > 0 at α=.05 by cluster bootstrap over instances and optimizer runs; claim threshold 2pt. **Mechanism (1b):** the fix/break decomposition of g(I1_ab,B_ab) against g(I_H*,B_ab): absorption predicts fewer fixes at equal breaks; interference predicts more breaks (classification rule: fix-deficit share ≥ 2/3 → absorption; break-excess share ≥ 2/3 → interference; else mixed). **Descriptive:** S_abs = 1 − g(I1_ab,B_ab)/ĝ_H(ℓ₁) with a delta-method CI; S_raw = 1 − g(I1_ab,B_ab)/g(I0,B_ab).

**SPECIFICITY PROBE — Δ_info (secondary confirmatory family).** At each half-instruction and seed, the instance-level contrast y(I×M_other) − y(I×M_own): y(I1a(s)×M_b) − y(I1a(s)×M_a) and y(I1b(s)×M_a) − y(I1b(s)×M_b), averaged over instances, seeds and directions, with the direction×half interaction fitted and reported. Interpretation, pre-registered: (i) bank divergence is reported first — the cross-half retrieval overlap (fraction of crossover instances whose top-7 from B_a and B_b are paraphrase pairs by embedding similarity ≥ 0.9, audited on 100 by a human) and item-level semantic overlap; (ii) the planted control must be recovered: on the 100 proc-targeted instances, own (B_a+proc10) beats other (B_b) at I1a by ≥ 15pt; (iii) then Δ_info ≥ 2pt (one-sided α=.05) → part of the absorbed content is episode-specific; |Δ_info| < 1pt with (ii) recovered → the absorbed content is generic, which is the expected outcome in E1 and is reported as such, not as a failure.

**SECONDARY ENDPOINTS.** (a) Full data: I1_ab×M_ab − I1_ab×M0, reported with its 90% CI (prediction 3). (b) Compile once: I1_ab×C_ab − I1_ab×M_ab with 90% CI (prediction 4); I0×C_ab vs I0×M_ab likewise. (c) Content vs format: g(I0,M_shuf), g(I1_ab,M_shuf) with CIs. (d) Coupling: I2_ab×M_ab vs max(I1_ab×M_ab, I1_ab×M0); I2_ab×M0 vs I1_ab×M0. (e) Compute: I1_ab+3×M0 vs I1_ab×M_ab — does search budget buy what memory buys at the optimized level? (f) Provenance vs wording (Tier 1): I1a×M_a^re vs I1a×M_a. (g) Coverage, bank format, oracle, D_opt/J, ladder: descriptive with CIs.

**MODERATOR ANALYSIS (reuses runs).** Per test instance: max dense similarity to any B_ab item plus an "instance-specific reusable content" label (100 cases human-validated, κ reported). The instance-level absorption contrast regressed on similarity with instance and run as crossed random effects.

## Baselines and Ablations

**Baselines that could plausibly win and would refute us.**
(i) **Δ_abs's CI covers zero while S_raw is large** — the drop in memory's gain at the optimized instruction is headroom: any accuracy-matched prompt takes the same amount away. The "un-optimized prompt" framing is withdrawn and the paper reports the headroom curve as its result.
(ii) **Δ_abs ≈ 0 and S_raw ≈ 0** — complementarity: memory and instruction optimization contribute independently.
(iii) **G7′ fails: memory adds nothing at a level-matched information-free instruction** — outcome B: memory's value is what generic prompting gives; the strongest form of the title, reported as the primary result.
(iv) **I1_ab×M_ab − I1_ab×M0 has a 90% CI above 2pt** — at full data the bank still adds to the best instruction; the practitioner should build both, and the paper says so.
(v) **I1_ab×C_ab − I1_ab×M_ab has a 90% CI below −2pt** — per-instance retrieval carries value no static compilation holds; the compile recommendation dies.
(vi) **g(I0,M_shuf) is within 1pt of g(I0,M_ab)** — the memory slot's value is format, not content; the entire two-container framing is moot for this pairing.
(vii) **I1_ab+3×M0 ≥ I1_ab×M_ab** — spending memory's budget on search buys at least what memory buys; the practical recommendation becomes "search more", not "compile".
(viii) **I2_ab×M_ab exceeds max(I1_ab×M_ab, I1_ab×M0) by more than the MDE** — co-adaptation synergy; the coupling the track is named for is real.
(ix) **Break-excess signature** — interference, not absorption; the headline becomes context competition.
(x) **Δ_info ≥ 2pt with the planted control recovered** — a real part of the absorbed content is episode-specific (this strengthens, not weakens, the substitution reading; it changes the mechanism section).
(xi) **The planted control is not recovered** — the crossover probe is insensitive; Δ_info is not interpreted.
(xii) **No in-scope pairing clears G1** — memory has nothing to absorb on 27–32B agents in either environment; the paper is the six-pairing report.

**Ablations that isolate the mechanism.**
- **The headroom curve (cell 2)** — the identification: memory's gain at accuracy-matched instructions that carry no episode content. Essential.
- **The provenance crossover with a planted half-specific control (cells 5, 6)** — episode-specific vs generic absorption; the control certifies the probe's sensitivity at the size that matters.
- **M_shuf** — content vs format at both levels.
- **I0+3 and I1_ab+3** — compute-matched search budget at both levels.
- **C_ab on the optimized instruction** — the deployment the paper would recommend, measured where it would be deployed.
- **I2_ab at full data** — the only arm that tests whether memory adds to an instruction that was allowed to see it.
- **Re-distilled bank, coverage-matched bank, oracle retrieval, D_opt/J (Tier 1)** — wording, exposure, retrieval quality and the demonstration container's optimized ceiling, each bounded rather than assumed.
- **Split-sample screening over agents, formats and environments** — relativity of the claim is measured and reported as co-equal results.

## Falsifiable Predictions

1. **Absorption beyond headroom (confirmatory).** Δ_abs ≥ 2pt, one-sided α=.05, by cluster bootstrap over instances and optimizer runs, in the confirmatory pairing; the achieved MDE is reported and, if it exceeds 3.5pt, the prediction is downgraded to directional before unblinding.
1b. **Mechanism.** The deficit is a fix deficit at equal breaks (absorption), not a break excess (interference).
2. **Specificity (secondary confirmatory).** The planted half-specific control is recovered (own beats other by ≥ 15pt on the targeted instances at I1a); bank divergence is reported; then Δ_info is within [−1, +1]pt in E1 — the absorbed content is generic — and ≥ 2pt if the confirmatory environment is E2, where task templates recur.
3. **Full data.** The 90% CI of I1_ab×M_ab − I1_ab×M0 lies below 2pt: the bank does not add materially to an instruction optimized on everything.
4. **Compile once.** The 90% CI of I1_ab×C_ab − I1_ab×M_ab lies above −2pt: a block compiled once replaces retrieval on top of the optimized instruction.
5. **Content, not format.** g(I0,M_shuf) is within ±1pt of zero while g(I0,M_ab) ≥ the gate.
6. **Headroom curve.** Memory's gain declines with no-memory level along {I0, H1, H2, I0+3}; the slope is reported and g(I_H*,B_ab) ≥ 3pt (G7′ re-confirmed on S_test).
7. **Coupling.** I2_ab×M_ab ≤ max(I1_ab×M_ab, I1_ab×M0) + MDE; I2_ab×M0 < I1_ab×M0 by ≥ 2pt (an instruction optimized with memory present depends on it).
8. **Compute.** I1_ab+3×M0 < I1_ab×M_ab − 1pt at I0's level the same contrast is reported; if the search budget matches memory, refutation (vii) applies.
9. **Screening and regimes.** At least one in-scope pairing clears G1, G2 and G7′ on S_dev2; the losing pairings' gains at I0 are within ±4pt (TOST) on S_test and are reported as co-equal results; the out-of-scope and cross-family checks have the same sign of Δ_abs.

**Refutation.** Outcome (i) — Δ_abs's CI covering zero with S_raw large — is the most likely way the hypothesis dies and is reported as the primary result: memory's apparent redundancy with prompt optimization is headroom. Outcome (iii) is the strongest form of the title and is also a primary result. A break-excess signature changes the headline to interference. Prediction 3 or 4 failing changes the recommendation, not the claim. If the planted control is not recovered, Δ_info is not interpreted. If no pairing clears G1, the paper is the memory-null report.

## Measurement and Noise Control

Three variance sources handled separately, per this program's house rules, with variance components taken from the program's own measurements rather than assumed.

**(a) Evaluation variance.** Every S_test instance runs under every cell with the same seed schedule; all comparisons are within-instance. Superiority tests are exact McNemar on fix/break counts (or their instance-level difference-of-differences analogue for Δ_abs and Δ_info), Holm-corrected within pre-declared families: F-A {Δ_abs: cell 3 vs cell 2 at I_H* — the confirmatory family}, F-B {specificity: cells 5, 6}, F-C {full data and compile: cells 3, 8, 9}, F-D {controls: cells 4, 7, 12–19}. Bounded-loss statements report 90% instance-level bootstrap CIs (10k resamples); no fixed-margin equivalence is claimed anywhere, and absence of significance is never reported as equivalence. Every delta carries its fix/break decomposition.

**(b) Optimizer variance.** 4 GEPA seeds for I1_ab, 3 per half, 2 for I2_ab. The program measured the between-seed spread of optimized-instruction accuracy at 1.0–1.6pt (n=500, two GEPA and two MIPROv2 runs) and the treatment-free decoding-seed spread at 2.8–3.4pt (three seeds, n=500). Cell comparisons use a mixed-effects logistic model with test instance and optimizer run as crossed random effects; CIs from a cluster bootstrap resampling runs as well as instances. Δ_abs and Δ_info are reported per seed, never as one number only.

**(c) Null replicates and the null floor.** Cells 1's pair is re-run at seed 37 (n=750); with the measured three-seed spread this defines the treatment-free |Δ| floor for level comparisons, expected ≈2pt at n=1,500. The crossover contrast has its own floor: the same contrast computed between two decoding seeds of the same bank, reported next to Δ_info. No effect is claimed unless it exceeds both its test threshold and the 95th percentile of the relevant floor.

**Minimum detectable effect (stated before running).** *Confirmatory (Δ_abs):* g(I1_ab,B_ab) averages four runs on 1,500 instances (SE ≈ 0.8pt including the ≤1.6pt between-run SD); g(I_H*,B_ab) uses two decoding seeds (SE ≈ 0.8pt); the two gains share instances, and their instance-level covariance is estimated from the runs (it can only reduce the SE). SE(Δ_abs) ≤ 1.15pt, one-sided 80%-power MDE ≤ 2.9pt; the claim threshold is 2pt and the achieved MDE is reported, with the downgrade rule in prediction 1. *Specificity (Δ_info):* 4 contrasts × 3 seeds on 1,000 shared instances; SE between 0.7pt (ρ=0) and 1.2pt (ρ=1) as in v4.3; MDE 1.8–3.1pt; the planted control (≈30pt on 100 instances → ≈3pt aggregate) sits above it. *Bounded-loss statements (predictions 3, 4):* SE ≈ 0.8–1.0pt at n=1,500, so a 90% CI half-width of ≈1.5pt; a true difference of 0 yields an upper bound below 2pt with probability ≈ 0.7, which is why these are reported as bounds with CIs, not tested. *Gates:* at n=300 the paired gain has SE ≈ 2.4pt; the lower-CI requirements mean a true 1pt effect passes G1 with probability ≈ 10% and G7′ with ≈ 20%; the split-sample design removes the selection inflation, and the S_test branch covers the remainder. Sub-MDE differences are inconclusive in either direction.

**Instrumentation per episode.** Tool calls, distinct documents, injected memory tokens and item IDs, instruction hash and half tag, seed, outcome; optimizer runs log per-rollout episode IDs (coverage), candidate lineage, and reflector inputs. A single script produces every table from the JSONL logs.

## Risk Factors and Limitations

1. **Screening failure (G1) and the level-matched gate (G7′).** If no in-scope pairing clears G1 on S_dev2, the bank is enriched once (larger k, relaxed dedup) and screening is re-measured; if it still fails, the study stops and reports six TOST results as co-equal findings. If G1 passes and G7′ fails, outcome B is the pre-registered primary result. Both are live branches: the field's own numbers on 27B agents are +0.6 to +3.6pt.
2. **Optimizer failure (G2).** GEPA-style optimization may not beat I0 (the coin-flip result). Branch pre-registered.
3. **Level matching is approximate.** The information-free family has three points; I1_ab's level may fall between them or outside the range. The interpolated and nearest-point estimates are both reported; extrapolation is a stated limitation; the moderator regression on level across all instructions is the sensitivity analysis.
4. **The information-free family is not information-free in the strict sense.** H2 ("decompose and verify") is the generic lesson a bank might also carry. That is the point: it bounds what generic prompting gives. If g(I_H*,B_ab) is small because H2 already says what the bank says, outcome B holds and is reported as such.
5. **Crossover exchangeability.** Random halves make B_a and B_b similar in content, so Δ_info is expected to be small in E1; it is a probe, not the claim, and its null is interpreted only with the planted control recovered and bank divergence reported.
6. **Half imbalance.** Reported from S_test (cells 5 own-vs-own comparisons) and absorbed by the two crossover directions through the fitted interaction.
7. **Lossy compilation.** C_ab depends on the compiler prompt; fixed on S_dev2, reported verbatim, treated as a lower bound; M_all_ab bounds it from the other side (Tier 1).
8. **Agent, bank and environment specificity.** Every quantity is reported per pairing and never pooled; two 27–32B dense agents of one family carry the confirmatory result, with a cross-family (medgemma-27b-it) and an out-of-scope (gpt-oss-120b) check as Tier 2. Generalization to tool/web agents with recurring subroutines is a stated limitation unless E2 wins screening.
9. **E2 feasibility.** ALFWorld must be installed on this node (package and game data reachable); if not, E1 is the only candidate and this is logged before screening.
10. **Statistical reach.** Δ_abs's MDE ≤ 2.9pt rests on the measured between-run spread transferring; the achieved MDE is reported and the downgrade rule applies. Bounded-loss statements can be inconclusive and are reported as such.
11. **Selection.** The confirmatory pairing is chosen on S_dev1 and gated on S_dev2; S_test is touched once. S_raw can still reflect the choice of a memory-favourable pairing and is descriptive; the other pairings are co-equal results.
12. **Positive-control artificiality.** The planted procedural items are synthetic; they certify sensitivity, not that natural half-specific content exists — the divergence statistic and the moderator analysis address the latter.
13. **No test-time self-editing (refused).** Absorption requires that both containers see identical episodes and that test instances stay paired; a bank that updates during evaluation breaks both. ReasoningBank's distillation format is adopted with its update step frozen after training; named as future work.
14. **No re-measurement of a published number (refused).** The targets' own settings cannot be reproduced on this node; the paper audits the evaluation template and states this.
15. **Verification of the closest work.** EvoAgentBench was read from a PDF supplied by the PI; the extracted text is committed with its SHA256 so a reader can check the quotations. The appendix of arXiv 2608.14036 and a targeted search for provenance crossovers in the prompt-optimization literature are on the pre-freeze re-scan list.
16. **Infrastructure.** 2×A100; non-persistent root filesystem; runs resumable and append-only; frontier key may be unavailable (local reflector fallback declared).
17. **Scope.** Procedural experience memory in agent benchmarks with recurring task structure; nothing is claimed about episodic personal memory or long-conversation memory.
