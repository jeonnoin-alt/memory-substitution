### proposal: whole_bank_confusability_ladder

### proposal: recall_success_dissociation
Title: Recall Ranks Retrievers, Success Does Not: A Two-Environment Audit of Experience Retrieval Against the Static Prompt with Manipulated Duplicate Availability
Short Hypothesis: Retriever recall (fraction of top-k items sharing the test task's type or binding) and the task-success residual over the best static prompt built from the same bank dissociate, and the dissociation is set by whether the retrieved binding is recoverable inside the episode. On ALFWorld the measured +17.2 net of BM25 k=3 over static3 is an exemplar-source effect, not a recall effect: a static prompt of three fixed self-generated bank exemplars of the goal's type sits within the ±8 floor of BM25, and above type recall the map is flat through same-scene recall (oracle same-scene at coverage 0/0.5/1) and exact same-game duplicates, because layout and object location are recoverable by looking. On WebShop, where the binding (which product satisfies the attribute set) is expensive to recover by search, the residual over the best static arm appears only when a same-product trajectory is in the bank, rises with the fraction of instructions for which one is retrieved (coverage 0/0.5/1), and the natural BM25 retriever's gain equals its measured product recall times the oracle gain. Recall above the type threshold is therefore uninformative unless the bank carries non-recoverable bindings, and retriever evaluation must report the success residual over a static arm built from the same bank at two or more duplicate-availability levels.

Experiments:
E0 ALFWorld bank and pools (about 2,400 episodes, about 2 h): A's bank and C's arm definitions are reused; the bank is supplemented by k=0 runs (2 seeds) on train games in the valid_seen scenes so that every valid_seen game has at least one (typically three) same-type same-scene success (games without one are reported and excluded from the coverage arms; expected under 5%); the exact-duplicate pool is each valid_seen game's own k=0 or static3 success from C's seeds, defining the subset G (expected at least 80 games; one extra non-evaluation static3 seed, 140 episodes, is run if smaller). All new ALFWorld runs are on valid_seen (140 games) with fresh seeds disjoint from C's: two selection seeds (3-4) and four evaluation seeds (5-8). E1 ALFWorld static arms, 140 x 6 = 840 cells each: k0; static3 (C's three fixed type-conditioned expert exemplars, about 430 tokens); static3-bank (three fixed bank successes per type, chosen once as BM25 medoids of the type, token-matched to static3); instr_all (type-agnostic six-procedure prompt); whole-bank (bank in context up to 16k, same-type items first, shortest-first within type, rule pre-registered). Comparator rule: the static arm with the highest mean success on seeds 3-4 (280 cells; ties within 4 net resolved toward static3); all residuals are computed on seeds 5-8 only (560 cells). 4,200 episodes. E2 ALFWorld retrieval ladder at k=3, seeds 5-8, 560 cells each: random-type (uniform over the bank; type recall about 1/6); same-type-random (uniform over same-type items; type recall 1, object-class and scene recall at base rate); BM25 on goal text over the natural bank (scene recall b_alf measured); oracle-scene at coverage c in {0, 0.5, 1}: for a pre-registered nested fraction c of games the same-type same-scene items are ranked first (by BM25 score among themselves), remaining slots filled by BM25 over other scenes; c=0 is BM25 with every same-scene item excluded, so per-cell scene recall equals c by construction; oracle-game on G: the game's own success at rank 1 plus two BM25 items. 7 x 560 = 3,920 episodes. ALFWorld total about 10,500 episodes, about 10 h on one vLLM replica at about 1,100 episodes/h. E3 WebShop setup and bank (one day of setup; about 4,800 episodes, about 3.7 h): Qwen3-32B ReAct agent on the WebShop text environment; T = the 500 standard test instructions; L = train instructions whose goal product equals a test goal product (count reported), run at k=0 with two seeds, plus random train instructions at one seed up to 3,000 instructions; successes (score = 1) form the bank; S = test instructions with at least one same-product bank success, pre-registered minimum 150 and cap 250 (fallback 1: the public human demonstrations are added to the bank; fallback 2: the exact-instruction rung X becomes the primary WebShop duplicate level and is labelled as a weaker binding); a held-out set of 300 train instructions not in the bank is reserved for comparator selection; the floor replicate (k0 on T with two non-evaluation seeds, 1,000 episodes) also supplies the exact-instruction pool and the subset X of test instructions with at least one own success. E4 WebShop static arms (selection set 300 x 1 seed; T x 2 seeds; S x 2 further seeds so S has 4): k0; fixed3 (three fixed bank successes of random categories, type-agnostic, token-matched to k=3); fixed3-cat (three fixed bank successes of the instruction's product category); procedure (about 80-token compiled procedure written by rule from the bank); whole-bank (shortest-first truncation at 16k, rule pre-registered). Comparator = best mean success on the selection set; if the winner is bank-dependent (whole-bank), residuals at c<1 are also reported over the best bank-invariant family. About 8,000-9,000 episodes. E5 WebShop retrieval ladder at k=3: random, same-category-random, BM25 on instruction text over the natural bank (product recall on S, b_ws, measured), and c=0 (BM25 with same-product items removed) on T x 2 seeds and S x 2 further seeds; oracle same-product at c in {0.5, 1} over a nested pre-registered split of S (same-product item at rank 1, remaining slots BM25) on S x 4 seeds; oracle-exact on X x 4 seeds (the instruction's own k0 success at rank 1 plus two BM25 items). About 7,200-9,000 episodes. WebShop total about 20,000-23,000 episodes, 15-18 h on one replica at about 1,300 episodes/h. E6 gate: BM25 k=3 (natural bank) minus k0 on T must clear the WebShop floor before any residual-over-static claim on WebShop; the comparator-free coverage contrasts are tested regardless. E7 analysis: per arm, type or category recall and binding recall (fraction of cells with at least one same-scene or same-product item in the top-3); residual over the comparator on evaluation cells; comparator-free paired contrasts between retrieval arms; for each environment a linear slope of the paired residual (versus that family's c=0 arm) on coverage over c in {0, 0.5, 1} plus the natural BM25 point at its measured recall, with bootstrap CI (1,000 resamples over tasks, jointly across arms); prefill tokens per episode logged as cached (static prefix) and uncached (retrieved prefix), residual per uncached prefill token as the cost axis. Whole study about 32,000 episodes, about 27 replica-hours, about 14 h wall-clock on the two A100s (one vLLM replica of Qwen3-32B per GPU; ALFWorld on one replica while WebShop bank generation runs on the other, then both replicas on WebShop arms), plus the WebShop setup day.

Baselines and Ablations:
Static arms first (the baseline, selected out-of-sample): ALFWorld k0, static3 (expert, type-conditioned), static3-bank (bank exemplars, type-conditioned), instr_all (type-agnostic), whole-bank; WebShop k0, fixed3 (type-agnostic), fixed3-cat (category-conditioned), procedure (type-agnostic), whole-bank (truncated). Manipulations: retriever ladder at matched k=3 (random-type or random, same-type-random or same-category-random, BM25, oracle same-binding, exact duplicate) and bank duplicate availability as oracle coverage c in {0, 0.5, 1} on both environments, with the natural bank as a fourth measured recall point. Ablations that isolate the mechanism: static3 versus static3-bank (exemplar source at fixed type recall; the manipulation claimed to remove the +17.2); same-type-random versus BM25 (object-class match versus type alone); c=0 BM25 versus natural BM25 (whether the natural retriever's same-scene or same-product hits carry anything); oracle-exact versus oracle same-binding (exact duplicate versus binding). The k=1 length ablations of the previous version are dropped: every retrieval arm has k=3 and the static exemplar arms are token-matched.

Falsifiable Predictions:
P1 (ALFWorld: the +17.2 is source, not recall; 560 evaluation cells, ±8 floor). P1a: random-type k=3 sits at or below -8 net against the comparator; falsified by random-type within ±8 of the comparator, produced by the random-type arm. P1b: BM25 minus static3 replicates C on fresh seeds (at least +8; C's seen value +14.3); falsified by +8 or less, produced by the BM25 and static3 arms. P1c: static3-bank is within ±8 of BM25 k=3, i.e. the manipulation that removes the +17.2 is replacing expert exemplars with fixed bank exemplars of the same type; falsified by BM25 at least +8 over static3-bank, produced by the BM25 arm paired with static3-bank (a three-exemplar prompt cannot contain the retrieved items, so this is not entailed by inclusion). P1d: same-type-random is within ±8 of BM25; falsified by same-type-random at least 8 below BM25 (object-class match matters), produced by the same-type-random arm. P2 (ALFWorld binding flat; comparator-free paired differences): oracle-scene c=1 minus c=0 is within ±8, and oracle-game minus oracle-scene c=1 is within ±8 on G; falsified by either difference at or above +8, produced by the c=1 arm (scene recall 1 by construction, at least one same-scene same-type item in every cell) or by the oracle-game arm (exact duplicate at rank 1). The oracle-game clause is the riskiest in the proposal and is kept as the strongest test of recoverability. P3 (WebShop binding, on S with at least 600 paired cells; floor the larger of the S estimate and ±8). P3a: oracle c=1 minus c=0 on S is at least +16 net; falsified by less than +8, produced by the c=1 arm on S (a same-product trajectory at rank 1 fails to transfer the binding). P3b: BM25 (natural bank) minus BM25 c=0 on S equals b_ws times the P3a effect within ±8 (the natural retriever's gain tracks its measured product recall), claimed when b_ws is at least 0.3 (pre-registered; expected, since the same-product instruction shares attribute words); falsified by a BM25 effect at least 8 above or below that product, produced by the BM25 arm on S (above: same-product hits are not what carries the BM25 gain; below: retrieved duplicates are not used). P3c: with same-product items removed (c=0), every retriever's residual over the comparator on T is within ±8; falsified by any c=0 retriever at or above +8, produced by the c=0 BM25 or same-category-random arm (category search strategies transfer without duplicates). P3d (secondary): oracle-exact minus c=0 on X is at least +16; falsified by less than +8, produced by the oracle-exact arm. P4 (headline, cross-environment slope): the linear slope of the paired residual on coverage, fit over c in {0, 0.5, 1} plus the natural BM25 point, with the stated MDE of 16 net per unit coverage, is within ±8 on ALFWorld with a 95% bootstrap CI excluding +16, and at least +16 on WebShop (S) with a CI excluding +8. Each clause is falsified separately: ALFWorld slope at or above +16 with CI excluding +8 (scene binding is informative), produced by the c=0.5 and c=1 oracle-scene arms; WebShop slope below +8 (product binding is uninformative), produced by the c=0.5 and c=1 arms on S; two slopes both at or above +16 or both within ±8 falsify the dissociation itself. P5 (gate, positive control): BM25 k=3 over the natural bank beats k0 on T by at least the WebShop floor; if it fails, produced by E6, P3c and every WebShop residual-over-static claim are withdrawn, while P3a, P3b, P3d and the WebShop slope (all comparator-free) are still tested and reported.

Measurement and Noise Control:
ALFWorld: 140 valid_seen games x 4 fresh evaluation seeds = 560 paired cells per arm, ±8 net floor (C's CI half-width for k3 minus static3 was about ±5 at 548 cells; ±8 is kept as the pre-registered floor, 0.47 of the measured +17.2 and about 0.27 of the Gate-0 gain); the comparator is selected on two separate seeds (280 cells) by a pre-registered rule, so no residual is computed on the cells that selected its baseline; manipulation effects are paired differences between retrieval arms and do not use the comparator; bootstrap CIs over games (1,000 resamples, jointly across arms). WebShop: T = 500 x 2 seeds = 1,000 cells; S at least 150 (capped at 250) x 4 seeds = 600-1,000 cells; the floor is estimated from two independent k0 replicates with different seed pairs before any claim (expected about ±6 net at 1,000 cells, about ±8 at 600) and the larger of the estimate and ±8 is used; success (score = 1) primary, WebShop score secondary; comparator selected on 300 held-out train instructions; bootstrap CIs over instructions. Slope: three coverage levels plus the natural point, per-level paired SE about 4 net, slope SE about 5.7 net per unit coverage, MDE about 16 (two-sided 0.05, 80% power); P4's thresholds are set at that MDE. Recall computed per episode from retrieved item ids and item metadata (type, scene, game; category, product id). Per-type ALFWorld and per-category WebShop results exploratory. Pre-registration before E1 of retriever definitions, the nested coverage splits, bank filters, truncation rules, the comparator rule and tie rule, the |S| minimum and the b_ws threshold, and floors.
