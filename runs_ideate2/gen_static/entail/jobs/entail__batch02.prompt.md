### proposal: cached_prefix_frontier
Title: Prefix Caching Moves the Break-Even: Measured Accuracy–GPU-Second Frontiers for Static, Whole-Bank and Retrieved Experience Prefixes in a Multi-Turn Agent
Short Hypothesis: In a multi-turn agent served with prefix caching, the cost of an experience prefix is set by how many distinct prefixes the arm emits across episodes and by how long the prefix is at decode time, not by its injected token count, so a matched-token budget mis-ranks the routes. Concretely: with caching off, an 80-item whole-bank prefix (~11k tokens) costs at least 3x the GPU-seconds per episode of k=3 retrieval (~430 tokens); with caching on it costs at most 1.5x, and k=3 retrieval costs at most 25% more than a type-conditioned static prompt because only the first turn's retrieved tokens are uncached. Retrieval from a small bank (24 or 80 items) keeps the accuracy of retrieval from the full 1,465-item bank while emitting few distinct prefixes, so retrieval's accuracy is available at static cost.

Experiments:
E0 (on disk, reused): Measurement A arms k=0, k=3, k=7 and Measurement C arms (type-conditioned 3 fixed exemplars; type-agnostic 6-procedure prompt) give accuracy on 274 games x 2 seeds. E1 new accuracy arms, 274 x 2 each: whole-bank-24 (4 items per type, ~3.4k tokens, one prefix), whole-bank-80 (13-14 per type, ~11k tokens, one prefix; truncation rule: stratified by type, shortest successful trajectories first), and BM25 goal retrieval k=3 from random type-stratified bank subsets of 24, 80 and 240 items; 2,740 episodes (~2.5 h). E2 cost runs: all nine arms x prefix caching {on, off} x ~270 episodes, arms interleaved in 3 blocks of 90 episodes, both GPUs, workers raised until each replica's running queue is at least 8; log per request prompt_tokens, cached_tokens, completion_tokens and per-turn context length from the vLLM usage fields, wall-clock per block and nvidia-smi utilization every 10 s; GPU-seconds per episode = 2 x wall-clock / episodes at saturated queue; 4,860 episodes (~4 h). E3 fit GPU-s = a x uncached_prefill + b x decode_tokens + c x sum over decode steps of context length on eight arms and predict the ninth (whole-bank-24). E4 frontier: success from E0/E1 against GPU-s per episode from E2 for both routes under both cache settings; Pareto set per setting; additional concurrency ladder (8/16/32 workers per replica) on whole-bank-80 and k=3, cost only, to expose KV-occupancy effects. Positive controls: (i) the caching toggle must cut the type-agnostic static arm's uncached prefill tokens by at least 5x, otherwise the cache is not functioning and no cost claim is made; (ii) k=3 accuracy under cache on versus off must agree within the ±8 floor, otherwise cost and accuracy cannot be paired.

Baselines and Ablations:
Baselines (static arms first, per the brief): k=0; type-agnostic 6-procedure prompt (1 distinct prefix); type-conditioned 3 fixed exemplars (6 distinct prefixes); whole-bank-24 and whole-bank-80 (1 prefix each). Retrieval route: BM25 goal retrieval k=3 from 24, 80, 240 and 1,465 items (distinct prefixes rising toward C(n_type,3)); k=7 from 1,465. Ablations: caching on/off (cost only); concurrency 8/16/32 per replica (cost only); retrieved items ordered canonically by item id so identical sets share a prefix versus ordered by score (cost only); whole-bank-80 item order type-blocked versus shuffled (accuracy, exploratory).

Falsifiable Predictions:
P1 (headline, cost-ranking reversal): with caching off, whole-bank-80 costs at least 3x the GPU-s per episode of k=3 retrieval; with caching on, at most 1.5x. Falsified by whole-bank-80 at or above 2x k=3 with caching on (decode attention over an 11k context and KV occupancy dominate, or the shared prefix is evicted under concurrency), produced by the whole-bank-80 versus k=3 cells of E2 under cache on; or by a cache-off ratio below 2, produced by the same pair under cache off. P2: with caching on, k=3 retrieval costs at most 25% more GPU-s per episode than the type-conditioned 3-exemplar static arm. Falsified by a premium at or above 50%, produced by the k=3 versus 3-fixed-exemplar cells under cache on (first-turn prefill dominance or per-turn cache misses would produce it). P3: whole-bank-80 success is within ±8 net of k=3 retrieval on both splits. Falsified by whole-bank-80 at least 8 net below k=3 (many-shot distraction), produced by the whole-bank-80 arm of E1; not entailed, since containing the items does not make the reader use them. P4: retrieval-from-24 and retrieval-from-80 are within ±8 net of retrieval-from-1,465 while their uncached prefill tokens under caching are at most 30% of the full-bank arm's. Falsified by success at least 8 net lower at 24 or 80 (item specificity matters), produced by the bank-size ladder of E1; or by a cache-hit fraction at or below 50% at bank 24 (prefix diversity from set/order variation), produced by that arm's cached_tokens logs. P5: the three-term cost model predicts the held-out whole-bank-24 arm's GPU-s per episode within 25%. Falsified by held-out error above 25%, produced by E3.

Measurement and Noise Control:
Accuracy: paired (game, seed) cells with A and C on 274 x 2; ±8 net floor for arm-versus-arm claims; margins stated as fractions of the Gate-0 gain (+27/+32 net at k=3): the ±8 in P3/P4 is 25-30% of that gain; per-type numbers exploratory. Cost: GPU-s per episode from wall-clock at saturated queue in 3 interleaved blocks per arm per cache setting, median and bootstrap CI over blocks; token quantities from per-request vLLM usage fields; utilization and throughput (episodes/min, tok/s per GPU) reported per arm; ratios in P1/P2 computed on block medians with CIs from 1,000 bootstrap resamples of episodes, and a prediction counts as confirmed only if its CI excludes the falsifying value. Cost-model fit reports R-squared and held-out error. Pre-registration of arms, truncation rule, concurrency and floors before E1.

### proposal: exemplar_lottery
Title: The Exemplar Lottery: Retrieval's Gain Over a Fixed Prompt Is a Quantile of the Static Arm's Draw Distribution, Not a Mean Shift
Short Hypothesis: A fixed exemplar prompt is one draw from the bank, and reported retrieval gains are measured against a single draw. On ALFWorld with Qwen3-32B, the success of a type-conditioned three-exemplar fixed prompt varies across random draws from the same expert bank by more than the mean gap between retrieval and the fixed arm; retrieval sits at the upper quantile of that draw distribution, does not beat the best held-out draw by more than the noise floor, and a one-time length rule (shortest successful trajectories per type) recovers it without a query. Retrieval's value on a template environment is selection robustness, and it has to be reported against the draw distribution with its spread.

Experiments:
E0 (on disk): A (k=0; k=3 BM25 retrieval from the full expert bank, R_full) and C (one fixed draw, reported later as its quantile in the lottery). E1: F_1..F_16, type-conditioned fixed prompts, each a uniform draw of 3 successful expert trajectories per type from the full bank, same slot and format as C's fixed arm, token-matched to k=3 (~430 tokens); 274 x 2 each, 8,768 episodes (~7.3 h). E2: S_1..S_8, the same from the self pool (on-policy detours); 4,384 episodes (~3.7 h). E3: 8 random type-stratified 80-item bank samples; for each, F_b (fixed draw of 3 per type from the sample) and R_b (k=3 BM25 retrieval from the sample), paired; 8,768 episodes (~7.3 h). E4: curation arms C_short (3 shortest successful trajectories per type), C_medoid (per-type medoids under the retriever's own representation), C_worst (3 longest per type, negative control); 1,644 episodes (~1.4 h). E5: held-out best-draw selection: choose the best of F_1..16 on seed 1 x valid_seen and compare to R_full on seed 2 x valid_unseen, and the mirror. E6: variance decomposition: crossed random-effects logistic model with game, draw and draw x seed intercepts; simple SDs of arm-level net scores across draws and across seeds reported alongside; bootstrap over games. Static arms are prefix-cached and retrieval arms are not; uncached prefill tokens per episode logged for every arm as a secondary cost axis. Total ~23.6k episodes (~20 h on two A100s), pre-registered draw seeds.

Baselines and Ablations:
Baselines: k=0; R_full (retrieval k=3 from 1,465 items); the 16 expert draws and 8 self-pool draws as the static-arm distribution; the 8 paired (F_b, R_b) bank samples; C's single draw located as a quantile. Curation arms as one-time static alternatives: C_short, C_medoid, C_worst. Ablations: draws restricted to scene-disjoint items for each test game (removes near-duplicate luck from the lottery); single-exemplar draws token-matched to k=1 for 8 draws if budget allows; the self-pool draws with detour steps removed by rule (tests whether the wider spread, if found, is from detours or from selection).

Falsifiable Predictions:
P1 (headline): the SD across the 16 expert draws of net success is at least 2x the within-draw seed SD, and the draw range (max minus min) is at least the mean gap R_full minus mean(F_d). Falsified by draw SD at or below seed SD, or a range below half the mean gap, produced by the F_1..16 arm if draws cluster (a live outcome, since expert trajectories within a type share the procedure). P2: R_full does not exceed the held-out best draw by more than 8 net. Falsified by R_full minus F_best-heldout above 8 net (retrieval carries per-instance information beyond exemplar choice), produced by E5. P3: self-pool draws have SD at least 1.5x the expert draws' SD and a mean at least 8 net lower. Falsified by SD_self at or below SD_expert, produced by E2 (self-pool successes are procedurally equivalent despite detours). P4: C_short lands within ±8 net of R_full and C_worst lands at least 8 net below R_full. Falsified by C_short at least 8 net below R_full (a cheap rule does not recover retrieval's selection), produced by E4; or by C_worst within ±8 of R_full (exemplar choice does not matter, which also removes P1's premise), produced by E4. P5: over the 8 bank samples the paired residual R_b minus F_b has mean within ±8 net and an across-sample SD larger than its across-seed SD. Falsified by a mean residual above 8 net (retrieval beats a same-bank fixed draw on average), produced by E3.

Measurement and Noise Control:
All arms on the 274 x 2 (game, seed) grid of A, so every comparison is paired; ±8 net floor for arm-versus-arm claims and margins of 25-30% of the Gate-0 gain (+27/+32). Draw SD is estimated over 16 arm-level net scores, each on 548 episodes (per-draw SE about 2 net), so a draw SD of 4 net is distinguishable from the seed SD estimated from the 16 paired seed differences; the chi-square CI on an SD from 16 draws is about ±35%, hence the 2x ratio in P1. Range and quantile statements carry bootstrap CIs from 1,000 resamples of games drawn jointly across arms to preserve pairing. The held-out selection in E5 removes the winner's curse from best-draw comparisons. Per-type spreads are exploratory. Pre-registration lists draw seeds, bank-sample seeds, curation rules and floors before any run.

### proposal: recall_success_dissociation
Title: Recall Ranks Retrievers, Success Does Not: A Two-Environment Audit of Experience Retrieval Against the Static Prompt with Manipulated Duplicate Availability
Short Hypothesis: Retriever recall (fraction of top-k items sharing the test task's type or binding) and the task-success residual over the best static prompt dissociate, and the dissociation is set by whether the retrieved binding is recoverable inside the episode. On ALFWorld, the residual over the best static arm is a step at type recall (random-type retrieval loses, BM25 and an oracle same-scene retriever sit within the floor) and is unchanged when same-scene items are removed from the bank, because object locations are recoverable by looking; on WebShop, where the binding (which product satisfies the attribute set) is expensive to recover by search, the residual appears only when a same-product trajectory is in the bank and tracks product recall. Recall above the type threshold is therefore uninformative unless the bank carries non-recoverable bindings, and retriever evaluation must report success residual over the static arm at two duplicate-availability levels.

Experiments:
E0 ALFWorld reuse: A (k=0; BM25 k=3) and C (static arms; the best static arm chosen on pooled cells). E1 ALFWorld retriever ladder at k=3, 274 x 2 each: random-type (uniform over the bank, type recall about 1/6), goal-BM25 (A, type recall about 1), oracle same-type-same-scene on valid_seen (binding recall 1; on valid_unseen it reduces to same-type); C's different-type placebo serves as the recall-0 point; 1,644 episodes (~1.5 h). E2 ALFWorld duplicate manipulation: BM25 k=3 with a per-game bank filter excluding same-scene items on valid_seen, paired with A's k=3; 548 episodes. E3 WebShop setup (one day) and bank: Qwen3-32B run on 3,000 train instructions at k=0; successes form bank-full; per test instruction, bank-dup (full) and bank-nodup (items whose goal product equals the test goal product removed); achieved same-product rates reported (target at least 30% versus 0%); the public human demonstrations are added if the self-success rate is thin. E4 WebShop arms, 500 test instructions x 2 seeds each: k=0; static arms: fixed 3 exemplars (token-matched to k=3), compiled procedure (~80 tokens, written by rule from the bank), whole-bank-in-context up to 16k with shortest-first truncation; retrieval k=3: random, instruction-BM25, oracle same-product, each under bank-dup and bank-nodup (cells that coincide are shared); about 10.5k episodes (~8 h). E5 gate: WebShop BM25 k=3 over k=0 must clear the floor before any residual claim. E6 analysis: for each retriever and bank level, recall@3 (type or category recall; binding recall = same scene or same product) and success residual over the best static arm at that bank level; residual plotted against binding recall for both environments; residual per uncached prefill token reported as the cost axis.

Baselines and Ablations:
Baselines (static arms first, per the brief): k=0; fixed 3 exemplars token-matched to k=3; compiled procedure prompt; whole-bank-in-context where it fits; on ALFWorld the C arms including the type-agnostic 6-procedure prompt. Manipulations: retriever ladder (random-type, BM25, oracle same-binding) and bank duplicate availability (dup versus nodup) on both environments. Ablations: k=1 versions of oracle and BM25 on WebShop (residual is not a length effect); type-agnostic procedure-only static on WebShop versus fixed exemplars; random-type retrieval on WebShop with bank-dup (isolates the item content from the binding).

Falsifiable Predictions:
P1 (ALFWorld step): the residual over the best static arm is at or below minus 8 net for random-type retrieval and within ±8 for BM25 and oracle same-scene. Falsified by an oracle residual above 8 net (scene bindings carry value that the static arm lacks), produced by the oracle arm on valid_seen; or by random-type retrieval within ±8 of the static arm (procedures do not matter, contradicting A), produced by the random-type arm. P2 (ALFWorld manipulation): excluding same-scene items changes the BM25 k=3 residual by less than 8 net. Falsified by a drop of at least 8 net, produced by E2. P3 (WebShop manipulation): under bank-nodup every retriever's residual over the best static arm is within the floor; under bank-dup, oracle same-product and BM25 residuals are at least 8 net with oracle at or above BM25. Falsified by a residual of at least 8 net under bank-nodup (retrieval carries value beyond duplicates, for example category search strategies), produced by the bank-nodup BM25 or oracle arm; or by oracle same-product within the floor under bank-dup (a duplicate trajectory does not transfer the binding), produced by the bank-dup oracle arm. P4 (cross-environment): the slope of residual on binding recall is within the floor on ALFWorld and at least 8 net per unit recall on WebShop under bank-dup. Falsified by same-sign slopes of the same size on both environments, produced by the two ladders jointly. P5 (gate, positive control): WebShop BM25 k=3 beats k=0 by at least the WebShop floor. If falsified, produced by E5, the WebShop side is declared uninformative and P3/P4 are not claimed.

Measurement and Noise Control:
ALFWorld: paired (game, seed) cells with A and C on 274 x 2, ±8 net floor; the best static arm is chosen on pooled cells across both splits so no per-split winner is selected; margins as fractions of the Gate-0 gain (+27/+32). WebShop: 500 x 2 paired cells; the floor is estimated from two independent k=0 replicates with different seed pairs before any claim (expected about ±6 net) and the larger of that estimate and ±8 is used; success (score = 1) primary, WebShop score secondary; residuals with bootstrap CIs over instructions (1,000 resamples, jointly across arms). Recall computed per episode from retrieved item ids. Per-category WebShop and per-type ALFWorld results exploratory. Pre-registration of retriever definitions, bank filters, truncation rules and floors before E1.
