### proposal: compression_erases_the_writer
Title: Compression Erases the Writer, Not the Gain: Abstraction Level of Transferred Experience Helps Only When Writer != Reader
Short Hypothesis: The benefit of compressing agent experience before transfer is an interaction with writer identity, not a main effect. For a reader consuming its own success-filtered pool, raw trajectories beat writer-compressed summaries and procedures at matched injected tokens; for a pool written by a different backbone the order reverses, because compression strips the writer-specific search, phrasing and binding content that only its author can imitate. The survey claim 'transferability rises with compression' (2604.15877) and the finding 'raw beats distilled skills' (2605.24117) are the two cells of one interaction: the sign of the raw-minus-compressed contrast is predicted by whether the writer is the reader, the writer's identity is undetectable at the procedure level, and stripping the writer's thoughts from raw traces moves self and foreign cells in opposite directions.

Experiments:
Domain and partition: ALFWorld. Bank = the 3,553 training games minus a held-out slice of 400 training games excluded from every pool; evaluation = 274 validation games (140 seen, 134 unseen) + the 400 held-out training games = 674 games per seed. Retriever fixed (Measurement A's); retrieval is deterministic per game, so every cell at a level sees the same retrieved game ids across seeds and cells. Writers: E = expert walkthroughs (1,465, minus held-out); Q = Qwen3-32B self pool (1,892 successes, minus held-out); X = EXAONE-4.0-32B self pool, collected on the same 3,553 training games at the same scaffold and temperature (about 3 GPU-hours), success-filtered. For E, which has no writer model, the compressor is the non-reader backbone so E stays fully foreign. Thought-stripped raw (L0s): the L0 items with thoughts removed, actions and observations kept including failed steps, template-normalised; at matched tokens k rises to about 5. Readers: Qwen3-32B and EXAONE-4.0-32B (family mismatch at approximately matched capability; both serving to be verified in vLLM before the first confirmatory cell). Per reader 56,616 episodes; both readers 113,232 episodes. Throughput: about 18 episodes/min per replica, one replica per A100; each reader's cells run on their own GPU in parallel, about 52 h each; GPU1 first collects the X pool (3 h); compression is batched (each backbone compresses Q, X and E at L1 and L2: 12 short jobs, about 1-2 h total). Manipulation checks (not predictions): token counts per level within +-10%; textual divergence between the two writers' L1 and L2 sets (embedding similarity and normalised edit distance per type) with a pre-registered rule: if the writers' L2 procedures are near-identical (normalised edit distance below 0.2 per type), L1 replaces L2 as the level of P1 and P3, and the first clause of P5 is reported as a manipulation-check outcome rather than a finding; per-type composition of each pool. Pre-registration of gates, margins, the level rule and the seed rule before the first confirmatory cell. Optional, outside the envelope above and only if the primary cells finish early: Qwen3-8B as a same-family lower-capability writer for the Qwen3 reader at L0 and L2 (2 cells at 2 seeds, about 1.5 GPU-h of collection), a descriptive capability-at-fixed-proximity arm.

### proposal: leave_one_type_out_split

### proposal: own_errors_pass_the_gate
Title: The Reader Cannot Grade Its Own Homework: Self-Authored Contamination Evades Read-Time Detection in Agent Experience Stores
Short Hypothesis: A reader asked to verify retrieved experience before acting misses planted errors more often when the host trajectory was written by itself than when the identical error is planted in a trajectory written by another backbone, because self-authored text is familiar (low perplexity under the reader) and LLM judges favour familiar text. Consequently the same planted error, once retrieved, costs the reader more success when its host is self-written than when it is foreign-written; an external reference (a static procedure prompt) closes at least half of the detection gap; and a content detector keeps most of the legitimate foreign gain that a writer-identity gate discards by construction.

Experiments:
Domain and partition as in Measurement A with the 400-game held-out training slice added: 674 evaluation games per downstream cell, k=3 retrieval within task type, retriever fixed as in Measurement A. Pools (hosts), each collected on the about 3,100 remaining training games under the same rule (one attempt per game plus a second attempt on games failed at the first, success-filtered; about 4 GPU-h per collected pool, matched budget): Q = Qwen3-32B, X = EXAONE-4.0-32B (shared with sibling proposals), E = expert walkthroughs, S' = Qwen3-32B under a second scaffold at temperature 1.0 (same backbone, lower proximity; verification cells only), plus each model's failed trajectories for family C3. Contamination families applied by one script identically to every host pool: C1 procedure graft (manipulation sub-sequence of type T replaced by that of type T' with room-consistent receptacle names; task and label stay T); C2 receptacle swap (final put target replaced by a plausible wrong receptacle of the same room); C3 natural errors (the writer's own failures relabelled as successes; content varies by writer by nature). A hard C1 variant (a single omitted or wrong step, e.g. no 'open fridge') is pre-registered if C1 recall exceeds 80% on both hosts. The headline family is chosen by the pre-registered 30-80% foreign-recall rule on a 100-item pilot per (reader, host, family). Detectors: before acting, the reader judges each retrieved item ('does this trajectory correctly solve its stated task? yes/no, one-line reason'); D0 zero-shot; D1 with the Measurement C static six-procedure prompt in the verification context; D-consensus majority of three D0 samples (A-MemGuard-style named baseline); D-NLL non-LLM baseline flagging the top-x% items by the reader's per-token NLL. Verification cells: headline family at >= 1500 planted and >= 1500 clean items per (reader, host) for hosts {Q, X, E, S'} x readers {Qwen3, EXAONE}, every item scored under both D0 and D1 (identical paired item sets); D-consensus on a 600/600 subset of the Q and X hosts; non-headline families at 600/600 on Q and X under D0 and D1; format-normalised arm (thoughts stripped to actions and observations) on Q and X under D0; prompt-position ablation for the Qwen3 reader; per-item reader NLL from vLLM prompt logprobs for every item (manipulation check: self-authored hosts have lower NLL; entailed, not a prediction). Downstream cells (674 games; bank rates within the retrieved task type; expected contaminated items among k=3 are 0/0.9/1.5 at 0/30/50%): the Gate-0 pair first (rate 0 and 50% C1, Q hosts, Qwen3 reader); C1 rate block rates {0, 30, 50%} x hosts {Q, X}: Qwen3 reader at 6 seeds (6 x 674 x 6 = 24,264 episodes; carries P1 and P5), EXAONE reader at 2 seeds (6 x 1,348 = 8,088; replication); a 100% Q->Qwen3 cell at 2 seeds (1,348) only if Gate 0 fails at 50%; D0-gated arms at 30% (flagged items dropped and refilled from the next retrieved rank) for hosts {Q, X} x both readers at 2 seeds (5,392); clean rate-0 three-writer mixed store (Q+X+E) with no gate, D0 gate and D1 gate: Qwen3 reader at 4 seeds (8,088; carries P6), EXAONE reader at 2 seeds (4,044); k=0 and static-prompt baselines per reader at 2 seeds (5,392). Total about 55k episodes (56.5k with the contingent 100% cell); at Measurement A's throughput of about 1,000 episodes per hour per replica this is about 55 replica-hours, plus 10-14 replica-hours of verification and paraphrase calls and about 8 GPU-h of pool collection (X, S'): about 75 replica-hours, about 2 days on two A100s. Baselines and Ablations:
No detector (plain retrieval) at every rate. D-NLL familiarity baseline (expected worst on self hosts: it encodes the bias). D-consensus (A-MemGuard-style self-consistency) on a 600/600 subset. D1 (external reference) vs D0 on identical item sets. Writer-identity gate on the clean mixed store as the provenance-style comparator, computed as mixed-minus-self (it coincides with the rate-0 self cell under the same retriever) and reported with a CI. Families C1, C2, C3 and the hard C1 variant. Host E, foreign to both readers: the two readers' recall on E should agree, a check that the self-foreign gap is not a reader main effect. Host S', same backbone as the Qwen3 reader under a second scaffold and temperature 1.0: proximity varied at fixed capability; its recall is expected to fall between the Q and X hosts in the order of reader NLL and is reported as a familiarity dose-response for P3 without a separate prediction. Format normalisation (thoughts stripped to actions and observations) for Q and X hosts, to separate authorship recognition by format from familiarity of content. Paraphrase 2x2 (paraphraser self vs other backbone; planted vs clean items). Detector prompt position (item before or after the task) for the Qwen3 reader. Type-stratified precision and recall, and false-positive rate by host for every detector. Memory-free and static-prompt baselines per reader; every gain reported net of the static prompt; retained-gain ratio of the clean foreign cells vs self reported per writer x reader with CIs as context for G9.

Falsifiable Predictions:
P1 (Gate 0): C1 at 50% bank rate in Q->Qwen3 reduces net success by >= 6 points vs the rate-0 bank (same games, seeds and retrieval ranks; paired half-width about +-2.5 at 6 seeds; the decision is taken after the first 2 seeds unless the estimate lies within +-3 of the margin, in which case the remaining seeds are run first). Falsified if harm < 6 at 50% and at 100% (contingent 100% cell); produced by the Q-host rate cells of the Qwen3 reader. P2 (headline authorship effect): D0 recall on headline-family items is >= 10 points lower on self-authored hosts than on foreign-authored hosts for each reader, and the reader x host-writer interaction (the sum of the two readers' foreign-minus-self gaps over game-paired items) has a 95% CI excluding 0; at 1500/1500 items per cell recall SE is about 1.3, each gap SE about 1.8 and the interaction SE about 2.6, so the 10-point margin is about twice the half-width. Falsified if recall(self host) >= recall(foreign host) for either reader, or if both readers show the same host ordering (a pool-difficulty main effect with no interaction); produced by the 2x2 D0 cells {Qwen3, EXAONE} x {Q hosts, X hosts}. P3 (familiarity mediates, paraphrase 2x2): on the NLL-qualifying self-paraphrases (per-token reader NLL at or below the 75th percentile of self-host items; >= 1300 within-item pairs; qualifying fraction reported), (a) paired D0 recall is >= 8 points below the unparaphrased originals, and (b) the self-paraphrase drop exceeds the cross-paraphrase drop (the same originals rewritten by the other backbone, reader NLL not lowered) by >= 5 points. Falsified for (a) if the paired drop's 95% CI (half-width about +-3) has upper bound < 4, a region that contains the whole +-3 equivalence band around zero, or if paraphrased items are detected more often; falsified for (b) if the difference of the two paired drops (half-width about +-4) has upper bound < 5, the outcome produced when paraphrasing as such, not familiarity, lowers detection; produced by the paraphrase cells on P2's foreign-host items. P4 (external reference closes the gap): on identical item sets scored under both detectors, D1 raises self-host recall by >= 8 points for each reader (within-item paired half-width about +-3), and gap_D1 <= 0.5 x gap_D0. Falsified if the paired self-host lift has 95% CI upper bound < 4 for either reader, or if gap_D1 - 0.5 x gap_D0 has a game-paired bootstrap 95% CI excluding 0 from above (D1 lifting both hosts equally produces exactly this); the gap clause is scored only if P2's D0 gap is >= 10 for that reader, otherwise only the lift clause is scored; produced by the D0 and D1 cells. P5 (per-retrieval harm): in the Qwen3 reader's 30% and 50% C1 cells (6 seeds), each episode that retrieved >= 1 planted item is paired with its rate-0 twin (same game, seed and retrieval ranks; only the planted items' content differs); the paired success loss is >= 5 net points larger on the self host Q than on the foreign host X (about 6,200 pairs per host; half-width about +-1.7 per host and +-2.4 on the difference after a game design effect of about 1.5, so the margin is at least twice the half-width and 0.8 of the Gate-0 minimum). Falsified if the game-clustered bootstrap CI of the self-minus-foreign loss has upper bound < 5, which includes every world in which foreign-written errors harm as much as or more than self-written ones; produced by the rate {0, 30, 50%} x host {Q, X} cells of the Qwen3 reader; the conditioning on retrieval uses the retrieval log, which is identical in both arms, so it selects the same (game, seed) pairs in both. Leverage (loss over clean gain) is reported with CIs as a descriptive quantity and carries no prediction. P6 (governance cost, G9): on the clean three-writer mixed store the D0 gate flags < 10% of clean foreign items (>= 1500 items, half-width about 1.5) and costs < 3 net points vs the ungated mixed store for the Qwen3 reader (4 seeds, (game, seed)-paired half-width about +-3). Falsified if the false-positive rate on clean foreign items is >= 15%, or if the paired cost's 95% CI lower bound exceeds 3 (a cost of >= 6, twice the half-width, produces this); produced by the rate-0 mixed-store cells with and without the D0 gate and by the clean foreign items of the D0 cells. The writer-identity gate's cost (mixed minus self-pool gain) is reported with its CI as the comparator and is not predicted, since it holds by construction.

Measurement and Noise Control:
Verification cells: headline family >= 1500 planted and >= 1500 clean items per (reader, host) (or every eligible trajectory if a host pool has fewer, with the eligible count and recomputed half-widths registered after the pilot), other families 600/600; recall SE about 1.3 (2.0 at 600), self-foreign gap SE about 1.8, reader x host interaction SE about 2.6, so P2's 10-point margin is about twice the half-width; items are the same underlying training games edited identically across hosts and paired by game wherever the Q, X and S' pools share a solved game, so the self-foreign contrast is game-paired; D0, D1 and the paraphrased versions are scored on identical item sets, so D1-D0 lifts and paraphrase drops are within-item paired differences with half-width about +-3 at >= 1300 items (discordance about 30%); the NLL-qualifying rule for self-paraphrases is fixed before the arm runs, and the qualifying fraction, mean NLL shift and length ratio are reported as manipulation checks; the cross-paraphrase arm holds content and paraphrase style constant while leaving reader NLL unchanged. Downstream cells: 674 games; 6 seeds for the Qwen3 reader's rate block (cell SE about 1 net, about +-2 after a game design effect of about 1.5), 4 seeds for its mixed-store cells (about +-2.5), 2 seeds elsewhere (about +-5, as in Measurement A); all contrasts are paired at (game, seed) with retrieval fixed and logged from the clean bank and planted items substituted in place at the same rank, so paired arms differ only in the planted items' content; planting is seeded and planted ids are recorded per episode, so P5's per-retrieval pairs are selected by a log that is identical in both arms; CIs by cluster bootstrap over games; Gate-0 margin 6 net is 0.22 of Measurement A's +27 and P5's margin 5 is 0.8 of the Gate-0 minimum. Injected tokens logged; gated arms refill to matched k so token counts match ungated arms within +-10%; paraphrases are held within +-20% of original length. Pre-registered rules: the headline family is the one whose D0 recall on foreign hosts lies in 30-80% (ceiling and floor avoidance), chosen on the 100-item pilot before confirmatory cells; all margins, gates and the Gate-0 interim rule are registered before the first confirmatory cell.
