### proposal: thoughts_do_not_travel
Title: Actions Travel, Thoughts Don't: The Writer's Reasoning Text Is the Non-Transferable Part of Cross-Backbone Agent Experience, and a Deterministic Strip Matches a Model-Aware Rewriter at Matched Tokens
Short Hypothesis: The component of a stored experience item that fails to transfer across backbones is the writer's reasoning text (ReAct thoughts, think blocks, analysis-channel text), not its action-observation sequence and not its scaffold. At fixed retrieved items and fixed reader scaffold, deleting the writer's thoughts raises a foreign reader's success by >= 5 net points and does not raise the writer's own (a writer x reader interaction on byte-identical injected strings); converting the scaffold while keeping the thoughts recovers little; the harm follows the author of the thoughts, not the author of the actions (the reader's own post-hoc thoughts on foreign actions remove it, a foreign backbone's post-hoc thoughts on the reader's own actions reproduce it); and a model-aware LLM rewriter at matched tokens adds nothing over the zero-cost strip. The fix for producer-consumer mismatch on this class of items is a parser, not a model.

Experiments:
Readers and writers: Qwen3-32B (Q), EXAONE-4.0-32B (E), gpt-oss-120b (G), served in vLLM 0.13, one replica per A100 (Gate V: k=0 >= 0.30 for E and G). Pools: Q-self (on disk) and E-self, G-self collected on the same 3,553 training games at the same episode budget (one attempt per game, success-filtered; about 7,100 episodes, 10-12 GPU-hours on GPU1 while GPU0 runs the Q->Q cells); expert pool X (on disk, no thoughts). A 'thought' is every model-generated token in a step except the action string (Thought lines and <think> blocks for Q and E, analysis-channel text for G). Items whose F1r form exceeds 1,500 tokens are dropped from that writer's pool for every serialization (count reported), so the retrieved set is identical across serializations and across readers. Retrieval: task-description embedding, k=3, fixed; within a writer pool every reader receives byte-identical items. Serializations: F1 verbatim in the writer's scaffold, thoughts kept; F1r thoughts kept verbatim, actions and observations re-serialized by the deterministic parser into the reader's scaffold template (F1r = F1 in same-backbone cells); F2 the same parser with the thought slot deleted (action + observation lines only, same k=3 items, fewer tokens); F3 the reader's own thoughts written teacher-forced over the writer's action-observation sequence in one offline pass (per-step length target = the writer's per-step thought length +/-20%, so F3 is token-matched to F1r per item); F4 a model-aware rewriter prompt on the reader model following arXiv:2605.30723's description (the reader rewrites the item for itself, free to restructure, add or delete), output capped per item at the F2 token count (one regeneration with a tighter instruction, then truncation; realized tokens logged; a cell is valid only if mean F4 tokens are within 10% of F2); F5 (reader Q only) the Q-self pool's own action sequences with thoughts written teacher-forced by E (F5-E), by G (F5-G) or by Q itself (F5-self, the post-hoc own-authored comparator, which is also the matched writer = reader case of F3), length-matched to Q's original thoughts; F6 thoughts only (actions and observations deleted). Cells and seeds (274 eval games per seed): Phase A, 6 seeds (1,644 episodes per arm): 6 cross cells x {F1r, F2} = 12 arms and 3 same-backbone cells x {F1, F2} = 6 arms, 29,592 episodes. Phase A': F1 in the 6 cross cells at 2 seeds (3,288); static3 (Measurement C's best static arm, cached) for E and G at 4 seeds plus 2 extra seeds for Q (2,740); k=0 for E and G at 2 seeds (1,096). Phase B, 4 seeds (1,096 per arm) unless stated: F3 and F4 in the 6 cross cells (13,152); F5-self, F5-E, F5-G at 6 seeds (4,932); F6 in 2 cross cells, reader Q and reader E each with the writer of largest Phase-A verbatim deficit (2,192); X->E and X->G in F2 form at 2 seeds (1,096); memory-absent F3/F4 (both prompts with the item field empty collapse to the reader writing an item from the task description alone) for the 3 readers at 2 seeds (1,644); F4 matched (Q rewriting Q-self items) at 2 seeds (548). Total about 60,300 episodes. At the 9-10 episodes/min across both replicas that the previous version's 2-day estimate for 27,000 episodes assumed, Phase A+A' (36,700 episodes; decides P1, P2 and Gate 0) takes about 2.7 days and Phase B about 1.7 days; pool collection and the offline F3/F4/F5 passes (about 11,400 reader passes and 3 x 1,900 rewriter passes, 4-6 GPU-hours) overlap on the second GPU during single-reader phases. Readers are sequenced Q, E, G with both GPUs serving the current reader and clients spread across replicas tuned to the vLLM queue; a reader's offline passes run while it is served. Pre-registered cut order if Phase B overruns: X, then F6 to one cell, then F3/F4 to the 4 cross cells with the largest Phase-A verbatim deficit. Capability is held fixed by construction: every claim is a within-cell contrast of serializations of the same items to the same reader; the 3 x 3 writer x reader factor is reported but no ordering claim is made on it.

### proposal: complementarity_not_proximity
Title: Complementarity, Not Proximity: What a Reader Keeps of a Foreign Writer's Experience Is Set by the Writer's Solutions to the Reader's Own Failures, Not by Action Agreement, Item Count or Optimality
Short Hypothesis: For success-filtered experience pools collected on the same task set at the same episode budget and serialized in the reader's scaffold, the net gain a reader keeps from a foreign writer's pool over its own matched-cost pool is governed by complementarity, the writer's solutions to the games the reader fails, summarized offline as kappa(W,R) = sum_t share_t * max(0, p_W(t) - p_R(t)); it is not governed by policy proximity (writer-reader action agreement), by the writer's overall success, by item count, or by trajectory optimality. Concretely: at matched per-type item count a foreign pool beats the reader's own pool exactly in the cells where kappa >= 0.08 and not where kappa <= 0.02, including mutual gain between two equally capable backbones with different type profiles; lowering action agreement at fixed capability (same backbone, other temperature or scaffold) moves the gain by less than 0.4 of the Gate-0 effect, while raising capability at the same lowered agreement moves it by more than 0.25; and for one writer at identical item count, its solutions to games the reader fails carry the gain while its optimal solutions to games the reader already solves are worth no more than the reader's own solutions to those same games.

Experiments:
Setup: ALFWorld, 274 validation games (140 seen / 134 unseen), six task types; readers Q = Qwen3-32B (verified; ReAct, thinking off, T=0) and E = EXAONE-4.0-32B (Gate V: serves in vLLM 0.13 on one A100 and reaches k=0 >= 0.30 at 4 seeds, else dropped). Writers: X expert (on disk); Q-self (on disk); Q-prox: same backbone, candidates T=1.0 same scaffold, T=0.7 Act-first scaffold, T=1.0 paraphrased scaffold, screened on 274 x 1 seed; the chosen candidate has k=0 within +/-3 of Q-self and pi(Q-prox,Q) at least 0.10 below pi(Q-self,Q) and closest to pi(Q-cap,Q) (fallback: nearest candidate, pi gap reported as covariate); Q-cap: thinking on, thoughts stripped so serialization equals Q-self, must reach k=0 >= Q-self + 12 on 274 x 2 (fallback: the Measurement-B QLoRA writer); E-self; G-self (gpt-oss-120b; Gate V-G: serves and collects at success >= 0.30, else dropped). Every backbone writer runs the same 3,553 training games once (matched episode budget; GPU-hours reported). Bank construction, identical for all pools: success filter; then per type t draw n_R(t) items at random (one fixed seed) where n_R(t) is the reader's own success count on t; a writer with fewer than n_R(t) successes on t contributes all of them and the deficit is filled with the reader's own items (fill fraction reported; a cell with fill > 10% gets its own self subsample R_match as comparator, reserve two cells = 3,288 episodes). Same retriever (task-description embedding, type-agnostic cosine), same injection template (reader's scaffold), k=3 throughout (the largest static residual, +17.2). Offline statistics before any transfer cell: pi(W,R) on 300 stratified states per pool (~1 GPU-hour); kappa(W,R) from the writer's per-type success on the collection run and the reader's k=0 per-type validation success (Q: existing runs plus 2 seeds to reach >= 4; E: 4 seeds, ~180 episodes per type); each cross cell is classed high (kappa >= 0.08), low (<= 0.02) or unclassed and the table is frozen. Cells (274 games x seeds): reader Q: static3 x6 (4 new), Q-self x6, Q-prox_m x6, Q-cap_m x6, X_m x6, E_m x6, G_m x6, triple self_int / X_int / X_comp x6 each (per type n(t) = min(n_R(t), reader failure count on t); self_int = the reader's own trajectories on a random subset G_t of its solved games, X_int = expert trajectories on the same G_t, X_comp = expert trajectories on an equal-size random subset of the reader's failed games of type t), blind3 placebo x6 (wrong-type items at the k=3 token count), X natural on disk x2 (coverage ablation): 11 new cells = 17,536 episodes (+548 k0). Reader E: k0 x4, static3 x6, E-self x6, X_m x6, Q-self_m x6, G_m x6 = 9,316. Collection: 4 writers x 3,553 = 14,212, screens ~1,400. Total ~43,000 episodes (1.8x the previous plan's 24,000 at ~3 days), about 5-6 days: GPU1 collects Q-cap, Q-prox, E-self, G-self while GPU0 serves reader Q; then both GPUs serve readers with clients spread across replicas; Gate V failures shrink it (no E: -14,500; no G: -6,800). Analysis: paired game x seed contrasts with cluster bootstrap CIs (2,000 resamples); rho per cell with CI; P2 cell-level tests plus a Fisher exact 2x2 (kappa class x outcome) over the 8 cross cells; per-type and seen/unseen breakdowns descriptive. Pre-registration of Gate V, manipulation checks, the frozen kappa table, margins and the cell list before the first confirmatory cell.

Baselines and Ablations:
k=0 per reader; static3 (Measurement C: 3 fixed type-conditioned expert exemplars, ~430 tokens) per reader, every gain reported net of it; the reader's own success-filtered pool at matched episode budget (denominator of rho and comparator of every Delta); count-matched vs natural expert pool for reader Q (coverage component of the natural rho(X->Q), whose numerator Measurement C already puts at +17.2 over static3); the self_int / X_int / X_comp triple (single-factor contrasts: writer identity at identical games and count, and game subset at identical writer and count); blind3 token-matched wrong-type placebo for reader Q (context-length bound; Measurement C's blind1 is -3 under k0 at k=1); manipulation-check cells reported in their own right (Q-prox k=0 and pi; Q-cap k=0 and pi); writer-denominator ratio rho_w for the Q<->E pair (Q-self_m->E over Q-self->Q, E_m->Q over E-self->E); R_match self subsample for any cell with fill > 10%; a second 2x2 with the writer's overall k=0 advantage as classifier, the competing predictor; seen vs unseen and per-type gains descriptive.
