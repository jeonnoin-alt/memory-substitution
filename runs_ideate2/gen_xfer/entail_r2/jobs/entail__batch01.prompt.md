### proposal: coverage_not_headcount
Title: Coverage, Not Headcount: What a Population of Writers Adds to an Agent's Experience Store at Matched Collection Cost
Short Hypothesis: At a fixed total collection budget, an experience store written by several policies is worth more to a reader than the reader's own store only by the task-type coverage the other writers add (types on which some writer succeeds more often than the reader), a statistic computable from the writers' collection logs before any reading. Once coverage, budget and per-type item counts are held fixed by construction, the number of writers (2 vs 8), the class of their diversity (scaffold/temperature variants of the reader's backbone vs other backbones) and the retrieval-time conflict among their items each move the reader's gain by less than the Gate-0 effect of 6 net, whereas a coverage change at the same budget and headcount moves it by at least that much; displacing half of the reader's own budget by a writer it dominates on every type costs nothing.

Experiments:

Each additional writer collects B/2 episodes on a random half of the train games (disjoint halves for pairs; the eighths used for N=8 are nested inside the halves), success-filtered, same trajectory format: Qwen3-32B plan-then-act; Qwen3-32B ReAct at T=0.7; Qwen3-32B ReAct with a self-check turn; Qwen3-32B ReAct at T=1.2 (the pre-registered dominated writer); EXAONE-4.0-32B ReAct and gpt-oss-120b ReAct at the full B (they are also those readers' self pools; halves reused); gpt-oss-120b plan-then-act at B/2 (eighth writer). Expert walkthroughs (1,465 items, no episode cost) are an item-matched writer reported separately. Per-writer per-type success profiles s_w(t) are logged at collection. Collection: 4 x 1,777 + 3,553 + 3,553 + 1,777 = 15,991 episodes.
- Predictor. Coverage complement C(S,R) = sum_t w_t * max(0, max_{w in S with items retained on t} s_w(t) - s_R(t)), w_t the validation type share; computed from collection logs and frozen, together with every store's composition, before the first evaluation cell.
- Stores at matched budget B (Qwen3-32B reader unless noted). SELF = self(B). DOM = self(B/2)+dominated(B/2): T=1.2 if s_w(t) <= s_self(t) on all six types; if it exceeds self on a type, its items on that type are dropped and the store is per-type item-count-equalized to SELF, so C(DOM) = 0 by construction. HCE (N=4) = HCM writers plus the expert item-matched at a quarter share (the earlier HET-cap; secondary cell). ### proposal: one_bad_apple_population_vote
Title: One Bad Apple in the Top-k: Contamination Geometry of a Population Store and the Cross-Writer Vote That Detects It
Short Hypothesis: A benign-looking wrong record (a success-formatted trajectory with a wrong procedure, or a failed trajectory relabelled as a success) planted in a multi-writer experience store harms a reader by whether it is present among the retrieved items at all rather than by its share of them: one wrong item among three costs most of the harm of three, one wrong item among seven costs most of the harm of one alone, and under natural retrieval harm tracks the logged chance that a wrong item is present rather than its mean share, so dilution by benign writers recovers only what it removes from the top-k. The only reader-side signal that scales with the population is disagreement of the item's procedure skeleton with same-type items written by other, independent writers, whose recall rises with the number of independent benign writers and is not beaten by the reader's own critique of the item; provenance exclusion is blind to poison carrying a trusted label and is costed on legitimate reuse.

Experiments:
- Clean population store P: Qwen3-32B self pool, EXAONE-4.0-32B pool, gpt-oss-120b pool (B/2 episodes each; shared with coverage_not_headcount if run, else collected here at ~1.5-4 GPU-h per writer on the second GPU while the first serves the reader), expert walkthroughs; provenance tags on every item; same retriever and injection as Measurement A. Every retrieval is logged per game (top-m ids, writer, rank, planted flag), so each cell's exposure rate e (fraction of games with >= 1 planted item in the top-k) and mean planted share s are measured, not assumed.
- Poison manufacture on heat/clean/cool (Measurement A gains +39..+55 there): (a) procedure grafts: successful trajectories of a given writer with the procedure step swapped or deleted (microwave<->fridge, sink step removed), navigation and object-location content untouched; (b) relabelled failures: the writer's own failed train trajectories of the type with the outcome label set to success (Qwen3 has 1,661 failed train games); (c) positive control: the same count of items carrying an explicit wrong instruction line. Carrier identity in {self (Qwen3-written), foreign (EXAONE-written)}, graft content held fixed across carriers so only style varies. Planted lists frozen and pre-registered: n_p per target type equal to the Qwen3 pool's count of successful items of that type (target 60 per type, 180 per carrier), so the 1-writer natural share is about 0.5. Poison items length-matched to the items they replace.
- Gate 0 (feasibility gate, not a prediction): a kind proceeds if its harm at share 1 (all top-3 items poisoned, k=3, Qwen3 reader) is >= 6 net on target-type games vs the clean store. Ratio precondition: the geometry and detector arms are scored only when the share-1 harm h of the kind they use is >= 24 net at 8 seeds; they use the most harmful benign-looking kind, and if none reaches 24 they use the positive control (pre-registered fallback) and the benign-looking result is reported as the G13 answer.
- Share geometry at k=3: controlled retrieved share in {1/3, 2/3, 1} by deterministic mixing of the nearest planted items into the top-3, poison positions randomized per game.
- Count-fixed k geometry: exactly one planted item forced into the top-k at k in {1, 3, 7} (share 1, 1/3, 1/7), the clean store at the same k as the paired reference; the k=3 cell is the share-1/3 cell.
- Dilution by writers (natural retrieval, n_p fixed): benign writers covering the target types 1 -> 2 -> 4 (Qwen3 alone; +EXAONE; +gpt-oss and expert), expected share 0.5 -> 0.33 -> 0.2, e and s logged per cell.
- Detectors, scored by precision and recall against planted labels (unit: planted item, n = 180 per carrier; also per retrieval occurrence) before any downstream number: D1 cross-writer skeleton vote (retrieve m=8, parse each item to a procedure skeleton with the programmatic ALFWorld action parser, flag an item whose skeleton disagrees with the majority of same-type items from writers other than its own), run with 1, 2 and 3 other writers in the vote pool; D1-self (same m, pool restricted to the item's own writer); D3 reader LLM critique of each retrieved item ('is this a correct way to do the task?', ~500 tokens per item), in-context and memory-absent; D4 provenance exclusion of the lowest-collection-success writer (blind to poison under a trusted label; costed on the clean store only). Downstream: flagged items replaced by the next unflagged item in rank order, run at share 1/3 (one planted item in the top-3, so recovery is bounded by recall under either geometry) for D1 and D3.
- Readers: Qwen3-32B (all cells); EXAONE-4.0-32B (clean, k=0, grafts at share 2/3 with EXAONE-written and Qwen3-written carriers), so the carrier x reader crossing varies proximity at fixed carrier capability.
- Budget (target-type games ~110; 8 seeds = 880 paired episodes per cell). Qwen3 reader, 16 cells at 8 seeds: clean k=3; graft share 1, 2/3, 1/3; positive control share 1; relabelled share 1; clean and one-item at k=1; clean and one-item at k=7; dilution 1/2/4 writers; D1 and D3 downstream at share 1/3; EXAONE-carrier grafts share 2/3 = 14,080. Qwen3 reader, 4 cells at 4 seeds: k=0; static-procedure prompt; token-matched placebo; D4 on clean = 1,760. Specificity: look-at-in-light games, clean vs planted, 8 seeds, ~720. EXAONE reader: clean k=3, EXAONE-carrier 2/3, Qwen3-carrier 2/3 at 8 seeds and k=0 at 4 seeds = 3,080. Total ~19,600 episodes plus ~7,000 items x 2 variants x ~500 tokens of offline D3 critique. At the ~7,000 episodes/day of the earlier estimate this is about 3 days, about 3.5 with the heavier k=7 and D3 cells, on two A100s (one serving the reader, the other collecting pools, then serving the second reader and D3), plus at most one day under the add-seeds cap.

Baselines and Ablations:
- Clean store P at k=1/3/7, k=0 and the static-procedure prompt (Measurement C) as the floor: harm is reported relative to the clean store at the same k, and clean-store gain relative to the static prompt, for both readers (EXAONE's clean gain also as a retained-gain ratio against Qwen3's gain from the same store). No transfer benefit is claimed, so no self-pool-at-matched-cost arm is required.
- Positive control (explicit wrong instruction, share 1, 8 seeds) run before any benign-looking kind and held as the pre-registered fallback poison for the geometry arms.
- Token-matched placebo: the same number of benign items from the same writer replacing the poison at the same positions (rules out item-count and position effects).
- Specificity control: grafts planted on look-at-in-light, where memory gain is about zero, expected harmless.
- D1 with m in {5,8,12} and with 1/2/3 other writers in the pool (offline on the retrieval logs, no episodes); skeleton parser accuracy on 100 hand-labelled items; D3 in-context vs memory-absent; D3 and D1 token cost per episode reported side by side.
- Provenance exclusion D4 on the clean store: net points lost on legitimate reuse, reported next to detector token costs.

Falsifiable Predictions:
Decision rule for every prediction: the named contrast is estimated by a joint bootstrap over games with seeds as blocks; the prediction is falsified when the 95% CI lies entirely on the falsifying side of the boundary and confirmed when it lies entirely on the predicted side; a straddling CI triggers the add-seeds rule (cap 12) and is otherwise reported inconclusive. h is the share-1 harm of the poison kind used, measured in the same run (precondition h >= 24 net at 8 seeds); each prediction names the falsifying world and its distance from the boundary at h = 24.
- P1 (grafts destroy the gain). Contrast: harm(grafts, share 1) / gain(clean P vs k=0) on heat/clean/cool, Qwen3 reader, k=3. Boundary 0.5; predicted >= 0.5. Falsifying world: the reader keeps most of the gain from the graft's untouched navigation and object-location content and takes the procedure from its prior, ratio <= 0.3 (with gain ~45 and harm and gain each +/-6.5, the ratio CI is about +/-0.15, so 0.3 is more than one CI from the boundary). Arm: graft share 1 vs clean vs k=0, 8 seeds. Gate 0 (>= 6 net) is a separate feasibility gate and is not a prediction.
- P2 (one bad apple; headline). Contrast D2 = harm(share 1) - harm(share 1/3) at k=3, the recovery from removing two of three wrong items, a directly paired difference between two poisoned arms (+/-6.5). Boundary 0.35 h; predicted D2 <= 0.35 h (presence gives D2 near 0). Falsifying world: harm proportional to share, D2 = 0.67 h (16 net at h = 24, more than one CI above the 8.4-net boundary); a majority-following reader gives D2 = h. Arm: share 1 vs 1/3 at k=3 (share 2/3 reported as the middle point), positions randomized, 8 seeds.
- P3 (one bad apple survives k). Contrast D3k = harm(one item at k=1) - harm(one item at k=7), each harm against the clean store at its own k (double difference, +/-9). Boundary 0.4 h1 with h1 = harm(one item at k=1); predicted D3k <= 0.4 h1. Falsifying world: harm follows share, one item among seven costs h1/7 and D3k = 0.86 h1 (about 21 net at h1 = 24, more than one CI above the 9.6-net boundary); a majority-following reader produces it. Arm: k in {1,3,7}, one forced item, clean at the same k, 8 seeds; k=3 reported as the middle point.
- P4 (dilution acts only through exposure). Contrast X = mean over the 1/2/4-writer cells of [harm_cell - h x s_cell], the pooled excess of harm over the share model, with s_cell each cell's logged mean planted share (about 0.5/0.33/0.2). Boundary 0.17 h; predicted X >= 0.17 h (presence model: harm_cell = h x e_cell with e = 1-(1-s)^3, giving X about 0.34 h). Falsifying world: harm = h x s_cell, X = 0 (a majority-following reader gives X about -0.06 h). X carries about +/-4 net (+/-0.17 h at h = 24), so both model values sit one CI from the 4-net boundary. Arm: dilution cells, natural retrieval, 8 seeds. Reported alongside, not scored: recovery from 1 to 4 writers as a fraction of the 1-writer cell's own harm (presence predicts about 45%, share 60%).
- P5 (relabelled failures are the lesser poison). Contrast D5 = harm(grafts, share 1) - harm(relabelled failures, share 1), paired (+/-6.5). Boundary 0.5 h; predicted D5 >= 0.5 h (skeletons of failed runs are mostly right; the reader copies procedure, not outcome). Falsifying world: the reader copies the failed run's wandering as readily as a wrong procedure, D5 near 0 (12 net below the boundary at h = 24). Arm: relabelled vs graft at share 1, 8 seeds. A relabelled harm below Gate 0 is a confirming outcome and is reported as the answer to G13's benign-record question; detector cells run on grafts regardless.
- P6 (the detector needs the population). D1 with 3 other independent writers in the vote pool reaches recall >= 0.7 at precision >= 0.7 on grafts and exceeds D1-self recall by >= 0.2; recall rises monotonically from 1 to 3 other writers with the 3-minus-1 difference >= 0.15. Falsified if D1-self is within 0.1 of D1 (one writer's redundancy suffices), if D1 recall < 0.5 (parser failure or natural skeleton variation floods the vote), or if the 1-other-writer pool already reaches the 3-writer recall within 0.05. Unit: planted items (n = 180 per carrier; Wilson CI about +/-0.07 at 0.7, +/-0.1 on a difference) and retrieval occurrences. Arm: vote-pool composition at fixed m=8, planted labels as ground truth, offline on the retrieval logs.

Measurement and Noise Control:
Harm is the paired per-game net success on target-type games (about 110 of the 274) with the clean store at the same k as the paired reference. Every claim-bearing cell runs at 8 seeds (880 paired episodes); scaling the brief's +/-8 at 274 x 2 seeds gives about +/-6.5 on a paired harm and on a paired difference between two poisoned arms that share games and seeds, and about +/-9 on a double difference across k. Add-2-seeds rule when a CI straddles a boundary, cap 12 seeds. Ratio-type contrasts (P1, P4, P7a) and every boundary expressed as a fraction of h are estimated by joint bootstrap over games with seeds as blocks, so the shared term h (which enters P2, P3, P4, P5, P7 and P8) is resampled together with the numerator. ### proposal: reader_pays_writer_detours
Title: The Reader Pays the Writer's Detours: Consumer-Side Cost of Transferred Experience Tracks the Writer's Episode Length, Not Its Success
Short Hypothesis: The consumer-side cost of using a foreign experience pool (served tokens, generated and observation tokens and steps per episode) is inherited from the writer's trajectory length: a reader copies the step structure of the retrieved item, so on-policy self pools (long, with detours) cost more per successful episode than minimal expert walkthroughs at equal success. Pruning replay-verified detours from self items at fixed content removes the inherited cost without losing success, and padding expert walkthroughs with benign detours adds it; consequently the ranking of writers by gain per consumer token differs from their ranking by success, and cost-matched comparisons across writers must charge the reader's served tokens, not only the injected ones.

Experiments:
Preparation (CPU env replay, about half a day): render all pools into one action-observation item format by replaying the writer's actions; label every item step by its replayed state diff (empty diff, including 'Nothing happens' actions and revisits without change, = detour); build self-pruned (all empty-diff steps removed, replay-verified to still complete the task; items failing replay dropped and counted by writer); at read time build expert-padded (d detour steps, d uniform on 0..14 drawn per game and seed, sampled from the self pool's detour distribution and inserted at random positions between walkthrough actions, replay-verified) and expert-corrupted (every retrieved item receives d uniform on 1..14 state-changing detours, a take/put pair that relocates the task object, so the replayed walkthrough fails). Retrieval ordering is computed once on the unmanipulated pools and reused in every cell, so a manipulation changes only the injected items' content and length. Item lengths are logged (mean steps: expert, self-raw, self-pruned, expert-padded by dose, EXAONE-raw, EXAONE-pruned). Collection: EXAONE k=0 pool on the 3,553 train games (about 3 GPU-hours) on one A100 while the other serves the Qwen3 reader cells; each self pool is one k=0 pass per backbone over the same games, so foreign and self pools share the collection budget. Reader cells on the 274 eval games: 548 episodes per 2 seeds (about 30 min per replica), 1,096 per 4 seeds. Qwen3 reader at k=3, 4 seeds: expert (replayed), expert-padded, self-raw, self-pruned, expert-corrupted (5 x 1,096 = 5,480). Qwen3 reader at k=3, 2 seeds: EXAONE-raw, EXAONE-pruned, k=0 (2 seeds added to Measurement C's 2), static3 (2 seeds added), self-raw in the writer's native rendering (format control), expert-padded with observation text truncated to the unpadded item's token count and self-raw with observation text truncated to the pruned item's token count (token-matched length controls) (7 x 548 = 3,836). EXAONE reader at k=3, 4 seeds: Qwen3-raw, Qwen3-pruned, own-raw, own-pruned (4 x 1,096 = 4,384). EXAONE reader at k=3, 2 seeds: expert, k=0, static3 (3 x 548 = 1,644). Total 19 cells, 15,344 episodes, about 14 replica-hours plus the 3-hour collection, about 8.5 wall-hours on two A100s; the pre-registered contingency (2 more seeds on up to eight manipulation and neutrality cells, 4,384 episodes, about 4 replica-hours) keeps the study under 2 days. Per episode we log success, steps, generated tokens, observation tokens, injected tokens, prompt tokens per request (summed as served tokens), the retrieved items' ids, doses and step counts, and whether the step cap was hit. Analyses: (i) paired success differences per cell with bootstrap CIs over games, stratified by type; (ii) reader steps and served tokens per episode, all episodes with right-censoring at the step cap (Tobit) and successful-only; (iii) the length-inheritance slope per manipulation pair and reader: within-game, within-seed change in reader steps regressed on the change in mean retrieved-item steps, Tobit at the cap as primary and OLS on jointly successful episodes as secondary; (iv) gain per 1k served tokens with bootstrap CIs, and the retained-gain ratio per writer x reader cell (reader's gain from the writer's pool over the writer's own gain from the same pool, and over the reader's self-pool gain), in success and per served token; (v) the pruning rule scored by precision and recall of its step removals against the replayed state-diff labels.

Baselines and Ablations:
k=0 (4 seeds for the Qwen3 reader, 2 for EXAONE); static3, the best static-procedure prompt of Measurement C (3 fixed type-conditioned expert exemplars, about 430 tokens), at 4 seeds for Qwen3 and 2 for EXAONE, every gain reported net of it; the wrong-type placebo blind1 reused from Measurement C (paired on the same games and seeds, no rerun); self-pool control at matched collection cost (each reader's own k=0 pass over the same 3,553 train games as the foreign pool); retained-gain ratio per writer x reader cell with CIs; expert-corrupted as the manufactured, positively controlled contamination arm (Gate 0: its harm must be at least 6 net for the neutrality cells to count as sensitive); token-matched length controls (padded items with observation text truncated to the unpadded token count; raw self items truncated to the pruned token count) to separate item steps from item tokens, which also compares the adaptation step (pruning) with direct injection of the unmodified item at matched tokens; native-rendering control for the self pool (writer's own reasoning text kept, item steps unchanged) to show format does not carry the length effect; the k=3 vs k=7 injected-token difference of Measurement C (430 vs 1,010 tokens) as the accounting positive control. Cut from the previous version because they carried no prediction: the k=1 cells, random-deletion pruning (replaced by the state-diff labels and by the padding pair, which varies length in the opposite direction with content held fixed) and the own-vs-other detour-source padding.

Falsifiable Predictions:
P1 (headline, length inheritance): for the Qwen3 reader at k=3, in each manipulation pair (expert vs expert-padded, dose 0..14; self-raw vs self-pruned), the within-game, within-seed change in reader steps regressed on the change in mean retrieved-item steps over all 1,096 paired episodes (Tobit at the step cap) has a slope of at least 0.3 with a 95% CI lower bound above 0.1. Falsifier: the slope's 95% CI upper bound is below 0.3 in either pair (the reader plans from the item rather than copying its path). Reachable: with 1,096 pairs, a residual SD of about 10 steps and an item-step-change SD of 4.3 (padding) or about 2.5 (pruning, pilot-estimated), the SE is about 0.07 and 0.12, so a true slope of 0 gives an upper bound of about 0.14 and 0.24, and a true slope of 0.1 is rejected in the padding pair with power about 0.8; the achieved SE is reported and the contingency seeds are pre-registered. Arm: the four manipulation cells at 4 seeds; injected tokens rise with padding by construction and are excluded from this estimand. P2 (pruning is success-neutral): in both readers at 4 seeds, self-pruned minus self-raw lies within 6 net with the 90% CI inside [-6, +6] (paired MDE about 5.3 net). Falsifier: pruned at least 6 net below raw with the 95% CI excluding 0, the outcome the cue-discarding account of arxiv:2605.24117 predicts; arm: the pruned vs raw cells (Qwen3 own pair, EXAONE own pair). P3 (padding is success-neutral, gated): expert-padded minus expert lies within 6 net (90% CI inside [-6, +6]) while expert-corrupted is at least 6 net below expert (the positive control). Falsifier: padded at least 6 net below expert with CI excluding 0 (detours contaminate content, not only cost); if the corrupted arm fails to reach 6 net harm the cell is declared insensitive and P3 is reported as untested, not confirmed; arm: expert-padded, expert-corrupted and expert cells. P4 (cost reranks writers): for the Qwen3 reader, gain per 1k served tokens (net success over k=0 divided by mean served tokens per episode, bootstrap CI over games) of the expert pool exceeds that of self-raw by at least 25% with the ratio's 95% CI lower bound above 1.0, while the paired success gains of the two pools are within 8 net. Falsifier: the ratio's CI upper bound is at most 1.0 (self-raw's gain per served token is at least the expert's), or the success gap is at least 8 net with CI excluding 0 so that the cost ranking merely follows success; arm: expert vs self-raw cells at 4 seeds with token accounting. P5 (foreign reader inherits, no self-recognition): EXAONE reading Qwen3-raw vs Qwen3-pruned at 4 seeds (same estimand and count as P1) shows a slope of at least 0.3, and within the EXAONE reader the slope on its own pair (own-raw vs own-pruned, 4 seeds) minus the slope on the Qwen3 pair has a 95% CI that includes 0 (SE of the difference about 0.17). Falsifiers, each with its own CI and neither conditional on P1's outcome: (a) the Qwen3-pair slope's CI upper bound is below 0.3; (b) the own-minus-foreign slope difference is at least 0.2 with its CI excluding 0 (inheritance is partly self-recognition); arm: the four EXAONE 4-seed cells; the Qwen3 reader's EXAONE-raw vs EXAONE-pruned pair at 2 seeds is reported as a wider-CI replication. None is entailed: the reader may ignore item structure (P1, P5), detours may carry object-location cues (P2), inserted detours may mislead (P3), and cost may simply track success (P4); P4's token gap is partly produced by P1's slope, but P4 fails independently whenever self-raw's success gain exceeds the expert's by 8 net or its per-token ratio is not above 1.

Measurement and Noise Control:
All cells are paired on the same 274 games with the same retrieval ordering; bootstrap over games, stratified by type. Success: Measurement C's paired CI half-width at 274 x 2 seeds is about 5.25 net (SE 2.7), the 8-net floor of the brief; the neutrality cells run at 4 seeds, where episode-level noise halves in variance (SE about 1.9, half-width 3.7, MDE about 5.3 net at 80% power), so the 6-net margin is above the resolvable MDE; the achieved SE is computed from the data and, if the achieved MDE exceeds 6 net, the neutrality cells get 2 more seeds from the contingency rather than the margin being reported as met. Cross-writer success differences keep the 8-net margin. Slope: the estimand is the within-game, within-seed paired difference, so eval-game difficulty cancels; the dose design (d uniform on 0..14, drawn per game and seed) makes the item-step change vary within game across seeds; expected SE 0.07 (padding) and 0.12 (pruning) at 1,096 pairs, from residual SD about 10 steps (all episodes) and item-step-change SD 4.3 and 2.5; after the first 2 seeds the achieved SDs are used to project the 4-seed SE and, if it exceeds 0.08 (padding) or 0.14 (pruning), the manipulation cells extend to 6 seeds within the contingency budget. Censoring: reader steps are right-censored at the run's step cap; the slope is estimated by Tobit on all episodes (primary) and by OLS on jointly successful episodes (secondary), and the censoring fraction per cell is reported. Cost: served tokens are the sum of prompt and completion tokens over all requests in an episode from the vLLM usage fields; generated, observation and injected tokens are also summed per episode, and injected tokens are reported separately so the by-construction part of any cost change is visible. Order: k=0, static3 and the unmanipulated writers first; manipulation cells only after the replayed expert cell reproduces Measurement C's k3 within 8 net (k3 minus static3 = +17.2, CI about +/-5). Throughput and queue occupancy per replica are logged; the EXAONE collection overlaps the Qwen3 reader cells so both A100s stay busy.
