### proposal: reconstruction_pays_only_across_the_writer_gap
Title: Reconstruction Pays Only Across the Writer Gap: Reader-Side Rewriting of Foreign Experience Helps by Policy Distance, Not Writer Capability, at Matched Tokens
Short Hypothesis: Training-free read-time reconstruction (the reader rewrites the k=3 retrieved items into one plan in its own action grammar, conditioned on its first observation; the plan replaces the items) adds nothing over verbatim injection at k=7 (a comparator with at least the reconstruction arm's consumer tokens and more items) when the writer is the reader itself, and adds an increment of at least one third of the Gate-0 verbatim gain (about 6.5 net given the measured k7 - static3 = +19.5) when the writer is a different 32B backbone or the same backbone under a different action scaffold. ### proposal: rewriter_trained_against_one_reader_matched_cost

### proposal: shared_blind_spot
Title: Shared Blind Spots: A Reader Is Most Harmed by, and Least Able to Detect, Contaminated Experience Written by Its Own Policy
Short Hypothesis: Contamination in an experience bank propagates in proportion to writer-reader policy proximity: failed trajectories relabelled as successes are more harmful per planted item and less detectable at read time when written by the reader's own policy or a same-backbone near variant than when written by a distant backbone, because read-time detection is the reader's own plausibility judgment, which endorsed those actions when it produced them. Consequences that the design tests: the harm ordering reverses when the reader is swapped (self > far for each reader, so it is proximity, not a property of any writer's failures); a judge from a different backbone detects a writer's masked failures better than the writer's own backbone does (a diagonal deficit in the judge x writer table) and the deficit survives when the failed terminal state is visible but the failure label is not; filtering the bank by a foreign judge recovers harm that filtering by the reader's own judgment cannot; manufactured wrong-procedure grafts (positive control) are harmful regardless of writer.
