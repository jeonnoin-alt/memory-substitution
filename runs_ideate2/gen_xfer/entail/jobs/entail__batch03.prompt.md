### proposal: coverage_not_headcount
Title: Coverage, Not Headcount: What a Population of Writers Adds to an Agent's Experience Store at Matched Collection Cost
Short Hypothesis: At a fixed total collection budget, an experience store written by several policies is worth more to a reader than the reader's own store only by the task-type coverage the other writers add (types on which some writer succeeds more often than the reader), a statistic computable from the writers' collection logs before any reading; the number of writers, and whether their diversity comes from proximity (scaffold or temperature variants of one backbone) or from capability (other backbones), add no predictive power once coverage is accounted for, and retrieval-time conflict rises with writers without governing gain.

Experiments:
- Writers and pools. B = 3,553 train episodes (the on-disk Qwen3-32B self pool, 1,892 successes). Each additional writer collects B/2 episodes on a random half of the train games (disjoint halves for pairs), success-filtered, same trajectory format: Qwen3-32B plan-then-act scaffold; Qwen3-32B ReAct at T=0.7; Qwen3-32B ReAct with a self-check turn; EXAONE-4.0-32B ReAct; gpt-oss-120b ReAct (both after vLLM serving is verified); expert walkthroughs as an item-matched writer (1,465 items, no episode cost, reported separately). Per-writer per-type success profiles s_w(t) are logged at collection.
- Stores at matched budget B. self(B); pairs self(B/2)+X(B/2) for every X; N=4 HET-prox = four Qwen3-32B variants at B/4 each (proximity varied at fixed capability); N=4 HET-cap = {Qwen3-32B ReAct, EXAONE, gpt-oss-120b, expert item-matched} at B/4 each (capability and family varied at fixed scaffold); N=4 equal-coverage control = the N=2 {Qwen3, gpt-oss} store plus two Qwen3 variants whose items are restricted to types on which they do not raise the store's max s_w(t), so the coverage complement is unchanged by construction while N and item diversity rise; N=8 all writers at B/8.
- Predictor. Coverage complement C(S,R) = sum_t w_t * max(0, max_{w in S} s_w(t) - s_R(t)), with w_t the validation type share; computed from collection logs and frozen before the first evaluation cell.
- Reading. Same retriever and injection as Measurement A; k=3 for all stores and k=1 for self(B), HET-cap and HET-prox; readers Qwen3-32B (all stores), EXAONE-4.0-32B (self_EXAONE(B), pairs with Qwen3 and gpt-oss, HET-cap, HET-prox) and gpt-oss-120b (self_gptoss(B), HET-cap). 274 validation games (seen+unseen) x 2 seeds, 4 seeds on headline cells.
- Outcomes per cell. Net success vs k=0; gain net of the static-procedure prompt (Measurement C); retained-gain ratio gain_R(S)/gain_R(self_R(B)) and, for backbone writers, gain_R(S)/gain_w(S); per-type gains; retrieval-time conflict rate (fraction of top-3 sets containing >= 2 distinct procedure skeletons for the type, from the programmatic ALFWorld action parser); below-self share (fraction of retrieved items from writers with s_w(t) < s_R(t)); injected tokens.
- Analysis. Spearman correlation of gain-over-own-pool with C across all store x reader cells; partial correlations of N, diversity class and conflict rate with C controlled; per-type decomposition of the HET-cap gain.
- Schedule. GPU0 serves the reader under evaluation while GPU1 collects the next writer's pool or serves the second reader; ~10 GPU-h of collection, ~24 evaluation cells (~14,500 episodes), about 2.5 days with both A100s busy.

Baselines and Ablations:
- k=0 memory-free reader.
- Static-procedure prompt (Measurement C), per type: every transfer gain is reported net of it.
- self(B) at k=3 and k=1 (matched-cost self-pool control) and self(B/2) alone (what displacing half the budget costs before any writer is added).
- Expert-only bank (Measurement A reference), with item-matched and episode-matched accounting reported side by side.
- Per-type item-count-equalized versions of the stores (separates coverage from item count).
- Two retrievers (BM25 on task string; dense on full item text) for the conflict-rate and share statistics, computed offline.
- Reader-as-writer cells (each backbone reads the population store) for the ratio denominator.
- Skeleton parser validated on 100 hand-labelled items before conflict rates are used.

Falsifiable Predictions:
- P1. HET-cap N=4 at budget B beats self(B) by >= 6 net (Qwen3-32B reader, k=3) and beats the static-procedure prompt by >= 6 net on heat/clean/cool, with >= 70% of its gain over self(B) located in types where some writer's s_w(t) >= s_R(t)+0.2. Falsified if the gain over self(B) is <= +4, or if <= 50% of it falls in such types, or if HET-cap <= static-procedure + 4. Arm: HET-cap N=4 vs self(B) vs static-procedure, paired per game, 4 seeds, per-type breakdown.
- P2. Across >= 12 store x reader cells, gain over the reader's own matched pool has Spearman rho >= 0.7 with the frozen C, and N and diversity class have partial |rho| < 0.3 once C is controlled. Falsified if rho < 0.4, or if N or class has partial |rho| >= 0.5. Arm: the N in {1,2,4,8} x composition grid; in particular the equal-coverage N=4 control vs the N=2 store with identical C, where a difference >= 6 net in either direction falsifies (positive: diversity per se helps; negative: conflict or dilution costs).
- P3. Displacing half of the reader's own budget by a dominated writer (s_w(t) <= s_R(t) on all t; chosen from the measured profiles, else Qwen3-32B at T=1.2 is collected for it) changes the gain by within [-4, +4] net relative to self(B) at k=3. Falsified by <= -6 (item count, not coverage, governs) or >= +6 (phrasing diversity governs). Arm: self(B/2)+dominated(B/2) vs self(B), 4 seeds.
- P4. For the Qwen3-32B reader, HET-cap's gain over self(B) on heat/clean/cool is >= 60% of the expert-only bank's gain over self(B) on those types at matched item count (model writers recover most of what the expert writer gives). Falsified if it is <= 40% with CI excluding 60%. Arms: HET-cap, expert-only (item-matched), self(B), per type.
- P5. Retrieval-time conflict rate rises with N (N=8 >= N=2 + 0.15 absolute) but does not predict gain once C is controlled (partial |rho| < 0.3). Falsified if conflict rate has partial rho <= -0.5 with C controlled (conflict, not coverage, governs; contributor_conflict_not_size would be rescued). Arm: the N grid with conflict rate logged per cell.

Measurement and Noise Control:
Paired design on the same 274 games x seeds; per-game differences bootstrapped over games (10,000 resamples), seeds as blocks. Headline cells (self(B), HET-cap N=4, HET-prox N=4, equal-coverage control, dominated displacement) at 4 seeds (about +/-5.7 net for a cell, paired differences about +/-4.5); other cells at 2 seeds (+/-8) with a pre-registered rule to add 2 seeds when a CI straddles the margin (cap 6). Margins are fractions of the Gate-0 effect of 6 net (full = 6, half = 3). Per-type claims use the type's game count and carry their own CIs. Reader decoding fixed (T=0 or fixed sampling seed), environment seeds fixed. C is frozen before the first confirmatory cell. Injected tokens per episode reported for every cell; all transfer gains reported net of the static-procedure prompt; retained-gain ratios with bootstrap CIs (games resampled jointly across numerator and denominator arms).

### proposal: one_bad_apple_population_vote
Title: One Bad Apple in the Top-k: Contamination Geometry of a Population Store and the Cross-Writer Vote That Detects It
Short Hypothesis: A benign-looking wrong record (a success-formatted trajectory with a wrong procedure, or a failed trajectory relabelled as a success) planted in a multi-writer experience store harms a reader by whether it appears among the retrieved items at all rather than by its share of them, so harm grows with k at fixed store composition and dilution by benign writers recovers little; the only reader-side signal that scales with the population is disagreement of the item's procedure skeleton with same-type items written by other, independent writers, whose recall rises with the number of independent benign writers, while provenance exclusion is blind to poison carrying a trusted label and is costed on legitimate reuse.

Experiments:
- Clean population store P: Qwen3-32B self pool, EXAONE-4.0-32B pool, gpt-oss-120b pool (B/2 episodes each; shared with coverage_not_headcount if run, else collected here at ~1.5-4 GPU-h per writer), expert walkthroughs; provenance tags on every item; same retriever and injection as Measurement A.
- Poison manufacture on heat/clean/cool (Measurement A gains +39..+55 there): (a) procedure grafts: successful trajectories of a given writer with the procedure step swapped or deleted (microwave<->fridge, sink step removed), rest untouched; (b) relabelled failures: the writer's own failed train trajectories of the type with the outcome label set to success (Qwen3 has 1,661 failed train games); (c) positive control: the same count of items carrying an explicit wrong instruction line. Poison writer identity in {self (Qwen3-written), foreign (EXAONE-written), expert}; graft content (the wrong procedure) held fixed across identities so only the carrier's style varies. Poison items length-matched to the items they replace.
- Gate 0: each poison kind at controlled retrieved share 1 (all top-3 items poisoned) on target types; a kind proceeds only if harm >= 6 net on target-type games vs the clean store.
- Geometry, controlled: retrieved share in {1/3, 2/3, 1} at k=3 by deterministic mixing into the top-3 (poison positions randomized per game); and k in {1,3,7} at a fixed natural planting of n_p items per target type (expected share about 0.1 per type), with the clean store at the same k as the paired reference.
- Dilution by writers: fixed n_p, benign writers covering the target types increased 1 -> 2 -> 4 (Qwen3 alone; +EXAONE; +gpt-oss and expert), natural retrieval, so expected share falls from about 0.5 to about 0.2.
- Detectors, scored by precision and recall against planted labels over every retrieved item in the evaluation, then downstream: D1 cross-writer skeleton vote (retrieve m=8, parse each item to a procedure skeleton with the programmatic ALFWorld action parser, flag an item whose skeleton disagrees with the majority of same-type items from writers other than its own; requires >= 2 other writers); D1-self (same m, vote pool restricted to the item's own writer); D3 reader LLM critique of each retrieved item ('is this a correct way to do the task?', ~500 tokens per item), also run memory-absent (without episode context); D4 provenance exclusion of the lowest-collection-success writer (cannot see poison under a trusted label; costed on the clean store only). Flagged items are replaced by the next unflagged item.
- Readers: Qwen3-32B (all cells); EXAONE-4.0-32B (clean, grafts at share 2/3 with EXAONE-written and Qwen3-written carriers, D1) to obtain both directions of the carrier-proximity manipulation.
- Budget: about 19 cells; Gate-0, share-1/3 and k-sweep cells at 4 seeds; about 14,000 episodes, roughly 2 days on two replicas, with one GPU serving the reader and the other collecting any missing pool or serving the second reader.

Baselines and Ablations:
- Clean store P at k=1/3/7; k=0; static-procedure prompt (Measurement C) as the floor, so harm is reported relative to the clean store and to the static prompt.
- Positive control (explicit wrong instruction) run before any benign-looking kind.
- Token-matched placebo: the same number of benign items from the same writer replacing the poison at the same positions (rules out item-count and position effects).
- Specificity control: poison planted on look-at-in-light, where memory gain is about zero, expected harmless.
- D1 with m in {5,8,12}; skeleton parser accuracy on 100 hand-labelled items; D3 in-context vs memory-absent.
- Provenance exclusion D4 on the clean store: net points lost on legitimate reuse, reported next to detector token costs.

Falsifiable Predictions:
- P1 (Gate 0). Procedure grafts at share 1 cost >= 6 net on heat/clean/cool relative to the clean store (Qwen3 reader, k=3). Falsified if harm < 6 with CI excluding 6 (the reader takes procedures from its prior rather than from the retrieved item). Arm: grafts share 1 vs clean, 4 seeds.
- P2 (one bad apple). Harm at controlled share 1/3 is >= 60% of harm at share 1 for grafts. Falsified if it is <= 40% (harm proportional to share). Arm: share 1/3 vs 2/3 vs 1 at k=3, positions randomized, 4 seeds.
- P3 (harm grows with k at constant fraction). At natural planting with n_p fixed, harm at k=7 >= 2x harm at k=3, and harm at k=1 <= half of harm at k=3. Falsified if harm at k=7 <= harm at k=3 + 3 net (harm follows expected share, which is constant in k). Arm: k in {1,3,7} on the same poisoned store vs the clean store at the same k.
- P4 (dilution). Increasing benign writers covering the type from 1 to 4 at fixed n_p recovers < 50% of the Gate-0 harm at k=3. Falsified if >= 50% is recovered. Arm: the 1/2/4-writer dilution cells.
- P5 (relabelled failures). Relabelled failures harm <= half as much as grafts at share 1 (their skeletons are mostly right; the reader copies procedure, not outcome). Falsified if relabelled harm >= graft harm - 2 net. Arm: relabelled vs graft at share 1. If relabelled failures fail Gate 0 this is reported as the answer to G13's benign-record question and detector cells run on grafts only.
- P6 (detector needs the population). D1 with >= 3 independent benign writers in the vote pool reaches recall >= 0.7 at precision >= 0.7 on grafts, and its recall exceeds D1-self (same m, own-writer pool) by >= 0.2. Falsified if D1-self is within 0.1 of D1 (one writer's redundancy suffices) or if D1 recall < 0.5. Arm: vote-pool composition at fixed m=8, planted labels as ground truth.
- P7 (downstream and cost). D1 filtering recovers >= 70% of the Gate-0 harm at share 2/3 with <= 10% of D3's per-episode token overhead. Falsified if D1 recovers < 50%, or if D3 recovers more than D1 at equal token overhead. Arm: D1 vs D3 vs no detector at share 2/3.
- P8 (carrier proximity at fixed content). Qwen3-written grafts harm the Qwen3 reader more than EXAONE-written grafts with the identical wrong procedure at the same share, and the ordering reverses for the EXAONE reader. Falsified if the foreign carrier harms as much or more for either reader (harm depends on content only). Arm: carrier writer x reader, share 2/3.

Measurement and Noise Control:
Harm is the paired per-game net success on target-type games (about 40% of the 274) with the clean store as the paired reference, so target-type cells run at 4 seeds (about 110 games x 4 seeds; paired differences about +/-6) and the add-2-seeds rule applies when a CI straddles a margin (cap 6). Non-target games are the specificity control. Bootstrap over games with seeds as blocks. The 60%/50% thresholds are fractions of the share-1 harm measured in the same run, never of the 6-net floor. Detector precision and recall carry Wilson CIs over the retrieved-item population (thousands of items) and are reported per poison kind and per carrier identity. Poison positions in the top-k are randomized per game; planted-item lists are frozen and pre-registered; retriever, injection format and token counts are identical across arms.

### proposal: who_gets_read
Title: Who Gets Read: Implicit and Explicit Trust Weighting of Writers in a Shared Experience Store, Costed Against Uniform Retrieval
Short Hypothesis: A plain retriever over a multi-writer store already weights writers implicitly: when the query carries the reader's own trajectory text, items written by the reader's own policy are over-retrieved (style homophily) even after scaffold formatting is stripped, and this costs the reader on types where a foreign writer is better; explicit weighting by each writer's per-type collection success, a writer-side statistic that costs no reader episodes, beats both uniform retrieval and proximity weighting, reader-side calibrated weights add little and transfer across readers, so trust in a shared store should follow the writer's type-level record rather than its identity or its nearness to the reader.

Experiments:
- Store M with equal per-type item counts (subsampled to the smallest writer's count per type) from four writers with provenance tags: self = Qwen3-32B ReAct (on-disk pool), prox = Qwen3-32B plan-then-act (same backbone, different scaffold), cap = gpt-oss-120b ReAct (different backbone, same scaffold; EXAONE-4.0-32B if gpt-oss serving fails), expert walkthroughs. Two item formats: raw (scaffold text kept) and canonical (actions and observations only). Indexes: the Measurement-A retriever plus a dense full-text index; BM25 on task string as the second retriever.
- Offline retrieval-share audit (no episodes): queries Q-task (task string) and Q-traj (task string + the reader's first three steps taken from its existing k=0 logs); retrieved writer share per type and its excess over the 0.25 store share, for both formats and both retrievers, for Qwen3-32B and EXAONE readers (each reader's own writer is its self writer; EXAONE's pool is added to M for that audit).
- Downstream cost of homophily: M with uniform retrieval under Q-task@3 vs Q-traj@3 (both injecting at step 3 so only the query differs), Qwen3-32B reader, k=3, per type; Q-task@0 (Measurement-A protocol) as the timing control.
- Explicit weighting on M under Q-task@0, same retriever scores re-ranked: uniform; proximity (boost same backbone+scaffold); global capability (boost by the writer's overall collection success); per-type writer-side quality (boost by s_w(t) from collection logs, expert capped at the best model writer's rate); per-type reader-side calibrated quality (boost by the reader's own success with each single-writer bank on c=20 train games per writer x type, 480 episodes, counted as governance cost); random re-ranking placebo at the same boost strength. Boost strength alpha tuned on a 60-game train slice and frozen.
- Reader-side weights matched and mismatched: EXAONE reader with its own calibrated weights, with Qwen3's calibrated weights, and with writer-side weights; the memory-absent cell is k=0, where any re-ranker is inert by construction (reported, not claimed).
- Single-writer stores at the same per-type item count for each writer (self, prox, cap, expert) as ratio denominators: retained-gain ratio of M under each weighting relative to the best single-writer store and to self(B) at matched collection cost, per type; descriptive analysis of global vs per-type weighting against whether the writers' per-type rankings cross.
- Cost accounting in one unit: episodes spent (pools, calibration) and tokens per evaluation episode for each arm, next to net gain and ratio.
- Budget: about 16 evaluation cells plus 2 x 480 calibration episodes; headline cells at 4 seeds; about 12,000 episodes, roughly 2 days; pools for prox and cap writers about 6 GPU-h if not already on disk; one GPU serves the reader while the other collects pools, runs calibration, or serves the second reader.

Baselines and Ablations:
- k=0 memory-free reader; static-procedure prompt (Measurement C); self(B) matched-cost self pool; expert-only bank (Measurement A reference).
- Uniform M as the governance-free baseline; random re-ranking with the same alpha as the placebo for re-ranking itself; alpha sweep.
- Raw vs canonical items (formatting vs policy-level homophily); BM25 vs dense retriever; Q-task@0 vs Q-task@3 (injection-timing control).
- Oracle per-type best single writer (upper bound, reported not claimed).
- Per-type item counts equalized across writers so share deviations are not item-count artefacts.

Falsifiable Predictions:
- P1 (homophily exists and is not formatting). Under Q-traj with the dense full-text retriever, the self writer's retrieved share exceeds its 0.25 store share by >= 0.15 on raw items and by >= 0.08 on canonical items, while under Q-task the excess is < 0.05 in both formats. Falsified if the Q-traj excess on canonical items is < 0.03 (homophily is formatting only), or the Q-traj raw excess is < 0.05 (no homophily), or the Q-task excess is >= 0.15 (a document-style prior, not the reader's query, drives it). Arm: the offline audit cells (Q-task/Q-traj x raw/canonical x retriever) over 274 x 2 seeds of k=0 logs, for Qwen3 and EXAONE readers.
- P2 (homophily costs). On types where a foreign writer's s_w(t) exceeds self's by >= 0.2 (expected heat/clean/cool), M-uniform under Q-traj@3 delivers >= 6 net less than under Q-task@3. Falsified if the difference is within +/-4 or Q-traj is better. Arm: Q-traj@3 vs Q-task@3, paired, 4 seeds, per type.
- P3 (record beats nearness). Per-type writer-side quality re-ranking beats uniform by >= 6 net on the complementary types and beats proximity weighting by >= 6 net; proximity weighting is <= uniform + 2. Falsified if proximity weighting >= quality weighting - 2 (proximity governs which items help, rescuing closer_beats_stronger), or if quality weighting <= uniform + 4 (the retriever's implicit weighting is already as good as the writer's record). Arms: uniform / proximity / global / per-type re-rankers on M, same reader, 4 seeds.
- P4 (calibration adds little and transfers). Reader-side calibrated weights add < 4 net over writer-side weights, and Qwen3-calibrated weights used by the EXAONE reader retain >= 70% of the gain of EXAONE's own calibrated weights over uniform. Falsified if calibration adds >= 6 net (reader-specific compatibility matters and must be paid for in the 480 episodes), or if the mismatched weights retain < 40%. Arms: writer-side vs reader-side matched vs reader-side mismatched, EXAONE and Qwen3 readers.
- P5 (mixing costs, weighting repays). Under uniform Q-task retrieval M retains <= 0.9 of the best single-writer store's gain, and per-type quality re-ranking raises the ratio to >= 1.0. Falsified if uniform M >= best single (mixing is free) or if the re-ranked ratio is < 0.9. Arms: M-uniform and M-per-type vs the four single-writer stores at equal per-type item count.

Measurement and Noise Control:
The retrieval-share audit is deterministic given the logs; its CI is a bootstrap over games, reported per type. Downstream cells are paired on games x seeds with bootstrap over games and seeds as blocks; headline cells (M-uniform Q-task@3, M-uniform Q-traj@3, M-per-type, M-proximity, self(B)) at 4 seeds (about +/-5.7 per cell, paired differences about +/-4.5), others at 2 seeds with the add-2-seeds rule (cap 6); margins as fractions of the Gate-0 effect of 6 net. Alpha frozen on a train slice before any validation cell; per-type item counts equalized; tokens per episode equal across re-rankers (same k, length-matched items) and reported; calibration episodes counted in the cost table; all gains net of the static-procedure prompt; retained-gain ratios with joint bootstrap CIs.
