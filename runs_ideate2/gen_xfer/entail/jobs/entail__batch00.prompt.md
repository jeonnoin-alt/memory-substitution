### proposal: reconstruction_pays_only_across_the_writer_gap
Title: Reconstruction Pays Only Across the Writer Gap: Reader-Side Rewriting of Foreign Experience Helps by Policy Distance, Not Writer Capability, at Matched Tokens
Short Hypothesis: Training-free read-time reconstruction (the reader rewrites the retrieved items into a plan in its own action format, conditioned on its current observation) adds nothing over verbatim injection when the writer is the reader itself, and adds a gain that grows with writer-reader policy distance (same backbone under a different scaffold/temperature; a different 32B backbone) but not with writer capability (same backbone in thinking mode), when the comparison is made against verbatim injection at matched consumer tokens (k=7) and net of a reconstruction placebo applied to the static procedure prompt.

Experiments:
- Banks (all from the 3,553 train games or the 1,465 expert walkthroughs; success-filtered; type-balanced to the expert pool's type distribution and length-capped at 2x the expert median steps so the writer manipulation is not a length manipulation; the uncapped variant is an ablation): W_exp expert; W_self Qwen3-32B standard ReAct T=0 (on disk); W_prox Qwen3-32B same mode, plan-then-act scaffold with different action phrasing, T=0.7 (collect, ~3 GPU-h; verify k=0 success within +/-5 net of W_self, else lower T); W_cap Qwen3-32B thinking mode, standard scaffold (collect; verify k=0 success differs from W_self by >= 8 net); W_other EXAONE-4.0-32B standard scaffold (collect after serving verification, ~3 GPU-h); W_big gpt-oss-120b only if it serves on one A100.
- Readers: R1 Qwen3-32B standard non-thinking; R2 EXAONE-4.0-32B. Same retriever as Measurement A, k=3 primary. Every bank is read by both readers.
- Conditions per (writer, reader): C0 no memory; C_static the Measurement-C six-procedure prompt; C_v3 verbatim k=3; C_v7 verbatim k=7 (token-matched to reconstruction, ~1,010 injected tokens); C_r3 reconstruction k=3 (one rewrite at step 0 into the reader's action format; a re-reconstruct-every-5-steps variant is an ablation); C_pl_static the reconstruction prompt applied to C_static text (memory-absent placebo); C_pl_mis the reconstruction prompt applied to k=3 items retrieved for a different task type (mismatched placebo, token-matched).
- Estimands: verbatim retained-gain ratio rho_v(w,r) = [S(r,C_v3,w) - S(r,C_static)] / [S(w,C_v3,w) - S(w,C_static)] where the writer policy reads its own bank; rho_r(w,r) with C_r3 in the numerator; reconstruction increment D(w,r) = S(r,C_r3,w) - S(r,C_v7,w), and net increment D*(w,r) = D(w,r) - [S(r,C_pl_static) - S(r,C_static)]; per-type breakdown for the six procedures; consumer tokens per episode (prompt + generated) and episodes/min per cell.
- Gate 0 (before any confirmatory cell): at least one foreign bank yields a verbatim gain net of static >= +10 for R1 at k=3; reconstruction on W_exp does not lower success by more than 4 net (safety). Pre-registered margins are fractions of this Gate-0 verbatim gain.
- Headline cells at 274 games x 4 seeds: (W_self,R1), (W_prox,R1), (W_cap,R1), (W_other,R1) under C_v7 and C_r3, plus the two placebos for R1; all other cells at 274 x 2 seeds. Total ~40 cells, ~24k episodes plus three bank collections; both A100s busy (one serves the reader, the other collects the next bank or serves the second reader); about 2.5 days.

Baselines and Ablations:
- Cheapest baseline first: C_static (Measurement C) and the reconstruction placebo on C_static, both before any retrieval cell.
- Direct injection of the unmodified items at matched tokens (C_v7) is the primary comparator for every reconstruction cell; C_v3 is reported as the cheaper comparator.
- Self-pool control at matched collection cost: W_self for R1 and W_other for R2 are collected at the same episode budget as the foreign banks; every foreign-bank gain is reported against that cell.
- Ablations: length-uncapped banks (does the increment track item wandering rather than writer identity); reconstruct-every-5-steps (does state-conditioning beyond step 0 add anything at its token cost); reconstruction with the writer identity revealed vs hidden in the prompt (does the reader use provenance).
- Named reimplementation: MemHarness-style reconstruction prompt without RL (2607.28272) as C_r3; the GRPO-trained version is out of budget and is named as the untested arm.

Falsifiable Predictions:
- P1 (headline interaction): D*(W_other,R1) - D*(W_self,R1) >= 1/3 of the Gate-0 verbatim gain with a 95% cluster-bootstrap CI excluding 0. Falsifier: the difference's CI includes 0 or is negative, i.e. reconstruction helps the reader's own bank as much as or more than the foreign bank. Arm: the (W_self,R1,C_r3/C_v7) cells versus the (W_other,R1,C_r3/C_v7) cells at 4 seeds; the self-bank cell can produce a large increment because self-written successes are wandering on-policy trajectories that rewriting could compress.
- P2 (proximity, not capability): D*(W_prox,R1) - D*(W_self,R1) >= 1/3 of the Gate-0 gain, while D*(W_cap,R1) - D*(W_self,R1) < that margin. Falsifier: the thinking-mode bank shows the larger increment (capability distance governs) or the different-scaffold bank shows none (format distance does not matter). Arms: the (W_prox,R1) and (W_cap,R1) reconstruction and verbatim cells.
- P3 (token-matched): for W_other->R1 and W_exp->R2, S(C_r3) > S(C_v7) by >= 4 net. Falsifier: C_v7 >= C_r3 in either cell (more verbatim items at equal tokens do as well as rewriting). Arm: the C_v7 cells, which Measurement A shows are not harmed relative to k=3.
- P4 (placebo): the memory-absent placebo increment S(C_pl_static) - S(C_static) is < 1/3 of D(W_other,R1). Falsifier: the placebo captures >= 2/3 of the increment (the gain is a planning step, not adaptation of the foreign item). Arm: C_pl_static and C_pl_mis for R1.
- P5 (symmetry across the store): the same W_self bank read by R2 shows rho_r(W_self,R2) - rho_v(W_self,R2) >= 0.15. Falsifier: rho_r <= rho_v for R2 (rewriting does not help the other backbone read Qwen3's experience). Arm: the (W_self,R2) C_v3 and C_r3 cells.
- P6 (where verbatim is negative): reconstruction of W_exp lifts look-at-in-light for R1 from -4.8 to >= 0 net. Falsifier: look-at-in-light stays <= -4.8 under C_r3. Arm: the per-type breakdown of (W_exp,R1,C_r3); secondary because the type cell has ~45 games.

Measurement and Noise Control:
Paired per-game success differences with games x seeds; cluster bootstrap over games (2,000 resamples, seeds nested) for every estimand and for the interaction contrasts; 274 x 2 seeds for screening cells (+/-8 net) and 274 x 4 seeds for the four headline cells and their placebos (+/-5.7 net; an interaction of two paired differences needs the doubled seed count). One pre-registered headline contrast (P1); P2-P6 are secondary; per-type breakdowns exploratory. Margins are pre-registered as fractions of the measured Gate-0 verbatim gain, not fixed numbers. Consumer-side tokens (prompt and generated, including the reconstruction output) and episodes/min are logged per episode and reported per cell; seeds fix the environment split and sampling; temperature 0 for readers except where the manipulation requires otherwise. Bank collections use nohup runners with append-only JSONL and pid files, workers tuned to the vLLM running queue (>= 8 per replica).

### proposal: reconstruction_launders_planted_procedure_errors
Title: Rewriting Launders What It Should Filter: Read-Time Reconstruction Propagates Planted Procedure Errors and Hides Them, While an Explicit Read-Time Gate Detects Foreign Errors Better Than the Reader's Own
Short Hypothesis: When benign-looking wrong items (procedure swaps, relabelled failures, receptacle swaps) are planted at controlled rates in an ALFWorld bank, reader-side reconstruction keeps most of the planted wrong step in its rewritten plan and makes the error harder for a downstream critic to detect than the original item (laundering), whereas an explicit keep/discard gate before use recovers at least half of the harm; and the gate's recall is lower on contaminated items written in the reader's own style than on the same contamination in another 32B backbone's style, for both readers, so the blind spot is a property of writer-reader proximity rather than of any one bank.

Experiments:
- Banks: W_exp, W_self (Qwen3-32B), W_other (EXAONE-4.0-32B, collected after serving verification); W_self and W_other type-balanced and length-matched to each other so the diagonal contrast is not a length contrast. Contamination operations applied identically to each bank: Op-A appliance swap (heat<->cool, clean->heat, bindings preserved; the item reads as a successful trajectory); Op-B relabelled failures (Qwen3: from its 1,661 failed train games; EXAONE: from its failures; expert: walkthroughs truncated before the goal step and annotated as complete); Op-C wrong final receptacle. Rates 0/10/25/50% of the affected types' items; planted items carry hidden labels for scoring.
- Readers R1 Qwen3-32B, R2 EXAONE-4.0-32B; same retriever, k=3. Conditions: C0; C_static (Measurement C six-procedure prompt); C_v3 verbatim; C_r3 reconstruct (as in the companion design, one rewrite at step 0); C_g3 gate (per item keep/discard with a one-sentence reason, kept items injected verbatim, no refill); C_gr3 gate then reconstruct; C_rule keyword rule (appliance token vs task-type keyword) then verbatim; a post-hoc critic (the reader prompted to judge correctness of a plan given its task) applied offline to original planted items and to the C_r3 rewritten plans.
- Estimands: harm H(w,r,op,rate,cond) = S(r,cond,w,0%) - S(r,cond,w,rate); recovery = 1 - H(cond)/H(C_v3); wrong-step persistence pi = fraction of C_r3 plans for retrievals that included a planted item in which the planted appliance or receptacle survives; laundering = critic recall on persisted rewritten items minus critic recall on the original planted items; gate precision/recall per (writer, reader, op, rate); retained-gain ratio in the 0% cells per writer x reader; consumer tokens and episodes/min per cell.
- Gate 0: under C_v3 for R1, H >= 6 net at some rate for at least one operation (positive control: Op-A at 50%). If no operation clears it, the study reports reader robustness with CIs and the laundering and gate claims are withdrawn.
- Schedule: Gate 0 first (3 ops x 3 rates x 2 writers for R1, 18 cells); then the 2x2 writer x reader diagonal at the Gate-0 passing rate under C_g3 (4 cells at 4 seeds); then C_r3, C_gr3, C_rule at the passing rate and at 0% for both readers (~20 cells); ~45 cells, ~26k episodes plus one bank collection; both GPUs serve one reader each; about 2 days.

Baselines and Ablations:
- Cheapest baseline first: C_rule (a keyword consistency check) is run before any LLM gate; C_static and C0 fix the floor and the reference for 'net' gains.
- Direct injection of the unmodified items (C_v3) is the comparator for every adaptation and gate cell; C_gr3 is token-matched to C_r3 by construction (the gate's reasons are not injected).
- Self-pool control: the 0% cells give the retained-gain ratio per writer x reader against each reader's own bank at matched collection cost; contamination harm is reported as a fraction of that clean gain.
- Memory-absent, matched and mismatched evaluation of the gate: false-positive rate on the clean 0% bank (precision), on the reader's own-type items (matched) and on items retrieved for the wrong task type (mismatched, which the gate should discard).
- Ablations: gate with writer identity revealed vs hidden; gate with the item's outcome label hidden vs shown (does the label drive the judgment); critic recall with the original item shown beside the rewrite (upper bound on detectability).

Falsifiable Predictions:
- P1 (Gate 0): Op-A at 25% or 50% produces H >= 6 net under C_v3 for R1. Falsifier: H < 6 at every rate and operation, i.e. the reader ignores planted procedure errors as in-context classifiers ignore wrong labels. Arm: the C_v3 x Op-A x 50% cell; the outcome is not entailed because the reader may follow the task keyword rather than the item.
- P2 (reconstruction does not filter): at the Gate-0 rate, pi >= 0.5 and H(C_r3) >= 0.75 x H(C_v3). Falsifier: H(C_r3) <= 0.5 x H(C_v3) or pi < 0.25, meaning the rewrite repairs the planted step. Arm: the C_r3 cell at the Gate-0 rate with per-plan persistence scoring.
- P3 (laundering): the post-hoc critic's recall on persisted rewritten plans is lower than on the original planted items by >= 0.15 absolute. Falsifier: recall on rewrites >= recall on originals - 0.05. Arm: the offline critic applied to both sets of the same planted items.
- P4 (own-style blind spot, 2x2): C_g3 recall on the diagonal cells (W_self->R1, W_other->R2) is lower than on the off-diagonal cells (W_other->R1, W_self->R2) by >= 0.10 for both readers. Falsifier: either reader shows recall(own) >= recall(foreign), or both readers show low recall on the same bank regardless of who reads it (a pool property, not proximity). Arm: the four C_g3 detection logs at the Gate-0 rate, 4 seeds.
- P5 (gate recovers without over-discarding): C_gr3 recovers >= 50% of H(C_v3) at the Gate-0 rate with gate precision >= 0.7, and on the 0% bank loses <= 4 net relative to C_v3. Falsifier: recovery < 50%, or the 0% loss exceeds 4 net (the gate discards good foreign items to buy robustness). Arm: the C_gr3 cells at the Gate-0 rate and at 0%.
- P6 (where an LLM gate is needed): C_rule reaches recall >= 0.8 on Op-A but <= 0.2 on Op-B, and C_g3 beats C_rule on Op-B by >= 0.2 recall. Falsifier: C_g3 does not exceed C_rule's Op-B recall by 0.2 (the cheap rule is enough). Arm: C_rule vs C_g3 on the Op-B bank.

Measurement and Noise Control:
Paired per-game differences between the contaminated and clean cells of the same bank, reader and condition; cluster bootstrap over games with seeds nested; 274 x 2 seeds for Gate-0 screening (+/-8 net) and 274 x 4 seeds for the P4 2x2 and the P2/P5 cells (+/-5.7). Detection metrics are computed over retrieval events (about 820 per cell at k=3 x 274 x 1 seed), which gives precision/recall CIs of about +/-0.04 by bootstrap over games; the P4 margin of 0.10 is set to twice that. Planted items are labelled at construction; persistence is scored by string match on the appliance or receptacle token with a manual audit of 100 rewrites. Margins are fractions of the Gate-0 harm. One pre-registered headline contrast (P4), P2/P3 secondary. Tokens and throughput logged per cell.

### proposal: rewriter_trained_against_one_reader_matched_cost
Title: A Rewriter Learned Against One Reader Is That Reader's: Transfer Fraction of Execution-Trained Experience Rewriters Across Scaffold, Capability and Backbone, Against Self-Collection at Equal GPU-Hours
Short Hypothesis: An experience rewriter trained by execution-feedback rejection sampling against one reader keeps less than half of its increment when applied to a different 32B backbone, more than three quarters when applied to the same backbone under a different scaffold, and an intermediate fraction when applied to the same backbone in thinking mode, so reader specificity follows policy proximity rather than capability; and for the held-out backbone the same GPU-hours spent on collecting its own bank buy at least as much as importing the foreign-trained rewriter.

Experiments:
- Foreign banks: W_exp (expert) and W_other (EXAONE-4.0-32B successes, collected after serving verification), pooled as the input bank for all rewriter training and evaluation; W_self (Qwen3-32B) is the self-pool control for reader A and the matched-cost arm for reader B is an additional EXAONE bank.
- Readers: A Qwen3-32B standard scaffold non-thinking; A' Qwen3-32B plan-then-act scaffold, T=0.7 (verify k=0 within +/-5 net of A); A'' Qwen3-32B thinking mode (verify k=0 >= 8 net above A); B EXAONE-4.0-32B (verify k=0 within +/-5 net of A; if not, B is reported as a joint backbone-and-capability change and P2 is restricted to A' vs A''). C gpt-oss-120b only if it serves on one A100.
- Rewriter training: for 1,000 training games, 3 candidate rewrites per game from the prompted Qwen3-32B rewriter at T=0.8 (input: task, first observation, k=3 items; output: one plan in the reader's action format), executed by the in-loop reader (3,000 episodes, ~2.5 GPU-h at 20/min), plus the verbatim run (1,000 episodes); keep rewrites where the reader succeeded and verbatim failed, and a 1:1 share of success-success pairs; QLoRA r=32 SFT, one epoch (~1.5 GPU-h). Same recipe for R_B with B in the loop. Rewriter backbone fixed at Qwen3-32B for both, so only the in-loop reader differs.
- Conditions per reader: C0; C_static (Measurement C); C_v3 verbatim; C_vtok verbatim at the k that matches the rewriter's output tokens; C_RA, C_RB the trained rewriters; C_R0 the prompted untrained rewriter; C_RA_empty (rewriter given the task and no item); C_RA_mis (items retrieved for a different task type); for B additionally C_selfcost (B's own bank enlarged by the episode-equivalent of R_A's training GPU-hours, read verbatim).
- Estimands: g(r,cond) = S(r,cond) - S(r,C_static); increment I(R,r) = g(r,R) - g(r,C_vtok); transfer fraction TF(R_A->r) = I(R_A,r)/I(R_A,A) and TF(R_B->r) = I(R_B,r)/I(R_B,B); retained-gain ratio per writer x reader for the verbatim cells; consumer tokens (rewriter output counts as consumer tokens) and episodes/min; training GPU-hours per rewriter.
- Gate 0: I(R_A,A) >= 6 net and I(R_B,B) >= 6 net at 274 x 4 seeds; a rewriter that fails its own reader has nothing to transfer and is reported as a null with the training-free R_0 as the fallback subject for P2.
- Budget: two rewriters ~8 GPU-h; evaluation ~32 cells (headline cells at 4 seeds) ~19k episodes; the rewriter adapter is served on the GPU not serving the reader; about 3 days including bank collection.

Baselines and Ablations:
- Cheapest first: C_static, then C_R0 (prompted rewriter, no training) before any trained cell.
- Direct injection of the unmodified items at matched tokens (C_vtok) is the comparator for every rewriter cell; C_v3 reported as the cheaper comparator.
- Self-pool control at matched collection cost: W_self for A; for B, C_selfcost converts R_A's training GPU-hours into EXAONE episodes at the measured episodes/GPU-h.
- Memory-absent (C_RA_empty), matched (training reader), mismatched (C_RA_mis and held-out readers) evaluation of each trained component, as the round rules require.
- Named reimplementation: the lightweight rewriter of 2605.30723 as C_RA/C_RB; SkillRevise-style trace-conditioned revision (2606.01139) is named as the untested alternative trainer.
- Ablations: rewriter trained on success-success pairs only (does the contrastive filter matter); rewriter applied at k=1; rewriter with reader identity in the prompt.

Falsifiable Predictions:
- P1 (reader specificity): I(R_B,B) - I(R_A,B) >= 1/3 of I(R_A,A), and symmetrically I(R_A,A) - I(R_B,A) >= the same margin. Falsifier: the foreign-trained rewriter is within the margin of the reader-trained one on either reader, meaning the rewrites are reader-agnostic cleaning. Arm: the (B,C_RA) vs (B,C_RB) and (A,C_RB) vs (A,C_RA) cells at 4 seeds; both rewriters are trained on the same input bank so the cell can produce equality.
- P2 (proximity before capability): TF(R_A->A') >= 0.75, TF(R_A->A'') between 0.5 and 0.75, TF(R_A->B) < 0.5, with the ordering TF(A'') > TF(B) holding at CI level. Falsifier: TF(R_A->B) >= TF(R_A->A'') (the capability change costs more transfer than the backbone change) or TF(R_A->A') < 0.5 (a scaffold change alone breaks the rewriter). Arm: the A', A'' and B rewriter and C_vtok cells.
- P3 (not memorized plans): I(R_A on C_RA_empty, A) <= 1/3 of I(R_A,A). Falsifier: the rewriter with no retrieved item keeps >= 2/3 of its increment, in which case the transfer measured is plan transfer and P1/P2 are re-labelled as such. Arm: C_RA_empty and C_RA_mis for A.
- P4 (matched cost for the held-out backbone): g(B,C_selfcost) >= g(B,C_RA). Falsifier: g(B,C_RA) - g(B,C_selfcost) >= +6 net, i.e. importing the rewriter trained against the other backbone is worth more than the same GPU-hours of B's own experience. Arm: the C_selfcost cell versus (B,C_RA).
- P5 (training buys something): I(R_A,A) - I(R_0,A) >= 1/3 of I(R_A,A). Falsifier: the prompted rewriter is within the margin of the trained one on the training reader. Arm: (A,C_R0) vs (A,C_RA).
- P6 (retained-gain ratio moves with the rewriter): for the W_other bank read by A, the retained-gain ratio under C_RA exceeds the verbatim ratio by >= 0.15. Falsifier: the ratio does not rise or falls. Arm: the (W_other,A) verbatim and C_RA cells with the EXAONE writer reading its own bank as the denominator.

Measurement and Noise Control:
Paired per-game success differences over 274 games x seeds; cluster bootstrap over games with seeds nested for increments, transfer fractions (ratio CIs by bootstrap of numerator and denominator jointly) and the P1 differences of differences; 274 x 4 seeds for the eight headline cells (A and B under C_RA, C_RB, C_vtok, C_selfcost), 274 x 2 elsewhere. Gate 0 at 4 seeds before any held-out cell. Margins as fractions of the Gate-0 increment; one pre-registered headline contrast (P1), P2 secondary, the rest exploratory. Reader temperature 0 except A'; rewriter decoding temperature 0 at evaluation. Consumer tokens per episode include the rewriter's generated plan; training GPU-hours are logged from the runner and converted to episodes at the measured rate for C_selfcost. The reader in the training loop is served on one GPU while the other serves the candidate-rewrite generator, then the QLoRA job runs on one GPU while the other serves the first evaluation cells.
