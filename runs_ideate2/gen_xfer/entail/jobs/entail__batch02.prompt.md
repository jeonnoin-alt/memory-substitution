For EACH proposal, number the items of "Falsifiable Predictions" P1, P2, ... in order of appearance (split prose at each
stated prediction). For each prediction:
1. depends_on: the arms, split, retriever, update rule or estimand it rests on (from Experiments / Baselines).
2. falsifier: the outcome that would falsify it, as the proposal states or implies it.
3. Decide whether that outcome is REACHABLE under the stated design or excluded by construction. Excluded-by-construction
   includes: the falsifying outcome needs information the split never gives the model; the estimand is an algebraic
   identity of another quantity it is compared with (shared measured terms); the update rule cannot produce it (an
   append-only store cannot forget, a chunk-trained adapter cannot retain, a warm-started adapter relearns faster than a
   cold one); the retriever cannot deliver the item class the prediction is about; a ceiling or floor forces the sign;
   the effect re-describes how a pool was built or filtered; a control deletes the very tokens the outcome is scored on;
   the arm that would show the opposite is absent.
4. Decide whether the falsifier is DISTINGUISHABLE at the stated power: compare the margin or threshold with the stated
   CI or MDE; if the falsifier lies inside the stated noise, the prediction is unresolvable, not open.
5. label: "entailed" (falsifier unreachable), "near_entailed" (reachable only through a mechanism the proposal does not
   name or control), "unresolvable" (reachable but inside the stated noise), "open". One sentence of reason; for
   anything but "open", the minimal change (an arm, a split, an estimand, seeds) that would make it open.
Also list shared_terms: pairs of predictions whose estimands share a measured term so that one determines the other.

### proposal: complementarity_not_proximity
Title: Complementarity, Not Proximity: What a Reader Keeps of a Foreign Writer's Experience Is Set by the Writer's Success on the Reader's Failure Types
Short Hypothesis: For success-filtered experience pools built on the same task set at the same episode budget, the fraction of gain a reader keeps from a foreign writer's pool (retained-gain ratio against the reader's own matched-cost pool, net of a static-procedure prompt) is governed by writer-reader complementarity (the writer's k=0 success advantage on the task types where the reader fails), not by policy proximity (writer-reader action agreement) and not by the writer's overall capability; a foreign pool beats the reader's own pool (ratio > 1) exactly in the cells where complementarity is positive, and proximity is inert above a format threshold.

Experiments:
Setup: ALFWorld, 274 validation games (140 seen / 134 unseen), six task types; readers Q = Qwen3-32B (verified), E = EXAONE-4.0-32B and G = gpt-oss-120b (Gate V: serve in vLLM 0.13 on one A100 each and reach k=0 success >= 0.30, else the reader is dropped and the matrix shrinks). Writers: X expert (on disk), Q-self (on disk, T=0 ReAct thinking off), Q-prox (same backbone; candidates: T=1.0 same scaffold; paraphrased Act-first scaffold; screened on 274 x 1 seed, the first candidate whose k=0 is within +/-3 net of Q-self and whose pi(Q-prox,Q) is at least 0.15 below pi(Q-self,Q) is used; manipulation check pre-registered), Q-cap (thinking on, thoughts removed from stored items so serialization equals Q-self; must reach k=0 >= Q-self + 8; fallback: the Measurement-B QLoRA writer, with its pi reported), E-self, G-self. Every backbone writer runs the same 3,553 training games once (matched episode budget; GPU-hours also reported), success-filtered, same retriever (task-description embedding, nearest by type-agnostic cosine), same injection template (the reader's scaffold), k=3; k=1 for the six Q-reader cells. Offline statistics: pi(W,R) via 300 stratified states per pool with the reader acting greedily on the writer's history (about 1 GPU-hour total), kappa(W,R) from the collection run's per-type success (writer) and the reader's k=0 validation per-type success. Cells: 3 readers x 6 writers at k=3 (18), k=1 for reader Q (6), k=0 (3), static-procedure prompt from Measurement C (3), self-opt vs self-rand for reader Q (2), mixed pool self+foreign at matched item count for the top foreign cell (1), token-matched placebo (unrelated-type trajectories at the k=3 token count) per reader (3): 36 cells x 548 episodes (274 x 2 seeds), 4 seeds for the P1, P3 and P4 cells (+8 cell-equivalents) = about 24,000 episodes. Scheduling: GPU0 serves the reader under evaluation while GPU1 collects the next writer pool (Q-prox, Q-cap, E-self, G-self: 12-18 GPU-hours, G slower), then both GPUs serve readers with clients spread across replicas; about 3 days. Analysis: rho per cell with game-level bootstrap CIs; Spearman(rho, kappa) and partial Spearman(rho, pi | kappa) over the 12 cross cells; per-type gains grouped into reader-failure types (p_R < 0.5) vs reader-success types. Pre-registration of Gate V, Gate 0, manipulation checks, margins and the cell list before the first confirmatory cell.

Baselines and Ablations:
k=0 per reader; static per-type procedure prompt (Measurement C) per reader, every gain reported net of it; reader's own pool at matched episode budget (the denominator); expert pool for reader Q (replicates Measurement A); Agent Memory Distillation-style hierarchical memory (arXiv:2608.07169, reimplemented without training) built from the strongest writer's pool for the strongest->weakest cell, to check the rule is not an artifact of raw-trajectory banks; k=1 vs k=3; token-matched placebo (same token count of unrelated-type trajectories) to separate content from context length; mixed pool (self + foreign, matched item count); self-opt vs self-rand (length-filtered own successes on failure types vs random subset of equal size, same collection cost); writer-denominator ratio rho_w for backbone writers; seen vs unseen split reported separately; Q-prox and Q-cap manipulation checks (k=0 and pi) reported as cells in their own right.

Falsifiable Predictions:
P1 (ordering of manipulations): for readers Q and E, |rho(Q-prox->R) - rho(Q-self->R)| < 0.20 while rho(Q-cap->R) - rho(Q-self->R) > 0.30. Falsified if the proximity contrast exceeds the capability contrast or the capability contrast is <= 0.10; produced by the cells Q-prox->Q, Q-cap->Q, Q-prox->E, Q-cap->E at 4 seeds. Not entailed: Q-prox items are verified to have a different action distribution (pi lower by >= 0.15) and could reduce transfer; Q-cap items could leave transfer unchanged if readers only need the procedure hint that the static prompt already supplies.
P2 (which cells exceed 1): across the 12 cross cells, rho > 1.15 when kappa > 0.05 and rho < 1.15 when kappa < 0.02; Spearman(rho, kappa) > 0.6 and partial Spearman(rho, pi | kappa) in [-0.3, 0.3]. Falsified by any cell with kappa > 0.05 and rho < 0.85 (e.g., G-self->Q if gpt-oss-120b is stronger but its pool does not beat Qwen's own), by a cell with kappa < 0.02 and rho > 1.15 (e.g., E-self->Q if capability-matched), or by partial correlation with pi > 0.5; produced by the cross cells plus the offline pi/kappa tables.
P3 (where the gain lands): in every cross cell with rho > 1.15, at least 70% of the net gain over the reader's own pool is carried by the reader-failure type group. Falsified if the gain is spread evenly or concentrated on reader-success types; produced by the per-type breakdown of the top three foreign cells at 4 seeds.
P4 (optimality mechanism): rho(self-opt->Q) - rho(self-rand->Q) > 0.20. Falsified if the difference is <= 0.05 (then the capability term acts through something other than trajectory optimality, e.g., recovery behaviour that length filtering removes, cf. arXiv:2501.11425); produced by the self-opt vs self-rand cells at 4 seeds.
P5 (placebo): token-matched unrelated-type trajectories give |net| < 4 points for every reader. Falsified if the placebo gains >= 8 (context length alone helps, which would contaminate every rho); produced by the placebo cells.

Measurement and Noise Control:
Paired design: same 274 games and the same seeds in every cell; 2 seeds per cell (noise floor about +/-8 net points), 4 seeds for the P1/P3/P4 cells; net points and rho with game-level cluster bootstrap CIs (2,000 resamples); rho margins of 0.15-0.20 correspond to 5-7 net points on a ~35-point self-pool gain for reader Q; for readers whose self-pool gain is below 20 net points, seeds are raised to 4 before any rho is interpreted. Gate V (E and G serve and reach k=0 >= 0.30) and Gate 0 (at least one foreign cell gives >= 10 net points over the static-procedure prompt for its reader; otherwise the transfer question on ALFWorld is moot and the study reports the negative with the Measurement C numbers) precede the matrix. Manipulation checks (Q-prox: k=0 within +/-3 of Q-self and pi lower by >= 0.15; Q-cap: k=0 >= Q-self + 8) are pre-registered and a failed check triggers the named fallback, not a silent substitution. Multiple comparisons: P2 is a single pre-registered 2 x 2 test (kappa sign x rho side, Fisher exact over 12 cells) plus cell-level CIs; per-type claims use the two-group split, not six types, because per-type n is 20-35 games per split. Retrieval is identical across readers for a given writer pool (same embedding, same index) so reader effects are not retrieval effects. Throughput and GPU-hours per pool are logged and reported as collection cost.

### proposal: thoughts_do_not_travel
Title: Actions Travel, Thoughts Don't: The Writer's Reasoning Text Is the Non-Transferable Part of Cross-Backbone Agent Experience, and a Deterministic Strip Matches a Learned Rewriter
Short Hypothesis: The component of a stored experience item that fails to transfer across backbones is the writer's reasoning text (ReAct thoughts, analysis-channel text), not its action-observation sequence: for writer != reader, deterministic action-only re-serialization of the writer's items into the reader's scaffold (zero LLM cost) recovers most of the same-backbone-minus-foreign deficit, for writer = reader the writer's thoughts are neutral or helpful, the value of thoughts is a reader-side property (the reader's own re-rationalization of foreign actions adds what its own thoughts add to its own actions), and a model-conditioned LLM rewriter adds nothing over the strip at matched tokens.

Experiments:
Readers Q, E, G (Gate V as in the matrix design: serve in vLLM 0.13, k=0 >= 0.30). Writer pools: Q-self (on disk), E-self, G-self collected on the same 3,553 training games (matched episode budget, success-filtered, 8-12 GPU-hours on GPU1 while GPU0 evaluates), expert X (on disk). Serializations built offline: F2 by a deterministic parser (action + observation lines, reader's scaffold template, no thought slots); F3 by one reader pass per item (about 1,900 items x 3 pools, ~1 GPU-hour per reader) with the reader shown the task, the action-observation sequence and asked to write its own thought per step; F4 by a rewriter prompt on the reader model following arXiv:2605.30723's model-aware alignment description (no training) and a SkillAligner-style execution-time rewrite (arXiv:2608.06880), cost in tokens logged; F5 by attaching E's or G's thoughts (from their own trajectories on the same training game, where both succeeded) to Q-self actions; F6 thoughts with actions removed. Retriever: task-description embedding, k=3, identical across serializations. Token matching: F1 vs F2 at matched k, and F2 at matched tokens (k raised until F1's token count is reached) reported separately. Cells: 6 cross cells x {F1, F2 at matched k, F2 at matched tokens, F3, F4} = 30; 3 same-backbone cells x {F1, F2, F3} = 9; reader Q: F5 (2 thought sources) and F6 (1) = 3; X pool for E and G (F2 only, the already-stripped anchor) = 2; k=0 and static procedure per reader = 6. 50 cells x 548 episodes = about 27,000 episodes, 2 seeds (4 seeds for the P1 cross cells with the smallest deficit), about 2 days on two A100s with both replicas serving readers and clients spread across them. Adapted components (F3, F4) are also evaluated memory-absent (rewriter/re-rationalizer prompt with no item, must equal k=0 within noise), matched (writer = reader) and mismatched.

Baselines and Ablations:
k=0; static-procedure prompt (Measurement C); reader's own pool verbatim at matched collection cost (the reference cell); expert pool (already-stripped anchor) for every reader; F1 vs F2 at matched k and at matched tokens (separates thought harm from token-count harm); F6 thoughts-only placebo; token-matched unrelated-type placebo; F5 thought-swap (foreign thoughts on own actions) and its mirror (own thoughts on foreign actions = F3 for the own writer); two named adaptation baselines (arXiv:2605.30723-style rewriter, SkillAligner-style execution-time alignment) with their token cost; k=1 for the two largest cross cells; memory-absent evaluation of F3/F4 prompts; seen vs unseen split.

Falsifiable Predictions:
P1 (cross-backbone strip): Delta_strip = S(F2) - S(F1) >= +5 net points at matched tokens in at least 5 of 6 cross cells, and <= 0 (within +/-4) in all 3 same-backbone cells. Falsified if Delta_strip <= 0 in the majority of cross cells (verbatim foreign thoughts as good or better), or if Delta_strip >= +5 in the same-backbone cells (thoughts are dead weight for everyone, a token-efficiency effect rather than a mismatch effect; the matched-token arm distinguishes these). Produced by the F1/F2 cross and same-backbone cells.
P2 (rewriter adds nothing): Delta_rewrite = S(F4) - S(F2) <= +3 net in every cross cell for both rewriter variants. Falsified if Delta_rewrite >= +6 in 2 or more of 6 cross cells (content adaptation matters beyond form). Not entailed: the rewriter can restructure or add procedure text. Produced by the F4 vs F2 cross cells.
P3 (thought value is reader-side): in each cross cell, [S(F3) - S(F2)] equals [S(F1) - S(F2)] measured in the reader's own-pool cells within +/-4 net. Falsified if the reader's own thoughts on foreign actions add >= 6 less than they add on its own actions (thoughts must match the author of the actions to help) or >= 6 more. Produced by the F3 cross cells against the F1/F2 own cells.
P4 (foreign thoughts harm even on own actions): S(F5) <= S(F2 own) - 5 for reader Q with E's and with G's thoughts. Falsified if F5 is within +/-4 of F2 own or better. Produced by the F5 cells.
P5 (placebo): S(F6) is within +/-4 of the static-procedure prompt for every reader. Falsified if thoughts alone give >= 8 net over the static prompt (the thoughts carry the procedure, and P1 would then be about thought style, not thought presence). Produced by the F6 cells.
Gate 0: at least one cross cell shows a same-backbone-minus-foreign verbatim deficit >= 6 net (otherwise P1's harm claim is still tested but the 'recovery' framing is dropped and the report is a null on format mismatch for these three backbones).

Measurement and Noise Control:
Paired by game and seed, 274 x 2 seeds per cell (+/-8 net floor), 4 seeds for cross cells whose F1 vs F2 difference falls inside +/-8 on the first two seeds; game-level cluster bootstrap CIs on every Delta; margins (+5, +3, +/-4) are set as fractions of the Gate-0 deficit (6 net) and of Measurement A's +27 gain; retrieval identical across serializations by construction (same embedding of task descriptions), so every Delta is a within-retrieval contrast; token counts per injected context logged and reported next to every gain (consumer-side tokens); the F3/F4 offline passes are run once and frozen, with their token cost added to the per-cell cost table; P1 tested as a pre-registered sign test over cross cells plus per-cell CIs; seen/unseen reported separately because Measurement A found them equal, which is itself a check on the procedure-bound reading.

### proposal: shared_blind_spot
Title: Shared Blind Spots: A Reader Is Most Harmed by, and Least Able to Detect, Contaminated Experience Written by Its Own Policy
Short Hypothesis: Contamination in an experience bank propagates in proportion to writer-reader policy proximity: failed trajectories relabelled as successes are more harmful per planted item and less detectable at read time when written by the reader's own policy or a near variant than when written by a distant backbone, because read-time detection is the reader's own plausibility judgment, which endorsed those actions when it produced them; a judge from a different backbone detects a writer's masked failures better than the writer's own backbone does (a diagonal deficit in the judge x writer table), and manufactured wrong-procedure grafts (positive control) are harmful and detectable regardless of writer.

Experiments:
Reader Q = Qwen3-32B primary; E = EXAONE-4.0-32B as second reader and as cross-judge (Gate V: serves in vLLM 0.13 and k=0 >= 0.30); G = gpt-oss-120b as writer and third judge if it serves. Failure pools: Q-self failures on disk (1,661 of 3,553 training games); Q-prox, E-self and G-self collected on the same 3,553 games (the successes serve the matrix design if run in the same program; here 8-14 GPU-hours on GPU1 while GPU0 evaluates). Masked contaminants: failures truncated at the last coherent action, terminal failure line removed, success line appended; coherence filter (<= 2 'Nothing happens', >= 1 correct object interaction); length reported per writer and matched across writers by selection. Unmasked variant keeps the terminal failure line. Graft: expert item with the procedure-specific steps swapped for the wrong procedure, success line intact. Contamination: per type, a fraction r of bank items replaced by contaminants of one writer at r in {0.125, 0.25, 0.5}; retrieval unchanged (task-description embedding, k=3; k=1 arm at 50 percent for Q-self). Cells for reader Q: 4 writers x 3 rates = 12; graft at 25 and 50 = 2; length-matched clean placebo at 50 = 1; clean base (rate 0) = 1; k=1 at 50 for Q-self and E-self = 2; self-pool base at 50 for Q-self and E-self = 2; two filter cells at 25 (own-judgment filter, cross-judge filter) = 2; static procedure and k=0 = 2: 24 cells x 548 episodes, 4 seeds for the 50 percent cells of Q-self, Q-prox, E-self and G-self (+4 cell-equivalents) = about 15,000 episodes; reader E cross-check at 50 percent for Q-self and E-self (2 cells) = 1,100 episodes. Detection: judge x writer table on 400 masked items per writer (4 writers x 400 x 2-3 judges, plus the unmasked set), offline, about 1 GPU-hour; judges evaluated memory-absent (no item: must abstain), matched (judge = writer backbone) and mismatched. Analysis: harm slope per writer by paired linear fit over rates; rho_c per cell; recall/precision with Wilson intervals; the diagonal-deficit test is the interaction term judge x writer, not a main effect, so a uniformly better judge does not confirm the hypothesis. Total about 1.5 days on two A100s plus pool collections. Pre-registration of Gate 0, margins, coherence filter and cell list before the first confirmatory cell.

Baselines and Ablations:
Clean expert bank (rate 0, replicates Measurement A) and clean self bank at matched collection cost; static-procedure prompt; k=0; length-matched clean placebo at 50 percent (clean items truncated to contaminant lengths) separating contamination harm from truncation harm; graft positive control; unmasked ablation (terminal failure line visible); terminal-line regex detector as the cheapest read-time filter; own-judgment filter vs cross-judge filter at 25 percent; k=1 vs k=3 (contaminant share of the injected context); judge x writer table with memory-absent, matched and mismatched evaluation; laundering check: the reader's re-rationalization pass applied to masked self failures, reporting the fraction that afterwards read as successes to each judge (adaptation propagates or filters); seen vs unseen.

Falsifiable Predictions:
P1 (harm ordering): at 50 percent, harm(Q-self) >= harm(E-self or G-self) + 4 net points for reader Q, where harm = S(clean) - S(contaminated). Falsified if foreign-writer failures harm as much or more, or if all writers are below Gate 0 (then relabelled failures are not a contaminant on this reader and only the graft is); produced by the 50 percent cells at 4 seeds. Not entailed: a distant backbone's failures (invalid-command loops, over-long analysis) could plausibly be more disruptive to imitate.
P2 (proximity gradient at fixed capability): harm(Q-self) >= harm(Q-prox) >= harm(E-self) with harm(Q-self) - harm(E-self) >= 4 and harm(Q-prox) strictly between them by >= 2 on each side. Falsified if Q-prox equals Q-self within 1 net while E-self differs (identity is the backbone, not the policy) or if the order is non-monotone; produced by the 50 percent cells of the three writers.
P3 (diagonal deficit): in the judge x writer recall table on masked items at matched precision, recall(judge = writer's backbone) is lower than recall(judge != writer's backbone) by >= 0.15 for both Q and E as judges (interaction test). Falsified if recall is uniform across writers for each judge, or if one judge is uniformly better with no diagonal deficit; produced by the offline detection table. Not entailed: an item's detectability could be a property of its text alone.
P4 (blind spot is judgment, not text): on unmasked items recall >= 0.85 for every judge x writer cell and the diagonal deficit shrinks below 0.05. Falsified if the deficit persists with the failure line visible (the deficit would then be a reading failure, not a plausibility blind spot); produced by the unmasked detection cells.
P5 (filter): at 25 percent Q-self contamination, the cross-judge filter recovers >= 60 percent of the harm while the own-judgment filter recovers <= 30 percent. Falsified if the own filter recovers as much as the cross filter (within 15 percentage points of harm) or if neither recovers 30 percent; produced by the two filter cells.
P6 (positive control and placebo): the graft costs >= 6 net at 50 percent (Gate 0) and the length-matched clean placebo costs <= 3 net. Falsified if the graft is below 6 (design failure: harsher graft, redo) or the placebo costs >= 6 (truncation, not contamination, drives the harm); produced by the graft and placebo cells.

Measurement and Noise Control:
Paired 274 games x 2 seeds per cell (+/-8 net floor), 4 seeds for the P1/P2 cells; harm slopes from paired fits over rates with game-level bootstrap CIs; margins (4 net, 0.15 recall) set as fractions of the Gate-0 effect (6 net at 50 percent) and of the detection SE (Wilson, about 0.025 at n = 400 per writer, so 0.15 is 6 SE); contaminant item length matched across writers by selection and the placebo arm bounds the truncation effect; contamination rate defined at the bank level per type so the expected contaminant share of the injected context is equal across writers (the realized share is logged per episode); the diagonal-deficit test is pre-registered as an interaction (judge x writer) with the judges' overall recall as a covariate; every judge is also scored memory-absent (must abstain) so a judge that says 'no' to everything does not score; rho_c reported per cell with CIs so contamination results share the retained-gain estimand of the matrix design; Gate 0 and Gate V precede all confirmatory cells; consumer-side tokens per cell reported.
