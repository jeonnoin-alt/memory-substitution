=== ENTAILMENT CHECK (automated pre-review; evidence, not a verdict) ===
For each prediction: what would falsify it, and whether the stated arms can produce that outcome at the stated power.

Proposal: shared_blind_spot — headline P1 is **unresolvable**; open 1 / entailed 1 / near-entailed 1 / unresolvable 3; verdict revise

- P1 [unresolvable] falsifier: Foreign-writer failures harm as much or more than the reader's own, or all writers fall below Gate 0.
  reason: Harm is already a cell difference, so harm(Q-self) - harm(foreign) is a difference of differences whose paired CI is roughly sqrt(2) times the stated +/-8 net floor (about +/-8 even at 4 seeds), while the margin is 4 net - inside the noise; the criterion is also disjunctive (Q-self must beat E-self or G-self by 4), which lets the prediction pass on the smaller of two comparisons and makes the stated falsifier strictly harder to reach than a pre-registered single contrast would be.
  fix: Pre-register one named foreign comparator (or require Q-self to exceed the maximum of E-self and G-self), state the paired MDE for the double difference, and set the margin above it - or raise seeds/rates until the double-difference CI half-width is below 3 net.
- P2 [unresolvable] falsifier: Q-prox equals Q-self within 1 net while E-self differs, or the harm order is non-monotone.
  reason: The ordering is asserted with 2-net gaps on each side of Q-prox and a 1-net equivalence in the falsifier, on double differences whose stated noise floor is +/-8 net (about +/-8 even after the 4-seed upgrade), so both the predicted monotone gradient and its falsifier are pure noise draws; a non-monotone order will in fact occur by chance a large fraction of the time regardless of the hypothesis, and the proximity manipulation itself (pi lower by >= 0.15 at matched k=0) is not defined or checked inside this proposal.
  fix: Restate the gradient as a slope over a measured pi axis with more than three writers and a pre-registered CI, restate Q-prox's construction and manipulation check here, and set the margins from a stated double-difference MDE rather than at 1-2 net.
- P3 [open] falsifier: Recall is uniform across writers for each judge, or one judge is uniformly better with no diagonal deficit.
  reason: Both outcomes are mechanically reachable - detectability could be a property of the item text alone, which the design allows to show up as a writer main effect - and the power is adequate for once: Wilson SE is about 0.025 at n = 400, so a recall difference of two cells has SE about 0.035 and the 0.15 threshold sits at roughly 4 SE, with the interaction framing and the memory-absent abstention check blocking the uniformly-better-judge and always-no artifacts.
- P4 [near_entailed] falsifier: The diagonal deficit persists at >= 0.05 with the failure line visible, or some cell's recall falls below 0.85.
  reason: The unmasked arm restores the explicit terminal failure line - the exact token the detection judgment is scored on - so recall is at ceiling for any judge that reads the item, and a ceiling compresses every cell difference toward zero; the falsifier is reachable only through a mechanism the proposal never names or controls (a judge that reads an explicit failure line and still calls the item a success), and the 0.05 band is itself only about 1.5 SE of a paired recall difference at n = 400.
  fix: Add a graded-evidence arm between masked and unmasked (failure line removed but the failed terminal state or the unrecovered subgoal left intact) so the deficit has room to persist off ceiling, and state the deficit-shrinkage test as an interaction CI rather than a sub-0.05 point band.
- P5 [unresolvable] falsifier: The own filter recovers within 15 percentage points of the cross filter, or neither recovers 30 percent of the harm.
  reason: The denominator is the harm at 25 percent, roughly half the Gate-0 effect defined at 50 percent (6 net), so 60 percent recovery is about 1.8 net points and the 30 percent boundary about 0.9 net, all measured in cells at 2 seeds against a stated +/-8 net floor - the entire predicted-versus-falsifying interval is an order of magnitude inside the noise; the ordering is also largely inherited from P3, since filter recovery at matched precision is a monotone function of the same recall gap.
  fix: Run the filter arms at the 50 percent rate where the harm is >= 6 net, at 4+ seeds, and state the criterion in net points recovered with a paired CI - or evaluate the filters offline on removed-contaminant counts, where n = 400 per writer gives real power.
- P6 [entailed] falsifier: The graft costs < 6 net at 50 percent, or the length-matched clean placebo costs >= 6 net.
  reason: The proposal itself removes the first falsifier from play - a graft below 6 net is declared a design failure to be redone with a harsher graft, so the protocol iterates the positive control until it passes and the outcome can never stand against the hypothesis - while the placebo half is an equivalence claim (<= 3 net) whose falsifying value (6) is inside the stated +/-8 2-seed floor, so it cannot resolve either.
  fix: Pre-register a fixed graft recipe with at most one named re-specification, report the failed version, and treat a persistent sub-6 graft as the reported negative it is; give the placebo 4+ seeds and an upper-CI-bound criterion instead of a point margin under the noise floor.
