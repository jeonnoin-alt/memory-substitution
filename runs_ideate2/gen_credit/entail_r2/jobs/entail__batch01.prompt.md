### proposal: surrogate_endpoints_for_item_credit
Title: Cheaper Per-Item Credit from Process Endpoints: Variance Reduction and Class-Specific Bias of Surrogate Outcomes for Leave-One-Out Memory Value
Short Hypothesis: Per-item leave-one-out value measured on the binary success flag wastes most of each re-run; process endpoints logged in the same re-runs (goal-condition progress, steps-to-success, first-divergence step) give per-item estimates with at least three times lower noise and hence at least three times fewer re-runs for the same rank reliability, but the step-based endpoints are biased for an identifiable item class (location-stale items lengthen the path without changing success, which an equivalence test on success can show) while goal-condition progress is not, a redundant duplicate changes nothing under any endpoint, a surrogate index calibrated on natural runs with every manufactured class held out removes most of the step-endpoint bias, and zero-re-run offline surrogates (teacher-forced log-probability change, DIG-style confidence gain) do not track counterfactual value.

### proposal: slot_vs_item_credit_permutation
Title: Slot or Item: Separating Position Credit from Content Credit in Retrieved Sets by Permutation and Leave-One-Out
Short Hypothesis: The per-position marginal value read off nested-k retrieval logs (k=1 within k=3 within k=7) confounds an item's content with its slot. Permuting the same k=3 set on the same (game, seed) separates the two. On sets whose three items share the game's task type, reversing the order is equivalent to no change within 5 net; on mixed sets, moving the single same-type item from slot 1 to slot 3 costs at least 8 net, and a planted conflicting item harms at least 8 net more in slot 1 than in slot 3. The premium is a slot-1 step, not a gradient and not lost-in-the-middle: slot 2 sits close to slot 3 on both the help side and the harm side, which is the signature of early adoption of the first item read. The nested-k rank-1 credit therefore contains a slot component of at least 8 net on mixed sets that must be removed before it is used as item credit, and the premium survives at k=7.

Experiments:
(0) Pools, fixed before any rollout from retrieval alone (no reader calls): every natural k=3 set of the 274 validation games and of the bank-disjoint train slice is classified as ALL-SAME (three items of the game's task type), MIXED (exactly one same-type item; its natural rank 1, 2 or 3 recorded) or ALL-CROSS (dropped). Pool sizes are set from the minimum detectable effect: ALL-SAME pool = 200 games; MIXED pool = 200 games (validation first, then train slice; if natural MIXED sets run short, a MIXED set is composed from the game's own natural top-7 list as its top same-type item plus its two top cross-type items, flagged as composed and reported separately); PROC pool = 160 procedure-type games (clean, heat, cool) whose natural k=3 set is ALL-SAME, overlapping the ALL-SAME pool where possible. Reader Qwen3-32B on two vLLM replicas, expert bank, prompt template and item numbering held fixed, token counts logged. (1) ALL-SAME order arm (P1): O = (a,b,c) and R = (c,b,a), 200 games x 6 seeds x 2 = 2,400 episodes; no-op repeat O' on 100 games x 4 seeds = 400 episodes. (2) MIXED forced-slot arm (P2 headline, P6): the same-type item in slot 1 (S1), slot 2 (S2) or slot 3 (S3) with the two cross-type items in their natural relative order; S1 and S3 at 200 games x 8 seeds = 3,200 episodes, S2 at 200 games x 6 seeds = 1,200 episodes; no-op repeat S1' on 100 games x 4 seeds = 400 episodes. Because the residual pair is identical across S1, S2 and S3, f(S1) - f(S3) is exactly the difference between the same-type item's leave-one-out credit measured in slot 1 and in slot 3 (the LOO cell f(residual pair) cancels), i.e. the slot component of the natural-order LOO credit; no separate LOO cell is needed and none is run. (3) Planted-conflict slot arm (P3, P4): a token-matched wrong-procedure item (CONFLICT class of the pairwise audit) replaces the natural rank-3 item and is shown in slot 1 (C1), 2 (C2) or 3 (C3) with the two natural same-type items shifted in their natural order: 160 games x 8 seeds x 3 = 3,840 episodes; the unplanted reference U is the ALL-SAME O cell for PROC games inside the ALL-SAME pool and is run at 4 seeds for the rest (at most 640, expected ~250 episodes). (4) k=7 arm (P5): for the MIXED pool, the same-type item plus the six top cross-type items of the game's natural ranked list, shown with the same-type item in slot 1 (S1@7) or slot 7 (S7@7): 200 games x 4 seeds x 2 = 1,600 episodes at ~1.5x the per-episode cost of k=3. (5) Nested-k reanalysis: the logs' k=1-vs-k=0 and k=3-vs-k=1 contrasts (the free per-position profile) are re-read against the slot components measured in (2) and (4), reported as absolute net with CIs, and the corrected profile is set beside the static3 residual of Measurement C. (6) Gates, run first: C1 vs U harm >= 10 net (PROC, 4 seeds) and S1 minus the logs' k=0 >= +10 net on validation MIXED games; the no-op repeats must give |f(O') - f(O)| and |f(S1') - f(S1)| <= 5 net, otherwise the design half-widths are recomputed from the observed flip rate and the affected pool is extended by up to 100 train-slice games at 4 seeds (capped at 1,600 episodes) before any prediction is read. Total ~13,300 episodes (~14,100 k=3-equivalents), ~12 h on two A100s at the ~1,200 episodes/h of the k-sweep; arms (1)-(3) are queued as one job across both replicas so neither GPU idles.

Baselines and Ablations:
No-op repeats (O', S1') as the empirical noise floor for order effects; ALL-SAME O vs R as the consistent-content control; forced-slot S1/S2/S3 on MIXED sets as the slot manipulation with the item set and the residual order fixed; planted-conflict C1/C2/C3 against the unplanted reference U to separate a slot-1 step, a gradient, lost-in-the-middle and recency on the harm side; k=7 S1@7 vs S7@7 for context length; the natural similarity order O on MIXED-LATE games (same-type item at natural rank 2 or 3, where O coincides with S2 or S3) versus S1 as the derived type-aware reordering gain; static3 from Measurement C as the type-level reference for absolute credits; composed-vs-natural MIXED sets, task type, natural rank of the same-type item and item token counts as covariates.

Falsifiable Predictions:
Design half-widths (95%, cluster bootstrap over games) are calibrated from Measurement C, where 548 paired cells gave +/-5.3 net for a content contrast; order contrasts on an identical item set have at most that variance: 1,600 paired cells +/-3, 1,280 +/-3.5, 1,200 +/-3.5, 800 +/-4.5. Every threshold pair below is separated by at least twice the design half-width, so the predicted and the falsifying outcome give non-overlapping CIs; an observed value between them is reported as inconclusive, not as support. P1 (ALL-SAME, O vs R, 200 games x 6 seeds, 1,200 cells): f(R) - f(O) is equivalent to zero within +/-5 net by two one-sided tests at 5% (the 90% CI lies inside (-5, +5), which at this power requires |f(R) - f(O)| <= 2 observed). Falsified if |f(R) - f(O)| >= 8 net, the CI then lying outside the band: order matters even among consistent items. Not entailed: primacy or recency can act on consistent items, and reversal moves the most-similar item to the last slot. P2, headline (MIXED forced-slot, 200 games x 8 seeds, 1,600 cells, +/-3): f(S1) - f(S3) >= +8 net. Falsified if <= +2 net (slot-invariant reader; the CI then excludes +8). This contrast is by construction the slot component of the same-type item's LOO credit and is reported in absolute net with its CI. P3 (PROC planted, 160 games x 8 seeds, 1,280 cells, +/-3.5): harm(slot 1) - harm(slot 3) = f(C3) - f(C1) >= +8 net. Falsified if <= +1 net, including any negative value (recency dominance). P4 (same cells): the premium is a slot-1 step: D12 = f(C2) - f(C1) >= +7 net and D23 = f(C3) - f(C2) >= -1 net. Falsified if D12 <= 0 (no step between slot 1 and slot 2; a P3 premium, if present, is then a slot-3 discount rather than first-exposure adoption) or if D23 <= -8 (slot 2 is the safest slot: lost-in-the-middle). P5 (k=7, MIXED, 200 games x 4 seeds, 800 cells, +/-4.5): f(S1@7) - f(S7@7) >= +9 net. Falsified if <= 0 (the premium vanishes with longer context). P6 (MIXED S1 vs S2, 200 games x 6 seeds, 1,200 cells, +/-3.5): f(S1) - f(S2) >= +7 net (the help-side premium is also a slot-1 step). Falsified if <= 0 (slot 2 is as good as slot 1; the P2 premium is then a slot-3 penalty). Not entailed by P2, which fixes S1 - S3 and says nothing about where S2 lies between them. Derived and reported with CIs but not pre-registered: the type-aware reordering gain f(S1) - f(O) on MIXED-LATE games and the corrected nested-k per-position profile.

Measurement and Noise Control:
All contrasts are within (game, seed) on an identical item set, so content and type credit are held fixed by construction and the static3 credit of Measurement C cancels inside every slot contrast; absolute credits (S1 - k0 and S1 - static3 on validation MIXED games, from the k-sweep and Measurement C logs) are reported against the measured +17.2 residual. Pools are fixed before rollout from retrieval alone and sized from the MDE: 1,600 cells for the headline (+/-3), 1,280 for the planted arm (+/-3.5), 1,200 for the ALL-SAME and S2 arms (+/-3.5), 800 for k=7 (+/-4.5); thresholds sit at twice the half-width. Cluster bootstrap over games (seeds nested in games) for all CIs; a permutation test over run labels within (game, seed) plus TOST for P1. The no-op repeats give the flip rate under no intervention, which is the noise-floor estimand and is checked against the Measurement-C calibration before any prediction is read (gate in Experiments 6). An ALL-SAME order null is a statement about how the reader uses consistent content (it can arise from interchangeable items as well as from an inert slot); it is the control the program needs, and the slot weight itself is identified only in the MIXED and planted cells, where content differs across slots. Reported as estimands: the flip rate, the per-game variance of the slot effect, the number of seeds at which the sign of a game's slot effect stabilizes (the per-set analogue of the per-item rank-stability count), and the reader calls per contrast at +/-3 (3,200 reader episodes per k=3 contrast). Covariates: task type (per-type reporting for every prediction), item token counts, natural rank of the same-type item (MIXED-EARLY vs MIXED-LATE), natural vs composed MIXED sets. Gates precede any null: C1 vs U >= 10 net harm and S1 - k0 >= +10 net gain.
