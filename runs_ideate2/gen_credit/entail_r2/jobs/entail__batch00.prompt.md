### proposal: masked_harm_pairwise_removal
Title: Masked Harm, Masked Help: Single-Item Removal Mis-scores Conflicting and Redundant Co-retrieved Experience, and the Pairwise Correction Costs Three Re-runs, Not Seven
Short Hypothesis: In a multi-step agent the leave-one-out credit of a retrieved item is masked by its co-retrieved neighbour with a predictable sign: the E x W interaction is positive (a correct expert item E absorbs at least half of a planted wrong-procedure item W's harm) and the E x E' interaction is negative (a paraphrase twin absorbs at least three quarters of E's help), whereas the published alternatives predict no interaction (the compliance trap: W is adopted whether or not E is present; the duplication dose effect: a second copy adds help again). A pruner that scores by single removal therefore keeps W in most sets and scores each twin as worthless; the two-player Shapley within the pair, which costs one re-run beyond single removal, deletes W at equal re-run cost and matches exact k=3 Shapley to within 3 net, at three re-runs per pair instead of seven.

Experiments:
Sets on 274 valid games; items: E = natural rank-1 expert item, W = E with its procedure action swapped (heat<->cool, clean->heat, receptacle swapped for pick-and-place; strengthened W* = wrong object and wrong procedure, used only if the positive control fails), E' = paraphrase of E token-matched within 10%, F = token-matched cross-type expert trajectory, S = the static type-level procedure sentence (Measurement C). Removal leaves a k-1 set with the remaining items in their original order, so minus-W of (E,W,F) and of (W,E,F) is the same arm v(EF). Arms and budget (one seed-pass = 274 episodes): headline pooled arms at 8 seeds, 2,192 episodes each: v(F), v(EF), v(WF), v(EWF), v(E'F), v(EE'F) = 13,152; single arms at 4 seeds, 1,096 each: v(WEF) (W first), v(EEF) (literal duplicate, the dose arm), v(SF) (static procedure) = 3,288; per-set subset: 96 games (pre-registered list, stratified by task type) x 8 further seeds (9-16) for v(EWF), v(EF), v(WF), v(F) = 3,072, giving 16 seeds per set on the four arms the pruners use; exact-Shapley completion on the same 96 games x seeds 1-8: v(E), v(W), v(EW) = 2,304, plus v(empty) from the k=0 logs at matched (game, seed), re-run where missing (<= 768); consumer-swap arm on the second backbone: v(F), v(EF), v(WF), v(EWF) at 2 seeds = 2,192, queued last. Total about 24,000 episodes (24,800 with the v(empty) re-run), about 20 h at 20 episodes/min on two vLLM replicas; if the envelope is 19 h the consumer-swap arm is dropped (21,800 episodes, 18.2 h). Order and pre-registration: (1) v(F) and v(WF) at 8 seeds run first (4,384 episodes) and give H = v(F) - v(WF) with CI +/-4; all thresholds are then fixed as fractions of H and h_alone = v(EF) - v(F) before any confirmatory arm; if H < 30 net, W* replaces W and v(W*F) is re-run (2,192, paid by dropping the consumer-swap arm); if strengthened H is in [20, 30) the four conflict arms run at 12 seeds instead of 8 (4,384 more, paid by dropping v(WEF), v(EEF) and the consumer-swap arm) so the band still exceeds the CI; if strengthened H < 20 the conflict half stops and is reported as a failure of the compliance trap to transfer to this reader, and the redundancy half runs in full; the same 12-seed swap applies to the redundancy arms if h_alone < 15. (2) v(EF), v(EWF), v(E'F), v(EE'F). (3) v(WEF), v(EEF), v(SF), the per-set extension and the Shapley completion, interleaved across both replicas. (4) consumer swap. Estimands: I_EW = v(EWF) - v(EF) - v(WF) + v(F) (masked harm; equals H minus the harm of W with E present, so positive under masking and zero under the compliance trap); I_EE' = v(EE'F) - v(EF) - v(E'F) + v(F) (masked help; negative under masking, zero under a dose effect); masking ratios reported as secondary descriptors; position contrast v(EWF) - v(WEF); dose contrasts v(EEF) - v(EF) and v(EE'F) - v(EF); E's help net of static, h_alone - [v(SF) - v(F)]; k=2 vs k=3 harm, [v(empty) - v(W)] vs H. Pruning decisions on the 96-set subset: single-removal credit c_i = v(FULL) - v(-i), seed-averaged per set; two-player Shapley within the pair phi_i = 0.5[(v(FULL) - v(-i)) + (v(-j) - v(-ij))], which adds the unmasked reference at weight one half and so changes each item's level but not the order within the pair (phi_W - phi_E = c_W - c_E identically), so the decision rule is a thresholded harm rule (delete if credit <= -H/3, i.e. -10 net at H = 30; sensitivity at -H/6 and -H/2), not an argmin; equal-cost comparison: single removal at 16 seeds (3 re-runs x 16 = 48 per set) vs pairwise at 12 seeds (4 x 12 = 48); rates over 96 sets with the paired difference; the noise-only expectation computed from the pooled means and the per-set SE with zero heterogeneity, and empirical-Bayes-shrunk credits, reported alongside. Shapley residual: D_i = phi_exact(i) - phi_pair(i) for i in {W, E}, where phi_exact (weights 1/3, 1/6, 1/6, 1/3) uses v(empty), v(E), v(W), v(EW) and the four shared arms; D is zero when F is inert and is otherwise the weighted (1/3, 1/6) sum of the k=2-vs-k=3 shifts in the item's marginal contribution. Cost: for each estimator, the number of seeds (2, 4, 8, 16) at which its cluster-bootstrap CI excludes zero, and the LLM calls per set at that reliability.

Baselines and Ablations:
Single removal (3 re-runs per k=3 set) vs pairwise removal (single removal plus one minus-both re-run for the flagged pair, 4) vs exact Shapley (7 re-runs) as the cost axis, compared at equal re-run budget (16 vs 12 seeds) and at equal seeds; additive prediction (sum of single credits) vs measured pair credit as the additivity test; position swap (E,W,F vs W,E,F); paraphrase twin vs literal duplicate as two repetition strengths at fixed k; W-alone and E-alone cells as the unmasked references; k=2 vs k=3 marginal contributions (Shapley completion) as the set-size ablation; static-procedure arm (S,F) as the Measurement C baseline for E's help; second backbone as a separate consumer-swap arm; seeds as a factor; raw vs empirical-Bayes-shrunk per-set credits and the harm-rule threshold (-H/6, -H/3, -H/2) as analysis ablations.

Falsifiable Predictions:

Arms: v(F), v(WF), v(EF), v(EWF) on 274 games x 8 seeds; four-arm interaction CI +/-5.7; band 10 net.
- P2 (masked help, not a dose effect): R = [v(EF) - v(F)] - [v(EE'F) - v(E'F)] = -I_EE' >= 0.75 x h_alone (>= 15 net at h_alone = 20), and the added help of a second copy, paraphrase v(EE'F) - v(EF) and literal v(EEF) - v(EF), is <= 0.25 x h_alone (<= 5 net); falsified if R <= 0.25 x h_alone (<= 5 net) or either second-copy contrast >= 0.6 x h_alone (>= 12 net), the duplication dose effect (the compliance trap's repeated-exposure amplification; CUE-R DUPLICATE). Arms: v(F), v(EF), v(E'F), v(EE'F) at 8 seeds (R CI +/-5.7, band 10); v(EEF) at 4 seeds against v(EF) on the same seeds (two-arm contrast +/-5.7, band 7).
- P3 (decision error at equal re-run cost): on the 96-set subset, a harm rule (delete if credit <= -H/3) on single-removal credit at 16 seeds deletes W in fewer sets than the two-player Shapley at 12 seeds (48 re-runs per set in both cases), paired difference >= 20 points (expected about 62% vs 83% at H = 30, I_EW = 15, per-set SE 16 net); falsified if the paired difference is <= 5 points (masking too small, or too rare across sets, to change the decision; at I_EW = 0 both rules delete W in about 90% of sets). Noise: per-set credit SE about 15-18 net at 16 seeds, single-rate CI +/-10, paired-difference CI +/-11 over 96 sets; the noise-only expectation from the pooled means (zero heterogeneity) and the 33% three-item chance level for an argmin rule are reported next to the observed rates.
- P4 (position dependence): v(EWF) - v(WEF) >= 0.4 x H (>= 12 net): W read before E is adopted before E is read, so masking is a primacy effect; falsified if <= 0.1 x H (<= 3 net): order-invariant reconciliation of the conflicting pair. Arm: v(WEF) at 4 seeds against v(EWF) on the same seeds (two-arm contrast +/-5.7, band 9).
- P5 (pairwise recovers exact Shapley): |D_W| <= 3 net and |D_E| <= 3 net, where D_i = phi_exact(i) - phi_pair(i) on the 96-game subset, i.e. the third item F leaves the pair's marginal contributions unchanged between k=2 and k=3; falsified if either |D| >= 9 net, a set-size effect (W's harm or E's help differs at k=2 and k=3) under which pairwise removal at k=3 does not stand in for exact Shapley and the seven-re-run estimator is needed. Arms: v(empty), v(E), v(W), v(EW) plus the four shared arms on 96 games x 8 seeds; D CI about +/-3.6 (weights 1/3 and 1/6 on four contrasts of +/-6.8), band 6.

Measurement and Noise Control:
All contrasts paired by (game, seed). Calibration from the starting position: a paired two-arm contrast is +/-8 net at 2 seeds (548 episodes), +/-5.7 at 4, +/-4 at 8, +/-2.8 at 16; a four-arm interaction is sqrt(2) wider (+/-8 at 4 seeds, +/-5.7 at 8, +/-4 at 16); the Shapley residual D combines four contrasts at weights 1/3 and 1/6 and is 0.53 x a contrast CI (+/-3.6 on 96 games x 8 seeds); a per-set credit at 16 seeds has SE about 15-18 net (per-episode paired-difference SD 60-70 net), so per-set decisions are reported with the noise-only expectation from the pooled means and with empirical-Bayes-shrunk credits as a secondary analysis. Every band is wider than the CI of its estimand and is set as a pre-registered fraction of H and h_alone, fixed after the positive-control arms and before the confirmatory arms; the net-point thresholds above are at H = 30 and h_alone = 20, with the 12-seed swap and stop rule if the controls fall short. The shared v(F) and v(EF) arms enter every interaction; the cluster bootstrap over games with seed as a factor propagates their noise, and an error in v(F) moves I_EW and I_EE' in the same direction, opposite to their predicted signs, so it cannot produce both predictions. Estimator cost is an estimand: for single removal, pairwise removal and exact Shapley we report the seeds (2, 4, 8, 16) at which the bootstrap CI of the class-level credit excludes zero and the LLM calls per set at that reliability; pairwise removal needs one re-run more than single removal per pair and four fewer than exact Shapley, and this is stated with the measured reliability, not assumed. Prompt hashes, token counts and item ids logged; retrieval frozen from the logs; one vLLM replica per GPU, workers tuned to the running queue, jobs under nohup with pid files and append-only JSONL.

### proposal: responsible_item_attribution_benchmark
Title: Which Item Did It? A Seed-Replicated Intervention Benchmark for Attributing Agent Failures to Retrieved Memory Items
Short Hypothesis: A failed ALFWorld episode can be attributed to a specific retrieved memory item only by seed-replicated intervention (re-running the same game with each item removed under 12 seeds); scored against that label, the cheap attributions current systems rely on - the reader's self-report, teacher-forced confidence gain over the failed trajectory, position priors, single-seed leave-one-out - identify the responsible item at least 20 points less often than a fresh 6-seed intervention replicate does on the same episodes, most memory-caused natural failures are not attributable to any single item at all, and duplicating a culprit converts single responsibility into joint responsibility that single-removal labels and single-output methods both miss.

Experiments:
E0 (free): position prior from the k-sweep logs (which position's items co-occur with failure), and a 500-episode seed-variance pilot at the pre-registered temperature (fixes the failure rate and p(S) variance used below). E1 natural pool: 274 valid games x 2 seeds plus 500 train games x 2 seeds at k=3 (1,548 episodes), expected about 240 failures (15%). Labelling per failure with the failed episode's retrieved set frozen: block A = 6 fresh seeds x (base + 3 single REMOVE arms) = 24 re-runs; block B = 6 further seeds x the same 4 arms = 24; emptied-set arm = 6 seeds at k=0; 54 re-runs per failure, about 13,000 episodes. Label from the pooled 12 seeds: r_i = p(S minus i) - p(S); memory-caused if p(empty) - p(S) >= 0.3 with a one-sided 90% Newcombe interval excluding 0; single-item attributable if the one-sided 95% Newcombe lower bound of max_i r_i exceeds 0 (at m = 12 this needs r_i of about 0.33 or more; analytic power 0.79 at true r = 0.5 and 0.96 at 0.7; per-item false-positive rate 5%); label = argmax_i r_i. Pre-registered extension: if fewer than 80 natural failures are attributable, 500 further train episodes are added (about 75 failures x 54 = 4,050 episodes). E2 manufactured compositions on the same games: planted-only = a wrong-procedure item replaces the natural item at position 1 or 3 (k stays 3), 240 episodes, expected about 60% failures, about 145 failures x block A (24) = 3,500 episodes, the culprit verified by the same rule at 6 seeds; 60 of these failures also get block B (1,440 episodes) so the 12-seed rule is calibrated on known culprits (detection rate, and flag rate on the co-retrieved natural items); two-culprit = the planted item plus a token-matched paraphrase of it replace positions 1 and 3 (set = culprit, culprit-copy, helpful), 120 episodes reusing 120 planted-only games and seeds, expected about 80 failures x (block A 24 + the 4 remaining subsets of 2^3 x 6 seeds = 24) = 3,840 episodes, giving single-removal, pairwise-removal and exact-Shapley responsibility. E3 attribution methods on every failed episode with cost logged: position prior (0 calls); self-report (one extra call, forced choice over the k items plus 'none', two prompts x 3 sampling seeds); teacher-forced confidence gain of the failed action sequence with vs without each item from the served model's prompt log-probabilities (4 scoring passes, no environment steps); semantic overlap between item text and the failing action (0 calls); intervention replicate = block C, 6 fresh seeds x 4 arms on every attributable natural episode (<= 120 x 24 = 2,880 episodes), from which the m-seed LOO curve (m = 1..6, subsampled from C only) and the method ceiling are computed; exact Shapley on 60 attributable natural episodes (4 extra subsets x 6 block-A seeds = 1,440 episodes), which also supplies the pairwise-removal interaction test; pairwise removal on <= 60 memory-caused non-attributable natural failures (3 pairs x 6 seeds = 1,080 episodes) to split them into jointly attributable vs set-level; token-matched REPLACE instead of REMOVE on 60 attributable episodes (3 x 6 = 1,080) as the ground-truth-definition ablation. E4 static-procedure arm: the same <= 120 attributable natural episodes re-run with the type procedure sentence in the prompt, 6 fresh seeds x 4 arms (2,880 episodes); block C is the paired no-sentence control. E5 consumer swap: 100 attributable natural episodes under the second local backbone, 6 seeds x 4 arms (2,400 episodes, served on GPU 1 while GPU 0 runs E4); block C is the paired same-reader control. Totals: about 36,000 episodes (E0 500; pool 1,548; E1 labelling 13,000; E2 9,100; E3 6,500; E4 2,880; E5 2,400), about 30 h at the measured 1,200 episodes/h over two replicas, up to 40,000 episodes / 33 h with the extension; E2 runs on replica 2 while E1 labelling runs on replica 1; block C, E4, E5, Shapley and pairwise cells start once the attributable set is fixed; pre-registration after E0.

Baselines and Ablations:
Position prior and random choice as floors; block-C fresh 6-seed LOO as the intervention ceiling on natural episodes and the oracle (planted culprit) as the ceiling in E2; split-half label reliability (block A argmax vs block B argmax); m in {1,...,6} fresh seeds for the accuracy-versus-seeds curve (natural: block C against the 12-seed label; planted: block A against the oracle); REMOVE vs token-matched REPLACE ground truth on 60 episodes; planting at position 1 vs 3; planted-only vs two-culprit compositions for the same planted item on the same games; single vs pairwise removal (interaction intervals) and exact Shapley on the two-culprit arm and on 60 natural episodes; jointly-attributable vs set-level split of non-attributable memory-caused failures; self-report with two elicitation prompts x 3 seeds (own-stability); confidence gain on the failed actions vs on the expert action sequence; abstention scoring on non-attributable episodes for methods with a 'none' option; gross vs static-procedure-net attributability (E4 vs block C); reader swap (E5 vs block C).

Falsifiable Predictions:
Definitions: L = 12-seed argmax label on attributable natural episodes; C = accuracy of the fresh 6-seed block-C LOO argmax against L on the same episodes (the intervention-replicate ceiling); PP = position-prior accuracy on the same episodes; every method-versus-ceiling gap is paired over episodes (McNemar; paired bootstrap 90% CI). Validity gate: if C - PP < 20 points the natural label cannot separate methods, and P1-P3 are scored on the E2 planted-only set against the oracle instead and reported as such. P1 Self-report: acc(self-report) <= C - 25 points on attributable natural failures; falsified if acc(self-report) >= C - 5 with the paired 90% CI of the gap excluding 25 (E3 self-report vs block C). Positive control: on E2 planted-only failures self-report must name the planted culprit in >= 60%, otherwise the elicitation is judged broken and P1 is reported as uninterpretable. P2 Confidence gain: acc(confidence gain) <= C - 20 on attributable natural failures; falsified if it is >= C (matches or beats the fresh 6-seed replicate with zero environment steps) with the paired 90% CI of the gap excluding 20 (E3 vs block C); the mechanism favours the method, since a culprit raises the probability of the actions it induced, so this is the prediction most likely to fail. P4 Attributability: among memory-caused natural failures, <= 45% are single-item attributable under the 12-seed rule; falsified if >= 65% with the 95% bootstrap CI excluding 45% (E1). Calibration arm: on the 60 planted-only failures with 12 seeds the rule must detect the planted culprit in >= 80% and flag a co-retrieved natural item in <= 15%; if detection is lower the interval is loosened to one-sided 90% before any prediction is scored (pre-registered); the flag rate on non-memory-caused failures is reported as the empirical false-positive rate; the non-attributable memory-caused remainder is split by pairwise removal into jointly attributable and set-level. P5 Composition: (a) in the two-culprit composition, joint responsibility - each copy's single-removal r < 0.3 while removing both copies passes the attributability rule at 6 seeds (one-sided 90%) - holds in >= 50% of failed episodes; falsified if <= 20% with the 95% CI excluding 50%, i.e. one copy usually suffices to remove (position dominance); the same estimand as a paired contrast: the planted item's single-removal r under planted-only minus under two-culprit on the same games is >= 0.3, falsified if <= 0.1 with the paired 90% CI excluding 0.3 (E2). (b) Additivity on natural attributable failures: the interaction r_ij - r_i - r_j has a 90% interval excluding 0 in >= 30% of (episode, pair) cells; falsified if <= 12% (the nominal rate is 10%) with a cluster bootstrap over episodes (E3 Shapley cell, 60 x 3 pairs). P6 Consumer swap: on 100 attributable natural episodes, the reader-2 6-seed argmax (same rule; 'no item passes' and 'reader 2 succeeds at base' count as disagreement and are reported separately) agrees with L at least 20 points less often than the reader-1 block-C replicate does on the same episodes; falsified if the paired gap is <= 5 points with the 90% CI excluding 20 (E5 vs block C). P7 Static net: on the same <= 120 attributable episodes, the fraction that remain attributable to the same item under the 6-seed rule is >= 25 points lower with the type procedure sentence (E4) than in the paired no-sentence fresh-seed control (block C); falsified if the paired gap is <= 5 points with the 90% CI excluding 25; regression to the mean is identical in both arms, so only the sentence can produce the gap.

Label: r_i is a difference of two 12-seed proportions (SE <= 0.20); the rule (one-sided 95% Newcombe lower bound > 0) has analytic power 0.79 at true r = 0.5 and 0.96 at 0.7 with per-item false-positive 5% (about 14% per episode), is calibrated empirically on 60 planted failures at 12 seeds, and its empirical false-positive rate is read off non-memory-caused failures; split-half reliability of the argmax label (block A vs block B) is reported with Spearman-Brown, and the full r_i and p(empty) distributions are released for re-thresholding. Ceiling: every cheap method is compared with the block-C fresh 6-seed LOO on the same episodes, so label noise depresses method and ceiling alike and cannot by itself produce a gap; comparisons are paired (McNemar; paired bootstrap 90% CI over episodes). Sample sizes: 60-110 attributable natural episodes expected, with the pre-registered extension guaranteeing >= 80; at n = 80 a paired accuracy gap has SE about 0.07 (90% CI +/- 11.5 points), so falsifying margins are 15-20 points for P1, P2, P6 and P7, and the 10-point secondary clause of P3 is labelled low-power; P4's proportion over about 100-130 memory-caused failures has a 95% CI of +/- 9 points against a 20-point margin; P5a over about 80 two-culprit failures +/- 11 points against a 30-point margin; P5b uses a cluster bootstrap over episodes for 180 correlated pair cells. Neither the m-seed curve nor the ceiling shares seeds with the label. Sampling temperature is fixed from the E0 pilot and reported; self-report own-stability over 3 seeds x 2 prompts; thresholds, the tie rule, the calibration fallback and the ceiling gate (C - PP >= 20) are pre-registered after E0; multiplicity across seven predictions is handled by reporting all of them; both replicas busy with throughput logged per cell.

### proposal: stale_item_score_lag_after_rule_change
Title: How Long Does a Stale Item Stay Valuable? Score Lag and Co-Retrieval Contamination After a Manufactured Rule Change
Short Hypothesis: After a rule change that invalidates one procedure class in ALFWorld, the leave-one-out value of items encoding the old procedure flips from strongly positive to negative, but outcome-reinforced per-item scores flip only after a lag set by co-retrieval: stale items retrieved together with fresh post-drift items keep collecting credit for successes the fresh items produce, so the lag is at least three times longer in mixed sets than in stale-only sets, the stale item's ground-truth harm is masked by co-retrieved fresh items (a positive removal interaction of at least 8 net), a large fraction of stale items that are still being retrieved survives score-based pruning after 1,000 post-drift games, and confidence-gain scores computed on held-out trajectories barely move.

Experiments:
Severity is fixed before any run and all thresholds are pre-registered before D0 runs. Drift D1: a wrapper over the ALFWorld text env rejects 'clean {obj} with {recep}' with 'Nothing happens.', accepts 'rinse {obj} in {recep}' by stepping the underlying clean action, and rewrites the admissible-command list and observations accordingly. Every ground-truth cell, stream and closed loop runs at D1. D2 (additionally requires 'turn on {recep}' first, tracked as a shim flag) is a separate, unconditional severity arm for the stale-harm measurement only; it is reported as a dose-response row and cannot substitute for D1 in any prediction. Bank: expert bank with a bank-target partition (target games' own expert items excluded); the stale class is the 60 old-form clean items with the highest pre-drift retrieval frequency on train games, capped at 60 so every stale item accrues >= 20 post-drift retrievals per composition in the streams. Targets: clean-type games, ~45 valid + ~300 held-out train = 350 games. A standard cell is 350 games x 4 seeds = 1,400 paired episodes (a two-cell paired contrast has a ~+/-7 net CI); cells that carry a pre-registered threshold run at 8 seeds = 2,800 paired (~+/-5 on the contrast). D0 (positive control, run first, 8 seeds): post-drift k=0 and stale-only k=3 at D1 (2 x 2,800 = 5,600). D2 severity arm (3 seeds, scheduled last): k=0 and stale-only k=3 at D2 (2 x 1,050 = 2,100). Pre-drift ground truth of the same 3 items per target: k=0, k=3, REMOVE of the item at position 1 (3 x 1,400 = 4,200; the 45-valid-game part of each cell exists and is reused). Post-drift ground truth by composition at D1: stale-only REMOVE stale (1,400; the stale-only k=3 cell is D0's); fresh-only k=3, which is also the token-matched REPLACE arm of the mixed set (the stale item replaced by a fresh post-drift self-pool item of matched length), and fresh-only REMOVE fresh (2 x 1,400); cross-type-mixed k=3 (1 stale + 1 fresh + 1 heat/cool item) and REMOVE stale (2 x 1,400); mixed (1 stale at position 1 + 2 fresh post-drift self-pool items with the new form) as the interaction block at 8 seeds: k=3, REMOVE stale, REMOVE fresh-1, REMOVE both (4 x 2,800 = 11,200), giving the interaction (REMOVE both - REMOVE fresh-1) - (REMOVE stale - k=3) with MDE ~7 net; composition block total 18,200. Static-procedure sentence arms (Measurement C): a stale sentence with the old form vs a fresh sentence with the new form, k=0 otherwise (2 x 1,400 = 2,800). D3 scorer streams: pre-drift stream of 1,500 train games (similarity retrieval) to learn scores; post-drift similarity-only stream of 1,500 games with clean-type games oversampled to 50% and randomly assigned stale-only or mixed sets; because retrieval does not depend on scores here, every scorer's trajectory is computed post hoc from the same logs; a no-drift control stream of 750 games bounds score movement from sampling alone (3,750 total). Lag = number of post-drift retrievals of a stale item until its score falls below the median score of fresh post-drift items at that point in the stream; reported as the median over the 60-item stale class, per scorer, per composition. D4 closed loop at D1: admission of post-drift successes on, score-weighted retrieval, clean-type at 50%, 1,000 post-drift games for the outcome-reinforced, TD(lambda) and usage-decay scorers (3,000) and for the LOO-anchored online scorer at 2x cost (2,000); pruning threshold pre-registered as the 25th percentile of the scorer's scores over all bank items at each checkpoint; per-item post-drift retrieval counts are logged, and retention of a stale item above the threshold is classified as updated-retained (>= 10 post-drift retrievals, so the score has been updated) or frozen-retained (< 10, score starved of updates); a fixed probe of the 45 valid clean games x 2 seeds at checkpoints +300/+600/+1,000 per scorer plus one k=0 probe (12 x 90 + 90 = 1,170) gives probe harm vs k=0. D5 confidence gain is computed offline from forward passes on held-out trajectories generated without the scored item: pre-drift successful k=0 trajectories, post-drift successful k=0 trajectories and post-drift successful stale-removed mixed-set trajectories, all from the cells above (no new episodes); the item-induced version (trajectories generated with the item in context) is computed alongside only to quantify its inflation. Total 42,820 episodes = 5,600 + 2,100 + 4,200 + 18,200 + 2,800 + 3,750 + 6,170; at ~20 episodes/min on two 32B replicas (one per GPU, both busy) this is ~36 hours, ~1.5 days.

Baselines and Ablations:
Usage-decay (SF-AMS-style) as the entailed negative control: it cannot flip while the item is retrieved, so its trajectory checks the pipeline rather than testing a claim; LOO-anchored online scorer as the reference estimator with stated 2x cost; stale vs fresh static-procedure sentences to separate item credit from type credit; token-matched REPLACE (fresh-only k=3 relative to mixed k=3) vs REMOVE; set composition as the manipulated variable (four compositions); the removal interaction (pairwise minus both single removals) vs single removal in the mixed block; drift severity D1 vs D2 as a separate dose-response arm, never a substitute; a no-drift control stream of equal retrieval load to bound score drift due to sampling alone; retention split into updated-retained vs frozen-retained from logged retrieval counts; the pre-drift bank's TD(lambda) provenance depth ablated (episode-level only vs with derived items); confidence gain on held-out trajectories vs on item-induced trajectories reported side by side to quantify the self-fulfilling inflation of the latter (forward passes only).

Falsifiable Predictions:
P1 (positive control, pre-registered at D1 only): post-drift stale-only k=3 nets <= -10 vs k=0 on clean targets at D1 while the same items net >= +30 pre-drift (k=3 minus k=0). Falsified if the D1 stale-only-minus-k=0 contrast's 95% CI (~+/-5 at 2,800 paired) lies entirely above -5, i.e. the reader recovers from the rewritten admissible-command list; produced by the D0 cells. The D2 arm is a separate severity result and cannot rescue P1; D3-D5 run at D1 regardless of P1, and P2-P5 nulls are reported as tests only if P1 holds (with no ground-truth flip there is no lag to measure). P2 (contamination, headline): under the outcome-reinforced scorer, the median flip lag of stale items in mixed sets is >= 3x the lag in stale-only sets. Falsified if the lag ratio's 95% CI lies below 1.5, which occurs if one stale item sinks mixed sets as often as three do (the compliance trap of 2607.10608 predicts exactly this, so the outcome is open); produced by the D3 post-drift stream with random stale-only/mixed assignment. P3 (survival under pruning, contamination channel only): at +1,000 games in the closed loop, the updated-retained fraction of the 60-item stale class (>= 10 post-drift retrievals and score above the pre-registered 25th-percentile threshold) is >= 30% for the outcome-reinforced and TD(lambda) scorers and <= 5% for the LOO-anchored scorer. Falsified if the outcome-reinforced updated-retained fraction is <= 10% (Wilson interval on n = 60), which happens if score-weighted retrieval concentrates stale items into sets that fail and admission of fresh successes displaces them; frozen-retained stale items are reported separately and do not count toward P3; the closed loop's endogenous composition and the within-D4 LOO-anchored contrast are what D3 cannot produce; produced by the D4 +1,000 checkpoint with per-item retrieval logs. P4 (confidence gain is blind to the world): the stale class's trajectory-averaged confidence gain, computed on held-out post-drift successful trajectories generated without the item (k=0 and stale-removed), stays >= 50% of its level on held-out pre-drift successful k=0 trajectories, while the class's LOO value flips. Falsified if the post-drift gain drops by >= 50% (bootstrap CI over items x trajectories excludes the 50% level), which happens if the item's likelihood advantage is concentrated on the procedural command step, where the item now lowers the probability of the executed 'rinse' action, rather than on the shared navigation and pick-up steps; produced by D5 forward passes on trajectories the item did not induce. P5 (co-retrieval masks harm; ground-truth non-additivity): in the mixed composition the removal interaction (REMOVE both - REMOVE fresh-1) - (REMOVE stale - k=3) is >= +8 net, i.e. removing the stale item helps by at least 8 more points once one fresh item is gone than when both are present. Falsified if the interaction's 95% CI (MDE ~7 net at 4 x 2,800 paired) has an upper bound below 8, the outcome if the reader's compliance with the stale item does not depend on the fresh items present (additive harm); a CI covering both 0 and 8 is reported as inconclusive, not as support; produced by the D2 mixed interaction block on all 8 seeds.

Measurement and Noise Control:
All values are paired contrasts on the same (game, seed, set) with game-level bootstrap CIs and seed blocking; standard cells 1,400 paired (two-cell contrast ~+/-7 net), pre-registered-threshold cells 2,800 paired (~+/-5); the mixed-block interaction is a four-cell contrast with MDE ~7 net at 2,800 per cell, stated as such, with its inconclusive zone declared in advance. The lag is a per-item survival time; its median and CI come from a bootstrap over the 60-item stale class and the lag ratio's CI from paired resampling across compositions; each stale item has >= 20 post-drift retrievals per composition by design (clean-type oversampled to 50%, class capped at 60). Retention fractions carry Wilson intervals on n = 60 per checkpoint, split updated vs frozen from logged per-item retrieval counts. Confidence gain: bootstrap over items x held-out trajectories, with the item-induced-trajectory version reported alongside to quantify self-fulfilling inflation. Scorer trajectories from the similarity-only stream share the same episodes, so scorer comparisons are paired. The no-drift control stream (750 games) bounds the score movement attributable to sampling noise, and the LOO-anchored online scorer states the minimum lag achievable at 2x episode cost, so each scorer's lag is reported as multiples of that reference. Variance of each scorer's per-item score across 3 bootstrap resamplings of the episode order of the same logs (no new episodes) is reported alongside the number of retrievals needed for a stable stale/fresh ranking; a method whose stable ranking needs more re-runs than the LOO-anchored scorer is flagged. Cost: LLM calls per scorer per 1,000 post-drift games, including the 2x of the anchored scorer.
