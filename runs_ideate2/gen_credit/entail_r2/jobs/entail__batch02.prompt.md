### proposal: stage_conditional_item_value
Title: The Same Item, Three Moments: Stage-Conditional Counterfactual Value of Retrieved Experience in Multi-Step Agents
Short Hypothesis: A retrieved item's counterfactual value depends on when in the episode it is consumed. Injected at the step where the procedure is due, a procedure-bound item (top-1 expert trajectory or the static type-conditioned exemplars) retains its entry-stage value within the measurement floor, even in the games the no-memory reader never reaches pickup; injected at a recovery point after the reader is stuck, a same-type expert item whose trajectory is location-mismatched with the current game becomes net harmful while a location-matched one still helps, and the matched item buffers the mismatched one non-additively; across same-type candidate classes the class x stage interaction is at least as large a component of per-(class, game) value as class identity, so per-item scores learned at entry lose their rank signal at recovery.

Experiments:
Setting: ALFWorld valid (274 games), Qwen3-32B reader on two vLLM replicas (one per A100), expert bank of train trajectories, paired contrasts on the same (game, seed, prefix), 20 episodes/min with both replicas busy. T0 (prefix source): k=0 runs with full action traces on 8 seeds (2,192; the 2 sweep seeds reused where traces exist, 6 regenerated = 1,644). Stages, pre-registered: S0 = step 0. ### proposal: pairwise_removal_interaction_audit
Title: Removal Is Not Additive: Interaction, Copy Count and Seed Cost of Per-Item Credit on Natural and Manufactured Retrieved Sets in a Multi-Step Agent
Short Hypothesis: In ALFWorld with a Qwen3-32B reader and k=3 retrieval, the counterfactual value of a retrieved item is not an item property: it depends on what is co-retrieved with it, and it does so often enough in the retriever's own sets that per-item credit is estimator-dependent. Three claims, each with a named refuting arm. (i) NATURAL (headline): on the retriever's own top-3 sets, single-removal (leave-one-out, LOO) credit and exact Shapley credit computed on disjoint seed halves agree less than two LOO estimates agree with each other, and LOO's top item differs from Shapley's in >= 20% of sets net of the seed-noise floor; a rank-1-dominated, additive retrieval distribution (cross-estimator agreement at least as high as within-estimator agreement) refutes it. (ii) SIGN: on manufactured pairs beside a natural third item, the pairwise interaction I(a,b) = f(S) - f(S-a) - f(S-b) + f(S-ab) on the same (game, seed) is negative for a correct plus a planted conflicting same-type procedure (the wrong item dominates and masks the correct item's value; additive reconciliation gives I ~ 0 and correct-item rescue gives a positive I the size of the wrong item's harm), positive for two complementary fragments, and zero within a pre-registered equivalence bound for a cross-type control. (iii) DOSE: copy count is inert: two content-identical paraphrase copies of the rank-1 item add <= +2 net over the item alone, so the +8 net that natural items 2-3 add over item 1 (Measurement C: k3 - k1) is content, not repetition; amplification of >= +8 net refutes it. The number of seeds at which a per-item removal rank stabilizes is an estimand measured on 8-seed split halves, not a footnote.

Experiments:
(1) Materials: expert bank; k-sweep retrieval logs (runs/sweep/k_sweep_final.jsonl) for the valid games and the same retriever applied to bank-disjoint train-stream games; Qwen3-32B reader on two vLLM replicas (one per GPU, clients spread across both). Games: all procedure-bound types (clean/heat/cool) in valid_seen+valid_unseen (~140) plus ~160 bank-disjoint train-stream games of the same types (partition verified before pre-registration) = 300 games. Natural items r1, r2, r3 = the retriever's rank 1-3 for the game. Cell tiers, nested so subsets are shared across arms: Tier A = 300 games x 6 seeds = 1,800 paired cells (+/-3.5 net); Tier N = 200 of those games (140 valid + 60 train-stream) x 6 seeds = 1,200 cells (+/-4 net); Tier B = the same 200 games x 4 seeds = 800 cells (+/-5 net); Tier S = 100 of the Tier N games x seeds 7-8 (with seeds 1-6 from Tier N, an 8-seed subsample). Baselines and Ablations:
Additive model (I = 0; predict f(S-ab) from the other three cells) as the null for every class and for the natural lattice; DIG-style single-item confidence gain (log-prob of the first procedure-relevant action with vs without the item, one forward pass) as the cheap proxy, scored by rank correlation against LOO and Shapley on the natural items; position swap (b_c r1 r3) to separate slot from content in the CONFLICT sign; slot-count reference (r1 f1 f2) and the degraded copy (d) for the dose arm; no-op repeats as the intervention-free noise floor and disjoint seed halves as the estimator floor (LOO-vs-LOO, Shapley-vs-Shapley); second background (x' cross-type) for the CONFLICT class; static3 arm (Measurement C) to net out type-level credit; CONFLICT Shapley completion on 400 cells as the positive control that a per-class LOO-vs-Shapley excess is detectable when interaction is planted; Phase 0 paraphrase check (p1 vs r1) as the construction check that survives from the REDUNDANT class; per-type reporting (clean/heat/cool).

Falsifiable Predictions:
P1 NATURAL (headline). Arm: the 2^3 lattice of the retriever's own top-3 set on 200 games x 6 seeds, LOO and exact Shapley computed on disjoint seed halves A = seeds 1-3 and B = seeds 4-6. Prediction: Spearman(phi_LOO on A, phi_SH on B), averaged with its mirror, over the 600 natural items is <= Spearman(phi_LOO on A, phi_LOO on B) - 0.15; and on the 100-game 8-seed subsample (LOO on seeds 1-4, Shapley on seeds 5-8) LOO's top item differs from Shapley's in >= 20% of sets net of the LOO(1-4)-vs-LOO(5-8) floor. Falsified if the cross-estimator agreement is >= the within-LOO agreement (under additivity phi_SH = phi_LOO in every cell and the less noisy Shapley can only agree better, expected cross agreement sqrt(rho_LOO x rho_SH) >= rho_LOO), or if the net top-item disagreement is < 5 points: the retriever's sets are rank-1-dominated and effectively additive, and LOO is as good as Shapley. Primary statistic (i) at 600 items (game-bootstrap SE of the correlation gap ~0.05, gap 0.15 = 3 SE); (ii) at 100 sets (SE ~5 points, gap 15 = 3 SE) is the decision-relevant secondary. Not entailed: the natural interaction terms are unmeasured, and the k3 - k1 = +8 net marginal is compatible with both additive and interacting sets. P2 CONFLICT (Tier A, +/-3.5 net). I = f(r1 b_c r3) - f(b_c r3) - f(r1 r3) + f(r3) <= -8 net: the planted wrong item dominates when both are present and masks the correct item's solo value. Falsified if I >= 0: additive reconciliation (I ~ 0) or correct-item dominance (I ~ +harm(b_c) >= +10 net: the reader follows r1 in slot 1 and rescues the b_c-harmed baseline). Both refuting outcomes are reachable under the Phase 0 gates, which fix only the extremes (harm(b_c) >= 10, gain(r1 | r3) >= 8) and not which item the reader follows; gap 8 > CI half-width. The position swap (b_c r1 r3) separates slot-1 dominance from content dominance (I_swap - I at ~+/-6 net, reported). P3 COMPLEMENTARY (Tier N, +/-4 net). I >= +8 net (AND structure: neither fragment alone carries the procedure). Falsified if I <= +2 net: the prior (k=0 success 0.54) or the natural r3 completes the missing half so each fragment alone retains most of the gain; gap 6 > 4. P4 CONTROL (Tier A, +/-3.5 net). |I| <= 3 net with the 95% CI inside the pre-registered equivalence bound [-6, +6] (TOST). Falsified if I <= -9 net (the cross-type item distracts at the first decision point and masks at least 60% of r1's solo gain; blind1's -3 net under k0 makes this reachable) or I >= +9; the bound exceeds the CI half-width and the falsifier lies 6.5 net beyond the confirm band. P5 DOSE (Tier A, +/-3.5 net). Delta_add = f(r1 p1 p2) - f(r1) <= +2 net with CI inside [-6, +6]: content-identical copies are inert, so the +8 net that natural items 2-3 add over item 1 is content, not repetition. Falsified if Delta_add >= +8 net (repetition amplification: two content-free copies reproduce the natural marginal value of items 2-3, as repeated exposure in the compliance trap would give) or <= -8 (repetition harm). Reachable because adoption of r1 at k=1 is far from saturated (k1 - k0 ~ +21 net, k3 - k1 = +8), and decoupled from any Phase 0 gate. Secondary (Tier B, +/-5): degraded dose f(d d' d'') - f(d) within +/-3 net, falsified at |Delta| >= 10 (three copies of an incomplete plan push the reader to skip, or to complete, the procedure step); slot-count reference f(r1 f1 f2) - f(r1) reported. P6 SEED (Tier N and Tier S). Split-half Spearman reliability of per-item LOO across natural items, Spearman-Brown corrected to the half size, is <= 0.5 at 2 seeds per half (600 items) and reaches >= 0.7 only at 4 seeds per half (Tier S, 300 items). Falsified if 2 seeds per half already give >= 0.7 (two seeds suffice; the seed cost is a footnote). SE ~0.05. Per-set sign reproduction is reported under the pre-registered tie rule (a zero half-mean counts as not reproduced; the tie-excluded rate alongside); if 4 per half is still < 0.7 the seed count reaching 0.7 is reported by Spearman-Brown extrapolation, with the LLM-call cost of LOO (4 runs) versus Shapley (8 runs) at matched reliability. P7 BACKGROUND. I' = the CONFLICT interaction with r3 replaced by a natural cross-type x' (Tier B, +/-5) minus I from P2 (+/-3.5), propagated CI ~+/-6: |I' - I| <= 3 net (dominance is a property of the pair). Falsified if |I' - I| >= 12 net (three-body: whether the wrong item dominates depends on the same-type third item's support for the correct one, as a majority-following reader would give); gap 9 > 6.

Measurement and Noise Control:
All contrasts are paired on (game, seed) with identical prompt template, item order and sampling settings; every arm runs on both GPUs. Noise floors: the no-op repeat f(S)' gives the empirical flip rate under no intervention and the null for I via a permutation test that reshuffles which of the two full-set runs is labelled f(S); estimator agreement is computed on disjoint seed halves so LOO and Shapley never share a run (a shared f(S) would cancel out of LOO's argmax and fake a zero floor), with LOO-vs-LOO and Shapley-vs-Shapley on the same halves as the within-estimator floors. Power: per-cell I is bounded in [-2, +2] with SD ~0.7, so Tier A (1,800 cells) gives ~+/-3.5 net, Tier N (1,200) ~+/-4, Tier B (800) ~+/-5 at 95% after cluster bootstrap over games (1,000 resamples); a difference of two class means propagates to ~+/-6. Every equivalence-type prediction (P4, P5, P7) names a pre-registered bound wider than the CI half-width and a falsifier at least one CI width beyond the bound; directional predictions (P2, P3) separate confirm and falsify bands by more than the CI half-width; P1 and P6 state their SEs on 600 items and 100 sets. Tie rules pre-registered: the top item is the strict argmax of the seed-mean; sets tied under either estimator are undetermined, excluded and their fraction reported; a zero half-mean is a non-reproduced sign. Phase 0 gates must pass before any null is interpreted. Seed-count convergence of per-item LOO and of per-class I are reported as estimands (G15); LLM-call cost per unit reliability is stated for LOO (4 runs per set), LOO + pairs (7) and exact Shapley (8), and Shapley needs twice the re-runs of LOO by construction. Static3 (Measurement C) is the anchor: CONFLICT item credits are also reported with static3 present, against the measured k3 - static3 = +17.2 net [+11.9, +22.4]; no prediction here claims that residual vanishes. All credits reported per task type (clean/heat/cool).
