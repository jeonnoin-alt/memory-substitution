### proposal: hole_backfill_static_counterfactual

Short Hypothesis: For the same retrieved item in the same (game, seed) at the same slot, three references give three different credits: hole (the slot left empty; what CUE-R REMOVE, Shapley-RAG and DIG estimate), backfill (the retriever's next-ranked bank item enters in rank order; exactly what pruning the item from the bank realizes for that game) and static (the type's best static exemplar takes the slot; Measurement C at item level). For natural expert items at rank 1 the backfill recovers at least 8 net of the hole loss, so hole credit over-states the realized value of pruning by at least that much; the item still carries at least 8 net beyond the static exemplar (about half of the measured set residual k3 - static3 = +17.2 sits in the top slot), giving the ordering s(H1) < s(S1) < s(B1); and summed hole credits over-predict the realized loss of pruning two items by at least 12 net, while the summed-backfill error is reported against a pre-registered +/-10 margin. Title: Harm Travels, Help Does Not: Re-measuring Learned Memory Utilities Against Replace-Static Intervention Value After Reader Swap, Store Growth and Layout Shift
Short Hypothesis: A learned per-item utility is a statement about one reader, one store and one query distribution. After the reader is swapped, the store grows with self-written items, or the layouts change from seen to unseen, the intervention-measured value of helpful expert items is lost (retained value falls by at least 10 net beyond what a same-reader re-measurement loses, or by at least 5 net relative to the unchanged store) while the value of wrong-procedure items persists (at most -10 net in every condition), so the learned utility keeps its power to flag harm and loses its power to rank help; and re-validating it by a short intervention costs fewer episodes than re-learning it from the stream under the new reader, judged against a criterion independent of both routes. Contribution in one sentence: intervention-measured transfer asymmetry of item value across three drift arms, each with a same-reader replication or composition control that nets out re-measurement noise, plus the cost curve of re-validation versus re-learning against an independent criterion.

Experiments:
- Ground-truth protocol (all cells): forced-retrieval intervention paired on (game, sampling seed). For item i of class C, 24 same-type evaluation games x 2 sampling seeds = 48 pairs (96 pairs = 24 games x 4 seeds in the three natural-composition EP cells). Arms per pair: WITH (i in the k=3 set) and REPLACE-static (i replaced by the type's static procedure sentence padded to i's token count; Measurement C baseline, so the primary estimand is item value net of the static prompt); REMOVE (k=2 set) only in the additivity probe. Two compositions: THIN = i at rank 1 plus the two natural cross-type retrievals as fillers, so i carries the set (chosen because in the natural k=3 set the measured +17.2-net residual over static3 is shared by three items, leaving about 4-6 net per item, below per-item noise); NATURAL = i plus the natural same-type top-2 of the store in force. Seed families: F1 = {s1,s2}, F2 = {s3,s4}, F3 = {s5,s6}. Per-item SE about 10 net at 48 pairs (7 net at 96); a 40-item cell = 1,920 pairs gives a class CI of about +/-4.5 net (3,840 pairs: +/-3.2); paired differences between two cells on the same items, games and seeds have CI about +/-5 (48 pairs) and +/-4 (96 pairs). Throughput 20 episodes/min per replica, 40/min on both GPUs: a 40-item 2-arm 48-pair cell = 3,840 episodes = 1.6 h; a 2-arm 96-pair cell = 7,680 episodes = 3.2 h.
- E0 Reader pilot and gates (0.5 h, one candidate per GPU): closed pre-registered candidate list in order: (1) Qwen3-14B, (2) Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct. Both are run on all 274 evaluation games (140 valid_seen + 134 valid_unseen) x 1 seed at k=0, static3 and k=3 (822 episodes each) and both are reported in full. Selection rule, fixed before any E2 cell: the first candidate in list order whose k=0 success on valid_seen lies in [0.30, 0.70] and whose k3 - k0 gain is >= +10 net. If neither passes, the B arm becomes a decoding-regime swap of the reference reader (thinking on versus off), pre-registered as the fallback and labelled the weaker manipulation in every table; there is no search beyond the two candidates. The chosen reader's k3 - static3 residual is reported as a moderator next to P1, whose sharp form is that a reader which demonstrably profits from the bank does not profit from the same items.
- E1 Learning pass (3 h per stream on one GPU; two streams in parallel, one per GPU): per-item outcome Q (count-average and EMA alpha=0.1) from one pass over the 3,553-game train stream under the reference reader with the expert bank and natural k=3 retrieval; MemQ-style TD(lambda) on the same log as a second scorer; feedback counts recorded. Second stream seed for test-retest. A mixed-stream variant refits the same Q on the union of the seed-1 log and the E4 unseen-game log, so that its LS rank is not fixed by seen-only training.
- E2 Ground-truth cells (33.2 h; hours per cell): classes EP (40 expert procedural items with positive nested-k position value in the existing logs), W (40 planted wrong-procedure items), LS (40 location-specific expert items whose receptacle claim holds in the seen layout; regex plus manual check). (a) R1: reference reader, expert bank, valid_seen, THIN, F1, 2 arms; EP, W, LS: 4.8 h. (b) R2: as R1 with F2, classes EP and W: 3.2 h (same-reader replication; the baseline for P1). (c) B: second reader, expert bank, valid_seen, THIN, F2, 2 arms; EP, W, LS: 4.8 h. (d) U: reference reader, expert bank, valid_unseen type-matched games, THIN, F1, 2 arms; EP, W, LS: 4.8 h. (e) U-edit: the 40 LS items with the location clause removed (token-matched), U as in (d): 1.6 h. (f) R-nat: reference reader, expert bank, valid_seen, NATURAL (expert store's same-type top-2), 96 pairs (F1 u F2), 2 arms, EP: 3.2 h. (g) R-swap: as (f) but the natural top-1 replaced by the highest-similarity self-pool item (one-item composition change, store unchanged), EP, 96 pairs: 3.2 h. (h) G: reference reader, store = expert bank + 1,892 self-pool items, NATURAL from the grown store (its own top-2, which may be self-written; the fraction of sets containing a self-written item is reported and, if below 0.5, the cell is re-run once with the self-written top-1 forced in, pre-registered), valid_seen, EP, 96 pairs, 2 arms: 3.2 h. (i) Composition replicates: 20-item subsamples of W and of LS re-measured under NATURAL in R (F1, 2 arms): 1.6 h. (j) Criterion K for P5: second reader, NATURAL (expert store), F3, EP, 2 arms, 48 pairs: 1.6 h. (k) Additivity probe: 20 EP items in R-nat with arms REMOVE-i, REMOVE-j (natural top-1) and REMOVE-ij, 48 pairs: 1.2 h.
- E3 Re-validation cost (stream 1.5 GPU-pair h; analysis free): (i) intervention route: the B THIN F2 pairs subsampled at 4, 8, 16, 24, 48 per item; (ii) re-learning route: the 3,553-game train stream under the second reader (3 h on GPU 1), Q_B fitted on prefixes of 500, 1,000, 2,000 and 3,553 games. Both routes are scored by Spearman with the independent criterion K (cell j: different composition and different seed family from route (i); K is measured under the natural-retrieval regime that route (ii) learns from, so it favours the stream route if anything). K's split-half reliability (s5 vs s6) is the ceiling drawn on both curves. Episodes are counted in total for both routes; no zero-marginal accounting.
- E4 Lag of the learned rule (0.1 h): the reference Q continues online through the 134 unseen games x 2 seeds under natural retrieval; for LS items the retrievals until Q (count-average and EMA) crosses below the store median are recorded and set against the intervention pairs needed to detect the LS class sign at -10 net (40 x 16 pairs = 640 pairs, CI +/-7.8). Reported as an estimand; no numeric prediction.
- Budget: E0 0.5 + E1 3.0 (two 3-h streams, one per GPU) + E2 33.2 + E3(ii) 1.5 + E4 0.1 = about 38 GPU-pair hours, about two days wall-clock with both GPUs busy: the second reader serves on GPU 1 for cells (c), (j) and E3(ii) while GPU 0 serves cells (d)-(i) and E1; nohup, pid files, resumable append-only JSONL; gates,

Baselines and Ablations:
- Scorers: per-item outcome Q (count-average, EMA alpha=0.1), MemQ-style TD(lambda), usage-count control, similarity-only control, the mixed-stream Q variant; Q_B re-learned under the second reader for E3.
- Ground truth: REPLACE-static (item value net of the type's static procedure sentence) in every cell as the primary estimand; REMOVE in the additivity probe; the nested-k position contrast from the existing logs as the free reference used only to pick the EP class.
- Composition as a manipulated variable: THIN and NATURAL for the EP class in R (cells a, f), a one-item swap control (g) and the grown store (h); 20-item NATURAL replicates of W and LS (i) so every class is reported under two compositions; the additivity probe (k) tests pairwise versus single removal on 20 EP items with their natural top-1 (interaction = value(REMOVE-ij) - value(REMOVE-i) - value(REMOVE-j)), reported with its CI and not assumed zero.
- Same-reader replication (b) on an independent seed family as the loss and flip baseline for P1.
- Class ablation: LS items with the location clause edited out under U (e), the mechanism conjunct of P3.
- Controls kept from the prior round: same pool and bank-target partition in all conditions; token-matched placebos; k=0 and static3 arms per reader as floors; the aggregate k3 - static3 residual (+17.2 net at the natural bank) re-reported per reader and condition as a sanity check.

Falsifiable Predictions:
- P1 (headline) Transfer asymmetry under reader swap. Selection: items with |REPLACE-static value| >= 10 net in R1 (THIN, F1); counts per class reported (pre-registered fallback: if fewer than 20 EP items pass, the cutoff drops to 7 net and is reported with the resulting CI). Retained value v' = sign(R1) x value. Same-reader loss L_RR,C = mean over selected items of [v'(R1) - v'(R2)] measures the selection's regression to the mean and is reported; swap loss L_C = mean over selected items of [v'(R2) - v'(B)], paired on item and game with seed family F2 in both cells, so neither term was used for selection and regression to the mean is excluded by construction. Prediction: L_EP >= 10 net and L_W <= 4 net, and the W class mean under B (all 40 items) is <= -10 net. Falsified if L_EP <= 3 net (help transfers), or L_W >= 10 net, or the W class mean under B is >= -5 net (harm does not transfer). Noise: about 25 selected EP items with per-item paired SE 14 net give CI about +/-5.5 on L_EP, so 10 versus 3 is resolvable; L_W over about 35 items has CI +/-4.6 (4 versus 10 resolvable); the W-under-B cell has CI +/-4.5 (-10 versus -5 at the edge, reported with its CI). The excess sign-flip rate f_RB - f_RR per class is reported with 1,000-item bootstrap CIs at 5, 10 and 15 net cutoffs as the descriptive companion, not as the test. Arms: E2 (a), (b), (c); moderator: the chosen reader's k3 - static3 residual from E0. Not entailed: the compliance-trap result predicts a weaker second reader is harmed less by wrong items (L_W large, W under B near zero), and a reader that follows expert procedures as the reference does gives L_EP near zero; both are reachable under cell (c).
- P2 Store-growth substitution (NATURAL, EP, 96 pairs). Gate (pre-registered): EP class mean REPLACE-static in R-nat >= +6 net (CI +/-3.2); if it fails P2 is void and reported as a failed positive control (expert items have no measurable individual value beside two expert neighbours, itself a within-expert-store substitution result quantified by the additivity probe). Prediction: G - R-nat <= -5 net and G - R-swap <= -5 net on the same items, games and seeds, i.e. the item is worth at least 5 net less in the grown store's natural set than in the expert store's set both without and with one self-written neighbour, so the drop is carried by the grown store and not by a self-written neighbour as such. Falsified if G - R-nat >= 0 net (no collapse) or G - R-swap >= 0 net (the one-item swap already produces the drop: a composition effect, not a store effect). Paired-difference CI about +/-4 net at 96 pairs, so -5 versus 0 is resolvable. Arms: E2 (f), (g), (h). Not entailed: the grown store's top-2 may remain expert items, or self-written neighbours may be worse substitutes and raise the item's value.
- P3 Stale-true items flip sign across layouts (THIN). Prediction: LS class mean REPLACE-static >= +10 net in R1 and <= -10 net in U, and the location clause carries the flip: U-edit - U >= +10 net on the same games and seeds. Falsified if the U effect is >= -2 net (the reader does not act on a wrong location claim), or R1 - U < 8 net, or U-edit - U <= +3 net (the flip is carried by the rest of the item). Noise: each cell CI +/-4.5; R1 - U on different game sets CI about +/-6.4; U-edit - U paired CI about +/-5; the gaps (12, 12 and 7 net) exceed the corresponding CIs. The seen-only Q rank of LS items is reported but not predicted (it is fixed by seen-only training); the mixed-stream Q's rank of the LS class median and the E4 lag are reported as estimands. Arms: E2 (a), (d), (e); E1 mixed-stream fit; E4.
- P4 Validity drop of the reference Q. Gate (pre-registered, separate from the falsifier): Spearman(reference Q, R1 REPLACE-static ground truth) pooled over EP, W, LS (120 items) >= 0.30, else P4 is void and reported as a failed positive control. Prediction: the pooled Spearman falls by >= 0.20 from R1 to B and from R1 to U (raw and disattenuated by split-seed reliability: s1 vs s2 for R1 and U, s3 vs s4 for B), and each drop exceeds the test-retest gap between the two E1 stream seeds' Qs by >= 0.10. Falsified if the drop is < 0.10 in both B and U, or does not exceed the test-retest gap. Secondary: within-class Spearman on EP alone, to show the drop is not only the W-versus-rest contrast that P1 predicts stays. Noise: bootstrap over the same 120 items of the difference of two Spearmans (SE about 0.06), so 0.20 versus 0.10 is resolvable. Arms: E2 (a), (c), (d) scored by E1. Shared term with P1: W values transferring floors the pooled drop; that is why the falsifier sits at 0.10 and the EP-only correlation is reported.
- P5 Re-validation cost against an independent criterion. Criterion K: B-condition EP values under NATURAL composition and seed family F3 (cell j), independent of route (i) in both composition and seeds, and measured under the retrieval regime route (ii) learns from; K's split-half reliability (s5 vs s6) is the ceiling shown on both curves. Prediction: the intervention route (B THIN F2 subsampled) reaches Spearman >= 0.5 with K at <= 16 pairs per item (<= 16 x 2 arms x 40 = 1,280 episodes), while the re-learned Q_B is <= 0.25 at 2,000 stream games (2,000 episodes) and does not reach 0.5 by 3,553. Falsified if Q_B reaches 0.5 by 1,000 games, or the intervention route is <= 0.25 at 48 pairs (thin-composition values do not predict natural-composition values under the new reader, so the cheap probe cannot re-validate either). Noise: Spearman over 40 items has SE about 0.12 at rho 0.5, so 0.5 versus 0.25 is two SE; bootstrap over items. Episodes counted in total for both routes. Arms: E3 (i), (ii); E2 (j).
- Gates (pre-registered pass/fail preconditions, not hypothesis tests; each has a reachable failing outcome and a stated consequence): G1 second reader from the closed two-candidate list under the E0 rule, both candidates reported, fallback = decoding-regime swap; G2 harm class manufacturable: W class mean REPLACE-static in R1 <= -10 n

Measurement and Noise Control:
Every comparison is paired on (game, sampling seed) across arms; comparisons across conditions on the same games (R1 vs R2 vs B; R-nat vs R-swap vs G; U vs U-edit) are also paired on item and game, and R1 vs U (different game sets) is reported with the independent-cells CI. Seed families F1 {s1,s2}, F2 {s3,s4}, F3 {s5,s6}: the selection set (R1, F1) never shares seeds with the cells a prediction is decided on (R2 and B use F2, K uses F3). Per-item SE about 10 net at 48 pairs and 7 net at 96; class CI +/-4.5 net at 1,920 pairs and +/-3.2 at 3,840; paired-difference CI about +/-5.5 over 25 selected items (P1), +/-4 at 96 pairs (P2), +/-5 at 48 pairs (P3 edit), +/-6.4 across different game sets (P3 R1 - U). Item-level statistics (losses, flip rates, Spearmans) are bootstrapped over items (1,000 resamples) with games as a second cluster; split-seed reliability of each cell is reported and used to disattenuate correlations, which are also reported raw. The regression to the mean of the |value| >= 10 selection is measured directly as the same-reader loss L_RR and excluded from the swap loss by construction. Learning-pass test-retest is measured by a second stream seed for the reference Q and bounds the P4 drop that estimator noise alone could produce. The aggregate k3 - static3 residual (+17.2 net at the natural bank, CI half-width about +/-5) is re-reported per reader and condition as a sanity check and is the reason the per-item cells use the THIN composition and the natural-composition cells use 96 pairs. Cost and noise are estimands: P5 reports episodes at each reliability for both routes with K's reliability ceiling; E4 reports the lag of the learned rule. Throughput per GPU (episodes/min, tok/s) is logged and both readers occupy one GPU each during the B, K and E3(ii) phases.
