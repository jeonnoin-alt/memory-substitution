### proposal: guilt_by_coretrieval
Title: Guilt by Co-retrieval: Measuring Reward Contamination of Learned Memory Utilities Against Leave-One-Out Ground Truth
Short Hypothesis: When an outcome reward is shared by all co-retrieved memory items, the learned utility of an innocent item is pulled toward the counterfactual value of whatever it is co-retrieved with (RoMeRL's memory-reward trap), and this contamination is a dose-response quantity: it grows with the fraction of an item's retrievals that co-occur with a planted wrong-procedure item, it is not removed by provenance-DAG credit (MemQ) or by reduced-order utility coordinates (RoMeRL) because both still pool siblings retrieved for the same task, and it is removed at a bounded cost by correcting a random 20% of stream episodes with one leave-one-out re-run per retrieved item. Contribution in one sentence: the first measurement of learned per-item memory utilities against intervention ground truth under manipulated co-retrieval, giving the contamination slope, the failure of two published remedies, and a costed fix.

Experiments:
- Ground-truth protocol (shared by all cells): forced-retrieval intervention on the same (game, seed). For a sampled item i of class C, take 24 same-type evaluation games x 2 seeds = 48 paired episodes with i forced into the k=3 set at rank 3 (the other two slots are the natural top-2 retrievals, or a manipulated composition). Arms per pair: WITH (i present), REMOVE (k=2 set), REPLACE-static (i replaced by the type's static procedure sentence padded to i's token count; Measurement C baseline, so REPLACE-static is item value net of the static prompt). Per-item effect = mean paired success difference; per-item SE about 0.10 at p=0.5; a 40-item class = 1,920 pairs gives a class CI of about +/-4.5 net. Cost per class per condition: 40 items x 48 pairs x 2 extra arms = 3,840 episodes = 3.2 h at 20 episodes/min on two replicas.
- E0 Positive controls, cheapest first (run before any confirmatory cell; ~5 h). (a) Planted wrong-procedure class W (40 items, one per task type x layout, each swapping the target receptacle or omitting the clean/heat/cool step): REMOVE effect must be <= -10 net, else contamination cannot be manufactured and the study stops. (b) Per-item Q learned at dose 0 must separate W from bystander class B with Spearman >= 0.3 against LOO on the pooled 80 items, else the scorer implementation is rejected.
- E1 Dose manipulation during learning (3 stream passes, ~9 h): for the 40 B items, intercept retrieval in the train stream and with probability d in {0, 0.25, 0.5} replace the rank-3 co-retrieved item with a same-type W item. Log per episode the retrieved ids, positions, outcome, and the planted flag. All four scorers are fit offline from each pass's log (per-item Q with count-based averaging and with alpha=0.1; MemQ TD(lambda) with lambda in {0.5, 0.9} over a DAG whose edges connect retrieved items to episodes and episodes to items written from them; RoMeRL-style coordinates: a 16-dim per-task-type utility state updated by the outcome, item utility read out as the projection of the item's embedding onto the state; corrected Q: for a random 20% of episodes run one REMOVE re-run per retrieved item and update only items whose removal flips success).
- E2 Ground truth (two compositions, ~9.6 h): forced-retrieval REMOVE and REPLACE-static for the 40 B items under (i) clean composition and (ii) composition with one W item present, plus the 40 W items under clean composition. Report per-item and class effects, the split-seed reliability of per-item LOO, and Spearman(scorer, LOO) per dose x composition with 1,000-item bootstrap CIs, raw and disattenuated.
- E3 Additivity (~5.3 h): 100 manufactured sets, 50 helpful+conflicting (B, W) and 50 helpful+redundant (B, B' of the same type and procedure), each on 16 paired games x 2 seeds, four arms: full, -B, -W (or -B'), -both. Interaction = pair-removal effect minus the sum of single-removal effects; report the fraction of sets with |interaction| >= 10 net and the rank agreement between single-removal scores and pair-conditioned scores.
- E4 Decision cost: false-prune rate (fraction of items in a scorer's bottom quartile whose LOO >= +5 net) per dose and scorer; cost of the corrected-Q estimator in extra episodes per stream episode versus a full LOO pass (3 re-runs per episode).
- Budget: E0 5 h + E1 9 h + corrected-Q re-runs 1.8 h + E2 9.6 h + E3 5.3 h = about 31 h on two A100s, one vLLM replica per GPU, workers tuned to keep each replica's running queue >= 8; nohup, pid files, resumable JSONL. Pre-registration of thresholds before E1.

Baselines and Ablations:
- Scorer baselines (one per family): per-item outcome Q (REMEMBERER/MemRL style); MemQ-style TD(lambda) over provenance DAG; RoMeRL-style reduced-order coordinates; usage-count (retrieval frequency) as a no-outcome control; similarity-only rank as a no-learning control.
- Ground-truth baselines: REMOVE versus REPLACE-static (item value net of the type's static procedure); nested-k position contrast from runs/sweep/k_sweep_final.jsonl as the free position-level estimate to compare against item-level LOO.
- Composition ablation: every per-item estimate reported under the clean and the W-containing composition; additivity ablation via E3.
- Learning-rule ablations: count-average versus constant-alpha Q; MemQ lambda in {0.5, 0.9}; RoMeRL state dimension in {4, 16, 64}; corrected-Q subsample rate in {10%, 20%, 40%} to trace the cost-recovery curve.
- Self-pool control: repeat E2 on 40 self-written items to test whether contamination slopes differ for self versus expert items.
- Token- and rate-matched placebo: the planted W item is token-matched to the item it displaces, and dose 0 passes receive an equally frequent replacement by a random same-type expert item, so the manipulation changes only wrongness, not set size or churn.

Falsifiable Predictions:
- P1 (headline) Contamination slope: Spearman(per-item Q, clean-composition LOO) over the 40 B items falls by >= 0.15 from dose 0 to dose 0.5, while the B class's clean-composition REMOVE effect changes by < 5 net between the dose-0 and dose-0.5 ground-truth cells (the items did not change; their scores did). Falsified if: the correlation drop is < 0.05 (bootstrap CI includes 0) although E0(a) confirmed that W items cause failures. Arm: E1 dose-0 versus dose-0.5 per-item Q scored on the same 40 B items in E2(i). Not entailed: the planted item may fail to change outcomes when a helpful item is present (compliance trap not triggered at k=3), in which case the shared reward carries no contamination; E0(a) and E2(ii) measure exactly that.
- P2 Published remedies do not fix it: at dose 0.5, neither MemQ TD(lambda) nor RoMeRL-style coordinates exceeds per-item Q in Spearman with LOO by more than 0.10. Falsified if: either scorer beats per-item Q by >= 0.15 with a bootstrap CI excluding 0.10. Arm: E1 dose-0.5 log, all scorers fit on the same log, scored against E2(i).
- P3 Costed fix: the 20%-subsample LOO-corrected Q at dose 0.5 recovers at least half of the P1 drop (its correlation is within 0.10 of dose-0 per-item Q) at <= 0.6 extra episodes per stream episode. Falsified if: recovery is < 0.05 or the corrected scorer needs the 40% subsample (1.2 extra episodes per episode) to reach the same point. Arm: corrected-Q cells at 10/20/40% in E1.
- P4 Non-additivity: in >= 30% of helpful+conflicting sets the sum of single-removal effects exceeds the pair-removal effect by >= 10 net, and in helpful+redundant sets the two single removals are each within +/-5 of zero while the pair removal is <= -10 net in >= 30% of sets. Falsified if: |interaction| < 5 net in > 80% of sets of either kind. Arm: E3 four-arm cells.
- P5 Decision consequence: at dose 0.5 the per-item Q false-prune rate on B items (bottom quartile with LOO >= +5) is >= 2x its dose-0 rate. Falsified if: the ratio is < 1.3 with the paired-bootstrap CI excluding 2. Arm: E4 on E1 dose cells.
- Positive control that must pass first: E0(a) W-class REMOVE effect <= -10 net and E0(b) dose-0 Spearman >= 0.3 on the pooled B+W items; if either fails the confirmatory cells are not run and the null is reported as a failed manipulation, not as a finding.

Measurement and Noise Control:
Paired design throughout: every intervention arm re-runs the same (game, seed) with the retrieved set changed only at the manipulated slot; the ALFWorld env is seeded and the vLLM sampler is seeded per episode. Per-item LOO reliability is estimated by split-seed halves (Spearman-Brown corrected) and used to disattenuate scorer correlations; both raw and corrected values are reported. Class cells are 40 items x 48 pairs = 1,920 pairs (about +/-4.5 net); the dose contrast is a within-item comparison (same 40 B items scored under three learning passes), with 1,000 item-level bootstraps for correlation differences. Run-to-run noise of the learning pass itself is measured by repeating the dose-0 pass with a second stream seed (3 h) and reporting the Spearman between the two dose-0 per-item Q vectors; a dose effect must exceed this test-retest gap. Pre-registered thresholds: 0.15 for P1, 0.10 for P2 and P3, 10 net for P4. Cost is an estimand: episodes per item to reach split-half LOO reliability 0.5 (expected 24 to 48 pairs) and extra episodes per stream episode for corrected Q. Both GPUs stay busy by running the E2 ground-truth cells on one replica while the next E1 pass runs on the other; throughput (episodes/min, tok/s per GPU) is logged with each status update.

### proposal: admission_pruning_audit_random_holdout
Title: Admit Everything Once: Auditing Write-Time Utility Scores and Outcome-Based Pruning Against Realized Counterfactual Item Value
Short Hypothesis: Write-time admission scores (A-MAC-style factor admission, RPE-critic surprise/utility, DIG-style confidence gain) do not predict the realized counterfactual value of a memory item beyond rank correlation 0.2, so bypassed items are as valuable as admitted ones; and outcome-based pruning beats random pruning only for items that have accumulated at least 8 feedback events, a threshold fewer than one store item in five reaches in a 3,553-game stream, so most pruning decisions in a self-evolving store are made at random-equivalent precision. Contribution in one sentence: a randomized-holdout audit that puts admission and pruning error rates, and the feedback count a pruning decision needs, on an intervention scale.

Experiments:
- Ground-truth protocol (shared by all cells): forced-retrieval intervention on the same (game, seed). For a sampled item i of class C, take 24 same-type evaluation games x 2 seeds = 48 paired episodes with i forced into the k=3 set at rank 3 (the other two slots are the natural top-2 retrievals, or a manipulated composition). Arms per pair: WITH (i present), ADD/REMOVE (item absent, k=2 set), REPLACE-static (i replaced by the type's static procedure sentence padded to i's token count; Measurement C baseline, so REPLACE-static is item value net of the static prompt). Per-item effect = mean paired success difference; per-item SE about 0.10 at p=0.5; a 40-item class = 1,920 pairs gives a class CI of about +/-4.5 net. Cost per class per condition: 40 items x 48 pairs x 2 extra arms = 3,840 episodes = 3.2 h at 20 episodes/min on two replicas.
- E0 Positive control, cheapest first (~3.5 h): 40 expert procedural items versus 40 planted wrong-procedure items under forced-retrieval ADD must separate by >= 15 net; otherwise the audit lacks power and stops.
- E1 Admission audit (~6.4 h of re-runs plus forward passes): shadow store = expert bank + 1,892 self-pool items + 40 planted W items. Three write-time scorers: (a) A-MAC-style factors (novelty = 1 - max cosine to store, task-type relevance, outcome confidence from the source episode's step count, redundancy with same-type items, specificity), combined by the published weighting where stated, else equal weights with a pre-registered threshold at the 60th percentile; (b) RPE-critic: surprise = reader NLL of the source trajectory's actions given the k=3 retrieved set at write time, utility = critic-predicted gain (a logistic model on surprise, novelty and type fitted on the first 500 stream games); (c) DIG-style confidence gain = change in reader log-probability of the successful action sequence of one held-out same-type train game with versus without the candidate in context (two forward passes). Sample 160 candidates stratified by each scorer's quartiles; measure ADD versus REPLACE-static on 24 games x 2 seeds each. Report Spearman(score, realized ADD) raw and disattenuated, and the realized ADD of admitted versus bypassed items per rule.
- E2 Stream passes (~6 h): pass A with per-item outcome Q (count-average) multiplying similarity in the retrieval rank; pass B with SF-AMS-style utility (usage, redundancy, recency) multiplying similarity. Log retrieved ids, positions, outcomes, and per-item feedback counts. Record the distribution of feedback events per store item and the retrieval share of the 40 planted W items per stream third.
- E3 Pruning audit (~5.8 h): for each of {random, outcome-Q bottom 20%, SF-AMS bottom 20%} and each feedback-count stratum {1-3, 4-7, >= 8}, sample 24 pruned items and measure realized LOO (REMOVE and REPLACE-static) on 16 games x 2 seeds. Metrics: harmless-prune rate (LOO <= 0), harmful-item capture (fraction of items with LOO <= -10 net that the rule prunes, using the E1 sample as the reference population), and the smallest stratum at which outcome-Q exceeds random by >= 0.15.
- E4 Composition check: the 40 planted W items and 40 highest-scoring admitted items are re-measured under a second composition (k=3 set with one self-written item swapped for an expert item) to test that admission-score rank agreement with realized value does not depend on the co-retrieved set (~3.2 h).
- Budget: E0 3.5 h + E1 6.4 h + E2 6 h + E3 5.8 h + E4 3.2 h = about 25 h on two A100s, one replica per GPU, E1 re-runs on one replica while E2 pass A runs on the other; nohup, pid files, resumable JSONL; pre-registration before E1.

Baselines and Ablations:
- Admission baselines: admit-all (shadow store), random admission at the same rate, similarity-to-existing-store threshold (novelty only), and the three write-time scorers.
- Pruning baselines: random pruning at matched count; outcome-Q; SF-AMS usage-decay; oracle pruning by realized LOO from E1 as the ceiling.
- Ablations: scorer components (novelty-only, redundancy-only, confidence-gain-only); DIG held-out game count 1 versus 3; feedback-count strata; outcome-Q with constant alpha versus count-average.
- Controls demanded by prior rounds: self-pool versus expert items reported separately; REPLACE-static placebo so admission value is net of the type procedure; token-matched planted items; both compositions in E4.
- Free baseline: nested-k logs give a position-level estimate for expert items to compare with the admission scorers on the same items.

Falsifiable Predictions:
- P1 (headline) No write-time scorer exceeds Spearman 0.2 (disattenuated 0.3) with realized ADD value over the 160-candidate sample, and admitted minus bypassed realized ADD is within +/-5 net for every rule. Falsified if: any scorer reaches Spearman >= 0.4 with a bootstrap CI excluding 0.2, or admitted minus bypassed >= 5 net with CI excluding 0. Arm: E1 forced-retrieval cells on admitted versus bypassed strata. Not entailed: E0 confirms that realized values differ across items, so a scorer could in principle track them; confidence gain in particular could.
- P2 Feedback threshold: outcome-Q pruning exceeds random pruning in harmful-item capture by >= 0.15 only in the >= 8 feedback-event stratum and by < 0.10 in the 1-3 stratum. Falsified if: the >= 0.15 advantage appears already at 1-3 events (large-effect items are identifiable from a handful of outcomes). Arm: E3 outcome-Q versus random within the 1-3 stratum.
- P3 Coverage: fewer than 20% of store items reach >= 8 feedback events in the 3,553-game stream under similarity x utility retrieval. Falsified if: >= 35% do. Arm: E2 pass-A retrieval log.
- P4 Usage is not value: SF-AMS-style pruning's harmful-item capture is within +/-0.10 of random in every stratum, while its harmless-prune rate exceeds random (it removes redundant items with LOO near zero). Falsified if: SF-AMS capture >= random + 0.20 in any stratum. Arm: E3 SF-AMS cells.
- P5 Entrenchment versus correction: the planted W items' retrieval share in the last stream third relative to the first is <= 0.7 under outcome-Q and >= 1.5 under SF-AMS utility. Falsified (Q part) if: the ratio under outcome-Q is >= 0.9, i.e. verified failures do not demote wrong items because the co-retrieved helpful items rescue enough episodes. Falsified (SF-AMS part) if: the ratio is <= 1.1 because redundancy and recency terms cancel the usage loop. Arm: E2 pass A and pass B retrieval logs. The SF-AMS part is the weaker prediction since the usage loop is positive feedback by construction; it is reported as secondary and the Q part is the tested claim.
- Positive control that must pass first: E0 expert-versus-W separation >= 15 net; if it fails the audit is reported as underpowered, not as a null.

Measurement and Noise Control:
All value measurements are paired on (game, seed) with the retrieved set changed at one slot. Per-item SE about 0.10 at 48 pairs; quartile strata of 40 items = 1,920 pairs give +/-4.5 net. Rank correlations use 1,000 item-level bootstraps and are disattenuated by split-seed reliability of realized value; both are reported. The E2 stream pass is repeated for pass A with a second stream seed to obtain the test-retest Spearman of per-item Q, which bounds how much of any pruning imprecision is estimator noise versus rule error. Harmful-item capture uses the E1 sample's realized values as the reference set, with the -10 net cutoff pre-registered. Cost is reported per decision: forward passes per candidate for each admission scorer, and episodes per item to reach split-half reliability 0.5 of realized value. Throughput logged per cell; both GPUs busy by overlapping E1 re-runs with E2 passes.

### proposal: utility_half_life_consumer_swap
Title: Harm Travels, Help Does Not: Re-measuring Learned Memory Utilities Against Leave-One-Out Value After Reader Swap, Store Growth and Layout Shift
Short Hypothesis: A learned per-item utility is a statement about one reader, one store and one query distribution: after the reader is swapped, the store grows with self-written items, or the layouts change from seen to unseen, the counterfactual value of helpful items collapses or changes sign while the value of wrong-procedure items persists, so the learned utility keeps its power to flag harm and loses its power to rank help, and re-validating it by intervention on a class sample costs fewer episodes than re-learning it from the stream. Contribution in one sentence: intervention-measured sign-transfer asymmetry of item value across three drift arms, with the lag and cost of the learned utility behind it.

Experiments:
- Ground-truth protocol (shared by all cells): forced-retrieval intervention on the same (game, seed). For a sampled item i of class C, take 24 same-type evaluation games x 2 seeds = 48 paired episodes with i forced into the k=3 set at rank 3 (the other two slots are the natural top-2 retrievals, or a manipulated composition). Arms per pair: WITH (i present), REMOVE (k=2 set), REPLACE-static (i replaced by the type's static procedure sentence padded to i's token count; Measurement C baseline, so REPLACE-static is item value net of the static prompt). Per-item effect = mean paired success difference; per-item SE about 0.10 at p=0.5; a 40-item class = 1,920 pairs gives a class CI of about +/-4.5 net. Cost per class per condition: 40 items x 48 pairs x 2 extra arms = 3,840 episodes = 3.2 h at 20 episodes/min on two replicas.
- E0 Cheapest first (~2 h): pilot the second backbone (candidates: Qwen3-14B or Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, whichever serves under vLLM on one A100) on 274 valid_seen games at k=0 and k=3; it is usable only if k=0 success lies in [0.30, 0.70] and the k=3 gain is >= +10 net (positive control), else the next candidate is tried. Positive control for the harm class: planted W items' REMOVE effect under the reference reader <= -10 net.
- E1 Learning pass (~3 h): per-item outcome Q (count-average and alpha=0.1) from one train-stream pass under the reference reader with the expert bank; record feedback counts. MemQ-style TD(lambda) fit on the same log as a second scorer.
- E2 Ground truth in four conditions (4 x 6.4 h): classes EP (expert procedural, positive nested-k position value), W (planted wrong-procedure, 40 items), LS (location-specific: expert items whose text names a receptacle location that holds in the seen layout; 40 selected by regex plus manual check), SW (self-written). Conditions: R = (Qwen3-32B, expert bank, valid_seen); B = (second backbone, expert bank, valid_seen); G = (Qwen3-32B, expert + self pool, valid_seen; forced item sits among the grown store's natural top-2); U = (Qwen3-32B, expert bank, valid_unseen, type-matched games). Each cell: 40 items x 24 games x 2 seeds, arms WITH / REMOVE / REPLACE-static. Sign flips are counted only for items with |value| >= 10 net in R.
- E3 Re-validation cost (~3.5 h): (i) intervention: subsample the B-condition pairs at 4, 8, 16, 24, 48 per item and compute the Spearman against the other seed half's full estimate; (ii) re-learning: run the train stream under the second backbone (3 h), fit Q_B on prefixes of 500, 1,000, 2,000 and 3,553 games, and compute the Spearman with the held-out-seed B ground truth. Report episodes to reach Spearman 0.5 for both routes, counting only episodes beyond the agent's normal operation for the stream route (zero) and total for the intervention route, and also total wall-clock episodes for both.
- E4 Lag under the learned rule: continue the reference Q online through the U-condition games (unseen stream of 274 x 2) and record, for LS items, the number of retrievals until Q crosses below the store median, versus the number of intervention pairs needed to detect the sign flip at the +/-5 net level.
- Budget: E0 2 h + E1 3 h + E2 25.6 h + E3 3.5 h + E4 0.5 h = about 35 h; during B and E3(ii) the second backbone runs on GPU 1 while the reference reader serves the G and U cells on GPU 0; nohup, pid files, resumable JSONL; pre-registration before E2.

Baselines and Ablations:
- Scorers: per-item outcome Q (reference), MemQ-style TD(lambda), usage-count control, similarity-only control; Q re-learned under the second backbone for E3.
- Ground truth: REMOVE and REPLACE-static (value net of the static procedure sentence) in every condition; the nested-k position contrast from the existing logs as the free reference for EP items.
- Composition: the G condition changes the co-retrieved set by construction; the R condition is additionally re-measured with one self-written item swapped in (second composition) so that the store-growth effect is separated from a mere set-composition change (~3.2 h, included in E2 if budget allows, else reported as pilot).
- Class ablation: LS items with the location claim edited out (token-matched) under U, to test that the sign flip is carried by the location claim and not by the rest of the item.
- Controls from prior rounds: same pool and bank-target partition in all conditions; self-pool items measured separately; token-matched placebos; memory-absent (k=0) arm per condition as the floor.

Falsifiable Predictions:
- P1 (headline) Sign-transfer asymmetry under reader swap: among items with |value| >= 10 net in R, the fraction whose REPLACE-static sign flips in B is >= 2x higher for EP than for W items (help is consumer-specific, harm transfers). Falsified if: the two flip fractions are within +/-0.10 of each other or W flips more often, with 1,000-item bootstrap CIs. Arm: E2 condition B, classes EP and W. Not entailed: a weaker reader could follow expert procedures equally well (no EP flips) or ignore wrong items (W flips), and the compliance-trap result predicts the opposite direction for weaker agents.
- P2 Store-growth substitution: the median REMOVE effect of EP items in G is <= 50% of its value in R, and their REPLACE-static effect in G is within +/-5 net of zero. Falsified if: the median REMOVE effect in G is >= 80% of R, or REPLACE-static stays >= +5 net. Arm: E2 condition G versus R on the same 40 EP items and games. Not entailed: self-written items may be worse than or non-overlapping with expert items.
- P3 Stale-true items: LS items have REPLACE-static >= +5 net in R and <= -5 net in U, and the reference-trained Q ranks the LS class median in the top half of the store. Falsified if: the U-condition LS effect is within +/-5 of zero (the reader does not act on location claims), or the LS class median Q rank is in the bottom half (the train stream already penalised them). Arm: E2 conditions R and U on the LS class; E1 Q ranks.
- P4 Validity drop: Spearman(reference Q, ground truth) pooled over the four classes falls by >= 0.20 from R to each of B, G and U. Falsified if: the drop is < 0.10 in all three, or the R correlation is itself < 0.30 (then the prediction is void and reported as a failed positive control). Arm: E2 all conditions scored by the E1 Q.
- P5 Re-validation cost: intervention on a 40-item class reaches Spearman 0.5 against held-out ground truth at <= 8 pairs per item (640 episodes per class) while the re-learned Q_B needs >= 2,000 stream games to reach 0.5 on the same items, a ratio >= 3 in total episodes. Falsified if: Q_B reaches 0.5 within 1,000 stream games, or intervention needs > 16 pairs per item. Arm: E3 (i) versus (ii) curves.
- Positive controls first: E0 second-backbone k=3 gain >= +10 net and W-class REMOVE <= -10 net under the reference reader; otherwise the study is reported as an unusable second reader or an unmanufacturable harm class, not as a null.

Measurement and Noise Control:
Paired on (game, seed) across arms and, for R versus G and R versus U comparisons, on the same items; the B condition uses the same games and seeds with a different reader, so item-level differences are within-item. Class cells 40 x 48 = 1,920 pairs (+/-4.5 net). Sign flips are counted only for items whose R value clears the +/-10 net cutoff, with flip fractions bootstrapped over items. Rank correlations are disattenuated by split-seed reliability of the ground truth in each condition and reported raw as well. Learning-pass test-retest is measured by a second stream seed for the reference Q (3 h) and bounds the P4 drop that estimator noise alone could produce; P4 must exceed that gap. The aggregate unseen-equals-seen result already measured on this node is the backdrop; the claim is at class level and the aggregate k=3 gain is re-reported per condition as a sanity check. Cost is an estimand in E3. Throughput per GPU is logged; the two readers occupy one GPU each during B and E3(ii).
