  reason: Items are selected on an R measurement whose per-item noise (10 net) equals the selection cutoff, and the two classes have asymmetric true magnitudes by construction (W is planted and gated at <= -10 net while EP values are small position-level gains), so regression to the mean alone gives EP a several-fold higher re-measurement sign-flip rate with no reader swap involved, and both mechanisms the proposal names (weaker reader, compliance trap) push in the same direction.
