- P1 [near_entailed] falsifier: pooled paired total-token difference (k=3 - k=0) > 0 with a 95% CI excluding 0, i.e. injecting memory costs more tokens than it saves
  reason: Gate 0 deletes the only regime that produces the falsifier (a small conversion rate where every episode pays ~430 tokens/step and few 30-step timeouts are shortened), and because prompt tokens grow super-linearly with steps, any gate-passing gain of >=15 points converts 30-step episodes into ~12-step ones and forces the negative sign; the confirming clauses (CI excluding +430 x mean steps, >=25% fall on three types) are also wider than the stated falsifier, leaving an unscored middle band.
  fix: Add an arm where the gain is known to be small (a low-gain or held-out type, or a bank deliberately below the gate) and report its token sign, and pre-register a symmetric decision band so that 'difference > 0 with CI including 0' is an outcome rather than a gap.
- P2 [open] falsifier: the memory-free SFT student's episodes are not shorter in total tokens than base k=0
  reason: The proposal itself calls direct SFT the weak baseline, so a student that fails to convert timeouts keeps hitting the 30-step cap and can plausibly spend the same or more tokens; the contrast is paired and token differences resolve at ~5% of the mean.
- P3 [entailed] falsifier: all three N* lie within 1.5x of each other
  reason: The three N* share one numerator and are three linear functionals of the same logged episodes, so their ratios reduce to (injected token delta) / (injected delta minus step saving) and to the measured prefix-cache hit rate; they can coincide within 1.5x only if the step saving is ~0 (the outcome Gate 0 stops the study for, and which P1 asserts is large) and the cache does nothing while the k=3 block sits in the prefix by construction.
  fix: Compute the three N* on a cell where the step saving is near zero by design (single-step task type, or an arm below Gate 0) so the conventions can converge, and pre-register the ratio test as a CI on (injected delta)/(total delta) rather than on three separately reported N*.
- P4 [entailed] falsifier: as stated, the CD student reaches base+k=3 accuracy at k'=0 or k'=1 while the SFT student needs k'>=3, making CD's per-episode saving large enough to repay the teacher pass
  reason: At a matched accuracy level the two N* share the denominator terms (same c_context(A), near-identical student episode cost) while CD's numerator strictly adds the teacher pass, so N*_CD > N*_SFT is arithmetic; the one stated escape is self-defeating under this proposal's own headline convention, since if total tokens fall with k (P1) then the SFT student needing k'>=3 makes it cheaper, not more expensive, than a CD student at k'=0.
  fix: Add an estimand where the teacher pass is amortized (charged once across multiple students, or excluded as a sunk pool-construction cost) and restate the falsifier as N*_CD <= N*_SFT at any shared accuracy level with a bootstrap CI excluding equality.
